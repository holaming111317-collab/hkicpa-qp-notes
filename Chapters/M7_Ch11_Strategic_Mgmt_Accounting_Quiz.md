**Retest R46** — M7 Ch7 Q5 五度交手（R34/R38/R42 未過）→ 新情境（計算題）
Penguin Ltd has bonds with a total market value of HK$80,000,000 and a yield to maturity of 4%. It has 4,000,000 ordinary shares currently trading at HK$20 each. The company has just paid a dividend of HK$1.00 per share, and dividends are expected to grow at 3% per annum indefinitely. The profits tax rate is 20%.
**Calculate Penguin Ltd's WACC. Show ALL components: kₑ, after-tax k_d, weights, and the final WACC.**

**Retest R47** — M7 Ch8 Q4 四度交手（R40/R43 未過）→ 新情境（計算題）
Koala Ltd operates in a zero-tax world. It generates perpetual annual cash flows of HK$400,000 and its cost of equity (unlevered) is 10%. The company borrows HK$1,000,000 at an interest rate of 6% and uses the proceeds to repurchase shares.
**Calculate the required rate of return on Koala Ltd's equity after the repurchase using MM Proposition 2 (kₑ = k₀ + (D/E)(k₀ − k_d)). Show all workings. Do NOT use the WACC formula.**

**Retest R48** — M7 Ch8 Q5 四度交手（R41/R44 未過）→ 新情境（計算題）

**Calculate V_L using MM with taxes (V_L = V_U + T_c × D). Show all workings.**

**Retest R49** — M7 Ch9 Q5 三度交手（R45 未過）→ 新情境（計算題）
Falcon Retail Ltd reports for the year: net sales HK$10,950,000; cost of goods sold HK$7,300,000; average inventory HK$400,000; average accounts receivable HK$600,000; average accounts payable HK$500,000. (Use a 365-day year.)
**Calculate the cash conversion cycle (CCC). Show each component: DIO, DSO, DPO.**

**Retest R50** — M7 Ch10 Q5 錯（EFN）→ 新情境（計算題）
Victoria Trading Ltd's current net sales are HK$8,000,000, forecast to grow by 25% next year. Total assets vary with sales at 60% of sales; accounts payable vary with sales at 10% of sales; total costs are 85% of sales; the tax rate is 25%; and the dividend payout ratio is 50%.
**Calculate the external funding needed (EFN) for next year. Show all components: ΔA, ΔAP, and retained earnings.**

**Question 1 (MCQ — technique classification)**

Harbour Electronics Ltd wants to determine the optimal quantity of a component to order each time. The component sells at a constant rate throughout the year, and management wants to find the order quantity at which the costs of holding the component (storage, financing, insurance and spoilage) exactly offset the costs of running short of it. Which inventory management approach is MOST appropriate?

A. Just-in-time (JIT) inventory system
B. Materials requirement planning (MRP)
C. Economic order quantity (EOQ) model
D. Product life cycle costing

**Question 2 (MCQ — cost of quality classification)**

Which of the following is classified as an EXTERNAL failure cost under cost of quality (COQ) theory?

A. Training employees to implement and maintain a total quality management (TQM) scheme
B. Auditing the quality systems to ensure they meet previously agreed specifications
C. Reworking defective materials identified before the products are delivered to customers
D. Repairing products returned by customers under warranty undertakings

**Question 3 (MCQ — framework discrimination)**

Which of the following statements BEST describes strategic management accounting (SMA)?

A. SMA is an internally focused system that quantifies all information and is best suited to implementing strategies developed elsewhere
B. SMA combines internal and external attributes — such as competitors' cost trends, prices and market share — to realise the organisation's objectives through forward-looking projections and analysis
C. SMA is the branch of accounting responsible for producing the statement of financial position and the statement of profit or loss for external users
D. SMA originated as an expansion of cost accounting and focuses on a single issue such as cost, volume or profit for a single fiscal period

**Question 4 (Calculation — target costing)**

Dragon Appliances Ltd can only sell its new rice cooker at the market-driven price of HK$249 per unit because of intense competition. The company requires a profit margin of 20% on the selling price. Calculate the target cost per unit (in HK$, to two decimal places if necessary).

**Question 5 (Calculation — throughput analysis)**

Using throughput analysis, calculate the return on investment (ROI) (express as a percentage).
