**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Nordwind GmbH is a company incorporated and tax resident in a jurisdiction which has NO comprehensive double tax agreement (CDTA) with Hong Kong. It manufactures ski equipment overseas. Which of the following Hong Kong activities, standing alone, would constitute a **permanent establishment (PE)** of Nordwind GmbH in Hong Kong?

A. Maintaining a showroom in Tsim Sha Tsui used solely for the storage, display and delivery of its ski equipment.

B. Maintaining a liaison office in Hong Kong which only collects market information and conducts advertising for Nordwind GmbH.

C. Appointing a Hong Kong representative who habitually negotiates and concludes sales contracts with Hong Kong customers on behalf of Nordwind GmbH.

D. A Nordwind GmbH director staying in the same Hong Kong hotel room for three weeks while attending trade fairs and meeting potential customers.

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

ShowTime HK Ltd, a Hong Kong resident company, enters into a contract directly with Golden Voice Inc., a company incorporated in the US, to procure a one-night concert performance in Hong Kong by its non-resident US singer. ShowTime HK Ltd pays the performance fee directly to Golden Voice Inc. The two companies deal with each other on a principal-to-principal basis, and ShowTime HK Ltd is not an agent of Golden Voice Inc. or the singer. Under which IRO provision may the non-resident be charged to Hong Kong profits tax in the name of ShowTime HK Ltd?

A. Section 20A, because any Hong Kong person making a payment to a non-resident is automatically an agent of that non-resident.

B. Section 20B, because it applies to Hong Kong resident persons who pay or credit sums to non-resident entertainers or sportsmen performing in Hong Kong, even on a principal-to-principal basis.

C. Section 14 only, and no assessment can be raised on ShowTime HK Ltd at all because it is not an agent.

D. No Hong Kong tax is payable because the singer performed in Hong Kong for one night only, which is below the 183-day threshold.

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

Alpen Foods AG, a company incorporated and tax resident in Switzerland, manufactures cheese overseas. It has no permanent establishment in Hong Kong and does not carry on a business in Hong Kong. It appoints DairyLink HK Ltd, an unrelated Hong Kong company, as its consignment agent to sell cheese to customers in Hong Kong and Mainland China. For the year, gross sale proceeds are: Hong Kong customers HK$8,000,000; Mainland China customers HK$12,000,000. The Commissioner has not approved any reduced rate. What is the consignment tax payable to the IRD under s.20A(3)?

A. HK$20,000,000 × 1% = HK$200,000

B. HK$8,000,000 × 1% = HK$80,000

C. HK$8,000,000 × 16.5% = HK$1,320,000

D. Nil, because Alpen Foods AG has no permanent establishment in Hong Kong and does not carry on a business there.
