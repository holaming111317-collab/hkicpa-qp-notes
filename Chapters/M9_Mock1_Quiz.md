**Question 2** — Additional assessment（普通個案）
The IRD raises an additional assessment in **February 2026** on Mr Ho for income omitted from his return for the year of assessment **2019/20**. There is NO fraud or wilful evasion.
(i) Statutory deadline? (ii) In time? (Yes / No) (iii) Section?

**Question 3** — Additional assessment（fraud 支線）

(i) Statutory deadline? (ii) In time? (Yes / No) (iii) Section?

**Question 4 (MCQ)** — Deductibility
Which of the following is DEDUCTIBLE under s.16(1) IRO in computing assessable profits?
A. A fine imposed on the company for a criminal offence
B. Rent paid for premises used in the production of assessable profits
C. Capital expenditure on the purchase of machinery
D. Domestic expenses of a director

**Question 5 (MCQ)** — Capital vs revenue
A company carries on substantially its whole business through one sole distributorship agreement, which is terminated. The compensation received for the termination is:
A. Revenue in nature — it compensates for lost profits
B. Capital in nature — it sterilises the company's profit-making structure
C. Revenue in nature — it is received in the ordinary course of business
D. Partly capital and partly revenue, apportioned by reference to the profits lost

**Question 6** — Consignment sales
A non-resident consignor sells goods to Hong Kong customers through a consignment agent in Hong Kong. Gross proceeds from sales to Hong Kong customers amount to HK$8,000,000. The consignor's actual profits arising in Hong Kong **cannot be readily ascertained**. Calculate the **assessable profits** and cite the governing section.

**Question 7 (MCQ)** — Two-tiered profits tax rates
A corporation has assessable profits of HK$2,500,000 for the year of assessment. Its profits tax payable is:
A. HK$412,500 (HK$2,500,000 × 16.5%)
B. HK$247,500 (HK$2,000,000 × 8.25% + HK$500,000 × 16.5%)
C. HK$206,250 (HK$2,500,000 × 8.25%)
D. HK$375,000 (HK$2,500,000 × 15%)

**Question 8** — Non-resident entertainer
A non-resident individual entertainer receives HK$1,200,000 for a performance in Hong Kong. Calculate the **Hong Kong profits tax payable** on a withholding basis.

**Question 9** — Rental value
Mr Cheung is provided with a rent-free residence by his employer. His income from the employer (before rental value) is HK$720,000. Calculate the **rental value** to be included in his assessable income. State the governing section.

**Question 10 (MCQ)** — Benefits in kind
Mr Wu's employer pays his son's school fees of HK$180,000 per year directly to the school. For salaries tax purposes, this payment is:
A. Not taxable — it is not paid to Mr Wu and cannot be converted into cash
B. Taxable — an education benefit provided by the employer is assessable
C. Taxable only if the fees exceed 10% of Mr Wu's salary
D. Not taxable — education expenses are personal and domestic in nature

**Question 11** — Time apportionment
Mr Chow has a Hong Kong employment with annual income of HK$1,095,000. During the year of assessment (365 days), he rendered services in Hong Kong for **300 days** and outside Hong Kong for 65 days. He does not qualify for full exemption. Calculate his **assessable income** under time apportionment and cite the governing provision.

**Question 12** — MPF deduction
Mr Yip's mandatory contributions to the MPF scheme for the year of assessment total HK$21,000. Calculate the **maximum deduction** allowable for salaries tax. State the governing section.

**Question 13** — Approved charitable donations
Ms Lee's income (before the donations deduction) for the year of assessment is HK$500,000. She made approved charitable donations of HK$200,000. Calculate the **maximum deduction** allowable. State the governing section.

**Question 14** — Property tax
A property is let for HK$30,000 per month for the whole year of assessment (12 months). Rates of HK$12,000 are paid by the **owner**. Calculate the **net assessable value (NAV)** of the property.

**Question 15** — Premium
A landlord receives a premium of HK$120,000 for granting a lease of **3 years**. Under s.5B(4) IRO, calculate the amount of premium to be included in the assessable value **for each year of assessment** (before the 20% statutory deduction).

**Question 16 (MCQ)** — Personal assessment
Which of the following persons is ELIGIBLE to elect personal assessment?
A. A Hong Kong permanent resident aged 18 or above with property income and business income
B. A limited company incorporated in Hong Kong
C. A non-resident individual with no Hong Kong income
D. A partnership carrying on business in Hong Kong

**Question 17** — AVD + BSD
A non-Hong Kong permanent resident purchases a Hong Kong residential property for HK$6,000,000. Calculate the **total ad valorem stamp duty (AVD) and buyer's stamp duty (BSD)** payable.

**Question 18 (MCQ)** — SSD
Mr Sin sells a residential property after holding it for **18 months**. The Special Stamp Duty (SSD) rate applicable is:
A. 20%
B. 15%
C. 10%
D. 0%

**Question 19 (MCQ)** — Source of office

A. The place where the director attends board meetings
B. The central management and control of the company
C. The place where the director resides
D. The place where the director's remuneration is paid

**Question 20 (MCQ)** — Withholding

A. Dividends
B. Interest
C. Royalties for the use of intellectual property in Hong Kong
