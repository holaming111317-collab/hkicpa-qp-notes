## Question 1 — Correct Answer: **HK$2,005**

**Model Answer (English):**
- Contract note (seller): HK$2,000,000 × **0.1%** = **HK$2,000**
- Instrument of transfer: **fixed HK$5**
- **Total stamp duty payable by the seller = 2,000 + 5 = HK$2,005**

【解題思路】
- 股票轉讓印花稅：**每張 contract note 0.1%**（2023 年 11 月起，由 0.13% 下調），買賣雙方各自俾自己嗰張；instrument of transfer 係 **fixed HK$5**。
- 呢條已經係**第 3 次交手**：W8 用 0.026（無呢個率）、W9 用 0.01（即 1%，大咗十倍）。0.1% = **0.001**，唔係 0.01。
- 口訣：**「0.1% + HK$5」**。2,000,000 × 0.001 = 2,000；加 5 = **2,005**。

## Question 2 — Correct Answer: **(i) 31 March 2025 (ii) Yes (iii) s.60(1)**

**Model Answer (English):**
"Under s.60(1) IRO, an additional assessment must be raised within 6 years after the end of the year of assessment. YA 2018/19 ended on 31 March 2019 → the statutory deadline is **31 March 2025**. The assessment raised in March 2025 is therefore **in time (Yes)**."

【解題思路】
- YA 2018/19 完結日 = **31 March 2019**；+6 年 = **31 March 2025**。
- 2025 年 3 月發出 → 死線前 → **Yes, in time**。
- Fraud / wilful evasion 先至係 10 年（proviso）；題目講明冇 → 用六年檔。

## Question 3 — Correct Answer: **HK$108,000 — s.9(2)**

**Model Answer (English):**
"Under s.9(2) IRO, the rental value of a rent-free residence provided by an employer is **10% of the employee's income from the employer**. Rental value = 10% × HK$1,080,000 = **HK$108,000**."

【解題思路】
- Rental value = **10% of income from the employer**——永遠唔係實際租金、唔係差餉估價。
- 1,080,000 × 10% = **108,000**。條文：**s.9(2)**（唔好寫 s.26(G)、唔好寫 s.9(1)）。

## Question 4 — Correct Answer: **d) HK$0**

**Model Answer (English):**
"SSD applies only where the residential property is held for **36 months or less**: 20% if held for 6 months or less; 15% if more than 6 but not more than 12 months; 10% if more than 12 but not more than 36 months. The property was held for **40 months**, which exceeds 36 months → **no SSD is payable (HK$0)**."

【解題思路】
- 三檔：**≤6 個月 20%｜>6–12 個月 15%｜>12–36 個月 10%｜>36 個月 0%**。
- 1 Mar 2023 → 1 Jul 2026 = 40 個月 > 36 → **零 SSD**。
- 陷阱位：見到大銀碼物業唔好反射式落 10%——**先數持有月數**。

## Question 5 — Correct Answer: **HK$18,000 — s.26G**

**Model Answer (English):**
"Under s.26G IRO, the deduction for mandatory MPF contributions is capped at **HK$18,000 per year**. Mr Yip's contributions of HK$25,000 exceed the cap → maximum deduction = **HK$18,000**."

【解題思路】
- MPF 強制性供款扣稅上限 = **每年 HK$18,000**（即每月 1,500 × 12），**s.26G**。
- 25,000 > 18,000 → 封頂 **18,000**。
