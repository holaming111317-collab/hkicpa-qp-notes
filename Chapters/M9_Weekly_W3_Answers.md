# M9 保溫卷 W3 — Model Answers & Explanations

**Question 1 — Correct answer: HK$80,000, s.20A(3)**
Under **s.20A(3)**, where a non-resident sells goods on consignment through a Hong Kong branch/agent and actual profits cannot be readily ascertained, assessable profits are deemed to be **1% of the gross proceeds from sales to Hong Kong customers**: 8,000,000 × 1% = **HK$80,000**.
（W2 你答 10%——嗰個係兩級制/其他嘢嘅數字，consignment 固定係 **1%**。條文係 s.20A(3)，唔係 s.15(5)。）

**Question 2 — Correct answer: b**
Under **s.9(2)**, where an employer provides a rent-free residence, the rental value is **10% of the employee's income from the employer**: 720,000 × 10% = **HK$72,000**. The actual rent paid by the employer (300,000) is irrelevant — (a) is the classic trap.

**Question 3 — Correct answer: b**
An HKPR acquiring his/her **first and only** residential property pays **AVD at Scale 2 rates** (the old lower rates) and is **exempt from BSD** (BSD only applies to non-HKPR buyers). The 15% flat AVD (Scale 1 Part 1) and BSD target non-first-time / non-HKPR buyers.

**Question 4 — Correct answer: b**
Compensation for the termination of an agreement that represents **substantially the whole of the business** is a **capital receipt** — it compensates for the sterilisation/destruction of the entire profit-making structure, not merely for loss of income in the ordinary course.

**Question 5 — Correct answer: HK$18,000, s.26G**
Mandatory MPF contribution = 5% × (30,000 × 12) = 5% × 360,000 = 18,000, capped at the statutory maximum of **HK$18,000** per year under **s.26G**. Deductible amount = HK$18,000.
