# M9 保溫卷 W4 — Model Answers & Explanations

**Question 1 — Correct answer: HK$60,000, s.9(2)**
Under **s.9(2)**, rental value = **10% of income from the employer**: 600,000 × 10% = **HK$60,000**. The actual rent paid by the employer (240,000) is **irrelevant**.
（W3 你揀咗 actual rent——呢個係 s.9(2) 嘅經典陷阱。Rental value 永遠係 **10% of income**，唔係市值租金、唔係實付租金。）

**Question 2 — Correct answer: b**
Compensation for terminating **one of several** ordinary commercial agreements, where the business structure is not significantly affected, is a **revenue receipt** — it simply replaces the income that agreement would have generated. Contrast: termination of a **sole** agreement representing substantially the whole business = capital (sterilisation of the entire profit-making structure).

**Question 3 — Correct answer: HK$18,000, s.26G**
Mandatory contribution = 5% × (40,000 × 12) = 5% × 480,000 = 24,000, but capped at the statutory maximum of **HK$18,000 per year** under **s.26G**. Deductible amount = **HK$18,000**.
（留意：個 cap 係**全年** 18,000（= 每月 1,500）。題目問「for the year」就答 18,000；條文係 **s.26G**，唔係 s.9。）

**Question 4 — Correct answers: (i) 31 March 2027 (ii) Yes (iii) s.60(1)**
YA 2020/21 ended 31 March 2021 → 6-year deadline = **31 March 2027** (s.60(1)). Discovery in March 2027 is **within time** — just inside the deadline.

**Question 5 — Correct answer: c**
Deemed assessable profits = 2/3 × 2,400,000 = 1,600,000. UK Co is a **corporation** → two-tiered rate **8.25%**: 1,600,000 × 8.25% = **HK$132,000**.
（(b) 120,000 = 7.5% 係個人/unincorporated 嘅率——主體係公司就用 8.25%。）
