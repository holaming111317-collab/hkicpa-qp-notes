**Question 1** — s.60(1) 輪轉（**新支線：fraud**）
In June 2026, the IRD discovers that Mr Fu **deliberately** omitted substantial income in his return for the year of assessment 2021/22, and there is evidence of **wilful intent to evade tax**.
(i) State the statutory deadline for raising an additional assessment.
(ii) Is the CIR still in time? (Yes / No)
(iii) Cite the governing section.

**Question 2** — Rental value 輪轉（計算題）
Ms Tai is employed with an annual salary of HK$960,000. Her employer provides her with a rent-free flat and pays the monthly rent of HK$30,000 directly to the landlord.
Calculate the rental value to be included in Ms Tai's assessable income. Cite the governing section.

**Question 3** — Consignment 輪轉（計算題）
DE Co, a non-resident company, sells goods on consignment through its Hong Kong branch. During the year of assessment, the branch's gross proceeds from sales to Hong Kong customers amount to HK$6,000,000. DE Co's actual profits cannot be readily ascertained.
Calculate the Hong Kong profits tax liability of DE Co. Cite the governing provision.

**Question 4 (MCQ)** — SSD 輪轉（18 個月檔）
Mr Yip bought a residential property on 1 March 2025 and sold it on 15 September 2026 (held for about 18 months) for HK$5,000,000. The SSD payable is:
a) HK$1,000,000 (20%)
b) HK$750,000 (15%)
c) HK$500,000 (10%)
d) HK$0 — SSD does not apply after 12 months

**Question 5 (MCQ)** — DIPN 21 輪轉（主體：**個人**）
HK Ltd pays a gross performance fee of HK$900,000 to Mr Bond, a UK-resident individual entertainer, for a show performed in Hong Kong. The Hong Kong profits tax payable is:
a) HK$0 — he has no permanent establishment in Hong Kong
b) HK$45,000
c) HK$49,500
d) HK$74,250
