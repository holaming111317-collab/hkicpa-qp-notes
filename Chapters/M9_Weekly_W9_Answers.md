# M9 保溫卷 W9 — Model Answers & Explanations

**Question 1 — Correct answer: HK$505**
- Sold contract note: **0.1%** × 500,000 = **HK$500**（2023 年起 0.1%）
- Instrument of transfer: **fixed HK$5**
- Total = **HK$505**
（W8 你用咗 0.026——唔知邊度嚟。死記：**0.1% + HK$5**。）

**Question 2 — Correct answer: HK$547,500**
Time apportionment under **s.8(1A)(a)**: assessable = income × **days in Hong Kong** ÷ 365 = 912,500 × (365 − 146) ÷ 365 = 912,500 × 219/365 = **HK$547,500**.
（W8 你乘咗離港日數——方向倒轉。留港日數先係課稅部分。）

**Question 3 — Correct answers: (i) 31 March 2027 (ii) Yes (iii) s.60(1)**
YA 2020/21 ended 31 March 2021 → 6-year deadline = 31 March 2027. Raised in February 2027 → in time.

**Question 4 — Correct answer: b**
An **education benefit** provided by an employer (paying the school fees of an employee's child) is **taxable** — following *Glynn*, the liability discharged is the employee's personal obligation, so it is assessable even though not convertible into cash (see also **s.9(2A)(b)**).

**Question 5 — Correct answer: b**
Held **exactly 12 months** → falls in the **>6–12 months** band → SSD rate **15%**: 8,000,000 × 15% = **HK$1,200,000**.
（≤6m → 20%；>6–12m → 15%（12 個月正正係呢檔）；>12–36m → 10%；>36m → 0%。）
