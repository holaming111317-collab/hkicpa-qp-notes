**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Dragon Retail Ltd is preparing a preliminary pro forma statement of financial position using the percent-of-sales method for a forecast 25% sales increase. Which of the following accounts should be carried at its current (unchanged) value in the preliminary pro forma statement because it changes only in response to management's financing decisions?

A. Accounts payable

B. Inventory

C. Notes payable

D. Accounts receivable

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

Harbour Manufacturing Ltd is estimating the incremental after-tax free cash flow of a new project that will be partly financed with bank debt. Which of the following treatments is correct in the FCF calculation?

A. Deduct the interest expense on the bank debt from the project's revenue, and apply the company's average tax rate to taxable income

B. Exclude interest expense because financing costs are reflected in the discount rate, and compute project taxes as EBIT multiplied by the company's marginal tax rate

C. Include the tax saving from interest deductibility in the project's free cash flow, since debt financing is used

D. Exclude depreciation from the tax computation because it is a non-cash charge

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

Pacific Toys Ltd has fixed costs of HK$24,000 per year, depreciation and amortisation of HK$6,000 per year, a unit selling price of HK$200 and a unit variable cost of HK$120. Which of the following statements is correct?

A. The pre-tax operating cash flow (EBITDA) break-even point is 375 units

B. The pre-tax operating cash flow (EBITDA) break-even point is 300 units and the accounting operating profit (EBIT) break-even point is 375 units

C. The accounting operating profit (EBIT) break-even point is 300 units

D. The pre-tax operating cash flow (EBITDA) break-even point is higher than the accounting operating profit (EBIT) break-even point

**Question 4 (Objective calculation)**

Lantau Electronics Ltd is evaluating a new project. Incremental revenue is HK$9,000,000 per year; incremental cash operating expenses are HK$6,000,000 per year; incremental depreciation and amortisation is HK$1,000,000 per year; incremental capital expenditure is HK$800,000; incremental additions to working capital are HK$300,000. The company's marginal tax rate is 25%. Calculate the project's incremental after-tax free cash flow (FCF) for the year. (Show your answer in HK$.)

**Question 5 (Objective calculation)**

Kowloon Trading Ltd's current net sales are HK$5,000,000 and are forecast to grow by 20% next year. Total assets vary with sales at 50% of sales; accounts payable vary with sales at 8% of sales; total costs are 80% of sales; the average tax rate is 30%; and the company pays out 60% of net income as dividends (no other liability or equity account changes). Calculate the external funding needed (EFN) for next year. (Show your answer in HK$.)
