## Question 1 — Correct Answer: **C (Economic order quantity (EOQ) model)**

**Model Answer (English):**
The EOQ model defines and determines the optimal inventory level as the one that minimises both shortage costs and carrying costs. It identifies the point at which carrying costs (storage, financing, insurance, spoilage) and shortage costs are the same. Because the model assumes items are sold at the same rate throughout the year, it suits this component's constant sales profile.

【解題思路】
- 題目關鍵字：「optimal quantity」、「holding costs offset shortage costs」、「constant rate throughout the year」——三個都係 EOQ 嘅 signature。EOQ 模型假設全年銷售速率一致，正好配合題目設定。
- **A (JIT) 錯**：JIT 係「貨品在需要時才送達、近乎零存貨」，重點係 timing 同供應商關係，唔係計算「最優訂貨量」嘅成本最小化模型。
- **B (MRP) 錯**：MRP 係電腦化系統，令存貨配合 **production schedules**，按訂單量推算原材料需求；題目無提及生產排程，只有穩定銷售同成本抵銷。
- **D (Product life cycle costing) 錯**：呢個係由產品構思到終止追蹤成本收入嘅長期成本法，同存貨訂貨量無關——典型「風馬牛」distractor。
- 考試貼士：scenario 題見到「minimise carrying + shortage costs」直覺揀 EOQ；見到「production schedule」揀 MRP；見到「exactly when needed」揀 JIT。

## Question 2 — Correct Answer: **D (Repairing products returned by customers under warranty undertakings)**

**Model Answer (English):**
External failure costs are costs incurred in remedying defects discovered by customers AFTER delivery. They include handling customer complaints, repairing products at customers' premises or returned to the company, meeting warranty undertakings, and transport/handling costs arising from returns or recalls. Warranty repairs on returned products are therefore an external failure cost.

【解題思路】
- 分界線：缺陷係「交付顧客之後」先被發現 → external failure。題目 "returned by customers under warranty" 明顯係交付後。
- **A 錯**：TQM training 係 **Prevention cost**（防止問題發生，屬設計/實施/運作品質系統嘅開支）。
- **B 錯**：Audits of quality systems 係 **Appraisal cost**（檢查監察是否符合規格），唔係 failure cost。
- **C 錯**：Rework **before delivery** 係 **Internal failure cost**——呢個係最強 distractor，同 D 只係差「交付前 vs 交付後」一線。「考官報告指出」考生喺 COQ 分類題最常混淆 internal 同 external failure：記住分界線係產品離開公司嗰一刻。
- 口訣：「預防、鑑定 = 主動付出；內部 = 出廠前出事；外部 = 客戶手上出事。」

## Question 3 — Correct Answer: **B**

**Model Answer (English):**
SMA is meant to combine internal and external attributes and to realise the objectives of the organisation through projections and analysis. Unlike traditional management accounting (which focuses on internal metrics), SMA considers external information such as cost trends, prices, market share of the company and its competitors, cash flows and access to resources, generating forward-looking projections and models.

【解題思路】
- B 完整咁講出 SMA 嘅本質：internal + external、forward-looking、實現組織目標——同原文 SMA 定義逐點對應。
- **A 錯**："internally focused + quantifies all information + implementing strategies" 描述嘅其實係**傳統 management accounting** 嘅角色（SMA 制訂策略、傳統 MA 執行）——呢個 distractor 專登將兩者調轉，考你識唔識分。
- **C 錯**：編製 statement of financial position 同 statement of profit or loss 俾外部使用者係 **financial accounting** 嘅職能，同 SMA 無關。
- **D 錯**："expansion of cost accounting" 同 "focus on a single issue (cost, volume or profit) for a single fiscal period" 都係 **management accounting** 嘅起源同特徵；SMA 恰恰係考慮一連串決策、跨多個期間、著重競爭環境。
- 考試貼士：呢類「邊句最準確描述 X」題，三個 distractor 通常各自偷換做另一個會計分支（MA / FA / cost accounting）——將四個分支嘅 focus 列成表背熟就萬無一失。

## Question 4 — Correct Answer: **Target cost = HK$199.20 per unit**

**Model Answer (English):**
Working (target costing — Exhibit 11.2: Market price − Desired profit = Target cost):
- Step 1: Desired profit = 20% × HK$249 = HK$49.80 per unit
- Step 2: Target cost = Market price − Desired profit = HK$249 − HK$49.80 = **HK$199.20 per unit**
The company must design and produce the rice cooker at HK$199.20 or less (covering all production costs over its entire life cycle), otherwise it will fail to achieve the desired 20% profit margin.

【解題思路】
- 第一步：認清 target costing 係「倒推」——市場價已定（HK$249，因競爭激烈公司無法定價），利潤係 "20% **on the selling price**"，所以 desired profit = 0.20 × 249 = 49.80。
- 第二步：Target cost = 249 − 49.80 = **199.20**。
- 常見錯誤 1：將 20% 當成 **cost-plus mark-up**（成本加成），即 target cost × 1.20 = 249 → 207.50。題目講明 "on the selling price"，基準係售價唔係成本，所以 207.50 係錯。
- 常見錯誤 2：直接用 249 × 20% = 49.80 當答案——呢個係 profit 唔係 target cost，記住公式 Market price − Desired profit = Target cost，要行多一步減。
- 考試貼士：呢類題 30 秒內要搞掂；寫低公式再代入，數字眼（HK$199、HK$249 呢類 price points）正正係原文 target pricing 概念嘅例子。

## Question 5 — Correct Answer: **ROI = 15%**

**Model Answer (English):**
Working (throughput analysis — three formulas):
- Step 1: Throughput = Revenue − Totally variable expenses = HK$4,800,000 − HK$1,200,000 = HK$3,600,000
- Step 2: Net profit = Throughput − Operating expenses = HK$3,600,000 − HK$2,600,000 = HK$1,000,000
- Step 3: ROI = Net profit ÷ Investment = HK$1,000,000 ÷ HK$4,000,000 = 0.15 = **15%**

【解題思路】
- 嚴格跟三條公式行：Throughput = Revenue − **Totally** variable expenses（題目已直接俾咗，唔使自己判斷邊啲係變動）→ 3,600,000。
- Net profit = Throughput − Operating expenses = 3,600,000 − 2,600,000 = 1,000,000。
- ROI = Net profit ÷ Investment = 1,000,000 ÷ 4,000,000 = 15%。
- 常見錯誤 1：喺 Step 1 將 operating expenses 都當變動成本扣埋（4,800,000 − 1,200,000 − 2,600,000 當 throughput）——咁樣 Step 2 會變成零，邏輯崩壞。記住 throughput 只減 totally variable expenses。
- 常見錯誤 2：ROI 用 Throughput ÷ Investment（3,600,000 ÷ 4,000,000 = 90%）——公式係 **Net profit** ÷ Investment，唔係 throughput。
- 常見錯誤 3：答案寫 0.15 而唔寫 15%——題目要求 "express as a percentage"，格式都要分。
- 考試貼士：Throughput analysis 下，改善決策首選**提高 throughput**（無理論上限），其次先係 cut operating expenses（最多減到零）；呢個概念點好容易變成獨立 MCQ。
