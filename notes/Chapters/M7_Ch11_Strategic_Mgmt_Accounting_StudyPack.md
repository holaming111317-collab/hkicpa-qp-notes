# M7 Chapter 11 Strategic Management Accounting Framework — Study Pack (Week 10)

## Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

### 1. 核心知識架構

- **Strategic Management Accounting (SMA) 的定位**：SMA 將企業的策略目標 (strategic business objectives) 與管理會計 (management accounting) 產生的資訊結合，產生前瞻性 (forward-looking) 的預測與模型。傳統管理會計著重內部指標 (internal metrics)、內省式 (introspective)；SMA 則著重外部資訊如成本趨勢 (cost trends)、價格、市場佔有率 (market share)、競爭者 (competitors)、供應商 (suppliers) 及科技 (technologies)。
- **SMA 的三角架構 (triangular structure) 與 QCT**：SMA 有三大考量——技術分析 (technical analysis)、行為考量 (behavioural considerations)、文化考量 (cultural considerations)；並以品質 (Quality)、成本 (Cost)、時間 (Time) 三因素 (QCT) 檢視，作為企業在市場中脫穎而出的關鍵。
- **會計各分支之連結 (linkages)**：財務會計 (financial accounting) 負責編製財務報表（statement of financial position、statement of profit or loss），以過去為主；成本會計 (cost accounting) 分析各生產階段的產品成本；管理會計結合兩者支援規劃 (planning)、控制 (control) 與績效評估 (performance evaluation)；SMA 再進一步把結論用於策略決策 (strategic decisions)。董事會 (board of directors) 訂立使命 (mission)、願景 (vision) 與長期目標 (goals)，高級管理層 (senior management) 據此制訂策略計劃 (strategic plans)，經由戰術 (tactics) 落實並以年度預算 (annual budgets) 量化。
- **業務規劃流程 (business planning process) 五步驟**：制訂策略 → 估計收入、開支及其他財務變數 → 編製營運預算 (operating budget) → 分析潛在風險與偏差 (risks and deviations) → 監察及檢討績效與結果 (monitor and review)。規劃是持續過程 (ongoing process)，需考慮最差至最佳情境 (worst-case to best-case scenario)。
- **七大策略成本管理技術 (strategic cost management techniques)**：JIT、TQM、product life cycle costing、target costing、quality costing、theory of constraints (TOC)、throughput analysis。本章計算重點集中於 target costing 與 throughput analysis 三條公式。

### 2. 必背考點清單

**A. SMA 核心概念**
- SMA 定義：結合策略目標與管理會計資訊，產生 forward-looking projections and models 以支援決策；同時考慮內部因素與外部因素（競爭者、供應商、科技、市場佔有率、成本結構、公司規模、財務資源、產品特點等「position」）。
- SMA 三角架構三考量：(1) Technical analysis（針對特定事件的資訊）；(2) Behavioural considerations（達成目標的最佳路徑）；(3) Cultural considerations（組織內共同信念 shared beliefs）。
- QCT：Quality、Cost、Time——企業用以在市場中區隔自己，並與顧客偏好及市場需求對照（例：McDonald's 自 1970s 起以 QCT 為業務及營運策略）。
- SMA 與傳統管理會計的關鍵差異：著重競爭環境 (competitive conditions)；考慮一連串決策與模式 (sequence of decisions and patterns)，而非單一議題（cost, volume or profit）。
- Management accounting 起源：是 cost accounting 的擴展 (expansion of cost accounting)。
- SMA 當前議題 (current issues)：全球化 (globalisation) 下的供應鏈風險管理 (supply chain risk management)；SMA 與傳統管理會計的協調 (coordination)；「價值」不限於財務衡量——包括 ESG 影響、品牌忠誠度 (brand loyalty)、消費者意見等無法以金錢量化的非財務議題。SMA 並非要取代 (replacement) 傳統管理會計：SMA 制訂前瞻策略，傳統管理會計負責落實執行。
- Behavioural finance 小考點：risk aversion = 不喜歡波動（向上或向下）；loss aversion = 不喜歡價值下降；過度看重近期資訊 = anchoring behaviour。

**B. Business Planning Process（Exhibit 11.1 五步驟，必背順序）**
1. Develop a strategy（由董事會或高級管理層制訂；策略以 profitability 及 market share 表達，並作為評估績效的基準 benchmark）
2. Estimate revenues, expenses and other financial variables
3. Create an operating budget（可以是 budgets 或 pro forma statements）
4. Analyse any potential risks and deviations
5. Monitor and review the performance and results
- Financial planning 主要用於估計未來現金流；strategic management plans 預測 1 年至 5 年或以上的公司績效。

**C. 存貨管理三方法（11.4.1，分類邊界常考）**
- **EOQ model**：決定令短缺成本 (shortage costs) 與持有成本 (carrying costs) 總和最小的最優存貨量；目標是兩成本相等之點。假設全年銷售速率相同 → 適合銷售穩定產品，不適合季節性/月度波動大的產品。
- **MRP (Materials Requirement Planning)**：電腦化系統，使存貨持有量配合生產計劃 (production schedules)；計算滿足特定訂單量所需的原材料及在製品存貨，配合準確銷售預測可維持低存貨。
- **JIT (Just-in-Time)**：貨品在需要時才製造、購入或送達，不長期備存；原材料在生產需要時準時到廠；成品端亦適用（直接由送貨上架，近乎零倉儲時間）。需要與供應商緊密關係 (close relationships with suppliers) 及精密物流 (finely tuned logistics)。

**D. TQM（11.4.2）**
- 目標：零缺陷 (zero defects)。收集缺陷數量與類型、返工成本 (rework costs)、保養/服務承諾成本等資訊。
- 四步循環 PDCA（Plan, Do, Check, Act）：(1) Identify and plan（確定問題、收集資訊、找出根本原因 root cause）；(2) Take action（制訂及實施修正、建立量度系統）；(3) Check the results（前後測試分析）；(4) Disseminate the information（記錄結果、提出建議）。持續重複。

**E. Product Life Cycle Costing（11.4.3）**
- 產品生命週期四階段（必背順序）：**Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline**。
  - Introduction：建立市場接受度，營銷開支大，成敗不確定，避免大额生產投資。
  - Growth：建立市場佔有率，推出新版本/衍生產品，擴充分銷網絡。
  - Maturity：競爭者多，防守市場佔有率，價格有下行壓力 → 可引入 target costing；現金流可能為正。
  - Decline：銷售下跌、削減成本保現金流、縮減分銷渠道、折價清貨、停產。
- Life cycle costing (LCC)：由發明 (invention) 至終止 (termination) 追蹤累積實際成本與收入；代表 total cost of ownership；與傳統成本會計只著重即時會計期間 (immediate accounting periods) 不同，LCC 提供長期盈利圖像。好處：更低成本、更實際的現金流評估、促進長期規劃、更理解增量成本 (incremental costs)。

**F. Target Costing（11.4.4，計算必考）**
- 核心公式（Exhibit 11.2）：

  **Market price − Desired profit = Target cost**

- 價格由供求定律 (laws of supply and demand) 決定，企業無法左右 → 盈利靠控制成本。
- 步驟：選擇市場區隔 (market segment / niche，如 luxury vs economy goods) → 調查顧客願付價格 → 設定 desired profit → 得出 target cost → 由生產、營銷、財務專家組成團隊設計符合品質規格且不超過 target cost 的產品。Target cost 必須包括產品整個生命週期的所有生產成本。
- Target pricing 概念顛倒 (reverse) 傳統程序：傳統是「設計產品 → 計算成本 → 定價」；target costing 是「市場價格（如 HK$199、HK$249 價格點 price points）→ 扣除利潤 → 倒推成本」。

**G. Quality Costing（11.4.5，四大分類必背）**
- COQ (Cost of Quality) 理論中，quality cost 指**低劣品質的成本 (cost of poor quality)**。
- 品質成本可佔銷售額 10%–30%，佔營運開支 25%–40%（數字出自原文，可考）。
- 四大類別（Total cost of quality = 四者之和）：
  1. **Prevention costs** 預防成本：防止品質問題（制訂規格 specifications、品質程序、QA 系統及部門、TQM 培訓 training）。
  2. **Appraisal costs** 鑑定成本：監察與評估是否符合規格（供應商預審 pre-approval assessments、品質系統審計 audits、驗證 inputs/processes/products）。
  3. **Internal failure costs** 內部失敗成本：交付顧客**之前**發現缺陷的補救（追查缺陷原因、返工 rework、不可修復存貨損失）。
  4. **External failure costs** 外部失敗成本：交付**之後**顧客發現缺陷的補救（處理投訴 complaints、維修、保養承諾 warranty undertakings、退貨/回收的運輸處理費）。
- Activity-based costing (ABC) 是識別及量化上述四類 TQM 成本的成熟工具。

**H. Theory of Constraints (TOC)（11.4.6）**
- 由 Dr. Eliyahu Goldratt 於《The Goal》(1984) 首次提出。核心：所有企業都有限制增長的約束 (constraints / bottlenecks)，修復約束比追求滿負荷運作更能提升利潤。
- 三套工具：**Five focusing steps**（識別及消除約束）、**Thinking processes**（分析解難，三問：What needs changing? What should it be changed to? What actions will bring about this change?）、**Throughput accounting**（量度績效及支援決策）。
- Five focusing steps：(a) 識別約束/瓶頸 → (b) 決定如何改造約束使系統以最高效率運作 → (c) 將消除約束列為最高優先，其他流程配合 (subordinate) → (d) 作出其他所需改變以消除約束 → (e) 完成後回到 (a) 處理下一個最大約束。
- 約束分內部 (internal：產能追不上需求——設備、員工技能、政策) 與外部 (external：產量超過市場需求 → 應著力創造需求)。
- Throughput accounting 觀點：傳統會計把存貨視為資產 (asset)，可能鼓勵囤貨虛增利潤；TOC 視存貨為負債 (liability)，因佔用現金並產生倉儲成本。傳統會計偏重削減開支（理論上限為零）；TOC 強調提高 throughput（無理論上限）。

**I. Throughput Analysis（11.4.7，計算必考）**
- 定義：以對**整間公司**（而非單一投資範疇）的影響來評估投資決策。假設單位層面只有材料成本屬變動，其他成本無論是否生產都會發生。
- 三條公式：

  **Throughput = Revenue − Totally variable expenses**（很少成本是完全變動，故 throughput 佔銷售百分比應相當高）
  **Net profit = Throughput − Operating expenses**（Operating expenses = 除計算 throughput 所用變動開支外的所有開支）
  **Return on investment (ROI) = Net profit ÷ Investment**（Investment = 為增加產能投入的現金）

- 決策優次：增加 throughput（無上限）優於削減 operating expenses（最多只能減到零）。

### 3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

**Pitfall 1：EOQ vs MRP vs JIT —— 三種存貨管理方法的分類邊界**
- 對比：**EOQ** 是「計算最優訂貨量」的數量模型，令 carrying costs 與 shortage costs 總和最小（假設銷售速率穩定）；**MRP** 是「配合生產計劃」的電腦化系統，按訂單量推算所需原材料與在製品；**JIT** 是「零存貨哲學」，貨品在需要一刻才到達，依賴供應商關係與物流。
- 例子：一家銷量全年穩定的公司想決定「每次訂幾多貨」→ 用 **EOQ**；一家按訂單生產、要確保原材料配合生產排程 → 用 **MRP**；豐田式零倉存、零件即叫即到 → 用 **JIT**。OTQ 給你一個 scenario 問屬於哪種方法，關鍵字：「optimal quantity / minimise holding & shortage costs」= EOQ；「production schedule / orders volume」= MRP；「arrive exactly when needed / very low inventory」= JIT。

**Pitfall 2：Prevention / Appraisal / Internal failure / External failure costs —— 品質成本四分類**
- 對比：前兩類是企業**主動付出**以獲得品質（Prevention = 事前防止問題發生，如 TQM training；Appraisal = 檢查監察是否符合規格，如 audits、supplier pre-approval）；後兩類是問題**已經發生**的補救成本，分界線在「**交付顧客之前或之後**」——Internal failure = 交付前發現（rework、追查原因）；External failure = 交付後顧客發現（warranty、complaints、recall transport）。
- 例子：「產品出廠前發現次品要返工」= **Internal failure**（不是 external，因未交付）；「保用期內免費維修」= **External failure**；「品質系統審計費」= **Appraisal**（不是 prevention，因它是檢查而非防止）。「考官報告指出」考生在分類題常把 appraisal 與 prevention 混淆——記住 prevention 是「防止問題出現」，appraisal 是「檢查問題是否存在」。

## Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

* [Flashcard 1]
  - Front (Question in English): What is strategic management accounting (SMA), and how does it differ from traditional management accounting?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - SMA = a specific field of accounting that brings together the strategic business objectives of a business with the information generated by management accounting, generating forward-looking projections and models for decision-making.
      - Traditional management accounting: internally focused, introspective, historical analysis for single entities/periods, focuses on a single issue (cost, volume or profit); an expansion of cost accounting.
      - SMA: externally focused — cost trends, prices, market share, competitors, suppliers, technologies; considers a sequence of decisions and patterns; focuses on competitive conditions.
      - SMA is NOT a replacement: SMA formulates forward-looking strategies; traditional management accounting implements them — coordination between the two is the key challenge.
    - [中文白話解釋]: SMA 就是將公司策略目標同管理會計資訊結合，望向將來、望向公司外面（競爭者、市場份額、科技等）；傳統管理會計只望公司內部、望單一議題。考試記住關鍵字：SMA = external + forward-looking + competitive conditions，兩者要協調而非互相取代。

* [Flashcard 2]
  - Front (Question in English): Describe the triangular structure of SMA and the QCT factors.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Triangular structure — three primary considerations: (1) Technical analysis (information about specific events); (2) Behavioural considerations (best path to achieve objectives); (3) Cultural considerations (shared beliefs within the organisation).
      - QCT factors: Quality, Cost, Time — used by companies to set themselves apart within their market, and contrasted with customer preferences / market demands.
      - Example: McDonald's since the 1970s — high quality, continuous cost control for affordable prices, fast delivery time.
    - [中文白話解釋]: SMA 三角 = 技術（數據分析）、行為（點行先達標）、文化（公司共同信念）；QCT = 品質、成本、時間，係公司用嚟同對手區隔嘅三個因素。MCQ 好易將「economic considerations」之類假選項混入，記死 technical / behavioural / cultural 三個字。

* [Flashcard 3]
  - Front (Question in English): List the five steps of the business planning process in order.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Step 1: Develop a strategy (set by the board of directors / senior management; expressed in profitability and market share; used as benchmark for performance evaluation).
      - Step 2: Estimate revenues, expenses and other financial variables.
      - Step 3: Create an operating budget (budgets or pro forma statements).
      - Step 4: Analyse any potential risks and deviations (worst-case to best-case scenarios).
      - Step 5: Monitor and review the performance and results. Planning is an ongoing process, adjusted as situations evolve.
    - [中文白話解釋]: 口訣「策、估、算、險、檢」——先制訂策略，再估收入開支，跟住出預算，分析風險偏差，最後監察檢討。OTQ 常考「邊一步先邊一步後」，記住策略永遠係第一步、監察檢討係最後一步。

* [Flashcard 4]
  - Front (Question in English): Distinguish between the EOQ model, MRP and JIT inventory systems.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - EOQ model: determines the optimal inventory level that minimises the sum of shortage costs and carrying costs (storage, financing, insurance, spoilage); the point where carrying costs = shortage costs; assumes constant sales rate → unsuitable for seasonal products.
      - MRP (Materials Requirement Planning): computerised system aligning inventory holdings with production schedules; calculates raw materials and in-process inventory needed for a specific order volume; combined with accurate sales forecasts keeps inventory low.
      - JIT: goods manufactured, purchased or delivered exactly when needed — components arrive at the factory precisely when required; finished goods delivered to end users on demand; requires close supplier relationships and finely tuned logistics; ideal system takes goods directly from delivery to shelf.
    - [中文白話解釋]: 三個方法目標都係降低存貨，但切入點不同：EOQ 計「最優數量」（成本最小化模型）；MRP 計「生產需要幾多料」（跟生產排程）；JIT 係「唔好擺貨，用到先至送到」。題目見 "minimise carrying and shortage costs" 揀 EOQ；見 "production schedule" 揀 MRP；見 "when needed, not before" 揀 JIT。

* [Flashcard 5]（計算卡）
  - Front (Question in English): Cute Toys Ltd sells stuffed toys at a market-driven price of HK$50 per unit and requires a profit margin of 10% on the selling price. Calculate the target cost per unit and state the target costing formula.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Formula (Exhibit 11.2): Market price − Desired profit = Target cost.
      - Desired profit = 10% × HK$50 = HK$5 per unit.
      - Target cost = HK$50 − HK$5 = **HK$45 per unit**.
      - Logic: prices are set by supply and demand (outside the company's control) → profitability is pursued by controlling costs; the company must produce at the target cost (or less), covering ALL production costs over the product's entire life cycle, or it will fail to earn the desired profit.
    - [中文白話解釋]: Target costing 係「倒推法」：市場話事定咗價（HK$50），公司想要 10% 利潤（HK$5），咁成本最多只可以係 HK$45。同傳統「成本加利潤定價」方向啱啱相反。計數時留意利潤率基準係 "on the selling price"，唔好乘錯做成本加成。

* [Flashcard 6]
  - Front (Question in English): List and define the four categories of cost of quality (COQ), with one example of each.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - 1. Prevention costs — costs of preventing quality problems (design/implementation/running of the quality system); e.g. TQM training, drawing up specifications.
      - 2. Appraisal costs — costs of monitoring/evaluating conformance to specifications; e.g. audits of quality systems, pre-approval assessments of suppliers, verification of inputs/processes/products.
      - 3. Internal failure costs — remedying defects found BEFORE delivery to the customer; e.g. rework of defective materials, investigating causes of defects, losses on unusable stock.
      - 4. External failure costs — remedying defects discovered by customers AFTER delivery; e.g. handling complaints, warranty repairs, transport/handling costs of returns or recalls.
      - Total cost of quality = sum of the four categories; in COQ theory "quality cost" = cost of POOR quality; quality costs may be 10%–30% of sales and 25%–40% of operating expenses; ABC is a proven tool to identify/quantify them.
    - [中文白話解釋]: 口訣「預、鑑、內、外」。分界線：主動付出（預防/鑑定）vs 出事補救（內部/外部）；補救再分「交貨前發現 = 內部、交貨後客戶發現 = 外部」。考試最鍾意將 warranty repair（外部）同 rework（內部）整蠱你。

* [Flashcard 7]（計算卡）
  - Front (Question in English): A division reports revenue of HK$2,000,000, totally variable expenses of HK$500,000, operating expenses of HK$900,000, and investment of HK$3,000,000. Using throughput analysis, compute throughput, net profit and ROI, and state the decision priority implied.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Throughput = Revenue − Totally variable expenses = 2,000,000 − 500,000 = **HK$1,500,000**.
      - Net profit = Throughput − Operating expenses = 1,500,000 − 900,000 = **HK$600,000**.
      - ROI = Net profit ÷ Investment = 600,000 ÷ 3,000,000 = **20%**.
      - Decision priority: the best improvements increase throughput (no theoretical upper limit); reducing operating expenses is less important because expenses can only be reduced to zero.
    - [中文白話解釋]: Throughput analysis 三步曲：先收入減「完全變動開支」得 throughput；再減 operating expenses 得 net profit；最後除以 investment 得 ROI。留意 "totally variable expenses" 通常只係材料，唔好將人工同間接費當變動成本扣。改善策略首選提高 throughput（無上限），唔係一味cut cost。

* [Flashcard 8]
  - Front (Question in English): Explain the theory of constraints (TOC), its five focusing steps, and how throughput accounting views inventory differently from traditional accounting.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - TOC (Goldratt, "The Goal", 1984): all companies face constraints (bottlenecks) that hold back potential; identifying and managing them raises profits; companies gain more by fixing constraints than by chasing full capacity.
      - Five focusing steps: (a) Identify the constraint; (b) Decide how to exploit/rework it so the system works at maximum effectiveness; (c) Subordinate everything else — make eliminating the constraint the highest priority; (d) Elevate — make any other changes needed to eliminate it; (e) Repeat the cycle for the next biggest constraint.
      - Three tools: five focusing steps; thinking processes (What needs changing? What to? What actions?); throughput accounting.
      - Throughput accounting: traditional accounting treats inventory as an ASSET (encouraging overproduction to inflate accounting profit); TOC treats inventory as a LIABILITY (ties up cash + storage costs). Constraints may be internal (demand > capacity) or external (output > market demand → create more demand).
    - [中文白話解釋]: TOC 重點係「搵出樽頸、集中火力消除佢」，五步循環記口訣「搵、用、從、升、loop」。最大陷阱：TOC 視存貨為負債（liability）而唔係資產——因為佔住現金兼要倉租。外部約束 = 生產多過市場需求，應該去創造需求而唔係加產能。
