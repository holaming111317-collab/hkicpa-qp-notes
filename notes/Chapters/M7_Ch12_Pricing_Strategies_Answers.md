**Question 1 — Correct Answer: B (Market skimming pricing)**

**Model Answer:**
Market skimming is the strategy of setting the initial price HIGHER than competing products to take advantage of consumers willing to pay a high price to be the first to own an exciting new product — the so-called "early adopters". After introduction, the price is lowered to capture additional customers at lower price points. Patent-protected or unique products (e.g. Apple's iPhones, new patented drugs) are classic cases. NovaTech's patented device, premium launch price and planned subsequent price reductions match skimming exactly.

**【解題思路】**
- 第一步：抓關鍵字 ——「patented / no competitor can replicate」、「price well above competitors」、「first to own」、「lower the price progressively」。呢啲全部係 skimming 嘅標誌：高開、搵 early adopters 埋單、之後逐步減價擴客。
- 第二步：排除干擾項。
  - **A 錯**：Penetration 係**低於**競爭對手定價，目的係快速上量搶 market share（如 FMCG 自家品牌、小米），方向同題目完全相反 —— 呢個係 skimming vs penetration 嘅經典概念混淆。
  - **C 錯**：Complementary product pricing 係一件產品（razor）低價求量，靠互補品（blades）賺錢；題目冇提任何互補產品。
  - **D 錯**：Volume discounting 係買大量先至平，與新產品上市定價無關。
- 考試貼士：見到 "early adopters"、"premium price at launch"、"price lowered after introduction" 即刻諗 skimming；見到 "rapidly gain market share / economies of scale" 先係 penetration。

**Question 2 — Correct Answer: C**

**Model Answer:**
Second-degree price discrimination is the practice of setting different prices for different quantities consumed — e.g. mobile data plans or hours with a personal trainer. First-degree discrimination (not third-degree) is charging the maximum possible price for every unit sold. Third-degree discrimination is charging different prices to different consumer groups (e.g. senior citizen transport fares or movie tickets). Under price discrimination, customers with more INELASTIC demand (who need the product more) are charged MORE, not less (e.g. funeral services). For discrimination to work, customer groups must NOT overlap or be able to resell to each other.

**【解題思路】**
- 第一步：背熟三度差別定價 —— 一度：每單位收客人願付嘅最高價；二度：按**數量/用量**差別收費；三度：按**顧客群組**差別收費。
- 第二步：逐項判斷。
  - **A 錯**：「charge the maximum for every unit」係 **first-degree** 嘅定義，唔係 third-degree —— 呢個係故意對調兩個度數嘅陷阱。
  - **B 錯**：方向調轉咗。需求越 inelastic（越需要）嘅客人應該收得**越貴**（書中例子：funeral services）；elastic 嘅客人才要平啲留住佢。
  - **D 錯**：差別定價成功嘅前提係客群**不能重疊**（唔可以互相轉售），否則平價買入嘅人會轉賣畀高價客群，策略即刻失效。
- 考試貼士：OTQ 好鍾意將「度數定義對調」同「elastic/inelastic 方向對調」做 distractor，兩個方向都要背到滾瓜爛熟。

**Question 3 — Correct Answer: D**

**Model Answer:**
Relevant costs are costs associated with a management decision that are likely to change in the future. Under the principle of relevance, only benefits and costs that DIFFER between options — i.e. future incremental (differential) costs and opportunity costs — should be considered. Sunk costs have already been incurred and will not change regardless of the decision, so they are NOT relevant. Absorbed fixed overheads that remain unchanged (unavoidable fixed costs) are likewise irrelevant to a special-order price. The original purchase price of an asset is a classic sunk cost (e.g. shares bought on the HKSE ten years ago — only today's transaction costs matter for the sell decision).

**【解題思路】**
- 第一步：記住 relevant costing 三大法則 —— (1) 只睇將來先發生嘅現金流；(2) sunk cost 唔理；(3) opportunity cost 要計。
- 第二步：逐項判斷。
  - **A 錯**：十年前嘅買入價係典型 sunk cost，無論今日賣唔賣都改變唔到，所以**不相關**。書中特別提到有人心理上一定想計返呢個價，但財務決策上係錯嘅。
  - **B 錯**：Sunk costs 定義上就係 irrelevant —— 呢個選項將定義掉轉。
  - **C 錯**：Absorbed fixed overheads 唔會因為接唔接張 special order 而改變（unavoidable），所以唔應放入最低報價；只有 incremental costs（如額外材料、加班工資）先相關。
- 考試貼士：遇見 special order / minimum price 題目，第一反應係問「呢項成本接單同唔接單有冇分別？」冇分別 → 剔除。

**Question 4 — Correct Answer: HK$132.50 per unit**

**Model Answer / Working:**

| Item | Working | HK$ |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed manufacturing overhead per unit | 300,000 ÷ 8,000 | 37.50 |
| Fixed selling & admin per unit | 200,000 ÷ 8,000 | 25.00 |
| Total fixed cost per unit | | 62.50 |
| Variable cost per unit | given | 45.00 |
| **Total cost per unit** | 62.50 + 45.00 | **107.50** |
| Desired ROI mark-up per unit | (25% × 800,000) ÷ 8,000 = 200,000 ÷ 8,000 | 25.00 |
| **Target selling price** | 107.50 + 25.00 | **132.50** |

The company expects income of HK$200,000 (25% × HK$800,000) on its investment, i.e. HK$25 per unit at 8,000 units. Adding this ROI mark-up to the total unit cost of HK$107.50 gives a target selling price of **HK$132.50**.

**【解題思路】**
- 第一步：背公式 —— Mark-up per unit = (Desired ROI % × Amount invested) ÷ Units produced。呢度 = (25% × 800,000) ÷ 8,000 = **HK$25**。
- 第二步：砌 total cost per unit —— 固定成本要**兩項加埋再除產量**：(300,000 + 200,000) ÷ 8,000 = HK$62.50，加 variable cost HK$45 = **HK$107.50**。
- 第三步：Cost + Mark-up = Target selling price → 107.50 + 25 = **HK$132.50**。
- 常見錯誤：
  - 用 ROI% 直接乘單位成本（25% × 107.50 ≈ 26.88）—— 錯！ROI 係對**投資額**計，唔係對成本計。
  - 漏計其中一項固定成本（只計 manufacturing overhead 唔計 selling & admin）—— 固定成本兩項都要入。
  - 將 mark-up 當 margin 用（107.50 ÷ 0.75）—— 題目講明係 ROI-based mark-up on cost，唔係 profit margin on sales。
- 考試貼士：呢條係書中 Illustrative Example 5 嘅變奏版，三部流程（fixed cost per unit → total cost → + ROI mark-up）記實就穩攞分。

**Question 5 — Correct Answer: HK$1,100 per cabinet**

**Model Answer / Working:**

| Relevant (incremental) costs | Working | HK$ |
|---|---|---|
| Refurbishment cost | given, per cabinet | 800 |
| Transportation cost | 120,000 ÷ 400 cabinets | 300 |
| **Minimum selling price per cabinet** | 800 + 300 | **1,100** |

The original manufacturing costs of HK$12,000 per cabinet are **sunk costs** — incurred five years ago and unchanged by whatever decision is made now — so they are irrelevant to the pricing decision. The only relevant costs are the future incremental costs of refurbishment (HK$800) and transportation (HK$300 per cabinet). Any price quoted above HK$1,100 per cabinet will generate a profit for Harbour Manufacturing Ltd. This is the relevant/incremental cost pricing strategy.

**【解題思路】**
- 第一步：識別 sunk cost —— HK$12,000/件嘅原始製造成本五年前已付，接唔接新客都收唔返，**唔入計**。呢個係全題最大陷阱：好多同學會用 12,000 加料再報價，結果最低價變成 HK$13,100，完全錯。
- 第二步：計未來 incremental costs —— refurbishment HK$800（每件）+ transportation HK$120,000 ÷ 400 = HK$300（每件），合共 **HK$1,100**。
- 第三步：下結論 —— 最低報價 HK$1,100；高過呢個數就係賺。邏輯同書中 Apply & Analyse 1（冷氣機 HK$500 + HK$200 = HK$700）一模一樣。
- 考試貼士：Relevant cost 題目嘅作答順序：先圈出所有「已發生/唔會變」嘅數剔走佢，再將「先將來、會改變」嘅成本逐項加，最後嗰句「任何高於最低價的報價均有盈利」寫埋落去更穩陣。
