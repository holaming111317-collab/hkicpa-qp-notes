**Question 1 (MCQ)**

NovaTech Ltd has just developed a patented wearable device that no competitor can currently replicate. It launches the product at a price well above competing devices to capture customers willing to pay a premium to be first to own it, and plans to lower the price progressively afterwards to attract additional customer segments.

Which pricing strategy is NovaTech Ltd adopting?

A. Market penetration pricing
B. Market skimming pricing
C. Complementary product pricing
D. Volume discounting

---

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

Which of the following statements about price discrimination is CORRECT?

A. Third-degree price discrimination means charging the maximum possible price for every unit sold to each individual customer.
B. Customers with more inelastic demand for the product should be charged a lower price than customers with elastic demand.
C. Second-degree price discrimination refers to setting different prices for different quantities consumed, such as mobile data usage.
D. Price discrimination is most effective when different customer groups can freely resell the product to one another.

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

Which of the following statements about relevant cost pricing is CORRECT?

A. The original purchase price paid for an asset ten years ago must always be included because it represents the true economic sacrifice.
B. Sunk costs are relevant because they measure the total resources consumed by the business.
C. Absorbed fixed overheads that do not change as a result of a special order should be included in the minimum price quoted.
D. Only future incremental costs and opportunity costs that differ between options are relevant when setting a minimum price for a special order.

**Question 4 (Objective calculation)**

Bright Products Ltd is pricing a new desk lamp. The per-unit variable cost is HK$45. Fixed manufacturing overhead is HK$300,000 and fixed selling and administrative expenses are HK$200,000. The company has invested HK$800,000 and targets a 25% return on investment (ROI). Planned production is 8,000 units.

Using total cost-plus pricing with the mark-up based on the desired ROI, calculate the target selling price per unit. (State your answer in HK$, to one decimal place if necessary.)

---

**Question 5 (Objective calculation)**

Five years ago, Harbour Manufacturing Ltd produced 400 custom-made display cabinets for a customer who went bankrupt before delivery. The original manufacturing costs (already incurred) totalled HK$12,000 per cabinet. The cabinets have been stored in a warehouse since then. A new customer is now interested in the whole batch. To complete the sale, Harbour must spend HK$800 refurbishment cost per cabinet and incur HK$120,000 total transportation expenses.

Using relevant cost pricing, calculate the minimum selling price per cabinet that Harbour should quote. (State your answer in HK$.)
