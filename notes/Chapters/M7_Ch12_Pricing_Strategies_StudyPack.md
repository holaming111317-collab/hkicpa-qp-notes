# M7 Pricing Strategies and Decisions — Study Pack (Week 10)

## Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

### 1. 核心知識架構

- **定價的五大影響因素 (Factors Affecting Pricing Policies)**：成本資料 (Cost Data)、需求因素 (Demand Factor — price taker vs price setter)、消費者心理 (Consumer Psychology — psychological pricing)、競爭 (Competition) 及政府政策 (Government Policy — price ceilings/floors、tariffs、Competition Ordinance (Cap. 619))。定價前必須先判斷企業是「受價者」還是「定價者」。
- **八種定價策略 (Types of Pricing Strategies)**：Cost-Plus、Skimming、Penetration、Complementary Product、Product Line、Volume Discounting、Price Discrimination 及 Relevant Cost Pricing。每種策略的適用情境與方向（高價 vs 低價）是 OTQ 概念辨析的重災區。
- **CSR 對定價的影響**：Cost of Quality（四分類：Preventive / Appraisal / Internal Failure / External Failure Costs）與 Environmental Costs（四分類：Conventional / Hidden / Contingent / Image and Relationship Costs）都會推高或改變成本基礎，必須納入定價考慮。
- **外部市場因素與內部成本結構**：外部三要素 = Demand、Competition、Government Regulation；內部則取決於 fixed vs variable cost 比例，並可用 Activity-Based Costing (ABC) 四步驟（cost pools → 選擇分攤成本 → cost drivers → denominator volume，ABC 用 practical capacity）提高成本及定價準確性。
- **非牟利機構定價 (Non-Profit Pricing)**：Voluntary Pricing (Pay What You Want, PWYW)、Ability to Pay、Versioning（又稱 quality discrimination）、Bundle Services / Memberships（目的在擷取 consumer surplus）。目標是達成使命 (mission) 而非利潤最大化。

### 2. 必背考點清單

**A. Cost-Plus Pricing（本章唯一官方公式，List of Formulas 收錄）**

- **Cost + Mark-up percentage × Cost = Target selling price**（Exhibit 12.5）
- Mark-up = Selling price − Cost = 產品的利潤（Exhibit 12.4）
- **ROI 基礎 mark-up**（Illustrative Example 5）：
  - Mark-up per unit = (Desired ROI % × Amount invested) ÷ Units produced
  - 例：(20% × HK$1,000,000) ÷ 10,000 units = HK$20 mark-up；售價 = VC 60 + FC 52 + 20 = **HK$132**
- **Margin vs Mark-up 兩種計法（Illustrative Example 2，極易考）**：
  - Net profit margin 25%（利潤佔售價）：Price = Cost ÷ (1 − margin) = 780 ÷ 0.75 = **HK$1,040**
  - Gross profit mark-up 30%（利潤佔成本）：Price = Cost × (1 + 30%) = **HK$1,014**
- 優點：簡單；缺點：**忽略需求 (ignores demand)**，且銷量越低，單位成本越高，需收取更高價格才能達到目標 ROI。
- Gross profit margin = (Sales − Variable cost) ÷ Sales —— 用 gross profit **rate**（而非金額）監察定價政策成效（Illustrative Example 1）。

**B. Relevant Cost Pricing（計算題核心）**

- Relevant cost = 與決策相關、未來會改變的成本 (cost associated with a management decision that is likely to change in the future)。
- **Sunk costs 不相關**：已發生、不論如何選擇都不會改變（如 10 年前已付的製造成本、股票的原始買入價）。
- **Incremental / differential costs**：因短期業務量變化而增加的成本，相關。
- **Opportunity cost 要計入**（Exam Practice Q12：small house 現時可賣 HK$3,000,000 即為機會成本）。
- 存貨材料：常用材料用 **replacement cost**；不再使用的材料用 **net realisable value**（放棄的出售收入）。
- 勞工：full capacity 時取「加班費」與「基本工資 + contribution forgone」兩者**較低者**；below capacity 只計實付時薪。
- Fixed overheads absorbed（不可避免）→ 不相關。
- Apply & Analyse 1：500 部冷氣機最低售價 = refurbishment HK$500 + transportation HK$200 = **HK$700/部**；高於 HK$700 的報價均有盈利。

**C. 策略概念辨析（OTQ 高頻）**

- **Skimming**：初期定價**高於**競爭對手，瞄準 early adopters（如 iPhone），其後逐步降價；適合專利/獨特產品。
- **Penetration**：定價**低於**競爭對手，快速搶佔 market share、達致 economies of scale（如 FMCG、小米手機）；與 skimming 方向相反。
- **Complementary Product**：一件產品低價求量（razor），靠互補品（blades）賺取高利潤。
- **Product Line**：相關產品組合定價；變化包括 Captive Pricing（KFC 套餐配菜）及 Loss Leaders（超市低於成本招徠）。
- **Volume Discounting**：大量購買享低價；三原因 = 吸引新客、增強競爭力、降低分銷成本；缺點 = 利潤率變薄、產品貶值、日後難加價。
- **Price Discrimination**：同一產品向不同顧客收不同價；**inelastic demand 的顧客收費較高**（如殯儀服務）；顧客群不可重疊。
  - First-degree：每單位收顧客願付的最高價
  - Second-degree：按購買**數量**差別定價（如 data plan）
  - Third-degree：按**顧客群組**差別定價（如長者車票/戲票）
- **Non-profit**：PWYW（買家自訂價，可低至零；德國實驗：大多付比正價少 19%，但客量增加）；Ability to Pay（銀行稱 "capacity"）；Versioning（同一產品不同型號不同價，適合 fixed cost 高、variable cost 低的產品如軟件）；Bundling（擷取 consumer surplus = 願付價 − 實付價）。
- **Psychological Pricing**：HK$149,999 vs HK$150,000；顧客由左至右讀價；優點包括落入較低 price band、方便折扣管理（以 '98' 結尾）、存貨控制（防員工盜竊）。
- **Government Policy**：price ceilings（上限）/ price floors（下限）、稅項、tariffs；predatory pricing 可能違法 —— 香港 **Competition Ordinance (Cap. 619)**，2013 年 1 月 13 日制定。
- **Break-even 定價**（Exam Practice Q10）：Break-even price = Total operating cost ÷ average attendance（510,000 ÷ 1,200 = HK$425）；Break-even attendance = Total cost ÷ ticket price（510,000 ÷ 400 = 1,275 人）。
- **Cost of Quality** = 與生產**低劣品質**產品相關的成本（rework、testing、after-sales service、replacing faulty products）；四分類必背。Quality of design（符合顧客期望）vs Quality of conformance（產品符合設計規格）。
- **Environmental Costs** 四分類：Conventional（原料能源）、Hidden（藏於 overhead）、Contingent（未來如 clean-up）、Image and relationship；油業可達 operating costs 的 20%。
- **ABC 四步驟**：(1) form cost pools；(2) decide which costs to allocate（只分攤 controllable capacity costs）；(3) identify cost drivers（因果關係最強者）；(4) denominator volume 用 **practical capacity** → 可揭示 unused capacity。

### 3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

**Pitfall 1：Profit Margin on Sales vs Mark-up on Cost（Cost-Plus 的兩條路）**

- 對比：「25% profit margin」指利潤佔**售價** 25% → Price = Cost ÷ (1 − 25%)；「30% mark-up on cost」指利潤佔**成本** 30% → Price = Cost × (1 + 30%)。同一成本 HK$780，前者得 HK$1,040，後者得 HK$1,014 —— 結果不同，不能互換。
- 例子：成本 HK$500，要 20% margin on selling price → 500 ÷ 0.8 = **HK$625**；若誤用 mark-up 計法會得 500 × 1.2 = HK$600，少了 HK$25。考試看清題目用 "margin" 還是 "mark-up on cost / profit on total costs"（如 Illustrative Example 4 的 "20% profit on its total costs" = mark-up on cost）。

**Pitfall 2：Market Skimming vs Market Penetration（方向完全相反）**

- 對比：Skimming = **高價**入場，榨取 early adopters 的支付意願，之後降價擴客；Penetration = **低價**入場，快速上量搶市佔、攤薄 fixed costs，之後才考慮加價。兩者都是新產品上市策略，但價格方向相反。
- 例子：藥廠專利新藥/Apple iPhone 高開 → skimming；Wellcome「No Frill」自家品牌/小米手機低開搶客 → penetration。題目見 "early adopters"、"premium launch" 即 skimming；見 "gain market share rapidly"、"economies of scale" 即 penetration。

## Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

* [Flashcard 1]
  - Front (Question in English): State the cost-plus pricing formula, and explain the two ways a "profit percentage" can be applied to a cost base.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Formula: Cost + (Mark-up % × Cost) = Target selling price
      - Mark-up on cost: Price = Cost × (1 + mark-up %) — profit expressed as % of cost
      - Margin on selling price: Price = Cost ÷ (1 − margin %) — profit expressed as % of price
      - Example (cost HK$780): 25% net profit margin → HK$1,040; 30% gross profit mark-up → HK$1,014
    - [中文白話解釋]: Cost-plus 就是在成本上加一個利潤百分比，但要分清該百分比是「佔成本」還是「佔售價」。佔成本用乘 (1+%)；佔售價（margin）要用成本除以 (1−%)。兩者結果不同，考試最常在此設陷阱。

* [Flashcard 2]
  - Front (Question in English): How is the mark-up per unit set when a company targets a desired return on investment (ROI) under total cost-plus pricing?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Mark-up per unit = (Desired ROI % × Amount invested) ÷ Units produced
      - Target selling price = Variable cost per unit + Fixed cost per unit + ROI mark-up per unit
      - Example: VC HK$60 + FC HK$52 + mark-up (20% × HK$1m ÷ 10,000 = HK$20) = HK$132
      - Weakness: ignores demand; lower sales volume → higher unit cost → higher required price
    - [中文白話解釋]: 公司想要某個投資回報率，就先算「想賺幾多錢」（ROI% × 投資額），再除以預計產量，得出每件產品要賺的 mark-up，加到總單位成本上就是目標售價。缺點是完全唔理市場需求，客人未必肯畀呢個價。

* [Flashcard 3]
  - Front (Question in English): Contrast market skimming and market penetration pricing strategies, with one example of each.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Skimming: initial price set HIGHER than competitors; targets early adopters willing to pay a premium (e.g. Apple's iPhones, patented drugs); price lowered later to capture more customers
      - Penetration: price set LOWER than competitors; aims for rapid volume, market share and economies of scale (e.g. FMCG private brands such as Wellcome 'No Frill', Xiaomi phones); the opposite of skimming
    - [中文白話解釋]: Skimming 係「高開後降」，賺首批忠實粉絲的溢價；Penetration 係「低開搶客」，用量換市佔同規模經濟。記住方向相反：skim = 撇油取高價，penetrate = 低價滲透市場。

* [Flashcard 4]
  - Front (Question in English): Explain the three degrees of price discrimination and the role of price elasticity of demand.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Price discrimination = charging different customers different prices for the same product/service
      - First-degree: charge the maximum each customer will pay for every unit
      - Second-degree: different prices for different quantities consumed (e.g. mobile data)
      - Third-degree: different prices for different customer groups (e.g. senior citizen transport fares, movie tickets)
      - Customers with more INELASTIC demand are charged MORE (e.g. funeral services); customer groups must not overlap
    - [中文白話解釋]: 差別定價即係「同人唔同價」。一度係逐個客人榨到盡；二度係按用量收費（買多平啲）；三度係按群組收費（長者優惠）。需求越「無彈性」（越需要件產品）的客群，可以收得越貴。前提係客群之間不能互相轉售/重疊。

* [Flashcard 5]
  - Front (Question in English): A company stored 500 air conditioners for 10 years (original total unit cost HK$10,000). To sell them now it must spend HK$500 refurbishment per unit and HK$100,000 total transportation. What is the minimum selling price per unit, and why are the original costs ignored?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Minimum price = refurbishment HK$500 + transportation (HK$100,000 ÷ 500 = HK$200) = HK$700 per unit
      - Original manufacturing costs are SUNK costs — already incurred, unchanged by any future decision → irrelevant
      - Any price above HK$700 yields a profit (relevant/incremental pricing strategy)
    - [中文白話解釋]: 十年前生產的成本係沉沒成本 (sunk cost)，做唔做呢單生意都改變唔到，所以定價時完全唔使理。只有將來先會發生的翻新費同運費先係 relevant cost，加埋 HK$700 就係最低售價底線，高過呢個數就有賺。

* [Flashcard 6]
  - Front (Question in English): List the four categories of the cost of quality and give an example of each.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Preventive costs: avoid failures — procedures, training, testing, preventive maintenance, supplier qualification
      - Appraisal costs: detect defects before shipping — testing components and final products, testing equipment
      - Internal failure costs: defects found before delivery — rework, scrap, re-testing, sales of seconds
      - External failure costs: defects after delivery — returns, warranty claims, product recalls, lawsuits, lost customers/revenue
    - [中文白話解釋]: Cost of quality 係指「品質唔好」帶嚟的成本，記憶口訣 P-A-I-E：預防（開事前）、檢驗（出貨前查）、內部失敗（出貨前發現要翻工）、外部失敗（到客人手先發現，代價最大，包括商譽損失）。內部改善通常平過等客人投訴。

* [Flashcard 7]
  - Front (Question in English): Describe the four pricing strategies commonly used by non-profit making entities.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Voluntary pricing (PWYW): customers choose what to pay, possibly zero; floor/suggested price optional
      - Ability to pay: buyers should not pay more than they can afford; progressive pricing; called "capacity" in banking
      - Versioning ("quality discrimination"): different models of the same product at different prices; suits high fixed cost / low variable cost products (e.g. software)
      - Bundle services/memberships: package priced lower than separate items; aims to capture consumer surplus (willingness to pay − price paid)
    - [中文白話解釋]: 非牟利機構唔以賺錢為目標，定價為達成使命。PWYW 畀客人隨心畀錢；Ability to pay 按負擔能力收費（有能力者補貼無能力者）；Versioning 同一產品出幾個版本不同價；Bundling 打包平賣，目的係將客人的 consumer surplus（願付與實付之差）變成機構收入。

* [Flashcard 8]
  - Front (Question in English): What are the key external market factors affecting pricing, and how does activity-based costing (ABC) improve cost-based pricing?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - External factors: Demand, Competition, Government regulation (plus user group, location/point of consumption, time/perishability)
      - ABC four steps: (1) form cost pools by activity; (2) allocate only controllable capacity costs; (3) choose cost drivers with the strongest causal relation; (4) use PRACTICAL capacity as denominator volume
      - Using practical capacity avoids overstating unit costs when volumes are low and reveals unused capacity, giving more accurate costs for pricing
    - [中文白話解釋]: 外部三寶：需求、競爭、政府規管。內部就靠成本結構定價。ABC 比傳統方法準，因為佢按活動歸集成本、只分攤可控成本、揀最有因果關係的 cost driver，而且用「實際可用產能」(practical capacity) 做分母 —— 咁樣唔會因為閒置產能而谷大單位成本，仲可以揭發 unused capacity 幫手控制成本。
