**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Kowloon Ventures Ltd is evaluating two **mutually exclusive** projects, Peak and Harbour. Both require an initial outlay of HK$2 million and have conventional cash flows. At the company's 10% cost of capital, Project Peak has an IRR of 18% and an NPV of HK$120,000; Project Harbour has an IRR of 15% and an NPV of HK$210,000. Which of the following is the correct decision and reasoning?

A. Select Project Peak because it has the higher internal rate of return.
B. Select Project Harbour because it has the higher net present value, which represents the bigger increase in the company's value.
C. Select both projects because both have a positive NPV and an IRR above the cost of capital.
D. Select Project Peak because a higher IRR always implies a higher NPV for mutually exclusive projects.

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

Shatin Manufacturing Ltd is deciding whether to replace an old machine. The old machine has a book value of HK$800,000, could be sold today on the used-equipment market for HK$500,000, and a feasibility study costing HK$120,000 has already been completed and paid for. In the incremental cash flow analysis of the replacement decision, which of the following correctly classifies these amounts?

A. The book value of HK$800,000 is a relevant cost; the sale value of HK$500,000 is a sunk cost; the study cost of HK$120,000 is an opportunity cost.
B. The book value of HK$800,000 is an opportunity cost; the sale value of HK$500,000 is a sunk cost; the study cost of HK$120,000 is a relevant cost.
C. The book value of HK$800,000 and the study cost of HK$120,000 are sunk costs and should be ignored; the sale value of HK$500,000 is an opportunity cost and must be included.
D. All three amounts are sunk costs and none should appear in the analysis.

**Question 4 (Calculation — write the numerical answer with units)**

Cheung Chau Ltd is appraising a three-year project. The project requires equipment costing HK$260,000 plus an immediate investment in net working capital of HK$40,000 (recovered in full at the end of Year 3). The project will generate after-tax operating cash inflows of HK$100,000 at the end of each of Years 1, 2 and 3. The equipment has no salvage value. The project's cost of capital is 10%.

Calculate the net present value (NPV) of the project, rounded to the nearest HK dollar, and state whether the project should be accepted.

**Question 5 (Calculation — write the numerical answer with units)**

Discovery Bay Ltd invests HK$500,000 in a project that produces constant after-tax cash inflows of HK$150,000 at the end of each year for five years. The discount rate is 10%. Cash inflows are received evenly throughout each year.

(a) Calculate the discounted payback period, rounded to two decimal places (in years).

(b) State the simple (undiscounted) payback period in years and briefly state one reason why the two figures differ.
