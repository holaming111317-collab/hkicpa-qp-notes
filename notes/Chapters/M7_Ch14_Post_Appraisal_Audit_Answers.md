**Question 1 — Correct answer: B**

**Model answer (English):**
The primary purpose of a post-completion audit (also called the follow-up stage) is to assess the success of the project — i.e. to determine whether the project exceeded, met or fell short of its expectations. Actual results (expenses, revenues, cash flows) are compared with expected results and initial assumptions, and differences must be explained.

【解題思路】
- 教材明確指出：「The aim of a post-completion audit … is to determine if projects have met expectations」，所以 B 係唯一正確答案。
- **A 錯**：revision of the budget 唔係主要目的；預算差異只係審計過程中比較嘅其中一環（考官報告指出：考生常將審計的附帶動作當成主要目的）。
- **C 錯**：manager performance review 唔係審計嘅主要目的；審計甚至應避免變成個人問責大會（pinpoint responsibility **without pre-assigning blame**），否則員工以後唔敢提案。
- **D 錯**：assigning responsibility for budget variances 明確被教材列為「唔係審計嘅用途」；審計用嚟改善未來項目表現，而唔係找替罪羊。

**Question 2 — Correct answer: B**

**Model answer (English):**
Continuous measurement uses clearly expressed criteria measured on a scale (e.g. 1–10) and is performed at the end of each phase of the project. As a monitoring system it is preferred over end-of-project methods because it highlights emerging problems before they become critical — allowing the company to compare progress with budget and timing standards at every phase and to make corrections, or even abandon the project, in time to limit losses.

【解題思路】
- 呢題考 timing（幾時做審計）+ continuous measurement 嘅本質。正解係 B：每個 phase 末評核、對照 budget 同時間標準、問題未惡化前已發現。
- **A 錯**：教材話 project goals could be redefined if necessary；continuous measurement 唔係用嚟鎖死目標。
- **C 錯**：idle labour（發現邊個員工得閒）唔係 continuous measurement 嘅重點 — 呢個係典型「似是而非」干擾項。
- **D 錯**：呢個選項暗藏「at the end of the project」— 係最致命嘅錯誤。Continuous measurement 係 **at the end of each phase** 進行，唔係項目完結先做（考官報告指出：考生經常混淆 continuous measurement 與 end-of-project evaluation 嘅時點）。

**Question 3 — Correct answer: B**

**Model answer (English):**
The marketing director's argument commits the sunk-cost fallacy. Under post-completion audit principles, significant capital already invested in launching a project does NOT justify continuing it if the project appears to be failing. It makes more sense to cut costs immediately if the future does not appear likely to yield the expected revenues and profits. The evaluation should revisit the initial assumptions (marketing effectiveness, consumer information, product testing, pricing competitiveness) and compare them with actual results, leading either to abandonment or to improvement steps.

【解題思路】
- 呢題考 abandonment decision rule：去留決定睇**未來**預期收入與利潤，唔睇已投入嘅資本（sunk cost）。
- **A 錯**：「唔可以浪費已投資嘅錢」正正係教材點名否定嘅諗法；繼續一個失敗項目只會擴大虧損。
- **C 錯**：preliminary impact assessment (PIA) 與去留決定嘅原則無關；而且教材提到公司往往因成本同人力原因唔願意做 PIA。
- **D 錯**：教材冇規定任何「最短觀察期」；六個月短唔短唔係重點，重點係未來現金流預期。呢個干擾項偷換概念，將原則問題變成時間問題。
- 實務上，audit 資訊令公司可以 abandon failing projects and limit losses — 放棄選項本身擴大咗管理層嘅選擇、降低風險。

**Question 4 — Correct answer: CPI = 0.80**

**Model answer (English):**
Working:
- CPI = Earned Value ÷ Actual Costs
- CPI = HK$4,800,000 ÷ HK$6,000,000 = **0.80**

Interpretation: the Cost Performance Index measures cost efficiency by dividing the value of work actually performed (earned value) by actual costs. A CPI below 1 indicates poor cost efficiency — the company incurred HK$6.0 million of actual cost to generate only HK$4.8 million of work value, i.e. a cost overrun that the post-completion audit must explain.

【解題思路】
- 記住公式：**CPI = Earned Value ÷ Actual Costs**（教材 §14.2.3, measure no. 4）。
- Step 1：認清 earned value = HK$4.8m（做咗嘅嘢值幾錢）；actual costs = HK$6.0m（實際使咗幾錢）。
- Step 2：4.8 ÷ 6.0 = 0.80。
- 常見錯誤：調轉分子分母（6.0 ÷ 4.8 = 1.25）— 咁會得出「表現好」嘅錯誤結論；記住 CPI < 1 = 超支。
- 常見錯誤：將單位寫成「HK$0.80」或百分比 80% — CPI 係一個無單位嘅 index/ratio，答 0.80 即可（寫 0.8 都可接受）。

**Question 5 — Correct answer: (a) SPI = 0.75; (b) Late**

**Model answer (English):**
Working:
- (a) SPI = total authorised project duration ÷ total final project duration
- SPI = 18 months ÷ 24 months = **0.75**
- (b) Since the final duration (24 months) exceeded the authorised duration (18 months), the project finished **late**; an SPI below 1 confirms schedule inefficiency (the project took one-third longer than authorised).

【解題思路】
- 記住公式：**SPI = total authorised project duration ÷ total final project duration**（教材 §14.2.3, measure no. 5，用嚟 determine project management efficiency）。
- Step 1：authorised duration = 18 個月；final duration = 24 個月。
- Step 2：18 ÷ 24 = 0.75。SPI < 1 即係延誤。
- 常見錯誤一：調轉分子分母（24 ÷ 18 = 1.33）— 會誤判項目「早咗/快咗」，其實 1.33 喺呢個公式下無意義，因為 final duration 永遠唔應該放分子。
- 常見錯誤二：(b) 答 "on time" — 因為項目「最終完成咗」。完成 (completed) 同準時 (on schedule) 係兩回事；教材指出大部分項目雖然完成，但 suffer from cost or schedule overruns。
- 考試貼士：CPI 同 SPI 都係「細過 1 = 差」，呢個一致性幫你快速 sanity-check 答案。
