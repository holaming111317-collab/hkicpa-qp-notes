**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Harbour Division of Kowloon Foods Ltd currently earns an ROI of 25% on average operating assets of HK$2,000,000. The division manager, whose annual bonus is based on the division's ROI, is evaluating a new project requiring an investment of HK$500,000 and expected to generate additional operating income of HK$100,000 per year. The company's required rate of return is 15%. Which of the following statements correctly describes the decision the manager is most likely to make, and its effect?

A. The manager will accept the project because the project's ROI of 20% exceeds the company's required rate of return of 15%, which is goal-congruent.
B. The manager will reject the project because its ROI of 20% is below the division's current ROI of 25%, even though the project's return exceeds the company's required rate of return.
C. The manager will reject the project because the project produces a negative residual income for the division.
D. The manager will accept the project because residual income, unlike ROI, is an absolute dollar measure and therefore always motivates acceptance of new projects.

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

A company is designing a balanced scorecard. Which of the following measures belongs to the internal process perspective?

A. Return on equity (ROE)
B. Customer retention rate
C. Production cycle time
D. Percentage of employees trained in food sanitation

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

Which of the following statements about financial performance measures is correct?

A. ROI, RI and EVA are leading indicators that predict the future performance of an organisation.
B. ROI, RI and EVA are lagging measures reflecting past performance, and contain limited information about an organisation's potential for future performance.
C. Financial measures highlight individual segment results rather than the aggregate performance of the company or division.
D. Financial measures provide forward-looking data that allow management to take quick corrective action.

**Question 4 (Objective calculation)**

Wan Chai Division reports operating income of HK$360,000 on average operating assets of HK$1,500,000. The company's required rate of return is 14%.

(a) Calculate the division's return on investment (ROI), expressed as a percentage.
(b) Calculate the division's residual income (RI), in HK$.

**Question 5 (Objective calculation)**

Lantau Ltd has an adjusted after-tax operating income of HK$4,200,000. Its adjusted total assets are HK$38,000,000 and its current liabilities are HK$6,000,000. The company's weighted average cost of capital (WACC) is 10%.

Calculate Lantau Ltd's Economic Value Added (EVA), in HK$.
