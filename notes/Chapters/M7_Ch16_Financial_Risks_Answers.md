## Question 1 — Correct Answer: **B (1, 2 and 4 only)**

**Model Answer (English):**

The four types of financial risk are credit risk, foreign investment risk, liquidity risk and market risk.

- Exposure 1 — a bond issuer failing to pay principal by the due date is **default risk**, a form of **credit risk** (financial risk). ✔
- Exposure 2 — being unable to find a buyer at any reasonable price for an unlisted asset is **asset liquidity risk** (financial risk). ✔
- Exposure 3 — losing the best salespeople to a competitor is a form of **sales risk**, which is a separate risk category, **not** a financial risk. ✘
- Exposure 4 — freezing of a company's assets located in a foreign country when that country defaults on its loans is **country risk**, classified in the Learning Pack as a type of **credit risk** (financial risk). ✔

Therefore exposures 1, 2 and 4 are forms of financial risk → **Answer B**.

【解題思路】
- 第一步:背出 financial risk 四兄弟 — credit、foreign investment、liquidity、market。凡係屬於呢四類(包括其細分)就係 financial risk。
- Exposure 1 係 textbook 嘅 default risk,屬 credit risk,無爭議。
- Exposure 2 關鍵詞係 "cannot find a buyer at any reasonable price" — 呢個係 asset liquidity risk(lack of a ready market),屬 liquidity risk。唔好同 business risk 混淆。
- Exposure 3 係最強干擾項:流失頂尖 salespeople 聽落好「財務」上痛,但 Learning Pack 明確將佢歸入 **sales risk**(competitors recruiting the best salespeople),而 sales risk 係獨立類別,唔屬 financial risk 四類之一。
- Exposure 4 係 country risk,source 將佢列為 credit risk 嘅 type(凍結海外資產),所以都係 financial risk。
- 點解 A 錯:漏咗 exposure 4(country risk 都係 credit risk 嘅一種)。點解 C 錯:收埋咗 exposure 3(sales risk)又漏咗 exposure 1。點解 D 錯:exposure 3 唔係 financial risk。

## Question 2 — Correct Answer: **A (1 = Avoidance; 2 = Mitigation; 3 = Transfer; 4 = Acceptance)**

**Model Answer (English):**

- Action 1 — not constructing a plant in a flood plain = **Avoidance**: the removal of exposure altogether to a known potential loss; it is designed not to be exposed in any sense to the hazard.
- Action 2 — wearing hardhats = **Mitigation**: identification and enactment of actions that *reduce* threat levels. The Learning Pack uses exactly this example: a hardhat will not prevent injury if a falling object hits a worker, but it reduces (mitigates) the degree of injury.
- Action 3 — buying casualty insurance = **Transfer**: the risk is assigned to the insurer in exchange for payment of a premium; insurance is the most common form of risk transfer.
- Action 4 — self-insuring minor claims with a deductible and 20% co-pay after cost analysis = **Acceptance**: assumption of a known risk, without attempting to remove it, because the cost of managing the risk exceeds the potential loss.

→ **Answer A**.

【解題思路】
- 判斷口訣:完全唔碰 = Avoidance;做咗嘢但風險仲在(細咗)= Mitigation;俾 premium 人哋孭 = Transfer;分析後自己硬食 = Acceptance。
- Action 2 係 source 原文例子(hardhats mitigate the degree of injury),所以一定係 mitigation — 呢個鎖死咗 B 同 C 都錯(B 將 1 同 2 對調;C 話 hardhats 係 transfer,但冇俾錢第三方承擔)。
- Action 3 有 premium、有 insurer,係最典型嘅 transfer;D 將 3 同 4 對調,話買保險係 acceptance — 錯,因為風險已經轉移咗俾保險公司,唔係自己承受。
- Action 4 有 "after analysis concludes that the cost of further action exceeds the potential loss" — 呢個係 risk acceptance 嘅標準定義;佢係 conscious decision,所以唔係 risk ignorance。

## Question 3 — Correct Answer: **A**

**Model Answer (English):**

- Investing in another country incurs **foreign investment risk**: changes in taxation imposed by the host country, rapid inflation, economic conflict or expropriation of assets, nationalisation of assets, and a volatile political climate.
- **Political risk**: mitigated by purchasing **political risk insurance**, which protects overseas investments against political violence or confiscation of assets, covering business disruption up to outright loss of the overseas business. (This is risk transfer.)
- **Currency risk (xeno depreciation)**: mitigated through **derivatives** against depreciation of the foreign currency, **or** by creating a **natural hedge — borrowing in the local currency (xeno) in Country X to finance the assets in Country X**. If the xeno depreciates, both the local assets and the local-currency liabilities fall in home-currency terms, offsetting each other.

→ **Answer A**.

【解題思路】
- 本題考兩層:(i) 政治風險用 political risk insurance 轉移;(ii) 貨幣貶值風險用 derivatives 或 natural hedge。
- **Natural hedge 方向係關鍵**:要對沖 xeno 貶值,就要借 xeno(當地貨幣)嚟融資當地資產 — 貶值時資產同負債一齊縮水,自然抵銷。
- 點解 B 錯:借 HK dollars 融資 Country X 資產,負債用港元計值,xeno 貶值時資產跌、負債唔跌,反而**加大**咗 exposure,係錯誤方向,唔係 hedge。
- 點解 C 錯:tightening(30 days → 15 days)同 credit insurance 係 **credit risk** 嘅緩減方法,同 currency/political risk 無關 — 呢個係典型嘅概念張冠李戴干擾項。
- 點解 D 錯:diversification 係緩減 **concentration credit risk**(擴大借款人池),唔可以對沖或 "eliminate" 貨幣風險;而且匯率風險只能緩減/對沖,唔能夠 "eliminate entirely"。

## Question 4 — Correct Answer: **70.0%**

**Model Answer (English):**

Loan-to-value (LTV) ratio = Amount borrowed ÷ Total value of the asset

LTV = HK$455,000 ÷ HK$650,000 = 0.7000 = **70.0%**

Interpretation: the lender will price the loan using risk-based pricing — the lower the LTV ratio, the lower the risk; at 70.0% the borrower has financed 30% of the equipment value with own funds.

【解題思路】
- 本章唯一計算公式,分子 = amount borrowed(HK$455,000),分母 = total value of the asset(HK$650,000),調轉會變 142.9% — 一見超過 100% 就要知自己摆錯咗。
- 455 ÷ 650 = 0.7 啱啱好,記得轉做 percentage 同埋題目要求 one decimal place → 70.0%。
- 常見錯誤:(i) 用資產值 ÷ 借款額;(ii) 答 "0.7" 而唔轉 percentage;(iii) 四捨五入做 70% 但題目指明要一位小數。
- 考試貼士:source 例子係 HK$310,000 ÷ HK$400,000 = 77.5%,格式同呢題一模一樣,照版煮碗就得。

## Question 5 — Correct Answer: **HK$630,000**

**Model Answer (English):**

Step 1: LTV = Amount borrowed ÷ Total value of the asset, so Maximum borrowing = LTV cap × Total asset value.

Step 2: Maximum borrowing = 70% × HK$900,000 = 0.70 × 900,000 = **HK$630,000**

Check: HK$630,000 ÷ HK$900,000 = 70.0% = the lender's maximum acceptable LTV. ✔

【解題思路】
- 呢題係 LTV 公式嘅反向應用:俾你 LTV 上限(70%)同資產值(HK$900,000),求 maximum amount borrowed。
- 重組公式:Amount borrowed = LTV × Asset value = 0.70 × 900,000 = 630,000。
- 驗算永遠值得做:630,000 ÷ 900,000 = 70%,啱啱好貼住上限,無超標。
- 常見錯誤:(i) 用 900,000 ÷ 0.70 = 1,285,714(將公式調轉咗);(ii) 用 30% × 900,000 = 270,000(計咗 borrower 自己要出嘅部分,唔係銀行借出嘅部分);(iii) 忘記 LTV 越低風險越低,「上限 70%」意思係借出額唔可以超過資產值嘅 70%。
- 考試貼士:見到 "will not grant any loan with LTV above X%" 即係 cap,答案一定係 cap × asset value,唔好用 100% − cap 嚟答。
