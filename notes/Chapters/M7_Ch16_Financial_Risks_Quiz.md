**Question 1 (MCQ)**

A company's treasurer lists the following four exposures:

1. A bond issuer in the company's portfolio fails to pay principal by the due date.
2. The company cannot find a buyer at any reasonable price for an unlisted asset it wishes to sell.
3. The company's sales team loses its best salespeople to a competitor offering higher commissions.
4. A foreign government freezes the company's assets located in that country after defaulting on its own loans.

Which of the above exposures are forms of **financial risk**?

A. 1 and 2 only
B. 1, 2 and 4 only
C. 2, 3 and 4 only
D. All of the above

---

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

A manufacturing company takes the following four actions:

1. Decides not to construct a new plant in a known flood plain.
2. Requires all shop-floor workers to wear hardhats, which reduce but do not eliminate injuries from falling objects.
3. Purchases a casualty insurance policy so that fire losses will be reimbursed by the insurer (subject to deductibles and policy limits) in exchange for a premium.
4. After analysis concludes that the cost of further action exceeds the potential loss, decides to self-insure minor employee health claims subject to a deductible and a 20% co-pay.

Which option correctly matches each action to a financial risk management approach?

A. 1 = Avoidance; 2 = Mitigation; 3 = Transfer; 4 = Acceptance
B. 1 = Mitigation; 2 = Avoidance; 3 = Transfer; 4 = Acceptance
C. 1 = Avoidance; 2 = Transfer; 3 = Mitigation; 4 = Acceptance
D. 1 = Avoidance; 2 = Mitigation; 3 = Acceptance; 4 = Transfer

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

HK Textiles Ltd, a Hong Kong company, invests in a garment factory in Country X. The factory's profits will be received in Country X's currency, the xeno, and management is concerned about depreciation of the xeno and the volatile political climate in Country X.

Which of the following correctly describes the mitigation steps available to HK Textiles Ltd **as described in the Learning Pack**?

A. Purchase political risk insurance; and create a natural hedge by borrowing in xeno in Country X to finance the assets in Country X.
B. Purchase political risk insurance; and create a natural hedge by borrowing in HK dollars in Hong Kong to finance the assets in Country X.
C. Tighten credit terms to customers in Country X from 30 days to 15 days; and purchase credit insurance on the factory assets.
D. Purchase political risk insurance; and eliminate currency risk entirely by diversifying the pool of borrowers in Country X.

**Question 4 (Calculation)**

A borrower is purchasing capital equipment with a total value of HK$650,000. The amount borrowed from the bank is HK$455,000. Calculate the borrower's **loan-to-value (LTV) ratio**, expressed as a percentage to one decimal place.
