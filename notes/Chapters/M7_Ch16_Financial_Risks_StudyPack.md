# M7 Financial Risks and Their Management — Study Pack (Week 12)

> 本章對應 HKICPA QP Associate Level Module 7 (Financial Management), Learning Pack Chapter 16。
> 考試形式(自 2025 年 12 月起):2 小時 30 分鐘、100% Objective Type Questions (OTQs)、閉卷、電腦應考。本章以概念辨析題為主,唯一計算考點為 Loan-to-value ratio。

## Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

### 1. 核心知識架構

- 本章將風險分為四大類:Financial risks(財務風險,包括 Credit risk、Foreign investment risk、Liquidity risk、Market risk)、Business risk(業務風險)、Operating risk(營運風險,按 Basel II 定義)及 Sales risk(銷售風險);考試最常考「邊類風險屬於邊個類別」嘅概念辨析。
- Key Risk Indicator (KRI) 係風險經理用嚟量化特定風險程度嘅工具,用作估計潛在未來損失;要同 Key Performance Indicator (KPI) 區分,亦要掌握 Risk appetite(風險胃納)嘅四種 profile:Averse / Minimal / Open / Cautious。
- Financial Risk Management 有四大方法:Avoidance(避免)、Mitigation(緩減)、Transfer(轉移,最常見形式係 Insurance)、Acceptance(接受);情境題會俾一個行動,要求你判斷屬於邊一種方法。
- Credit risk 嘅緩減方法有六種:Risk-based pricing、Credit insurance、Credit derivatives (CDS)、Covenants、Diversification、Tightening;當中 Loan-to-value ratio 係本章唯一必考計算。
- 風險管理唔係獨立職能;Risk avoidance(有意識咁遠離風險)同 Risk ignorance(唔知有風險)嘅分別係考官熱門陷阱。

### 2. 必背考點清單

**A. Types of Risk(風險類別)**

| 類別 | 定義 | 細分/例子 |
|---|---|---|
| Financial risk | Any risk involved with financial transactions, lending, capitalisation and, in the extreme, default or bankruptcy | Credit risk、Foreign investment risk、Liquidity risk、Market risk |
| Business risk | Risk of not earning profits or meeting profit goals; or suffering a net loss instead of a profit; independent of capital structure | Internal risks(e.g. labour strikes, uninsured disaster losses);External risks(e.g. natural disasters, terrorist attacks, loss of a large customer, regulatory changes, price competition)。五種分類:Strategic / Financial / Compliance / Operational / Natural disaster or human-made events |
| Operating risk | Basel II (June 2004) 定義:'The risk of loss resulting from inadequate or failed internal processes, people and systems or from external events.' | Fraud、sabotage、embezzlement、poor training;Basel II 三大支柱:minimum capital requirements、supervisory review、market discipline;Basel III 自 2013 年起實施 |
| Sales risk | The potential for sales failures | Poor lead generation、inadequately trained sales staff、competitors recruiting the best salespeople |

**B. Credit Risk 細分(借方同貸方都面對 credit risk)**

- **Default risk**: borrower unable to make timely payments on a loan;default can be partial or full(secured loan partial default → lender acquires collateral)。
- **Country risk**: loss from freezing of assets in a foreign country when that country defaults or freezes assets(與政治不穩有關)。
- **Concentration risk**: loss from exposure in a single company or industry(e.g. 2008–2009 financial tsunami = 單一行業;Enron 2000 = 單一公司)。
- **Sovereign risk**: a government is unable or unwilling to make loan repayments or defaults on loan guarantees;最易喺 severe recession 時發生。
- **Counterparty risk**(a form of default risk): counterparty does not meet obligations on contracted loans, bonds, derivatives or insurance policies;often results from liquidity problems。

**C. Credit Risk 六大緩減方法(常考 list 題)**

1. **Risk-based pricing** — 對高風險借款人收較高利率,把風險分散於整體借款人;基於 credit history、payment history、loan purpose、amount borrowed、credit rating 及 **loan-to-value ratio**。
2. **Credit insurance** — lender pays a premium; the insurer assumes the credit risk。
3. **Credit derivatives** — **Credit default swap (CDS)**: the seller pays the buyer if a debt default occurs(the seller insures the buyer against credit default)。
4. **Covenants** — contractual stipulations:periodic financial reports、restrictions on dividends / share repurchases、caps on further borrowing;debt-to-equity ratio 或 interest coverage ratio 惡化可觸發 demand for full repayment。
5. **Diversification** — expanding the pool of borrowers to offset concentration risk。
6. **Tightening** — limiting or reducing maximum credit;or shortening repayment terms(e.g. 30 days → 15 days)。

**D. 必背公式:Loan-to-value ratio(LTV)**

- **LTV = Amount borrowed ÷ Total value of the asset**
- The lower the ratio, the lower the risk(lender changes interest rate based on LTV)。
- Source example: equipment worth HK$400,000; borrowed HK$310,000 → LTV = 310,000 ÷ 400,000 = **77.5%**。
- 考試貼士:LTV 可以三個方向出題 — (i) 俾借款額同資產值計 LTV;(ii) 俾目標 LTV 上限同資產值計 maximum borrowing;(iii) 比較兩個借款人邊個風險高。記住「越低越安全」。

**E. Foreign Investment Risk 及 Currency Risk 緩減**

- Foreign investment risk 包括:host country 稅制改變、rapid inflation、economic conflict or expropriation of assets、nationalisation of assets、volatile political climate;較輕微形式:regulatory requirements、accounting standards、reporting requirements、external audit rules 差異。
- Currency valuation 變化會影響 liquidity 及盈利能力。
- 緩減方法(source 明確提及):
  - **Political risk insurance**(轉移):covers political violence、confiscation of assets、business disruption to outright loss of overseas business。
  - **Derivatives**(對沖 naira 等貨幣貶值)。
  - **Natural hedge(自然對沖)**: borrow in local currency in the host country to finance local assets,作為對沖當地貨幣貶值嘅 natural hedge。

**F. Liquidity Risk 與 Market Risk**

- **Asset liquidity risk**: lack of a ready market due to a too-wide bid–ask spread or other inhibiting factors。
- **Funding liquidity risk**: liabilities may not be paid when due because of illiquid conditions(too little cash)。
- **Ready market** 定義要認:a recognised established securities market with independent bona fide offers to buy and sell, price determined almost instantaneously, payment received within a relatively short time。
- **Market risk** = risk of loss due to movement in the price of assets;細分:**Equity risk**(stocks, indices, mutual funds)、**Commodity risk**(futures position or option 反向移動)、**Interest rate risk**(unfavourable changes in rates)、**Currency risk**(currency exchange product, e.g. FOREX)、**Margin risk**(short positions 要 post collateral;funds not available to satisfy a margin call)。

**G. Key Risk Indicator (KRI) 與 Risk Appetite**

- **KRI**: a tool to assign degrees of risk to specific activities or threats; estimates potential for future losses and consequences;provides warnings of emerging risk exposure;**monitors KNOWN risks**(不能預測所有風險);provides information about the probability of a risk occurring and potential costs。
- **KPI**: measures the level of performance of internal routines;**不是** potential future risk events 嘅指標。兩者可一齊用。
- **Risk appetite** = the degree or level of risk the company accepts as either affordable or unavoidable。四種 profile:**Averse**(唔想承受任何風險)、**Minimal**(只採取安全步驟)、**Cautious**(偏好安全行動及有限回報,同 minimal 類似)、**Open**(願意承受與潛在利潤匹配嘅風險)。
- KRI 開發循環(Exhibit 16.1):Identify major risks → List the underlying causes → Analyse the metrics of these causes → Define the thresholds and gaps in metrics → Describe data, frequency of events and reporting → Backtest to identify how to prevent future events → 回到起點再監察。
- 風險分類矩陣:High impact × High likelihood(最高優先,通常成本最高)、High impact × Low likelihood、Low impact × High likelihood、Low impact × Low likelihood;KRI 選擇重點放喺 high impact and high likelihood。
- 有效 KRI 三個屬性(Illustrative Example 2):**easily measured、significant、makes sense**。

**H. Financial Risk Management 四大方法**

1. **Avoidance** — removal of exposure to hazards;not responsive to realised losses;預先了解風險後完全迴避(e.g. 唔喺 flood plain 起廠房、佩戴 safety goggles)。**不等於 risk ignorance**。
2. **Mitigation** — reduction in threat levels(唔係完全消除);四步:Risk identification → Impact assessment → Assessment and analysis(tracking)→ Risk mitigation(Exhibit 16.2);緩減後五個選項:Acceptance / Avoidance / Control / Transfer / Monitoring。
3. **Transfer** — assigning the risk to another party;最常見形式係 **Insurance**(付 premium);其他:**Derivatives**(options 對沖 market risk;derivatives 對沖 currency exchange rate risk)、**Outsourcing by contract**(將 penalty 轉嫁 vendor)。
4. **Acceptance** — assumption of a known risk without attempting to remove it;當 managing the risk 嘅成本高於潛在損失時合理(e.g. self-insure health benefits with deductible + 20% co-pay + catastrophic coverage above ceiling)。
- **Risk ignorance** ≠ acceptance:acceptance 係經分析後嘅 conscious decision;ignorance 係 unaware/unconcerned(兩種情況:known risks 無向管理層匯報;或根本無做 risk assessment)。

### 3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

**Pitfall 1:Risk Avoidance vs Risk Ignorance(避免 vs 無知)**
- 對比:Avoidance 係**知道風險存在**,經評估後主動採取行動完全移除暴露;Ignorance 係**根本不知道或不關心**風險,無任何管理動作。Acceptance 都係「知道但選擇承受」,同 ignorance 嘅「唔知所以無做嘢」完全唔同。
- 例子:工人喺危險區域**戴 safety goggles** = avoidance(知風險、有行動);工人**唔戴 goggles 因為佢哋唔知有風險** = ignorance。考試見到 "no action taken because management was unaware of the risk" → 一定係 risk ignorance,唔係 acceptance!

**Pitfall 2:Business Risk vs Operating Risk(業務風險 vs 營運風險)**
- 對比:Business risk 係**賺唔到目標利潤或出現淨虧損**嘅風險,源於經營環境變化(消費者偏好、價格競爭、通脹、失業),與 capital structure 無關;Operating risk 係**internal processes、people and systems 失效或外部事件造成損失**(Basel II 定義),例如 fraud、sabotage、embezzlement、data errors,唔直接關盈利能力事,容易被低估。
- 例子:因為價格競爭導致 profit 唔達標 = business risk;員工 embezzlement 導致資產損失 = operating risk。記憶口訣:Business risk 問「賺唔賺到錢」,Operating risk 問「內部系統/人員出唔出問題」。

## Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

* [Flashcard 1]
  - Front (Question in English): What are the four types of financial risk, and what are the three other broad categories of risk an organisation faces?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Four financial risks: **Credit risk, Foreign investment risk, Liquidity risk, Market risk**
      - Other categories: **Business risk, Operating risk, Sales risk**
      - Financial risk = any risk involved with financial transactions, lending, capitalisation, and in the extreme default or bankruptcy
    - [中文白話解釋]: 記住口訣「財務風險四兄弟」:Credit(借錢唔還)、Foreign investment(海外投資被沒收/國有化)、Liquidity(想賣賣唔出)、Market(價格反向波動)。呢四個先係 financial risk;Business、Operating、Sales risk 係另外三個大類,OTQ 最鍾意將佢哋溝埋嚟考你分類。

* [Flashcard 2]
  - Front (Question in English): Distinguish default risk, country risk, concentration risk, sovereign risk and counterparty risk (all forms of credit risk).
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Default risk: borrower unable to make timely payments on a loan (partial or full)
      - Country risk: loss from freezing of a company's assets located in a foreign country
      - Concentration risk: loss from exposure in a single company or industry (e.g. Enron; 2008–09 financial sector)
      - Sovereign risk: a government unable or unwilling to make loan repayments or honour loan guarantees
      - Counterparty risk (a form of default risk): a counterparty fails to meet obligations on loans, bonds, derivatives or insurance policies, often due to liquidity problems
    - [中文白話解釋]: 五個都係 credit risk 家族:Default = 借款人不還錢;Country = 外國政府凍結你喺當地嘅資產;Concentration = 雞蛋放晒同一個籃(單一公司/行業);Sovereign = 國家政府本身唔還錢或不履行擔保;Counterparty = 交易對手違約,通常因為佢流動資金出事。見到 "government unwilling to repay" 揀 sovereign;見到 "single industry exposure" 揀 concentration。

* [Flashcard 3]
  - Front (Question in English): A borrower purchases equipment worth HK$500,000 financed by a loan of HK$365,000. Calculate the loan-to-value (LTV) ratio and state its risk implication.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - LTV = Amount borrowed ÷ Total value of the asset
      - LTV = HK$365,000 ÷ HK$500,000 = **73%**
      - The lower the LTV ratio, the lower the risk to the lender
      - Lenders use LTV in risk-based pricing: higher LTV → higher perceived risk → higher interest rate
    - [中文白話解釋]: LTV 就係「借幾多 ÷ 件資產值幾多」。呢條係本章唯一計算公式,分子永遠係借款額、分母永遠係資產總值。比率越低代表借款人自己落嘅本越多,銀行風險越細;比率越高,利率就會越貴(risk-based pricing)。

* [Flashcard 4]
  - Front (Question in English): List the six ways a lender can mitigate credit risk.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - 1. Risk-based pricing (higher rates for higher-risk borrowers, based on credit history, credit rating, LTV etc.)
      - 2. Credit insurance (insurer assumes the credit risk for a premium)
      - 3. Credit derivatives — credit default swap (CDS): seller pays buyer if a debt default occurs
      - 4. Covenants (periodic financial reports; restrictions on dividends, share repurchases, further borrowing)
      - 5. Diversification (expand the pool of borrowers to offset concentration risk)
      - 6. Tightening (limit/reduce maximum credit; shorten repayment terms, e.g. 30 days → 15 days)
    - [中文白話解釋]: 六招口訣:「價、保、衍、契、散、縮」— 收貴息(pricing)、買保險(insurance)、CDS 衍生工具、借貸契約條款(covenants)、分散借款人(diversification)、收緊額度或還款期(tightening)。呢個係典型 list-type OTQ,常見陷阱係將 "diversification" 寫成 "concentration" 或者混入非 lender 動作。

* [Flashcard 5]
  - Front (Question in English): A Hong Kong company invests in a plant in an overseas country and will receive returns in that country's currency. Identify the main risks and two mitigation steps for currency-related exposure.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Main risk: foreign investment risk — changes in host-country taxation, rapid inflation, expropriation/nationalisation of assets, volatile political climate; plus currency valuation risk
      - Mitigation 1: purchase **political risk insurance** (covers political violence, confiscation of assets, business disruption)
      - Mitigation 2 (currency): use **derivatives to hedge depreciation** of the foreign currency, or create a **natural hedge** by borrowing in the local currency to finance the local assets
    - [中文白話解釋]: 海外投資兩大威脅:政治層面(資產被沒收/國有化)同貨幣層面(當地貨幣貶值)。政治風險用 political risk insurance 轉移;貨幣風險可以用 derivatives 對沖,或者用 natural hedge — 喺當地借當地貨幣嚟融資當地資產,貶值時資產同負債一齊跌,互相抵銷。

* [Flashcard 6]
  - Front (Question in English): Distinguish asset liquidity risk from funding liquidity risk, and state what a 'ready market' means.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Asset liquidity risk: lack of a ready market to sell an asset due to a too-wide bid–ask spread or other inhibiting factors
      - Funding liquidity risk: liabilities may not be paid when due because of illiquid conditions (too little cash)
      - Ready market: a recognised established securities market with independent bona fide offers to buy and sell, where a price is determinable almost instantaneously and payment is received within a relatively short time
    - [中文白話解釋]: Asset liquidity 係「件資產賣唔賣得出去」嘅問題(bid–ask spread 太闊、無市場);Funding liquidity 係「你夠唔夠現金還到期債」嘅問題。一個講賣產,一個講還債,OTQ 會將兩個定義調轉嚟考你。

* [Flashcard 7]
  - Front (Question in English): What is a KRI, how does it differ from a KPI, and what are the four risk appetite profiles?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - KRI (key risk indicator): a tool to assign degrees of risk to specific activities/threats and estimate potential future losses and consequences; provides early warnings of emerging risk exposure; monitors **known** risks; gives probability and potential cost information
      - KPI (key performance indicator): measures the level of performance of internal routines — **not** a metric of potential future risk events
      - Risk appetite profiles: **Averse** (no steps leading to exposure), **Minimal** (only safe steps), **Cautious** (safe courses of action with limited rewards), **Open** (accepts risks matched to potential profits)
    - [中文白話解釋]: KRI 係「望將來」— 預警未來可能嘅損失,只監察已知風險,唔係萬能;KPI 係「度而家」— 衡量內部流程表現,同未來風險事件無關。Risk appetite 四級:Averse 最保守(零暴露),Minimal 同 Cautious 都偏好安全,Open 就願意「有回報先至有風險」。

* [Flashcard 8]
  - Front (Question in English): A company buys fire insurance, requires workers to wear hardhats (which reduce but do not eliminate injury), decides not to build a plant on a flood plain, and self-insures minor health claims with a deductible and co-pay. Match each action to a risk management approach.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Buy fire insurance → **Transfer** (risk assigned to insurer in exchange for a premium — the most common form of transfer)
      - Wear hardhats → **Mitigation** (reduces the degree of injury; threat level reduced, not removed)
      - Not building on a flood plain → **Avoidance** (complete removal of exposure to a known hazard)
      - Self-insure minor claims with deductible + co-pay → **Acceptance** (conscious assumption of a known risk because management cost exceeds potential loss)
    - [中文白話解釋]: 四大方法嘅判斷關鍵:完全唔碰 = Avoidance;做咗嘢但風險仲在(只係細咗)= Mitigation;俾錢人哋孭 = Transfer(保險係最常見);分析過成本效益後決定自己硬食 = Acceptance。記住 acceptance 必須係 conscious decision,否則就係 risk ignorance。
