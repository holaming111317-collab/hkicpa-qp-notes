## Question 1 — Answer: **D**

**Model Answer (English):**
A lease running for more than one year is generally considered **long-term financing**. A seven-year lease of office premises therefore falls into the long-term category; because business leases generally require no deposit, they essentially represent 100% debt financing.

- Option A is incorrect: commercial paper is a short-term promissory note with maturities of **1 to 270 days** (typically 30 days) — a money market instrument.
- Option B is incorrect: bankers' acceptances are issued at a discount with maturities of **1 to 180 days** (typically 90 days) — short-term money market instruments.
- Option C is incorrect: an operating (demand) line of credit is a standard short-term bank financing tool, priced at the floating prime lending rate.
- Option D is correct: a lease longer than one year is generally treated as long-term financing.

【解題思路】呢題考「短期 vs 長期」嘅分類邊界。關鍵分界線係 **一年**：少於一年 = money market 短期工具。三個錯誤選項都係教科書白紙黑字嘅短期工具（CP 1–270 日、BA 1–180 日、operating line of credit），佢哋嘅「日數」係提示。陷阱位在於 leasing 雖然喺課文編排上放喺短期融資一節，但原文講明「**超過一年嘅租約一般視為長期融資**」—— 七年租約明顯過界。考試見到年期數字，第一步先問自己：過唔過一年？

## Question 2 — Answer: **A**

**Model Answer (English):**
The correct liquidation ranking is: **senior secured debt → subordinated debt → preference shareholders → common shareholders**. Debt holders rank ahead of all equity holders; senior debt has preference over junior/subordinated debt; preference equity is senior to common equity; common shareholders are the residual owners and are last in line. Illustrative Example 3 applies exactly this waterfall: with HK$17m of liquidation proceeds and HK$15m owed to creditors, only HK$2m remains for shareholders, and a 5% equity holder receives only HK$100,000.

- Option B is incorrect: preference shares are **equity** and can never rank ahead of debt; nor can subordinated debt rank ahead of senior debt.
- Option C is incorrect: it reverses the debt seniority (subordinated ranks **below** senior/unsubordinated debt) and puts common equity ahead of preference equity.
- Option D is incorrect: it is the exact reverse of the correct order — common shareholders are **last**, not first.

【解題思路】記住大原則：**債先股後**。債入面再分 senior → junior/subordinated（subordinated = 無抵押兼排名最低嘅債）；股入面再分 preference → common。每個錯誤選項都係將某一層調轉：B 將 equity 放喺債前面；C 將 subordinated 放喺 senior 前面兼 common 放喺 preference 前面；D 係全反轉。呢個 ranking 亦解釋點解越 junior 嘅證券投資者要求越高回報 —— 風險補償同索償次序直接掛鈎。

## Question 3 — Answer: **C**

**Model Answer (English):**
This is a **PIPE (private investment in public equity)**: a transaction in which a **public company** sells **unregistered** stock to an investor (such as a hedge fund or other institutional investor), almost always **at a discount** to compensate for the limited marketability of the securities. As part of the PIPE contract, the company commonly agrees to **register the restricted securities, usually within 90 days** of closing; if the issuer cannot register them, it may be required to pay damages of as much as 1.5% per month. A PIPE gives the issuer faster access to capital at lower cost than a public offering.

- Option A is incorrect: an IPO is the **first** sale of common stock by a company in the public market — BestCo is already listed.
- Option B is incorrect: a general cash offer is a sale of **registered** securities **open to all investors** by a public company; here the shares are unregistered and sold privately to one investor.
- Option D is incorrect: a rights offering gives **existing shareholders** the right to buy shares; an insured/standby rights offering merely adds an investment bank backstop for unexercised rights — the buyer here is an outside hedge fund, not existing shareholders.

【解題思路】呢題考集資渠道嘅邊界分類。題目有三個決定性關鍵字：**已上市（public company）+ 未註冊股份（unregistered）+ 折讓賣畀單一機構投資者（hedge fund at a discount）+ 90 日內註冊承諾** —— 四點齊就係 PIPE 嘅定義特徵。A 錯在「first sale」；B 錯在 general cash offer 係公開、註冊、面向所有投資者；D 錯在 rights issue 對象係現有股東。分類題要訣：問自己 (1) 公司上咗市未？(2) 證券註冊咗未？(3) 賣畀邊個？三條答案就會指向唯一選項。

## Question 4 — Answer: **3.62%**

**Model Answer (English):**

Bond equivalent yield formula (money market convention, 360-day year):

i_bcy = (Face Value − Issue Price) / Issue Price × 360 / Days to maturity

Step 1: Discount = HK$50,000,000 − HK$49,700,000 = HK$300,000

Step 2: Periodic yield = HK$300,000 / HK$49,700,000 = 0.0060362 (≈ 0.6036% for 60 days)

Step 3: Annualise: 0.0060362 × 360/60 = 0.0060362 × 6 = 0.03622

**Bond equivalent yield ≈ 3.62%** (quoted annual yield)

【解題思路】逐步拆解：(1) 折扣 = 面值 − 發行價 = 300,000，呢個就係投資者嘅利息。(2) 回報率要除以**買入價（49.7m）**而唔係面值，因為投資者實際付出 49.7m —— 用面值做分母係最常見錯誤（會得出 0.6%×6 = 3.60%，看似好接近但係錯）。(3) 全年化用 **360 日**（貨幣市場慣例），60 日即乘 6；用 365 日係另一常見錯誤（會得出約 3.67%）。(4) 記住呢個 3.62% 係 **promised yield**，即發行人唔違約先收到嘅利率，亦係持有至到期嘅最高回報。

## Question 5 — Answer: **HK$45,000,000**

**Model Answer (English):**

Borrowing-base formula: Credit limit = 75% × Accounts Receivable + 50% × Inventory

Step 1: 75% × HK$40,000,000 (accounts receivable) = HK$30,000,000

Step 2: 50% × HK$30,000,000 (inventory) = HK$15,000,000

Step 3: Maximum credit limit = HK$30,000,000 + HK$15,000,000 = **HK$45,000,000**

(This mirrors Illustrative Example 1, where 75% × HK$18,536,000 + 50% × HK$74,196,000 = HK$51,000,000.)

【解題思路】呢題嘅陷阱係題目入面嘅 **cash HK$6,000,000** —— borrowing base 公式**只計 receivables 同 inventory**，cash 完全唔入公式。錯誤做法包括：(1) 將 cash 加埋入去（得出 51m）；(2) 將 cash 都當 100% 計入 borrowing base；(3) 百分比對調（50% receivables + 75% inventory = 42.5m）—— receivables 變現能力高過存貨，所以 receivables 攞 75%、inventory 只攞 50%，記唔住邊個高就用常識推：應收款通常好快變現金，存貨仲要賣得出先收到錢，所以存貨折讓深啲。考試做呢類題，第一步係圈返公式需要嘅變數，其他數字一律當干擾項。
