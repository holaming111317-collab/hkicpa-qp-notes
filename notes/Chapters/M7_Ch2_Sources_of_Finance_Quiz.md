**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Which of the following sources of finance would normally be classified as **long-term financing** rather than short-term (money market) financing?

A. A 30-day commercial paper issued at a discount by a creditworthy listed company
B. A bankers' acceptance with a 90-day maturity arising from an export transaction
C. An operating (demand) line of credit priced at prime plus 1%
D. A seven-year lease of office premises under which the lessee pays no deposit

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

On liquidation of a company, which of the following correctly ranks the claims from **first paid to last paid**?

A. Senior secured debt holders → subordinated debt holders → preference shareholders → common shareholders
B. Preference shareholders → subordinated debt holders → senior secured debt holders → common shareholders
C. Subordinated debt holders → senior secured debt holders → common shareholders → preference shareholders
D. Common shareholders → preference shareholders → subordinated debt holders → senior secured debt holders

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

BestCo Ltd is already listed on the Main Board of the Hong Kong stock exchange. It sells unregistered shares at a discount to a hedge fund, and undertakes to register the shares within 90 days of closing so that they can later be resold in the secondary market. This transaction is best described as:

A. An initial public offering (IPO)
B. A general cash offer open to all investors
C. A private investment in public equity (PIPE)
D. An insured (standby) rights offering to existing shareholders

**Question 4 (Calculation)**

Delta Ltd issues commercial paper with a face value of HK$50,000,000 at an issue price of HK$49,700,000, with 60 days to maturity. Using the bond equivalent yield convention, calculate the quoted annual yield on this commercial paper. (Show your answer as a percentage, rounded to 2 decimal places.)

**Question 5 (Calculation)**

Kowloon Traders Ltd applies to its bank for a line of credit. The bank's borrowing-base formula caps the line at 75% of accounts receivable plus 50% of inventory. Extracts from the company's latest statement of financial position:

| Item | HK$ |
|---|---|
| Cash | 6,000,000 |
| Accounts receivable | 40,000,000 |
| Inventory | 30,000,000 |

Calculate the maximum credit limit available to Kowloon Traders Ltd. (State your answer in HK$.)
