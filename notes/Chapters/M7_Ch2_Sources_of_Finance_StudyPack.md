# M7 Sources of Finance — Study Pack (Week 2)

## Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

### 1. 核心知識架構

- 本章核心係分辨企業籌資嘅各種途徑，首先按年期劃分：少於一年屬短期融資（short-term financing），即貨幣市場（money market）工具，包括 commercial paper、bankers' acceptance、line of credit、bank overdrafts、term loans、family/friends loans、leasing；多於一年屬長期融資（long-term financing / funded debt），包括 common stock、preference stock、long-term loans、bonds、hybrid securities（warrants、convertible bonds、coco bonds）。
- 第二層分類係債務與權益（debt vs equity）：debt 要還本付息、利息通常可扣稅（tax deductible）、唔會攤薄控制權；equity 唔使還、但會攤薄擁有權（dilution of ownership）及分享未來利潤。
- 第三層係集資渠道（raising capital）：venture capitalists → initial public offering (IPO) → general cash offer → private market（private placement、private equity、PIPE）→ bank loans，反映企業由初創到上市嘅融資階梯。
- 第四層係 internal financing（retained earnings、effective working capital management）與 external financing 嘅取捨：internal 平、冇抵押、唔攤薄，但金額有限；external 金額大但貴兼多限制。
- 計分位集中喺幾條公式：commercial paper 嘅 bond equivalent yield（360 日年）、borrowing base 信貸額度（75% receivables + 50% inventory）、interest coverage ratio 要求盈利、net working capital、inventory turnover 同 days in inventory。

### 2. 必背考點清單

**A. 短期融資（Short-Term Financing, < 1 year）**

1. **Commercial Paper (CP)**
   - 性質：short-term promissory notes，usually unsecured，maturities **1–270 days**，典型 **30 days**，金額大（CP/BA 市場最少約 US$1 million）。
   - 多以折讓價發行（issued at a discount from face value），到期收 face value，差額即利息。
   - **Bond equivalent yield 公式**（用 **360-day year**）：

     i_bcy = (Face Value − Issue Price) / Issue Price × 360 / Days to maturity

     例：face HK$100m、售 HK$99.55m、30 日 → (0.45/99.55) × 12 = **5.424%**。
   - 報價利率 = **promised yield**（如發行人唔違約、如期付款，投資者實收嘅利率；亦係持有至到期可賺嘅**最高**回報）。有 default risk 時，**expected return < promised yield**。
   - **Yield spread** = CP yield − 同年期 T-bill yield，係對 default risk 嘅補償；default risk 越高，promised yield 越高。
   - 評級（最高短期信貸質素）：S&P **A-1** / Moody's **P-1** / Fitch **F1**。
   - 流動性支持：backup line of credit，commitment fee 約 **0.125% p.a.**；經 dealer 發行佣金約 **5 basis points (0.05%)**。
   - **Asset-backed CP (ABCP)**：以特定資產（如 receivables）抵押，由 **special purpose vehicle (SPV)** 發行；maturity ≤ 270 days，典型 **90–140 days**；credit enhancement 例如 over-collateralisation 或外部保險。

2. **Bankers' Acceptance (BA)**
   - 用於跨境貿易（cross-border trading）：出口商收到進口商開出、銀行擔保嘅 bill of exchange；銀行「accept」後可貼現套現。
   - 折讓發行，maturity **1–180 days**，典型 **90 days**。
   - 成本 = yield + 銀行 stamping/acceptance fee（通常 **0.50–0.75%**，視乎信貸風險）。
   - 關鍵：BA 由**銀行嘅信用**支持，唔係公司嘅信用。

3. **Line of Credit**
   - 借款人只就已提取金額付息；設立費約 **0.50%**，且對 **unused balance** 亦收費。
   - **Operating / demand line of credit**：技術上屬 demand loan，銀行可隨時取消（但實務上鮮會）；標準成本係 **prime lending rate**（浮動）；大多數公司係「prime plus」借款人。
   - **Term / revolving line of credit**：金額隨現金狀況「revolve」；年期至少 **364 days**、一般最長 **5 years**，可每 6 個月續期（「evergreen」）；364 日嘅原因：1 年（365 日）嘅 line 要銀行預留更多資本。
   - 用途：直接借款、back up CP、或支持 forward foreign currency contracts；銀行著重 liquidity 用途而非 credit enhancement，material adverse change 時可撤回。
   - **Borrowing base 上限公式**：credit limit ≤ **75% × accounts receivable + 50% × inventory**（Illustrative Example 1：75%×18,536,000 + 50%×74,196,000 = 13,902,000 + 37,098,000 = **HK$51,000,000**；cash 唔計入）。
   - 財務限制（covenants）：例如 minimum current ratio、minimum net shareholders' equity、minimum **interest coverage ratio**（Illustrative Example 2：借 HK$50m @ 9.75%，coverage 1.75× → interest 4,875,000 × 1.75 = required earnings before interest **HK$8,531,250**）。

4. **Bank Overdrafts**：戶口結餘到零時自動啟動嘅信貸延伸，可繼續提款至某上限；**利率通常高於 bank loan**。

5. **Term Loans**：有 **fixed maturity** 及 **fixed repayment schedule**，用作較長期需要（如設備購置）；**可同時屬短期或長期融資**。**Bullet / balloon loans**：期內只付息、到期一次過還本；**amortising loans**：每月連本帶息攤還（annuity）。通常固定利率（但亦可以 prime 浮動）。

6. **Family/Friends Loans**：初創最常見來源；彈性大、低息或零息；缺點係唔還錢會鬧翻。基本條款：**principal、interest、repayment terms**。

7. **Leasing Financing**：lessor（法定擁有人）出租、lessee 取得使用權；**超過一年一般視為 long-term financing**；通常唔使按金 → 實質 **100% debt financing**；租約完結後控制權歸還 lessor（salvage right）。

**B. 長期融資（Long-Term Financing, > 1 year, 又稱 funded debt）**

8. **公開 vs 私人發行**：public offering 須向 **SFC** 提交 **prospectus**；private placement 用較簡單嘅 **offering memorandum**。證券法例同 prospectus 要求**同時適用於 debt 及 equity** 發行。

9. **Common Stock（普通股）**
   - Residual ownership；清盤時**排最尾**（先 creditors、再 preference shareholders）。
   - Statement of financial position 上嘅 common equity 組成：**common stock（par value；香港唔用 par value）、additional paid-in capital、retained earnings、accumulated comprehensive income or loss**；**treasury stock 係 contra-equity account**（扣減權益）。
   - 三大基本權利：**vote at shareholders' meeting；receive declared dividends；receive remaining property on dissolution**（股息必須由 board 宣派先有權收）。
   - **Limited liability**：股東最多蝕晒本金，上行潛力無限。
   - Multiple classes（如 Ford Class B、Berkshire A/B）：A/B 命名純屬任意；non-voting shares 通常有輕微股息溢價補償；只有買到 voting shares 先可以 takeover。

10. **Preference Stock（優先股）**
    - Equity interest，**senior to common equity**；基本型 **straight preference stock** = **perpetuity**（無到期日、定額股息，估值當永續）。
    - 有期限變種：**retractable**（投資者有權賣返畀發行人，典型 retraction date 5 年）、**soft-retractable**（發行人可揀付現金或普通股）、**mandatory redeemable**（指定日期或事件到期；金融機構發行因可當 regulatory equity capital）、**trust preference security**。
    - 股息種類（Exhibit 2.3）：**fixed / floating（benchmark）/ adjustable rate（ARPS，跟 HIBOR 等 base rate 浮動，市價貼近 par）/ participating（固定股息 + 額外分紅，常見於 VC，可能有 cap）/ cumulative**。
    - **Cumulative provision**：未找清 preference dividends（包括 arrears）之前，**唔准派 common dividends**；有時 arrears 累積會觸發 voting rights。
    - 其他特徵：convertible（內嵌轉換權，多數 callable，發行人可強制轉換）、mandatory convertible、多數 **non-voting**，但可有 **contingent voting right**（唔派息時生效）。

11. **Bonds（債券）**
    - 年期術語：< 10 年 = **note / medium-term note (MTN)**；**4–10 年 = intermediate-term bond**；**> 10 年 = bond / long-term bond**。
    - **Security**：有抵押 = secured debt（mortgage bonds、asset-backed bonds）；**debenture** = 只靠發行人 general credit 嘅債務。Unsecured debt 投資者要求較高回報。
    - **Seniority**：senior debt > junior debt；**subordinated** = 無抵押且排名低於其他債務；**unsubordinated** = 冇其他無抵押債務排名高過佢。越 junior，投資者要求嘅風險補償越高。
    - **Indenture agreement**：發行人與投資者嘅法律協議，載明 interest、maturity、collateral、callability、convertibility。**Affirmative covenant** = 承諾做（如準時付款）；**negative covenant** = 禁止（如唔准再抵押資產、限制派息）。
    - **Embedded options**：**callable bond** = **發行人**有權以 call price 贖回（有 call protection 期間；利率跌時被贖回風險升 → 投資者要求更高回報）；**convertible bond** = **投資者**有權轉換成普通股；conversion value 構成債券價值下限（bond floor = conversion value 與純債券價值兩者取低作為最低價格基準）。
    - **Bond ratings**：最高 **AAA/Aaa**；最低 investment grade = **BBB/Baa**；低於此 = **junk bond / high-yield bond**。兩大原則：**stable rating philosophy**（睇結構性因素、唔理週期波動）及 **hierarchy principle**（評級針對特定債務、唔係間公司）。六大評級因素：core profitability、asset quality、strategy & management、statement of financial position strength、business strength、miscellaneous。

12. **Hybrid Securities**
    - **Warrant**：持有人有權轉換/換取另一證券（如普通股），係 **call option 嘅企業融資版本**，但年期長。與 call option 兩大分別：**call option 係兩個外部投資者之間嘅交易、對公司無影響、年期短；warrant 由公司發行、年期長**。常用作 sweetener；可分 **naked warrants**（獨立發行）及 **detachable warrants**（可分拆出售）。
    - Call option 價值：**exercise price 越低越值錢；到期時間越長越值錢；相關資產波幅越大越值錢；利率越高越值錢**。
    - **Rights issues**：direct rights offerings（無 backstop）vs insured/standby rights offerings（投資銀行包銷未行使部分，較貴）。
    - **Convertible bonds**：按預定 ratio 由 **bondholder 決定**轉換；轉換價通常設定為股價要升至少 **15–20%** 先值得轉；因有轉換價值，**票面利率較低**、利息開支少、利息或可扣稅、轉股後公司再無責任；缺點：**dilution** 攤薄股基、可能壓低股價、影響日後 seasoned share sales。
    - **Coco bonds (contingent convertible bonds)**：類似 convertible bond 但多一重觸發條件 —— 須達到特定 **capital conditions** 先轉換；**對公司有利多過對投資者**。

**C. 集資渠道（Raising Capital）**

13. **Venture Capitalists**：1960 年代起以 limited partnerships 集資；初創難獲傳統融資因為 **(1) 風險太高、(2) 缺乏生產性資產、(3) information asymmetry**。減險手法：**staged funding（通常 3–7 個階段）、要求創辦人自己出資、syndication、行業專門知識**。VC 通常攞 **convertible preference stock**；**唔係長線投資者**，一般 **3–7 年**後退出（strategic buyer / financial buyer / IPO）。

14. **IPO**：公司**首次**公開發售普通股。優點：集資額大、可 follow-on seasoned offerings、創辦人保留控制權、有次級市場、易吸引管理人才。缺點：**成本高**（legal、accounting、printing、filing fees 等）、持續披露合規成本（香港 **GEM 合規成本低於 Main Board**）、披露帶來競爭劣勢、季度申報令公司短視。投資銀行三大服務：**origination、underwriting（承擔風險）、distribution（轉售予公眾）**。

15. **General Cash Offer**：上市公司向所有投資者發售證券，可能係最平嘅融資方式。程序 5 步：證券種類及金額 → 董事會批准 → 向監管機構註冊 → 定價 → 截止。**Competitive sale**（公開投標）vs **negotiated sale**（與單一 underwriter 商議）；市場穩定下 **vanilla bonds 用 competitive 較平**；**equity 幾乎全部用 negotiated**。發行成本：發行 ≥ US$500m 時，common stock 約 **3.63%**、non-convertible corporate bonds 只約 **0.65%**；有明顯 economies of scale。

16. **Private Market**
    - **Private placement**：向投資者出售**未註冊**證券；約一半企業債經私人市場出售；銀行幫手搵買家、安排交易、議價，但**唔包銷**；優點：資金成本可能較低、較易重新商議條款、快、彈性大；缺點：**limited marketability → 投資者要求更高 yield**、轉售有限制。
    - **Private Equity (PE)**：傾向投資**較成熟**公司；持貨 **3–5 年**；基金多為 limited partnership（**general partners** 管理 + **limited partners** 出資）；**leveraged buyout** = 以大量借貸（可達投資額 3–4 倍）收購；PE 公司 **agency costs 較低**（GP 即係擁有人）；監管負擔細。
    - **PIPE (Private Investment in Public Equity)**：**上市公司**向 hedge fund 等私人出售未註冊股份，**幾乎一定折讓**（補償流動性限制）；公司通常承諾 **90 日內註冊**；註冊唔成可能要賠最多 **1.5% per month**。

17. **Bank Loans**：最常見係 **prime-rate loan**（用於 working capital；銀行常要求每年有段時間 **zero-loan balance**，防止當長期錢用）。Term loans 一般 **1–15 年**，典型 **1–5 年**，係商業銀行最常用嘅長期融資形式。

**D. Internal vs External Financing**

18. **Internal financing 來源**：**retained earnings**（最常見）及 **effective working capital management**（亦包括出售資產、追收欠款）。Retained earnings 係股東對總資產嘅 claim，但**唔係對任何特定資產嘅 claim**。
19. **Retained earnings restrictions**（內部融資嘅主要缺點）：**legal**（如購入 treasury shares 要限制同額保留盈利）、**contractual**（長期債務合約限制派息）、**voluntary**（董事會自設限制）。
20. **Internal financing 優點**：資金成本低、資金用途限制少、**唔使抵押**、**唔攤薄擁有權**；缺點：金額較細，受限於企業自身產生嘅資金。
21. **Debt financing 優點**：貸款人對 equity 無 claim、**唔攤薄**、還款可預測、**利息常可扣稅**、較易安排、唔使向貸款人定期匯報。缺點：**必須還本付息**、固定利息推高 break-even point、需要現金流、貸款條款限制經營、債多被視為高風險、可能要抵押。
22. **Equity financing 優點**：**唔使還**、無信貸紀錄公司亦可集資、投資者可帶來 expertise 及網絡。缺點：**分享未來利潤（股息）**、**攤薄擁有權、可能失去控制權**。

**E. Working Capital 管理要式**

23. **Working capital（gross working capital）= current assets**；**Net working capital (NWC) = current assets − current liabilities**，量度流動性，多數公司 NWC 為正。
24. **Cash conversion cycle**：由**實際支付原材料貨款**到**收到銷售現金**嘅時間；越短代表營運資金效率越高。管理目標：拖長找數（唔罰息前提下）、存貨極小化、快收 receivables。
25. 持有現金三大原因：**transactions motive**、**precautionary motive**（應付突發危機/機會）、**compensating balances**（銀行要求嘅最低存款）。
26. **Float（collection time）**三段：delivery/mailing time → processing delay → availability delay。縮短 float = 減少現金需求。
27. **Permanent working capital** = 全年最淡季都必須維持嘅最低營運資金。三大融資策略：**maturity matching**（季節性需求用短期借款、永久營運資金及固定資產用長期融資 —— 最基本嘅減險法）；**long-term funding**（全部用長期債/股）；**short-term funding**（短期債連部分永久營運資金及固定資產都包埋，平但要再融資、利率風險高）。
28. **Inventory turnover = Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory**；**Days in inventory = 365 / Inventory turnover**。製造業三類存貨：raw materials、work in process、finished goods。
29. **EOQ (economic order quantity)**：數學模型決定最佳訂貨量，**平衡 reorder costs 與 carrying costs**，使總存貨成本最低。**JIT（just-in-time）**：由 Toyota 等日本公司首創，大幅削減存貨成本但增加供應商斷供風險。

### 3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

**對比 1：Callable Bond vs Convertible Bond —— 「邊個揸個 option？」**

- **Callable bond**：個贖回權喺**發行人（issuer）**手上 —— 利率下跌時發行人可以 call price 贖回債券，投資者「被提早收回」，所以要**更高回報**作補償；通常有 call protection period。
- **Convertible bond**：個轉換權喺**投資者（bondholder）**手上 —— 股價升穿轉換價（一般要高 15–20%）投資者先會轉股；因為對投資者有利，所以**票面利率較低**。
- 例：利率由 6% 跌到 4%，發行人會想 call 番隻 6% 嘅 callable bond 再發新債（對 issuer 有利）；反之股價大升時，convertible bond 持有人會行使轉換賺股價升幅（對 investor 有利）。考試見到「option to buy back」= issuer（callable）；見到「option to convert into shares」= investor（convertible），方向調轉就係陷阱。

**對比 2：Promised Yield vs Expected Return —— 「報價 ≠ 你實收」**

- **Promised yield**：CP 報價利率，即發行人**唔違約**、如期付款時投資者收到嘅利率，係持有至到期可賺嘅**最高（maximum）**回報。
- **Expected return**：考慮 default risk 後嘅預期回報，**必然 ≤ promised yield**；只有零違約風險時兩者先相等。
- 例：CP 報價 5.424%，係 promised yield；如果發行人有違約可能，expected return 會低過 5.424%。常見錯誤選項係「投資者持有至到期可以賺得**多過** promised yield」—— 呢句一定錯，因為 promised yield 已經係上限。與此相關嘅 **yield spread = CP yield − T-bill yield**，正正就係 default risk 嘅補償。

## Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

* [Flashcard 1]
  - Front (Question in English): How do you calculate the bond equivalent yield (BEY) on a commercial paper issued at a discount? What day-count convention is used?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - BEY = (Face Value − Issue Price) / Issue Price × 360 / Days to maturity
      - Uses a **360-day year** (money market convention)
      - Discount = the interest the investor receives (buys below face value, receives face value at maturity)
      - Example from text: (HK$100m − HK$99.55m)/HK$99.55m × 360/30 = 0.004520 × 12 = **5.424%**
    - [中文白話解釋]: 記住公式係「折扣 ÷ 買入價 × 360 ÷ 到期日數」。分母係**買入價**唔係面值，因為投資者實際付出嘅係買入價；全年化用 360 日（貨幣市場慣例），唔好用 365。呢個 yield 只係 promised yield，即發行人唔違約先收到嘅上限回報。

* [Flashcard 2]
  - Front (Question in English): A bank sets a company's maximum line of credit using a borrowing base of 75% of accounts receivable plus 50% of inventory. How is the credit limit computed, and which current assets are excluded?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Credit limit = 75% × Accounts Receivable + 50% × Inventory
      - Cash and other current assets are **excluded** from the borrowing base
      - Worked example (Illustrative Example 1): 75% × HK$18,536,000 + 50% × HK$74,196,000 = HK$13,902,000 + HK$37,098,000 = **HK$51,000,000**
      - Companies must also meet covenants (e.g. minimum interest coverage ratio) and provide periodic abbreviated financial statements
    - [中文白話解釋]: 呢條 borrowing base 公式只認 receivables（75%）同 inventory（50%）兩樣嘢，cash 唔計落去 —— 考試好鍾意喺題目入面加個 cash 數字引你誤加。Receivables 折讓少啲（75%）因為變現能力高過存貨（50%）。

* [Flashcard 3]
  - Front (Question in English): Distinguish between an operating (demand) line of credit and a revolving (term) line of credit.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Operating/demand line: technically a demand loan, cancellable at any time; for operating purposes; NOT meant to back up CP; standard cost = floating prime lending rate (most borrowers pay "prime plus")
      - Revolving/term line: term of at least 364 days and up to 5 years, renewable (e.g. "evergreen" 5-year line renewed annually); a firm credit commitment — bank must hold capital against it; CAN back up CP or forward FX contracts
      - Setup fee ~0.50%, also charged on the unused balance; 364-day term exists because a 365-day line requires more bank capital
      - Bank may withdraw/adjust the line upon a material adverse change in credit quality
    - [中文白話解釋]: 簡單記：operating line = 短期、隨時可取消、俾日常營運用；revolving line = 有承諾、有年期（364 日至 5 年）、可以用嚟 backup CP。「364 日」呢條界線係考點 —— 剛好短過一年，因為足一年嘅額度要銀行預留更多資本。

* [Flashcard 4]
  - Front (Question in English): What are the three basic rights of common shareholders, and where do common shareholders rank in a liquidation?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Right 1: vote at any shareholders' meeting of the company
      - Right 2: receive any dividend pay-out **declared** by the company (no right to a dividend until declared by the board)
      - Right 3: receive the remaining property of the company on dissolution
      - Liquidation ranking: **last in line** — after creditors and after preference shareholders (residual ownership)
      - Protected by limited liability: downside capped at initial investment, unlimited upside
    - [中文白話解釋]: 口訣「vote、dividend、residual」三權。記住兩個「last」：派息要等債主同優先股收完先輪到普通股；清盤都係排最尾。仲有股息唔係權利，board 宣派咗先算數 —— 呢個細節經常喺 MCQ 陷阱出現。

* [Flashcard 5]
  - Front (Question in English): Explain the cumulative provision and the contingent voting right of preference shares, and name the dividend types of preference stock.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Cumulative provision: no common dividends may be paid until preference dividends are paid in full, both current and **arrears** — prevents common shareholders from using voting power to suspend preference dividends
      - Contingent voting right: voting rights that **kick in when the issuer fails to pay** the specified dividend (most preference stock is otherwise non-voting)
      - Dividend types (Exhibit 2.3): fixed; floating (benchmark e.g. HIBOR); adjustable rate (ARPS, auction/benchmark reset — market value stays near par); participating (fixed + share of income if common dividend exceeds a set amount, may have a cap); cumulative (missed dividends must be paid first)
      - Straight preference stock = perpetual, valued as a perpetuity; retractable shares typically valued as if maturing at the retraction date (typically 5 years)
    - [中文白話解釋]: Cumulative 即「舊賬未清、新息免問」：優先股嘅欠款（arrears）未找晒，普通股一仙股息都唔准派。Contingent voting right 係「唔派息就有票」嘅保險機制。ARPS 因為股息跟住市場利率浮動，所以市價長期貼近面值。

* [Flashcard 6]
  - Front (Question in English): How is the minimum required earnings before interest computed when a line of credit carries a minimum interest coverage ratio covenant? (Use: HK$50m borrowing, 9.75% interest, 1.75× coverage.)
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Step 1: Interest = Principal × rate = HK$50,000,000 × 9.75% = HK$4,875,000
      - Step 2: Required earnings before interest = Interest × coverage ratio = HK$4,875,000 × 1.75 = **HK$8,531,250**
      - Logic: Interest coverage ratio = Earnings before interest / Interest ≥ covenant minimum
    - [中文白話解釋]: Interest coverage ratio = 息前盈利 ÷ 利息開支。條約要求 coverage ≥ 1.75，即盈利至少係利息嘅 1.75 倍。考試做法：先計利息（本金 × 利率），再乘以 coverage ratio。唔好倒轉用除 —— 除會計出「最多可以借幾多」，乘先係「至少要賺幾多」。

* [Flashcard 7]
  - Front (Question in English): What are the two major differences between a warrant and a call option, and why are warrants used as "sweeteners"?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Difference 1: a call option is a transaction **between two external investors** — one party's gain is the other's loss, with **no impact on the company**; a warrant is **issued by the company itself**
      - Difference 2: call options usually have **very short maturities**; warrants are **long-term**
      - Warrant = corporate finance equivalent of a call option (right to buy the underlying at the exercise price before expiration)
      - Sweetener use: attached to debt to make financing attractive (e.g. early-stage mining companies attracting venture capital while keeping interest payments low)
      - "Naked" warrants = issued alone; "detachable" warrants = can be sold separately
    - [中文白話解釋]: 兩大分別記「邊個發行」同「年期」：call option 係場外兩個投資者對賭、同公司無關、短年期；warrant 係公司自己發行、長年期。Sweetener 嘅作用係令債券更吸引 —— 因為加咗 warrant，公司可以畀低啲利息都借到錢。

* [Flashcard 8]
  - Front (Question in English): Compare debt financing with equity financing as forms of external financing, and contrast external with internal financing overall.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Debt advantages: no dilution of ownership; lender has no claim on future profits beyond principal + interest; payments known/predictable (fixed-rate); **interest often tax deductible**; easier to arrange; no regular reporting to lenders
      - Debt disadvantages: must be repaid; fixed interest raises break-even point; requires cash flow; loan restrictions/covenants; more debt = riskier; may require collateral
      - Equity advantages: does not have to be repaid; accessible to firms with poor/no credit history; investors bring expertise and networks
      - Equity disadvantages: shares future profits via dividends; dilutes ownership and can cause loss of control
      - Internal (retained earnings, working capital): lower cost, fewer restrictions, no collateral, no dilution — BUT smaller amounts; restrictions on retained earnings can be **legal, contractual or voluntary**
    - [中文白話解釋]: 一張表記晒：debt = 要還、利息可扣稅、唔攤薄；equity = 唔使還、但攤薄兼分利潤。Internal financing（保留盈利 + 營運資金管理）最平最自由，缺點係「量細」。三種 retained earnings restrictions（legal / contractual / voluntary）係常考分類，記住例子：買 treasury shares 屬 legal；債務合約限制派息屬 contractual；董事會自己設限屬 voluntary。
