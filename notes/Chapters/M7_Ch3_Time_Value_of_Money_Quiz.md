**Question 1 (MCQ)**

An investment contract pays HK$1,000 per year for four years. The discount rate is 8%. Which of the following statements is most correct?

A. If the first payment is made at the end of year 1, the present value is HK$3,577.
B. If the first payment is made today, the present value is HK$3,312.
C. If the first payment is made today, the present value is HK$3,577, which exceeds that of the ordinary annuity because the cash flows are shifted forward and discounted less.
D. The present value is identical whether payments occur at the beginning or the end of each period, because the total undiscounted cash flows are the same.

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

A lender quotes the same annual percentage rate (APR) on all its loans. Under which compounding arrangement will the effective annual rate (EAR) equal the APR?

A. Semi-monthly compounding (24 times a year)
B. Monthly compounding (12 times a year)
C. Quarterly compounding (4 times a year)
D. Annual compounding (once a year)

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

Mrs. Lau is offered two perpetual cash flow streams. "Grow" pays HK$100 in one year, growing at 4% per year forever. "Shrink" pays HK$1,000 in one year, declining at 2% per year forever. Her opportunity cost is 5% per year. Which of the following statements is most correct?

A. Grow is preferred because its cash flows grow rather than shrink.
B. Shrink is preferred because its present value of HK$14,285.71 exceeds Grow's present value of HK$10,000.
C. The present value of Grow cannot be computed because its growth rate exceeds its first payment.
D. Both have the same present value because both are perpetuities discounted at 5%.
