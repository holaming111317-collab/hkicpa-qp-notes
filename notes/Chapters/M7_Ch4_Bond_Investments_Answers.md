## Question 1 — Answer: **B** (Coupon rate > current yield > YTM)

**Model Answer:**

The bond trades at HK$1,100, i.e. above its face value of HK$1,000, so it is a **premium bond** (coupon rate 8% > market rate).

- Coupon rate = annual coupon ÷ face value = HK$80 ÷ HK$1,000 = **8.00%**
- Current yield = annual interest ÷ market price = HK$80 ÷ HK$1,100 = **7.27%**
- YTM must be **lower than 7.27%**, because the investor pays HK$1,100 but will only receive HK$1,000 at maturity — the capital loss at maturity drags the total return below the current yield. (Cf. the chapter's Hopefully Inc example: coupon 8%, price 1,100, CY 7.27%, YTM 6.62%.)

Therefore: **coupon rate > current yield > YTM**.

**【解題思路】**
第一步：判斷債券係溢價定折價。價格 1,100 > 面值 1,000，係 premium bond，即 coupon rate 高於市場利率（亦即高於 YTM）。第二步：CY 以市價做分母（80/1,100 = 7.27%），一定低過以面值做分母嘅 coupon rate（8%）。第三步：YTM 仲要計埋到期時嘅資本虧損（1,100 買入、1,000 收回），所以再低過 CY。錯誤選項分析：A 係折價債嘅排名（方向完全倒轉，係最常見混淆）；C 錯在 CY 永遠唔會高過 premium bond 嘅 coupon rate；D 錯在將 YTM 同 CY 對調——YTM 計及資本虧損，一定係三者最低。記憶口訣：溢價債「coupon > CY > YTM」，折價債反轉。

## Question 2 — Answer: **C** (A 10-year zero coupon bond)

**Model Answer:**

Two bond theorems from the chapter determine price volatility for a given change in interest rates:

1. **Longer maturity → greater volatility**: long-term bonds have more price volatility than short-term bonds of similar risk.
2. **Lower coupon rate → greater volatility**: prices of lower coupon bonds change more than those of higher coupon bonds.

The 10-year zero coupon bond combines the **longest maturity (10 years)** with the **lowest possible coupon (0%)**, so its price is the most sensitive to a 1% rise in interest rates. This mirrors the chapter's Apply and Analyse 2: when the discount rate rose 1%, the Landmade Info zero coupon bond fell 9.1%, more than the identical-maturity 8% coupon bond (−6.5%) and far more than the short-maturity bonds (Lord Inc, 3-year: −2.7%).

**【解題思路】**
比較四隻債券，用「年期越長越波動、票息越低越波動」兩條定理。A 只有 3 年期，最早出局；D 係 8 年期中票息；B 同 C 都係 10 年期，但 C 係 zero coupon（票息 0%，係最低可能票息），所有現金流集中喺到期日，折現影響最大，所以波動最大。教材實證：同樣 10 年期、折現率同升 1%，Landmade zero coupon bond 跌 9.1%，而 8% coupon bond 只跌 6.5%。常見錯誤：揀 B 嘅同學只記得「年期長」而忽略「票息低」呢個第二因素；揀 D 嘅同學可能誤以為 8 年期加 6.5% 票息比 10 年期更波動——年期效應主導，10 年 > 8 年。

## Question 3 — Answer: **B**

**Model Answer:**

**B is correct.** Callable bonds give the **issuer** the option to 'call' or repurchase outstanding bonds at predetermined prices at specified times. A necessary condition for the call is that **market interest rates decline** — the issuer can then redeem the old bonds and issue new ones at a lower rate. Because calls create higher risk for investors, the call price generally **exceeds the par value** to provide an incentive to investors.

Why the other options are wrong:

- **A is wrong**: the call option belongs to the **issuer**, not the bondholder, and it is exercised when rates **fall**, not rise. Selling back to the issuer when rates rise describes a retractable/putable bond.
- **C is wrong**: a retractable (putable) bond gives the **bondholder** (not the issuer) the right to sell the bonds back at predetermined prices before maturity, and the necessary condition is that interest rates have **gone up** (not fallen).
- **D is wrong**: a sinking fund provision requires the **issuer** (not bondholders) to set money aside every year so that there are enough funds to pay off the bonds at maturity.

**【解題思路】**
呢題考「邊個有權 + 咩情況行使」嘅雙重配對。核心口訣：「息跌 issuer call；息升 holder put」。A 同 C 都係將 call 同 put 嘅權利人/息率方向調亂；D 就將 sinking fund 嘅責任人由 issuer 寫成 bondholders。仲要記住 call price 一般高於面值（premium over par），係補償投資者被封頂嘅風險。呢類 concept-discrimination 題係 OTQ 常客，溫書時將 callable / retractable / extendible / sinking fund 整成一個對照表最穩陣。

## Question 4 — Answer: **HK$922.78**

**Model Answer (full working):**

Semi-annual compounding adjustments (m = 2):

- Coupon per period: C/m = (8% × HK$1,000)/2 = **HK$40**
- Number of periods: m × n = 2 × 5 = **10**
- Discount rate per period: i/m = 10%/2 = **5%**

Bond price = PV of coupon annuity + PV of face value:

- PV of coupons = 40 × [1 − (1.05)⁻¹⁰]/0.05
  - (1.05)¹⁰ = 1.628895; (1.05)⁻¹⁰ = 0.613913
  - Annuity factor = (1 − 0.613913)/0.05 = 7.721735
  - PV of coupons = 40 × 7.721735 = **HK$308.87**
- PV of face value = 1,000 × (1.05)⁻¹⁰ = 1,000 × 0.613913 = **HK$613.91**

**PB = 308.87 + 613.91 = HK$922.78**

Check: market rate (10%) > coupon rate (8%) → the bond must sell at a **discount** (below HK$1,000) ✓

**【解題思路】**
第一步：半年付息做三個同步調整——coupon ÷ 2 = 40、利率 ÷ 2 = 5%、年期 × 2 = 10 期。呢個係最常見嘅失分位：唔調整、或者只調整其中一兩項都會錯。第二步：coupon 部分用年金現值公式，面值部分用單一現金流折現。第三步：兩個現值相加 = 922.78。最後用常識驗算：市場利率 10% 高於 coupon rate 8%，債券一定要折價（低於 1,000），答案合理。常見錯誤答案來源：唔除息率（用 10% 折 10 期）會得出約 861；用年度付息（n=5, i=10%, C=80）會得出 924.18——考試時要認清 "paid semi-annually" 呢個字。

## Question 5 — Answer: **HK$624.60**

**Model Answer (full working):**

Zero coupon bond pricing formula:

PB = Fmn / (1 + i/m)^(mn)

where Fmn = HK$1,000; i = 8% per annum; m = 2 (semi-annual compounding); n = 6 years.

- Rate per period: i/m = 8%/2 = **4%**
- Number of periods: m × n = 2 × 6 = **12**

PB = 1,000 / (1.04)¹²

- (1.04)¹² = 1.601032
- PB = 1,000 / 1.601032 = **HK$624.60**

**【解題思路】**
Zero coupon bond 冇 coupon，成條年金部分等於零，淨係折現到期面值。留意題目講明 semi-annual compounding，所以 8% 要除 2 得每期 4%，6 年乘 2 得 12 期——同付息債嘅調整邏輯一樣。(1.04)^12 = 1.601032，1,000 ÷ 1.601032 = 624.60。驗算邏輯：zero coupon bond 必定以 deep discount 發行（遠低於面值），624.60 合理。常見錯誤：唔調整複利頻率直接用 (1.08)^6 = 1.586874，會得出 630.17；或者乘錯期數（例如用 n=6、i=4% 得出 790.31）。記住 zero coupon bond 係波動最大嘅債券，價格對利率好敏感——呢個概念同 MCQ Q2 互相呼應。
