**Question 1 (MCQ)**

A bond has a face value of HK$1,000, a coupon rate of 8% paid annually, and is currently trading at HK$1,100. Which of the following correctly ranks the bond's coupon rate, current yield and yield to maturity?

A. YTM > current yield > coupon rate
B. Coupon rate > current yield > YTM
C. Current yield > coupon rate > YTM
D. Coupon rate > YTM > current yield

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

Which of the following bonds will experience the GREATEST percentage price change if the market rate of interest rises by 1%?

A. A 3-year bond with a 6.1% coupon paid semi-annually
B. A 10-year bond with an 8% coupon paid annually
C. A 10-year zero coupon bond
D. An 8-year bond with a 6.5% coupon paid semi-annually

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

Which of the following statements about bond provisions is correct?

A. A callable bond gives the bondholder the option to sell the bond back to the issuer when interest rates rise.
B. An issuer is most likely to exercise a call provision when market interest rates have declined, and the call price generally exceeds the par value of the bond.
C. A retractable (putable) bond allows the issuer to shorten the maturity of the bond when interest rates fall.
D. A sinking fund provision requires bondholders to set money aside every year to repurchase the bonds.

**Question 4 (Calculation)**

A bond has a face value of HK$1,000, a coupon rate of 8% paid semi-annually, and exactly 5 years to maturity. The market rate of interest on similar bonds is 10% (with semi-annual compounding). Calculate the current price of the bond. (Round your final answer to two decimal places.)

**Question 5 (Calculation)**

A zero coupon bond has a face value of HK$1,000 and matures in exactly 6 years. Interest is compounded semi-annually and the annual market rate of interest is 8%. Calculate the current price of the bond. (Round your final answer to two decimal places.)
