# M7 Equities Valuation — Phase 3 Quiz (100% OTQ, ALL IN ENGLISH)

> 限時 12.5 分鐘・閉卷・請用英文作答（MCQ 寫選項字母；計算題寫數值及單位）

**Q1 (MCQ).** Identify which of the following statements about the constant-growth (Gordon) dividend valuation model is correct.

A. The model uses the most recently paid dividend (D0) directly as the numerator, i.e. P0 = D0 / (R − g).
B. The model produces valid results only when the dividend growth rate (g) is smaller than the required rate of return (R).
C. The model is the most appropriate tool for valuing a firm whose dividends are growing at 35% per year for the next several years.
D. The model assumes that dividends remain constant in dollar terms forever.

**Q2 (MCQ).** Harbour Ltd has just sold a major asset and now holds an unusually large amount of cash. The board wishes to distribute these proceeds to shareholders as a one-time payment, and the amount is much larger than any normal dividend. Identify the type of dividend the company is most likely to use.

A. Regular cash dividend
B. Extra cash dividend
C. Special cash dividend
D. Scrip dividend

**Q3 (MCQ).** Karson Ltd paid a dividend of HK$2.00 per share yesterday. Dividends are expected to grow at a constant rate of 6% per year indefinitely. The required rate of return on the stock is 14%. Calculate the current price of the stock. (Do not round intermediate calculations. Round the final answer to two decimal places.)

A. HK$25.00
B. HK$26.50
C. HK$15.14
D. HK$10.60

**Q4 (Calculation).** Delta Bank has issued perpetual preference shares with no maturity date. The shares pay an annual dividend of HK$6.00 per share. Investors require a rate of return of 12% on shares of this risk class. Calculate the current value of one preference share. (Round to two decimal places and state the currency unit.)

**Q5 (Calculation).** Investors expect Epsilon Ltd to grow at a constant rate of 6% per year indefinitely. The company's next dividend, payable in one year, is expected to be HK$2.20 per share. The stock is currently priced at HK$40.00 per share. Calculate the required rate of return implied by the current share price. (Express your answer as a percentage to one decimal place.)
