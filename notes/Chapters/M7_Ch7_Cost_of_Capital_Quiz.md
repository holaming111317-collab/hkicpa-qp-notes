**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Harbour Textiles Ltd is financed by HK$400 million of debt and HK$600 million of equity at **book value**. The current **market values** are HK$350 million of debt (trading below par) and HK$1,050 million of equity. The pre-tax cost of debt is 6%, the cost of equity is 12%, and the marginal tax rate is 30%. Which statement about the WACC calculation is correct?

A. The weights should be based on book values (debt 40%, equity 60%), because book values reflect the historical capital actually raised by the firm.
B. The weights should be based on market values (debt 25%, equity 75%), because WACC represents the opportunity cost of capital for the firm's investors as of today.
C. The weights should be based on book values, because market values fluctuate too much to provide a stable discount rate.
D. Either book or market value weights may be used; both approaches give the same WACC since the component costs are unchanged.

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

Beacon Holdings Ltd's WACC computation uses the following inputs: bonds issued five years ago with a coupon rate of 7%, a current yield to maturity on those bonds of 5.5%, preference share cost of 9%, cost of common equity of 13%, and a marginal tax rate of 25%. Which of the following correctly states the costs to enter the WACC formula?

A. Debt at 7.0% pre-tax; the coupon rate is used because it is the contractual cost shown in the financial statements.
B. Debt at 5.5% pre-tax adjusted to 4.125% after tax; the current YTM is the relevant opportunity cost and only debt receives the tax adjustment.
C. Debt at 5.5% after applying no tax adjustment; taxes increase rather than decrease the cost of debt.
D. Debt at 5.5% adjusted to 4.125% after tax, and preference shares at 9% adjusted to 6.75% after tax, because all financing costs benefit from the tax shield.

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

Summit Industries Ltd uses its firm-wide WACC of 12% to evaluate all new projects. Management is assessing two proposals: Project Alpha, which has the same systematic risk as the company's existing portfolio of projects and will be financed in the same debt-to-equity proportions as the company as a whole; and Project Beta, which is in a different industry with substantially higher systematic risk. Which of the following statements is correct?

A. The firm's WACC may be used for both projects, because WACC is always the correct discount rate for any project of the firm.
B. The firm's WACC may be used for Project Alpha only; Project Beta requires a risk-adjusted WACC reflecting its own risk and capital structure, e.g. benchmarked against comparable listed companies.
C. The firm's WACC may be used for Project Beta only, because riskier projects always deserve the firm's average discount rate.
D. The firm's WACC may be used for both projects provided both have conventional cash flows, since cash flow conventionality is the key condition.

**Question 4 (Objective calculation)**

Calculate Metro Foods' after-tax weighted average cost of capital (WACC). Give your answer as a percentage rounded to two decimal places.

- Marginal tax rate: 25%

Calculate Pacific Lighting's after-tax WACC. Give your answer as a percentage rounded to two decimal places.
