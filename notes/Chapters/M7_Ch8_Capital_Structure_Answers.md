**Question 1 — Correct Answer: C**

**Model Answer:**
Under M&M Proposition 1, when all six conditions hold (no taxes, no information or transaction costs, no bankruptcy costs, equivalent borrowing costs for companies and investors, symmetrical market information, and capital structure decisions not affecting real investment policies), capital structure is irrelevant to firm value: VFirm = VDebt + VEquity stays constant — the pie is only sliced differently. However, issuing debt introduces financial risk: debt holders have the first claim on cash flows, so the residual cash flows to shareholders become more volatile. By M&M Proposition 2, kcs = kAssets + (VDebt/Vcs) × (kAssets − kDebt), the required return on equity rises as the debt-to-equity ratio rises.

**【解題思路】**
題目講明六個 M&M 條件全部成立（包括無稅），所以第一個反應係：資本結構無關 (irrelevant)，公司價值不變。但注意呢條題考嘅係概念嵌套——「公司價值不變」唔等於「權益成本不變」。財務重組唔改變資產現金流嘅風險 (business risk 不變)，但改變股東承擔嘅風險 (financial risk 上升)，所以 kcs 上升。
- A 錯：稅盾要有稅先存在，題目已指明 no taxes。
- B 錯：即使債平過股，喺 M&M 世界權益成本會相應上升，WACC (= kAssets) 不變，價值不變。
- D 錯：呢個係最常見嘅陷阱——一半啱（價值不變）一半錯（kcs 不變）；債務比例上升 → financial risk 上升 → kcs 必升。

**Question 2 — Correct Answer: D**

**Model Answer:**
Financial risk arises from the capital structure: debt holders have the first claim on the cash flows generated by the company's assets, and fixed interest and principal payments must be made before anything is paid to shareholders. The more debt in the capital structure, the greater the uncertainty of the cash flows claimable by shareholders — this is the financial risk captured in the second term of M&M Proposition 2, (VDebt/Vcs) × (kAssets − kDebt). Options A, B and C are all components of business risk: business risk reflects the systematic variation in (1) unit sales, (2) unit prices, (3) the costs of producing and selling the firm's products, and (4) the degree of operating leverage in the production process — these exist even for an all-equity firm.

**【解題思路】**
呢條係典型嘅概念界線題：四個選項全部都係「風險來源」，考生要分清邊啲歸 business risk、邊啲歸 financial risk。記憶口訣：business risk 有四個來源——unit sales、unit prices、生產銷售成本、operating leverage，全部同「做生意」有關，零負債都存在；financial risk 只有一個來源——資本結構入面嘅債務所要求嘅固定還款。見到選項提 sales/prices/operating leverage 就係 business risk；見到 interest and principal payments to lenders 先係 financial risk。
- A、B、C 錯：三者都係 textbook 列明嘅 business risk 組成部分。
- D 啱：唯一直接講債務固定還款令股東現金流更波動，正正係 financial risk 嘅定義。

**Question 3 — Correct Answer: B**

**Model Answer:**
This is the underinvestment problem. The firm's assets (HK$32.5 million) are worth less than the face value of its debt (HK$50 million), so if the firm is sold, lenders receive everything and shareholders receive nothing. If shareholders inject HK$5 million into the positive NPV project (NPV = 17.5 − 5 = HK$12.5 million), the firm will be able to repay the lenders in full (HK$50 million), but both the HK$5 million invested and the HK$12.5 million of NPV created accrue to the lenders — shareholders lose HK$5 million. Shareholders of a financially distressed firm therefore have an incentive to turn down positive NPV projects. Without leverage, neither asset substitution nor underinvestment would occur.

**【解題思路】**
呢條考 agency costs of debt 兩個兄弟概念嘅界線。判斷關鍵：asset substitution 係「接受咗唔應該接受嘅嘢」（股東將低風險資產換成高風險資產，甚至投資負 NPV 項目）；underinvestment 係「拒絕咗應該接受嘅嘢」（股東拒絕正 NPV 項目，因為利益全歸債權人）。題目個項目係正 NPV（17.5 − 5 = 12.5 > 0）而股東拒絕，所以係 underinvestment。
- A 錯：asset substitution 係股東想投資更高風險資產（對債權人不利嘅「換資產」），呢度股東係拒絕投資，方向相反。
- C 錯：signalling theory 講嘅係發債/減債公告點樣被市場解讀，同呢度股東唔肯注資無關。
- D 錯：pecking order 講融資來源嘅成本排序（internal funds → debt → equity），唔解釋點解正 NPV 項目會被拒絕。

**Question 4 — Correct Answer: 30%**

**Model Answer (full working):**
1. Value of the all-equity firm (perpetuity): VFirm = CF / i = HK$400,000 / 0.20 = HK$2,000,000.
2. Under M&M Proposition 1, the firm value is unchanged by the restructuring, so after borrowing HK$800,000:
   VEquity = 2,000,000 − 800,000 = HK$1,200,000.
3. Annual interest payment = 800,000 × 5% = HK$40,000.
   Annual cash flow available to shareholders = 400,000 − 40,000 = HK$360,000.
4. New cost of equity: kcs = 360,000 / 1,200,000 = 0.30 = **30%**.
5. Cross-check with M&M Proposition 2: kcs = kAssets + (VDebt/Vcs) × (kAssets − kDebt) = 0.20 + (800,000/1,200,000) × (0.20 − 0.05) = 0.20 + (2/3) × 0.15 = 0.20 + 0.10 = **30%**. ✔

**【解題思路】**
步驟一：用永續公式 CF ÷ i 計全權益公司價值 = 400,000 ÷ 0.20 = HK$2,000,000。步驟二：M&M Prop 1 話重組不改變公司價值，發咗 HK$800,000 債之後，股權剩 HK$1,200,000。步驟三：每年利息 = 800,000 × 5% = HK$40,000，股東每年袋 400,000 − 40,000 = HK$360,000。步驟四：新權益成本 = 360,000 ÷ 1,200,000 = **30%**。記住用 M&M Prop 2 覆核：20% + (0.8/1.2) × (20% − 5%) = 30%，兩條路殊途同歸。
常見錯誤：(1) 用舊股權價值 HK$2,000,000 做分母（會得出 18%）；(2) 忘記扣利息直接 400,000 ÷ 1,200,000 = 33.3%；(3) 用 VDebt/VFirm 而唔係 VDebt/Vcs 代入 Prop 2。考試時兩個方法都識計，互相覆核最穩陣。

**Question 5 — Correct Answer: HK$14,900,000**

**Model Answer (full working):**
1. Value of the unlevered firm, using AFTER-TAX perpetual cash flows:
   VUnlevered = CF × (1 − t) / i = 2,000,000 × (1 − 0.30) / 0.10 = 1,400,000 / 0.10 = HK$14,000,000.
2. Present value of the tax shield on the perpetual bond issued at par:
   PV of tax shield = D × t = 3,000,000 × 0.30 = HK$900,000.
3. Value of the levered firm after the debt issuance:
   VLevered = VUnlevered + PV of tax shield = 14,000,000 + 900,000 = HK$14,900,000.

**【解題思路】**
呢條係「M&M 有稅版」嘅標準考法，兩步曲：先計無槓桿價值，再加稅盾。最關鍵嘅陷阱係第一步**一定要將現金流稅後化**——因為公司有稅，股東只能享受稅後現金流，所以 VU = CF × (1 − t) ÷ i = 2,000,000 × 0.7 ÷ 0.10 = HK$14,000,000。第二步：永續、按面值發行嘅債券，稅盾現值有捷徑 D × t = 3,000,000 × 30% = HK$900,000（推導：每年稅盾 = 利息 × t，用債息率折現永續年金，債息率約走）。加埋 = HK$14,900,000。
常見錯誤：(1) 忘記稅後化，VU 計成 2,000,000 ÷ 0.10 = 20,000,000，再加稅盾變 20,900,000；(2) 稅盾用錯公式，例如將利息（3,000,000 × 債息）當一年稅盾而冇折現成永續；(3) 以為無稅 M&M 適用而答公司價值不變。記住：題目有講 tax rate，就唔係 Prop 1 嘅無稅世界，發債**會**提升公司價值。
