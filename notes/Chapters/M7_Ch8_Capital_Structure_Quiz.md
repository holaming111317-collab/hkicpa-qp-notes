# M7 Capital Structure Theories — Phase 3 Quiz (100% OTQ, ALL IN ENGLISH)

**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Harbour Foods Ltd operates in a world where all six Modigliani–Miller Proposition 1 conditions are satisfied (including zero taxes). The company is currently all-equity financed and is considering issuing perpetual bonds to repurchase 30% of its shares. Which of the following statements is correct?

A. The value of the firm will increase because interest payments create a tax shield.
B. The value of the firm will increase because debt is cheaper than equity.
C. The value of the firm will remain unchanged, but the required return on the remaining equity will rise.
D. The value of the firm will remain unchanged, and the required return on the remaining equity will also remain unchanged.

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

Which of the following is a source of FINANCIAL risk, rather than business risk, to the shareholders of a company?

A. Systematic variation in the company's unit sales.
B. Systematic variation in the prices the company charges per unit.
C. The degree of operating leverage in the company's production process.
D. The fixed interest and principal payments the company must make to its lenders before any payment to shareholders.

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

Kwun Tong Manufacturing Ltd is financially distressed, with debt of face value HK$50 million outstanding while its assets are worth only HK$32.5 million. Management identifies a risk-free project costing HK$5 million that will return HK$17.5 million with certainty, but the HK$5 million must be raised by issuing new shares. The existing shareholders refuse to fund the project. Which concept BEST explains the shareholders' behaviour?

A. The asset substitution problem, because shareholders prefer riskier assets.
B. The underinvestment problem, because the value created by the positive NPV project would accrue to the lenders rather than the shareholders.
C. Signalling theory, because issuing new shares sends a negative signal to the market.
D. The pecking order theory, because new equity is the most expensive source of finance.

**Question 4 (Calculation)**

Aberdeen Marine Ltd generates perpetual annual cash flows of HK$400,000. The firm currently has no debt outstanding and its cost of equity capital is 20%. Management plans to borrow HK$800,000 at an annual interest rate of 5% (the debt will be perpetual) and use the proceeds to repurchase HK$800,000 of its equity. Assuming Modigliani–Miller Proposition 1 holds, calculate the cost of equity capital of Aberdeen Marine Ltd after the change in capital structure. (State your answer as a percentage.)

**Question 5 (Calculation)**

Sheung Wan Trading Ltd has pre-tax annual cash flows of HK$2,000,000 that are expected to continue indefinitely. The marginal corporate tax rate is 30% and the discount rate appropriate for its cash flows is 10%. The company issues a HK$3,000,000 perpetual bond (trading at par) and uses the proceeds to pay a one-time special dividend. Calculate the value of Sheung Wan Trading Ltd after the debt issuance. (State your answer in HK$.)
