**Question 1 — Correct answer: B**

**Model answer (English):**
The price-to-book ratio (market value of equity per share ÷ book value of equity per share) is a market-value ratio because it combines market price data with financial statement data, exactly like the P/E ratio and EPS used by stock markets to evaluate a company.

- A is wrong: the total asset turnover ratio (Sales ÷ Total assets) is an asset management efficiency (activity) ratio.
- C is wrong: the times-interest-earned ratio (EBIT ÷ Interest) is a financial leverage / long-term solvency ratio, of primary interest to creditors.
- D is wrong: the gross profit margin is a profitability ratio.

【解題思路】呢題考比率家族分類 (concept-discrimination)，考官最常見嘅陷阱就係將唔同家族嘅比率放埋一齊。記憶口訣：market-value ratio 一定要有「市場價格」成份（share price / market value），所以只有 P/B、P/E、EPS、dividend yield 呢類先入呢個家族。turnover 尾字 = efficiency；margin / return 字頭 = profitability；interest / debt / equity multiplier = leverage。

**Question 2 — Correct answer: B**

**Model answer (English):**
DuPont equation:
ROE = Net profit margin × Total asset turnover × Equity multiplier
ROE = 6% × 1.25 × 1.6 = 0.12 = 12.0%

【解題思路】杜邦公式三個組件直接相乘：6% × 1.25 = 7.5%（呢個係 ROA，所以 C 係陷阱，停喺兩步就會中伏）；再乘 1.6 = 12.0%。A (9.6%) 係 6% × 1.6，漏咗 total asset turnover；D (15.0%) 係錯誤加法或者亂砌。考試貼士：見到三個數乘埋就係 ROE，中間步驟 ROA = margin × turnover 仲可以順手檢查。

**Question 3 — Correct answer: B**

**Model answer (English):**
EBIT return on assets (EROA) = EBIT ÷ Total assets. EBIT is a measure of earnings available to both debtholders and shareholders (before interest and tax), and total assets equal debt plus equity — the total investment made by both groups. EROA is therefore more effective than ROA at measuring how efficiently assets are used in operations, because ROA divides net income (income to shareholders only) by total assets (investment by both groups) and is obscured by financing decisions and taxes.

- A is wrong: numerator/denominator mismatch — net income belongs to shareholders, total assets belong to both.
- C is wrong: ROE measures the return on shareholders' investment only, not total asset efficiency.
- D is wrong: the net profit margin has sales, not investment, in the denominator.

【解題思路】本章嘅 golden rule：分子係股東收入，分母就只計股東投資；分子係全體（股東+債權人）嘅經營盈利，分母就要計全體投資。EBIT 係未扣利息稅項嘅盈利，自然對應 total assets（= debt + equity）。呢個概念同時解釋點解 ROA 會被槓桿「污染」而 EROA 唔會。

**Question 4 — Correct answers: (i) Current ratio = 1.60 times; (ii) Quick ratio = 0.80 times**

**Model answer (English):**
(i) Current assets = Cash + Marketable securities + Accounts receivable + Inventory
= 60,000 + 20,000 + 120,000 + 200,000 = HK$400,000
Current ratio = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities = 400,000 ÷ 250,000 = **1.60 times**

(ii) Quick ratio = (Cash + Marketable securities + Accounts receivable) ÷ Current liabilities
= (60,000 + 20,000 + 120,000) ÷ 250,000 = 200,000 ÷ 250,000 = **0.80 times**

Interpretation: the current ratio above 1 looks healthy, but the quick ratio below 1 warns that the company may not be able to pay off all current liabilities from its most liquid ('quick') assets, because inventory — the least liquid current asset — accounts for half of its current assets.

【解題思路】步驟一：先夾晒四項流動資產計 current ratio。步驟二：quick ratio 記住分子口訣「C + MS + AR」，一定要剔除存貨 — 呢度係最常見失分位，好多考生將存貨照計落去就會變返 1.60。步驟三：兩個比率差距大（1.6 vs 0.8）代表流動資產過度依賴存貨，答題時可以補一句 interpretation 攞滿分。

**Question 5 — Correct answer: CCC = 45 days**

**Model answer (English):**
Step 1 — Inventory turnover = COGS ÷ Average inventory = 3,650,000 ÷ 500,000 = 7.3 times
Days' inventory = 365 ÷ 7.3 = 50 days

Step 2 — Receivables turnover = Sales ÷ Average accounts receivable = 5,840,000 ÷ 400,000 = 14.6 times
Days' receivables = 365 ÷ 14.6 = 25 days

Step 3 — Payables turnover = Net credit purchases ÷ Average accounts payable = 3,650,000 ÷ 300,000 = 12.167 times
Days' payables = 365 ÷ 12.167 = 30 days

Step 4 — Cash conversion cycle = Days' inventory + Days' receivables − Days' payables
= 50 + 25 − 30 = **45 days**

【解題思路】記住每個 days 比率都係 365 ÷ turnover；亦可以直接用捷徑：Days' inventory = 365 × Average inventory ÷ COGS = 365 × 500,000 ÷ 3,650,000 = 50 days，如此類推。最大陷阱係公式最後嗰吓：CCC 係「加存貨、加應收、減應付」，唔係三樣加埋（咁會錯得 105 days）；亦唔好倒轉應收應付（咁會得負數）。Operating cycle = 50 + 25 = 75 days，如果題目問 operating cycle 就唔好扣應付 — 兩條公式只差一項，考試必分清。
