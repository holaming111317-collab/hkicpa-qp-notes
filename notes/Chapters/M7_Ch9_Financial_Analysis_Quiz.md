**Question 1 (MCQ)**
Which of the following ratios is classified as a market-value ratio?

A. Total asset turnover ratio
B. Price-to-book ratio
C. Times-interest-earned ratio
D. Gross profit margin

**Question 2 (MCQ)**
Harbour Gate Ltd reports a net profit margin of 6%, a total asset turnover of 1.25 times and an equity multiplier of 1.6. Using the DuPont equation, what is the company's return on equity (ROE)?

A. 9.6%
B. 12.0%
C. 7.5%
D. 15.0%

**Question 3 (MCQ)**
Which of the following ratios divides a measure of earnings available to BOTH debtholders and shareholders by the total investment made by BOTH groups, and is therefore considered a better measure than ROA of how efficiently assets are used in operations?

A. Return on assets (Net income ÷ Total assets)
B. EBIT return on assets (EBIT ÷ Total assets)
C. Return on equity (Net income ÷ Total equity)
D. Net profit margin (Net income ÷ Net sales)

**Question 4 (Calculation)**
Kowloon Trading Ltd's statement of financial position shows: cash HK$60,000; marketable securities HK$20,000; accounts receivable HK$120,000; inventory HK$200,000; current liabilities HK$250,000.

Calculate (i) the current ratio and (ii) the quick ratio. State both answers in "times" (to 2 decimal places).

**Question 5 (Calculation)**
Lantau Retail Ltd reports the following for the year: net sales HK$5,840,000; cost of goods sold HK$3,650,000; net credit purchases HK$3,650,000; average inventory HK$500,000; average accounts receivable HK$400,000; average accounts payable HK$300,000. (Use a 365-day year.)

Calculate the company's cash conversion cycle (CCC) in days.
