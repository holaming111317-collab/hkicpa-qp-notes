# M7 Financial Analysis and Profitability — Study Pack (Week 9)

## Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

### 1. 核心知識架構

- 本章核心係用**五大比率家族 (five ratio families)** 去評估企業財務狀況：短期償債/流動性 (short-term solvency/liquidity)、財務槓桿/長期償債 (financial leverage/long-term solvency)、資產管理效率 (asset management efficiency / activity / turnover)、盈利能力 (profitability)、市場價值 (market-based / market-value ratios)；再配合**杜邦系統 (DuPont system)** 將 ROE 拆解作診斷。
- 股市指標 (stock exchange indicators)：Price-to-Book ratio (P/B)、Earnings per Share (EPS)、Price–Earnings ratio (P/E)，將市場價格數據與財務報表數據結合。
- 短期財務規劃 (short-term financial planning) 靠營運週期 (operating cycle) 及現金轉換週期 (cash conversion cycle, CCC) 估算營運資金需要；比率判斷好壞要用 trend analysis、industry group analysis、peer group analysis 三種基準比較。
- 現金為王：利潤唔等於現金 (profits are not cash)，營運現金流 (cash flow from operations) 先係真正衡量企業產生現金能力的指標；自由現金流 (free cash flow, FCF) 反映扣除資本開支同股息後剩餘可用現金。
- 盈利預測與財務策略：percentage of sales method 預測外部融資需求 (external financing requirement, EFR)；內部增長率 (internal growth rate, IGR) 與可持續增長率 (sustainable growth rate, SGR) 界定唔借錢/唔改資本結構下嘅最高增長；過度交易 (overtrading) 與過度槓桿 (excessive leverage) 係兩大警號，槓桿程度用 DOL、DFL、DCL 量度。

### 2. 必背考點清單（核心公式與應用條件）

**Stock Exchange Indicators / Market-Based Ratios**
- Price-to-book ratio (P/B) = Market value of equity per share ÷ Book value of equity per share。P/B < 1 代表公司未能為股東創造合理價值。
- Earnings per share (EPS) = Net income ÷ Shares outstanding。
- Price–earnings ratio (P/E) = Price per share ÷ EPS。P/E = 25.4 即市場願意為每 HK$1 盈利付 HK$25.40；高 P/E 可能代表預期高增長，亦可能係當年盈利異常低；EPS 為負數時 P/E 無意義。
- Forward P/E = Price per share ÷ Estimated EPS (EEPS)。
- Book value per share (BVPS) = Shareholders' equity ÷ Number of shares。
- Dividend yield = Dividend per share (DPS) ÷ Current share price (P)。
- Dividend payout ratio = DPS ÷ EPS。
- EBITDA multiple = Total enterprise value (TEV) ÷ EBITDA；TEV = equity market value + market value of debt，用於收購估值。

**Short-term Solvency / Liquidity Ratios**
- Current ratio = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities。低於 1 表示流動資產可能唔夠償還流動負債。
- Quick ratio (acid test) = (Cash + Marketable securities + Accounts receivable) ÷ Current liabilities；剔除存貨因為存貨係流動資產中流動性最低。

**Financial Leverage / Long-term Solvency Ratios**
- Total debt ratio = Total liabilities ÷ Total assets。
- Debt-to-equity ratio = Total debt ÷ Total equity。
- Equity multiplier = Total assets ÷ Total equity = 1 + Debt-to-equity ratio。（三個槓桿比率經 statement of financial position identity 互相連繫：TA = TL + SE，知一個可推其餘兩個。）
- Times-interest-earned (TIE) = EBIT ÷ Interest。
- Cash coverage ratio = (EBIT + Depreciation and/or Amortisation) ÷ Interest。

**Asset Management Efficiency Ratios**
- Receivables turnover = Sales ÷ Accounts receivable (average/ending)；Days' receivables = 365 ÷ Receivables turnover。
- Payables turnover = Net credit purchases ÷ Accounts payable（或 COGS ÷ AP）；Days' payables = 365 ÷ Payables turnover。
- Inventory turnover = COGS ÷ Inventory；Days' inventory = 365 ÷ Inventory turnover。
- Fixed asset turnover = Sales ÷ Net fixed assets；Total asset turnover = Sales ÷ Total assets。

**Profitability Ratios**
- Gross profit margin = (Net sales − COGS) ÷ Net sales。
- Operating profit margin = EBIT ÷ Net sales（用 EBITDA 則為 EBITDA margin）。
- Net profit margin = Net income ÷ Net sales；可再拆成 (EBIT/Net sales) × (EBT/EBIT) × (Net income/EBT)。
- EBIT return on assets (EROA) = EBIT ÷ Total assets。
- Return on assets (ROA) = Net income ÷ Total assets。
- Return on equity (ROE) = Net income ÷ Total equity。
- Average value = (Beginning value + Ending value) ÷ 2；ROA/ROE 宜用平均資產/權益。

**DuPont System**
- ROA = Net profit margin × Total asset turnover。
- ROE = ROA × Equity multiplier。
- DuPont equation: ROE = Net profit margin × Total asset turnover × Equity multiplier = (Net income/Net sales) × (Net sales/Total assets) × (Total assets/Total equity)。三大改善途徑：profit on sales、asset efficiency、financial leverage。

**Short-term Financial Planning**
- Operating cycle = Days' inventory + Days' receivables。
- Cash conversion cycle (CCC) = Days' inventory + Days' receivables − Days' payables。CCC 為負代表公司有剩餘現金可投資；越短越好。

**Growth & Other Key Formulas**
- Free cash flow = Net cash provided by operating activities − Capital expenditures − Dividends。
- Profitability index (PI) = PV of future cash flows ÷ Initial cost；PI > 1 接受（等同正 NPV）。
- Internal growth rate (IGR)：開盤資產基礎 IGR = ROA × b；收盤資產基礎 IGR = (ROA × b) ÷ (1 − ROA × b)，其中 b = retention rate = 1 − dividend payout。
- Sustainable growth rate (SGR)：開盤權益 SGR = ROE × b；收盤權益 SGR = (ROE × b) ÷ (1 − ROE × b)。
- DOL = % change in EBIT ÷ % change in sales；DFL = % change in EPS ÷ % change in EBIT；DCL = DOL × DFL = % change in EPS ÷ % change in sales。

**考試貼士**：題目若話「closing balance」，IGR/SGR 要用除返 (1 − ROA×b) 或 (1 − ROE×b) 嘅版本；「opening balance」則直接相乘。兩個版本計出嚟理論上完全一樣，千祈唔好溝亂。

### 3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

**Pitfall 1：ROA vs EROA vs ROE — 分子分母要配對**
- ROA = Net income ÷ Total assets：分子係股東應佔盈利，分母卻係股東+債權人嘅總投資，所以會被融資決策同稅項扭曲 (obscured by financing decisions and taxes)。
- EROA = EBIT ÷ Total assets：分子係股東+債權人共享嘅經營盈利，分母係雙方總投資，配對正確，更能衡量資產營運效率。
- ROE = Net income ÷ Total equity：分子分母都係股東層面。
- 原則：if the numerator is income to shareholders, the denominator should be shareholders' investment only；if the numerator is total profits from operations, the denominator should be total investment by both debtholders and shareholders。
- 例子：一間公司 Net income HK$60 萬、EBIT HK$100 萬、Total assets HK$1,000 萬、Equity HK$500 萬 → ROA = 6%，EROA = 10%，ROE = 12%。高槓桿會令 ROE > EROA，睇數時要諗清楚邊個先係營運效率、邊個係股東回報。

**Pitfall 2：Current Ratio vs Quick Ratio — 存貨係關鍵分別**
- Current ratio 用晒所有流動資產；Quick ratio 只計 Cash + Marketable securities + Accounts receivable，剔除存貨。
- 原因：inventory has the least liquidity of the current assets — 瀕臨倒閉公司嘅存貨可能唔值賬面值，債權人難以收回資金，所以銀行會再睇 quick ratio。
- 例子：CA = HK$800（其中存貨 HK$400），CL = HK$500 → Current ratio = 1.6（睇落健康），Quick ratio = (800 − 400) ÷ 500 = 0.8（其實隨時冇現金還債）。兩個比率差距越大，代表流動資產越依賴存貨。
- 「考官報告指出」：考生經常喺 OTQ 中將比率歸錯家族（e.g. 將 quick ratio 當成 market-value ratio、將 inventory turnover 當成 profitability ratio），或者喺 quick ratio 計算時漏咗剔除存貨 — 答題前一定要默返 quick ratio 嘅分子係「C + MS + AR」。

## Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

* [Flashcard 1]
  - Front (Question in English): How are EPS and the P/E ratio calculated, and what does a P/E of 14 signify?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - EPS = Net income ÷ Shares outstanding.
      - P/E ratio = Price per share ÷ EPS.
      - A P/E of 14 means the market places a value of HK$14 on every HK$1 of net income; investors pay HK$14 for a claim on HK$1 of earnings because prices reflect current earnings plus future cash flows.
      - High P/E may signal expected rapid earnings growth — or just unusually low current earnings; P/E is meaningless when EPS is negative or very low.
    - [中文白話解釋]: EPS 就係將純利攤分落每股；P/E 就係股價除以 EPS，話你知投資者願意為每 1 蚊盈利俾幾多錢。P/E 高可能係市場睇好增長，亦可能係今年盈利特別低，所以一定要同競爭對手同市場趨勢比較先有結論。

* [Flashcard 2]
  - Front (Question in English): A firm has cash HK$100, marketable securities HK$50, accounts receivable HK$250, inventory HK$400 and current liabilities HK$500. Calculate the current ratio and quick ratio, and explain the difference.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Current assets = 100 + 50 + 250 + 400 = HK$800.
      - Current ratio = 800 ÷ 500 = 1.6.
      - Quick ratio = (100 + 50 + 250) ÷ 500 = 0.8.
      - The quick (acid test) ratio excludes inventory because inventory is the least liquid current asset; a quick ratio below 1 warns the firm may be unable to pay current liabilities from its most liquid assets.
    - [中文白話解釋]: 流動比率 1.6 表面健康，但速動比率只有 0.8，因為一半流動資產係存貨，未必套到現。銀行睇兩個比率嘅差距就知公司有幾依賴賣存貨先還到短期債。

* [Flashcard 3]
  - Front (Question in English): State the DuPont equation and the three levers management can use to maximise ROE.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - ROE = Net profit margin × Total asset turnover × Equity multiplier.
      - = (Net income/Net sales) × (Net sales/Total assets) × (Total assets/Total equity).
      - Three levers: (1) profit on sales (margin), (2) efficiency of asset use (turnover), (3) financial leverage (equity multiplier).
      - ROA = Net profit margin × Total asset turnover; ROE = ROA × Equity multiplier.
      - Increasing leverage raises ROE but increases risk.
    - [中文白話解釋]: 杜邦公式將 ROE 拆做三瓣：賺錢能力（邊際利潤）、資產用得叻唔叻（週轉率）、用幾多借貸（權益乘數）。想谷大 ROE 可以三管齊下，但靠借貸谷 ROE 係有代價嘅 — 風險會同步上升。

* [Flashcard 4]
  - Front (Question in English): Given a total debt ratio of 0.65, calculate the equity multiplier and the debt-to-equity ratio.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Total debt = 0.65 × Total assets; Total equity = 0.35 × Total assets (TA = TL + SE).
      - Debt-to-equity = 0.65 ÷ 0.35 = 1.86.
      - Equity multiplier = Total assets ÷ Total equity = 1 ÷ 0.35 = 2.86.
      - Check: Equity multiplier = 1 + Debt-to-equity = 1 + 1.86 = 2.86.
    - [中文白話解釋]: 三個槓桿比率係一個三角形關係，靠 TA = TL + SE 互相轉換。記住 Equity multiplier = 1 + D/E，考試見到任何一個數都可以推返其餘兩個。

* [Flashcard 5]
  - Front (Question in English): How are the operating cycle and the cash conversion cycle (CCC) computed, and what does a negative CCC indicate?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Operating cycle = Days' inventory + Days' receivables.
      - CCC = Days' inventory + Days' receivables − Days' payables.
      - Days' ratios = 365 ÷ respective turnover (inventory, receivables, payables).
      - A negative CCC (e.g. −10) means the company has excess cash to invest for 10 days; a lower CCC implies higher liquidity.
      - CCC lengthens when: inventory builds in peak seasons; lenient credit policy slows collections; it shortens when suppliers extend payment terms.
    - [中文白話解釋]: 營運週期係由入貨到收錢嘅總日數；現金轉換週期再扣返你拖供應商嘅日數，先係你真正要墊資嘅時間。CCC 越短越好，負數即係人哋嘅錢喺你手度滾住，仲有閒錢可以投資。

* [Flashcard 6]
  - Front (Question in English): What is free cash flow (FCF) and why is it more informative than net cash from operating activities?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - FCF = Net cash provided by operating activities − Capital expenditures − Dividends.
      - Operating cash flow ignores the investment in new fixed assets needed to maintain operations and the dividends needed to keep investors happy.
      - FCF shows whether the company has enough cash, after funding operations and capex, to pay investors via dividends and share buy-backs.
    - [中文白話解釋]: 經營現金流睇落好靚都未算數，因為公司要俾錢維修機器、添置資產先維持到生意，仲要派息氹股東。扣晒呢啲必要開支剩低嘅先係自由現金流，先係真正可自由運用嘅錢。

* [Flashcard 7]
  - Front (Question in English): Distinguish the internal growth rate (IGR) from the sustainable growth rate (SGR), including both formula versions.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - IGR = maximum growth rate achievable without external funding.
        - Opening asset balance: IGR = ROA × b; Closing asset balance: IGR = (ROA × b) ÷ (1 − ROA × b).
      - SGR = maximum growth rate sustainable without changing the capital structure.
        - Opening equity balance: SGR = ROE × b; Closing equity balance: SGR = (ROE × b) ÷ (1 − ROE × b).
      - b = plowback/retention ratio = Addition to retained earnings ÷ Net income = 1 − Dividend payout ratio.
    - [中文白話解釋]: IGR 話你知唔借錢、唔發新股，單靠留返嘅盈利可以增長幾快；SGR 就准你維持現有槓桿借錢，所以一定高過 IGR。記住 b 係留存比率，唔係派息比率；用期末結餘計就要除返 (1 − ROA×b)。

* [Flashcard 8]
  - Front (Question in English): Define DOL, DFL and DCL and state how they are related.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - DOL = % change in EBIT ÷ % change in sales; high DOL = high fixed costs relative to variable costs → EBIT volatile vs sales.
      - DFL = % change in EPS ÷ % change in EBIT; DFL of 1.25 means EPS moves 1.25% per 1% change in EBIT.
      - DCL = % change in EPS ÷ % change in sales = DOL × DFL.
      - Total earnings risk = business risk (operating leverage) + financial risk (financial leverage).
    - [中文白話解釋]: 經營槓桿量度銷售變化點樣放大落 EBIT；財務槓桿量度 EBIT 變化點樣放大落 EPS；兩者乘埋就係綜合槓桿，由銷售直達 EPS。固定成本越高、借貸越多，兩層放大器就越勁，好景時勁賺、逆境時勁蝕。
