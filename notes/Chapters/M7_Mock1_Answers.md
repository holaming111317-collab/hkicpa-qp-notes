## Question 1 — Correct Answer: **9.17%**

**Model Answer (English):**
- Cost of equity (dividend growth model): kₑ = D₁/P₀ + g = 0.50/8.00 + 5% = 6.25% + 5% = **11.25%**
- Market values: E = 1,000,000 × 8.00 = 8,000,000; D = 4,000,000; V = 12,000,000
- WACC = (8/12)(11.25%) + (4/12)(6%)(1 − 0.165) = 7.50% + (1/3)(5.01%) = 7.50% + 1.67% = **9.17%**

【解題思路】
- 三寶：權重用 **market value**（股數 × 股價，唔係面值）、kₑ 用 DGM（D₁/P₀ + g）、**只有債**有稅盾 ×(1 − t)。
- 呢條係你嘅頭號惡魔（第五次交手先斬）——Mock 入面再錯嘅話，每日默寫 WACC 直至考試日。

## Question 7 — Correct Answer: **HK$949.24**

**Model Answer (English):**
"Semi-annual periods: n = 6, r = 5% per period, coupon = 1,000 × 8% / 2 = HK$40.
Price = 40 × annuity factor(5%, 6) + 1,000 × PV factor(5%, 6) = 40 × 5.0757 + 1,000 × 0.7462 = 203.03 + 746.22 = **HK$949.24**"

【解題思路】
- 半年複利：息率減半（10%→5%）、期數加倍（3→6）、coupon 減半（80→40）。
- **常識檢查**：coupon 8% < YTM 10% → 一定折價（低過 1,000）。計出嚟高過面值 = 即刻知錯。

## Question 8 — Correct Answer: **(i) HK$120 (ii) 20%**

## Question 11 — Correct Answer: **12.5%**

**Model Answer (English):**

【解題思路】
- 括號入面係 k₀ − kd（5%），再乘 D/E（0.5）。唔好掉轉做 kd − k₀。

## Question 13 — Correct Answer: **HK$300,000**

**Model Answer (English):**
"EFN = ΔA − ΔAP − retained earnings
ΔA = 60% × 2,000,000 = 1,200,000
ΔAP = 15% × 2,000,000 = 300,000
Net income = 10% × **total** sales of 12,000,000 = 1,200,000; retained = 1,200,000 × 50% = 600,000
EFN = 1,200,000 − 300,000 − 600,000 = **HK$300,000**"

【解題思路】
- 三大陷阱（你中晒過）：NI 用 **total forecast sales**（12M）唔係 ΔS；retained 係**減**唔係加；ΔAP 唔好漏。

## Question 19 — Correct Answer: **3.26 years**

**Model Answer (English):**
"Discount each year's flow at 10% and accumulate:
Y1: 150,000/1.1 = 136,364 → cum 136,364
Y2: 150,000/1.21 = 123,967 → cum 260,331
Y3: 150,000/1.331 = 112,697 → cum 373,028
Y4: 150,000/1.4641 = 102,452 → cum 475,480
Shortfall after Y3 = 400,000 − 373,028 = 26,972; fraction = 26,972/102,452 = 0.26 → **3.26 years**"

【解題思路】
- 逐年折現累積 + 插值，**唔可以一條式直出**（舊債：500,000×1.1÷150,000 嗰種亂砌）。表格式計，快而準。
