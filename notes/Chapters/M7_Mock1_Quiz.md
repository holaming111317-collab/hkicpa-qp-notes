**Question 1 (Calculation)** — Cost of capital
ABC Ltd has 1,000,000 ordinary shares currently trading at HK$8.00 each, and debt with a market value of HK$4,000,000. The company will pay a dividend of HK$0.50 per share next year; dividends are expected to grow at 5% per year indefinitely. The pre-tax cost of debt is 6% and the profits tax rate is 16.5%. Calculate the company's **WACC**.

**Question 2 (MCQ)** — Financial environment
Which of the following is NOT a money market instrument?
A. Treasury bills
B. Commercial paper
C. Ordinary shares
D. Certificates of deposit

**Question 3 (MCQ)** — Performance measurement
Division X currently earns an ROI of 25%. The company's required rate of return is 15%. A new project is available offering a return of 20%. If the divisional manager is evaluated solely on ROI, the manager will most likely:
A. Accept the project — 20% exceeds the company's required return of 15%
B. Reject the project — 20% is below the division's current ROI of 25%
C. Accept the project — residual income will increase
D. Be indifferent — the decision depends on cash flows, not ROI

**Question 4 (Calculation)** — Time value of money
A bank offers a nominal annual interest rate of 12% compounded monthly. Calculate the **effective annual rate (EAR)**, as a percentage to 2 decimal places.

**Question 5 (Calculation)** — Financial analysis
Company P has a net profit margin of 8%, a total asset turnover of 1.5 times, and an equity multiplier of 2.0. Using the **DuPont analysis**, calculate the return on equity (ROE).

**Question 6 (MCQ)** — Capital structure
According to Modigliani-Miller Proposition I (assuming no taxes), when a company increases its gearing by issuing debt to repurchase shares:
A. The value of the firm increases because debt is cheaper than equity
B. The value of the firm decreases because financial risk rises
C. The value of the firm is unchanged, and the cost of equity rises
D. The value of the firm is unchanged, and the WACC rises

**Question 7 (Calculation)** — Bond valuation
A bond has a face value of HK$1,000, an annual coupon rate of 8% paid **semi-annually**, exactly 3 years to maturity, and a yield to maturity of 10% per annum (semi-annual compounding). Calculate the **bond price**.

**Question 8 (Calculation)** — Pricing
A product has a variable production cost of HK$60 per unit. Allocated fixed manufacturing overhead is HK$30 per unit, and allocated fixed selling and administrative overhead is HK$10 per unit. The company requires a profit of HK$20 per unit. Using **total cost-plus pricing**, calculate (i) the selling price per unit and (ii) the mark-up percentage on total cost.

**Question 9 (Calculation)** — CAPM
The risk-free rate is 3%, the expected market return is 9%, and the beta of stock Z is 1.2. Using CAPM, calculate the **required return** on stock Z.

**Question 10 (Calculation)** — Investment appraisal
A project requires an initial outlay of HK$200,000 plus a working capital investment of HK$30,000 at t0. Cash inflows are HK$100,000 at the end of each of Years 1 to 3. The working capital is fully recovered at the end of Year 3. The cost of capital is 10%. Calculate the **NPV** and state whether the project should be accepted.

**Question 11 (Calculation)** — Capital structure
An all-equity firm has a cost of capital of 10%. It issues debt costing 5%, resulting in a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.5. Assuming no taxes, use **MM Proposition II** to calculate the cost of equity.

**Question 12 (Calculation)** — Financial risks
A bank lends HK$360,000 secured on equipment with a total value of HK$600,000. Calculate the **loan-to-value (LTV) ratio**, expressed as a percentage to one decimal place.

**Question 13 (Calculation)** — Financial forecasts
Sales are forecast to increase by HK$2,000,000, reaching HK$12,000,000 in total. Total assets vary directly with sales at 60% of sales; accounts payable vary spontaneously at 15% of sales. The net profit margin is 10% and the dividend payout ratio is 50%. Calculate the **external financing needed (EFN)**.

**Question 14 (MCQ)** — Strategic management accounting
In the balanced scorecard, "average production cycle time" belongs to which perspective?
A. Financial
B. Customer
C. Internal process
D. Learning and growth

**Question 15 (Calculation)** — MM with taxes
An unlevered firm is valued at HK$10,000,000. It issues permanent debt of HK$4,000,000. The corporate tax rate is 16.5%. Under **MM with corporate taxes**, calculate the value of the levered firm.

**Question 16 (MCQ)** — Relevant costing
Which of the following is a RELEVANT cost when deciding whether to accept a one-off special order?
A. Absorbed fixed manufacturing overhead
B. Development costs already incurred for the product
C. The contribution forgone from regular sales displaced by the order
D. Allocated head office administrative costs

**Question 17 (Calculation)** — Working capital
Extracts from the accounts: inventory HK$219,000; trade receivables HK$150,000; trade payables HK$120,000; annual credit sales HK$1,825,000; annual cost of sales HK$1,095,000. Calculate the **cash conversion cycle** (in days).

**Question 18 (Calculation)** — Equity valuation
A share will pay a dividend of HK$2.00 next year. Dividends are expected to grow at 4% per year indefinitely, and the required rate of return is 12%. Using the dividend growth model, calculate the **share price**.

**Question 19 (Calculation)** — Investment appraisal
A project costs HK$400,000 and generates cash inflows of HK$150,000 per year for 4 years. The cost of capital is 10%. Calculate the **discounted payback period** (in years, to 2 decimal places).

**Question 20 (MCQ)** — Post-investment appraisal
A project is performing badly after HK$5,000,000 has already been spent on it. Management is deciding whether to abandon it. Which statement is CORRECT?
A. The HK$5,000,000 spent is relevant — abandoning the project wastes that investment
B. Only future incremental cash flows are relevant — the HK$5,000,000 is a sunk cost and must be ignored
C. The project should never be abandoned once more than half the budget has been spent
D. Sunk costs are relevant whenever they exceed HK$1,000,000
