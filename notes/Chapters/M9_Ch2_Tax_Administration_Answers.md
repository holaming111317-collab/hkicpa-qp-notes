## Question 1 — Answer: B

**Model Answer:**
The assessment raised is an **estimated assessment under s.59(3) IRO**. An estimated assessment may be raised where no return is filed, where an unacceptable return is filed, or where books are not kept in satisfactory form. Here, Mr Kwan failed to file his 2022/23 profits tax return and his books were unsatisfactory, so the assessor was entitled to assess him based on an estimate of his profits. To object to such an estimated assessment raised in the absence of a return, Mr Kwan must file a valid completed return together with the notice of objection within the one-month objection period; otherwise the objection is invalid.

**【解題思路】**
- 關鍵事實係「無交報稅表」+「帳簿唔妥」→ 呢個係 s.59(3) 估計評稅嘅典型觸發條件。呢類評稅係**首次**評稅，唔係追加。
- A 錯：standard assessment (s.59(1)) 只喺納稅人交咗可接受嘅報稅表時先發出；Mr Kwan 根本無交表。
- C 錯：additional assessment (s.60(1)) 係喺已經有評稅、但發現納稅人 not adequately taxed 時追加嘅；呢度之前並無任何評稅。呢個係考官最常考嘅分類邊界 —— estimated（無表→估）vs additional（評漏咗→加）。
- D 錯：s.82A 係「罰則」性質嘅補加稅 (additional tax)，唔係一般評稅，而且題目無講 IRD 要罰佢，只係評稅；failure to file 唔會「自動」變 s.82A 罰款。
- 記憶口訣：無表/表唔得/簿唔妥 → s.59(3) estimated；評少咗 → s.60(1) additional（6 年，fraud/wilful evasion 10 年）。

## Question 2 — Answer: C

**Model Answer:**
Statement **C** is correct. Under s.70A IRO, a taxpayer may apply to correct an assessment that is excessive by reason of an error or omission in a return (or an arithmetical error/omission in the tax computation) within **six years after the end of the year of assessment concerned, or six months after the relevant notice of assessment is served, whichever is the later**.

**【解題思路】**
- C 啱：s.70A 嘅時限就係「課稅年度結束後 6 年」同「評稅通知書送達後 6 個月」取較後者。記住呢條係「更正無心之失」嘅條文，即使評稅已 final and conclusive 都仲用得。
- A 錯：呢個係偷龍轉鳳！「6 年 / 6 個月取較後」係 s.70A 嘅時限；objection (s.64(1)) 嘅時限係評稅通知書日期起 **1 個月**。考官最鍾意將呢兩條時限對調。
- B 錯：holdover of provisional tax 嘅申請死線係「繳稅到期日前 28 天」或「暫繳稅繳稅通知書日期後 14 天」，取較後者 —— 唔係「評稅通知書後 1 個月」（嗰個係 objection 嘅時限）。
- D 錯：同 objection 唔同，局長對暫繳稅緩繳申請**無酌情權**接受逾期申請；「不在港／患病可延期」只適用於 objection (s.64(1)(a)) 同埋向 BOR 嘅上訴。

## Question 3 — Answer: B

**Model Answer:**
Statement **B** is correct. The additional tax was raised under **s.82A IRO** — the administrative penalty regime used where the offence is not so serious as to warrant prosecution or the evidence is insufficient for a criminal prosecution. Procedurally, the IRD must give the taxpayer a notice specifying the reasons and inviting written representations within **21 days**, consider the representations, and then raise and serve the additional tax assessment, which is assessed personally by the Commissioner or a Deputy Commissioner. The maximum additional tax is **300% of the tax undercharged**: 300% × HK$300,000 = HK$900,000, so the HK$600,000 imposed is within the statutory cap. An appeal against an s.82A additional tax assessment lies to the **Board of Review** (not to the Court). After an s.82A assessment, no criminal charge may be brought on the same facts under s.80(2) or s.82(1).

**【解題思路】**
- 題眼係「唔夠嚴重去檢控／證據不足做刑事檢控」+「通知 + 書面申述」→ 呢個係 s.82A 行政罰嘅標準程序（21 天申述期）。
- B 啱：上限 = 少徵稅款 × 300% = 300,000 × 3 = 900,000；600,000 係上限之內；s.82A 嘅上訴去 Board of Review。
- A 錯：s.82(1) 係**刑事** wilful evasion 條文，由法院判刑（罰款 + 監禁），唔係由 IRD「raise additional tax」；而且刑事案舉證責任在控方、標準係 beyond reasonable doubt。
- C 錯：s.80(2) 雖然涵蓋 incorrect return 連少徵稅款，但係**民事法庭程序**，罰則由**法院**判（level 3 fine + 最高 300%），唔係 IRD 行政評定；納稅人舉證標準係 balance of probability，唔係 beyond reasonable doubt。
- D 錯：s.80(1) 只涵蓋**無涉及少徵稅款**嘅一般違規（罰款 level 3）；呢度明明有 HK$300,000 少徵稅款，所以 s.80(1) 唔適用。
- 分類邊界總結：民事法庭 → s.80(1)/s.80(2)；刑事法庭 → s.82(1)；行政（無法庭程序、上訴去 BOR）→ s.82A。

## Question 4 — Answer: **HK$138,600**

**Model Answer / Working:**
- Tax in default: HK$120,000, due 10 January 2024, paid 25 October 2024 → late for more than six months.
- **Step 1 — 5% surcharge** (delay not more than six months): 5% × HK$120,000 = **HK$6,000**.
- **Step 2 — 10% surcharge** (delay over six months): added to the total tax in default **including the 5% surcharge imposed earlier**: 10% × (HK$120,000 + HK$6,000) = 10% × HK$126,000 = **HK$12,600**.
- **Step 3 — Total payable**: HK$120,000 + HK$6,000 + HK$12,600 = **HK$138,600**.

**【解題思路】**
- 逾期超過六個月，兩級附加費都要計：先 5%，再 10%。
- 最大陷阱：第二級 10% 嘅基數係「欠稅 + 之前已加嘅 5%」（即 126,000），唔係淨欠稅 120,000。如果錯用 120,000 × 10% = 12,000，就會得出 138,000 呢個錯答案。
- 另一常見錯誤：以為兩級係「二選一」（只加 10%：120,000 × 1.10 = 132,000）—— 唔係，係先加 5% 再喺總額上加 10%。
- 額外知識：如果 IRD 循法院追討，欠稅人仲要付 court fee HK$630、writ cost HK$300 同判決利息；IRD 亦可以向僱主/銀行/債務人發追稅通知書，甚至申請 Departure Prevention Direction 阻止離港。

## Question 5 — Answer: **31 March 2026** (s.60(1) IRO — six-year time limit)

**Model Answer / Working:**
- The year of assessment 2019/20 ended on **31 March 2020**.
- Under s.60(1) IRO, an additional assessment must be made within **six years of the end of the year of assessment** to which it relates.
- Six years after 31 March 2020 → **31 March 2026**. The IRD's additional assessment in May 2025 is therefore **within the time limit and valid**.
- The extended **ten-year** limit under s.60(1)(b) (i.e. until 31 March 2030) applies ONLY where the undercharge involves **fraud or wilful evasion**, which is expressly excluded here.
- Note: there is no time limit on the **collection** of tax once assessed.

**【解題思路】**
- 計時起點係「課稅年度**結束**日」（2019/20 → 31/3/2020），唔係首次評稅日期（15/3/2021），亦唔係發現少報嘅審計日期。由 31/3/2020 起計六年 → 31/3/2026。
- 題目特別講明「no fraud or wilful evasion」，目的係考你分唔分到 6 年同 10 年兩條時限：10 年 (s.60(1)(b)) 只限欺詐或蓄意逃稅。如果你答 31 March 2030，就係中咗呢個陷阱。
- 分類邊界：呢度係 additional assessment (s.60(1))，因為 2019/20 已經有過評稅、而家發現評少咗；唔好同 s.59(3) estimated assessment 撈亂。
- 相關對照：s.70A 納稅人更正 error/omission 都係「課稅年度結束後 6 年（或通知書送達後 6 個月，取較後）」；紀錄保存係交易後 7 年 (s.51C)；通知 chargeability 係基期結束後 4 個月 (s.51(2))。呢四個數字（1 個月 / 4 個月 / 6 年 / 7 年）係 OTQ 必考時限。
