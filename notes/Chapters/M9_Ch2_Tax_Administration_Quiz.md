C. Additional assessment under s.60(1), because the taxpayer had not been adequately taxed
D. Additional tax assessment under s.82A, because failure to file a return always attracts a penalty assessment

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

The IRD considers that the offence is not serious enough to warrant prosecution and the evidence is insufficient for a criminal prosecution. The Commissioner gives Ms Liu a notice specifying the reasons and invites her written representations, which she submits within the required period. The IRD then raises an assessment of additional tax of HK$600,000 on her. Which of the following is CORRECT?

A. The additional tax was raised under s.82(1) and Ms Liu may appeal to the Court.
B. The additional tax was raised under s.82A; the maximum additional tax is HK$900,000; and Ms Liu may appeal to the Board of Review.
C. The additional tax was raised under s.80(2); the penalty is assessed by the Court; and Ms Liu bears the burden of proof beyond reasonable doubt.
D. The additional tax was raised under s.80(1) and the penalty is a fine at level 3 only, so no additional tax could have been imposed.
