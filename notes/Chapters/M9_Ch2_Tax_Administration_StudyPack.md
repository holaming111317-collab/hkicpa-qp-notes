# M9 Administration of the Tax System in Hong Kong — Study Pack (Week 2)

## Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

### 1. 核心知識架構

- **稅務局架構與權力 (IRD structure and powers)**：稅務局 (IRD) 是香港的稅務機關，隸屬財經事務及庫務局 (Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau) 轄下九個部門之一，由稅務局局長 (Commissioner of Inland Revenue, CIR) 及兩名副局長領導，下設六個單位（Commissioner's Unit、Headquarters Unit、Units 1–4）。IRD 依據《稅務條例》(IRO Cap. 112) 有權要求報稅表、索取資料、約見問話 (s.51) 及申請搜查令 (s.51B)；稅務人員須遵守保密規定 (s.4)，但依 DTA/TIEA 交換資料不屬違規。
- **報稅責任 (Returns)**：香港不設「pay as you earn」制度，僱主與僱員各自獨立申報。個人報稅表 BIR60 每年五月首個工作天發出，一般須於一個月內遞交；利得稅報稅表（法團 BIR51、其他 BIR52）每年四月首個工作天發出。如未獲發報稅表但須課稅，須在有關課稅年度基期結束後四個月內通知局長 (s.51(2))。
- **評稅與反對 (Assessments and objection)**：三類評稅 —— 標準評稅 (standard assessment, s.59(1))、估計評稅 (estimated assessment, s.59(3))、補加評稅 (additional assessment, s.60(1))。IRD 對利得稅採「先評後查」(assess first, audit later, AFAL)。不滿評稅須於評稅通知書日期起一個月內書面反對 (s.64(1))；程序鏈為：Objection → CIR determination → Board of Review (s.66) → Court of First Instance (s.69) → Court of Appeal → Court of Final Appeal。
- **緩繳與更正 (Holdover and error/omission claim)**：反對期間稅款原則上仍須繳付 (s.71(2))，可申請有條件或無條件緩繳 (conditional/unconditional holdover)；暫繳稅緩繳申請有嚴格期限且不得延期。評稅若有錯誤或遺漏 (error or omission)，可依 s.70A 在六年內（或評稅通知書發出後六個月，以較後者為準）申請更正。
- **罰則與追稅 (Offences, penalties and payment)**：四大罰則條文 —— s.80(1)、s.80(2)（民事、法院判罰）、s.82(1)（刑事、舉證須 beyond reasonable doubt）、s.82A（行政罰，以補加稅形式處罰，上限為少徵稅款的 300%，向 Board of Review 上訴）。逾期繳稅加徵 5% 附加費（六個月內），超過六個月再加 10%。

### 2. 必背考點清單

**IRD 架構與法定機構**
- IRD 六個單位：Commissioner's Unit（appeals、tax treaties）、Headquarters Unit、Unit 1（profits tax — corporations and partnerships）、Unit 2（salaries tax、profits tax — sole-proprietorships、property tax — sole owners、personal assessment 之 processing）、Unit 3（collection、stamp duty、estate duty、business registration）、Unit 4（field audits and investigations）。
- 保密：s.4 IRO；法律要求披露時例外 (s.49(5))；按 DTA/TIEA 交換資料不違反保密條文。
- Board of Inland Revenue：獨立於 IRD；主席為財政司司長 (Financial Secretary)；負責訂明報稅表格式及制訂 Inland Revenue Rules（須立法會批准）。
- Board of Review (s.65)：獨立法定組織，審理稅務上訴的一審審裁處 (tax tribunal of first instance)；由主席、10 名副主席及不多於 150 名成員組成，全部由行政長官委任；每次聆訊由一名主席加兩名成員組成，多數決；閉門聆訊 (in camera)；無權判給訟費 (no costs order)；對瑣屑無聊上訴可徵收不超過 HK$25,000 費用；事實裁斷為最終 (facts found are final)。

**Returns — 期限必背**
- s.51(1)：任何人須應要求遞交物業稅／薪俸稅／利得稅／綜合報稅表。
- s.51(2)：無收到報稅表而須課稅者，須於基期結束後 **4 個月**內通知 CIR。
- 個人報稅表 BIR60：每年五月首個工作天發出，一般 1 個月內交回；獨資業務東主延至八月首個工作天（電子報稅再自動延 1 個月）；由稅務代表代理一般多獲 1 個月延期。
- 利得稅報稅表（BIR51 法團／BIR52 非法團）：每年四月首個工作天發出，1 個月內交回（12 月年結通常延至八月中、3 月年結延至十一月中）。
- 物業稅報稅表 BIR57（聯權／分權共有人）；非居民利得稅報稅表 BIR54。
- 僱主責任 (s.52)：新僱員到職 3 個月內填報 IR56E (s.52(4))；僱員離職前 1 個月申報 (s.52(5))；僱員將離港逾 1 個月，須於離港前 1 個月申報 (s.52(6)) 並扣起應付款項 (s.52(7))；僱主報稅表須於收到後 1 個月內遞交 (s.52(2))；薪酬紀錄保存至少 7 年 (s.51C)。

**Assessments**
- 三類評稅：standard (s.59(1))；estimated（無報稅表、報稅表不被接受或帳簿不妥，s.59(3)）；additional（少評稅款，s.60(1)）。
- 補加評稅時限：有關課稅年度結束後 **6 年**內 (s.60(1))；涉及 fraud 或 wilful evasion 延長至 **10 年** (s.60(1)(b))。徵稅本身無時限。
- 評稅最終確定 (final and conclusive, s.70)：未依時提出有效反對或上訴；反對被撤回或駁回；雙方同意修訂評稅 (s.64(3))；BOR 裁定且拒絕轉介法院。
- 反對估計評稅（無報稅表情況下發出）時，必須連同有效報稅表一併於一個月反對期內遞交，否則反對無效。
- 業務紀錄保存期：不少於交易完成後 **7 年** (s.51C)。

**Objection / Holdover / Error or Omission**
- Objection (s.64(1))：評稅通知書日期起 **1 個月**內書面提出，述明反對理由；舉證責任在納稅人 (burden of proof on taxpayer, s.68(4))。逾期可因不在香港、患病或其他合理因由獲准延期 (s.64(1)(a))。CIR 裁定可 confirm / reduce / increase / annul 評稅 (s.64(2) 須在合理時間內裁定)。
- Holdover of tax in dispute (s.71(2))：conditional holdover（須購買 Tax Reserve Certificates 或提供銀行擔保 banker's undertaking）；unconditional holdover（罕見，敗訴後須付稅加利息，利率由終審法院首席法官指定）。TRC 受 Tax Reserve Certificates Ordinance (Cap. 289) 規管。
- 暫繳稅緩繳 (holdover of provisional tax)：物業稅 s.63O、薪俸稅 s.63E、利得稅 s.63J；常見理由為應評稅值／入息／利潤 likely to be less than 90% of the provisional amount assessed。申請期限：**繳稅到期日前 28 天**或**暫繳稅繳稅通知書日期後 14 天**，以較後者為準 —— 局長**無酌情權延期**。
- Error or omission claim (s.70A)：課稅年度結束後 **6 年**內，或有關評稅通知書送達後 **6 個月**內（以較後者為準），可就更正報稅表中的錯誤或遺漏、或稅款計算的算術錯誤申請更正。若評稅是按當時通行慣例作出，則不得更正。案例：*Extramoney Ltd v CIR*（'error or omission' 包括 error of law）；*Moulin Global Eyecare Trading Ltd v CIR* (2014)（董事蓄意造假屬 deliberate act，非 error，s.70A 不適用）。

**Offences and Penalties（Exhibit 2.1 對比必背）**
- s.80(1)：無合理辯解的一般不遵從（不涉及少徵稅款），民事，罰款 level 3（HK$10,000），法院判罰，納稅人須以 balance of probability 舉證，向法院上訴。
- s.80(2)：無合理辯解提交不正確報稅表並涉及少徵稅款，民事，罰款 level 3 + 最高少徵稅款 300%，法院判罰。
- s.82(1)：wilful evasion，刑事，控方須證明 beyond reasonable doubt 及 dishonest intention (mens rea)；簡易程序定罪：level 3 + 300% + 監禁 6 個月；循公訴程序定罪：level 5（HK$50,000）+ 300% + 監禁 3 年。
- s.82A：行政罰 —— 由 CIR 或副局長親自評定補加稅 (additional tax)，最高為少徵稅款的 300%，無法庭程序；程序：發通知述明理由 → 納稅人 21 天內書面申述 → 考慮後發出補加稅評稅；向 Board of Review 上訴；其後不得就同一事實再依 s.80(2) 或 s.82(1) 檢控。
- CIR 可選擇：檢控 (prosecution)、以罰款代替檢控 (compound, s.80(5)/s.82(2)，見 DIPN 11 [Revised])、或評定補加稅 (s.82A)。
- Reasonable excuse：IRO 無定義；BOR 接受 honest belief of non-taxability、reasonable reliance on expert advice、simple slip of the mind；不接受 ignorance of law、low level of education、incompetent staff。
- 案例：*Koo Ming Kown & Murakami Tadao v CIR* [2021] HKCA 1037 —— 公司作為納稅人的罰則不能自動加諸董事身上。
- 罰則主要目的：commercial restitution（彌補延遲收稅的損失）、阻嚇、教育。

**Appeals**
- 向 BOR 上訴 (s.66)：CIR determination 後 **1 個月**內書面提出，述明上訴理由並將副本送交 CIR；患病或不在港可獲延期。
- 繞過 BOR 直達 CFI (s.67)：雙方於向 BOR 遞交上訴後 **21 天**內書面同意。
- BOR → CFI (s.69)：BOR 裁決日期（或書面送達日期）起 **1 個月**內，只限 point of law，須申請許可 (leave to appeal)，須證明有 reasonable prospect of success 或符合 interests of justice。
- 繞過 CFI 直達 CA (s.69A)：稅款重大、具公眾重要性、案件特別困難或有其他充分理由，經法院許可 leapfrog 至 Court of Appeal。
- 向 BOR 遞交上訴所需文件：Notice of Assessment、Commissioner's determination、taxpayer's representations、statement of grounds of appeal。

**Payment of Taxes**
- 逾期 ≤ 6 個月：加徵 **5%** 附加費；逾期 > 6 個月：再加徵 **10%**（以包括先前 5% 在內的欠稅總額計）。
- 薪俸稅一般分兩期繳付（付款日通常在一月至四月之間）；物業稅及利得稅通常只有一個繳款日。
- 追稅措施：法院訴訟（另付 court fee HK$630、writ cost HK$300 及判決利息）、向第三者（僱主、銀行、債務人）發追稅通知書、申請 Departure Prevention Direction 阻止欠稅人離港。

### 3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

**Pitfall 1：Objection (s.64) vs Error or Omission Claim (s.70A)**
- Objection：針對「評稅員的判斷」（例如某項扣除被拒絕），須在評稅通知書日期起 **1 個月**內提出；逾期只有在「不在香港／患病／其他合理因由」下才可能獲接納 (s.64(1)(a))。
- s.70A claim：針對報稅表中的 **error or omission**（無心之失，如算術錯誤、漏報免稅額），時限為課稅年度結束後 **6 年**或評稅通知書送達後 **6 個月**（以較後者為準）；即使是 final and conclusive 的評稅也可更正。
- 例子：納稅人在 2019/20 報稅表因 transposition error 多報了薪金 —— 這是 inadvertent error，應用 s.70A 更正（時限寬得多）；若納稅人只是不同意評稅員拒絕某扣除 claim，報稅表本身無錯 —— 只能依 s.64 在 1 個月內反對。考官常把兩條時限對調作為干擾選項：「objection 可在 6 年內提出」是錯的。

**Pitfall 2：Estimated Assessment (s.59(3)) vs Additional Assessment (s.60(1))**
- Estimated assessment：納稅人**未交報稅表**、報稅表不被接受或帳簿不妥時，評稅員「估計」應評稅額；屬首次評稅，不是追加。反對此類評稅時必須連同填妥的報稅表一併遞交，否則反對無效。
- Additional assessment：已經有評稅，但發現納稅人「未被充分評稅」(not adequately taxed)，就**追加**評稅；時限為課稅年度結束後 6 年（fraud/wilful evasion 為 10 年）。
- 例子：Chichi 沒有交利得稅報稅表，IRD 估算其利潤發出評稅 —— 這是 estimated assessment；其後審計發現已評稅的年度少報了版稅收入 —— 追加的是 additional assessment，受 6 年時限約束。切勿以為「估計評稅也要等 6 年時限」或「additional assessment 可以取代從未提交的報稅表」。

## Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

* [Flashcard 1]
  - Front (Question in English): What are the requirements for a valid objection to an assessment under s.64(1) IRO, and what special rule applies when objecting to an estimated assessment raised in the absence of a return?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Must be in writing, stating precisely the grounds of objection;
      - Must be lodged within ONE MONTH after the date of the notice of assessment;
      - Late objection may be accepted only if the taxpayer was prevented by absence from Hong Kong, illness or other reasonable excuse (s.64(1)(a));
      - For an estimated assessment raised in the absence of a return, a valid completed return MUST be filed together with the notice of objection within the one-month objection period, otherwise the objection is invalid;
      - Burden of proof is on the taxpayer to show the assessment is incorrect or excessive.
    - [中文白話解釋]：反對必須書面、講清楚理由、並且在評稅通知書日期起一個月內交到稅務局；過期只可以靠「離港、生病、其他合理因由」求情。如果係針對無報稅表情況下發出的估計評稅，一定要連同填妥嘅報稅表一齊交，否則反對無效。舉證責任在納稅人。

* [Flashcard 2]
  - Front (Question in English): A taxpayer discovered an error in her 2018/19 tax return (an inadvertent transposition error that overstated income). Under s.70A IRO, what is the deadline to apply for correction, and in what circumstances will NO correction be allowed?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Deadline: within SIX YEARS after the end of the relevant year of assessment, OR SIX MONTHS after the relevant notice of assessment is served, whichever is the LATER;
      - For 2018/19 (year of assessment ended 31 March 2019): deadline = 31 March 2025 (or 6 months after the notice if later);
      - 'Error or omission' includes arithmetical errors, omission to claim an expense/deduction/allowance, error or omission of fact, and even an error of law (Extramoney Ltd v CIR);
      - NO correction if the assessment was made in accordance with the practice prevailing at the time the return/statement was made;
      - NO correction for a deliberate act — fraudulent filing is not an 'error' (Moulin Global Eyecare Trading Ltd v CIR).
    - [中文白話解釋]：s.70A 係用嚟更正「無心之失」，時限係課稅年度結束後六年、或者評稅通知書送達後六個月，兩者取較後。如果評稅係跟當時通行做法做嘅，就唔可以更改正；蓄意造假（如 Moulin Global 案）唔算 error，s.70A 幫唔到。

* [Flashcard 3]
  - Front (Question in English): Name the three types of tax assessments under the IRO, their authorising sections, and the time limit for raising an additional assessment.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Standard assessment — s.59(1): raised when an acceptable return is filed;
      - Estimated assessment — s.59(3): raised when no return is filed, the return is unacceptable, or books are not kept in satisfactory form;
      - Additional assessment — s.60(1): raised where the taxpayer has not been adequately taxed;
      - Time limit for additional assessment: within 6 years after the end of the year of assessment concerned; extended to 10 years in cases of fraud or wilful evasion (s.60(1)(b));
      - There is NO time limit on the collection of tax once assessed.
    - [中文白話解釋]：三種評稅要連條文一齊背：正常報稅後發出嘅係 standard（s.59(1)）；無交表或帳簿唔合格就用 estimated（s.59(3)）；評漏咗就用 additional（s.60(1)）。補加評稅一般六年內要發出，涉及欺詐或蓄意逃稅就十年；但稅一經評定，追收係無時限嘅。

* [Flashcard 4]
  - Front (Question in English): What is the deadline for applying for a holdover of provisional tax, and can the Commissioner extend it? Contrast this with the late-objection rule.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Deadline: application must be received by the Commissioner not later than 28 days before the payment due date of the provisional tax, OR 14 days after the date of the notice for payment of provisional tax, whichever is the LATER;
      - The Commissioner has NO discretionary power to extend this time limit;
      - Contrast: a late objection under s.64(1)(a) MAY be accepted if the taxpayer was prevented by absence from Hong Kong, illness or other reasonable excuse;
      - Typical holdover ground: assessable profits / net chargeable income / assessable value for the year is likely to be less than 90% of the provisional amount assessed (ss.63J / 63E / 63O).
    - [中文白話解釋]：暫繳稅緩繳申請死線係「繳稅日前 28 天」同「通知書日期後 14 天」取較後，而且局長完全無權延期——同反對唔同，反對遲咗仲可以用合理因由求情。記住 90% 呢個門檻：預計今年應課稅額低過暫繳額九成係最常用理由。

* [Flashcard 5]
  - Front (Question in English): Compare the four penalty regimes: s.80(1), s.80(2), s.82(1) and s.82A IRO — nature of proceedings, burden of proof, who assesses the penalty, maximum penalty, and appeal route.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - s.80(1): civil; taxpayer proves reasonable excuse on balance of probability; Court assesses; fine at level 3 (HK$10,000); appeal to Court;
      - s.80(2): civil; incorrect return with tax undercharged; Court assesses; level 3 fine + up to 300% of tax undercharged; appeal to Court;
      - s.82(1): CRIMINAL (wilful evasion); prosecution proves beyond reasonable doubt with mens rea (dishonesty); Court assesses; summary conviction: level 3 + up to 300% + 6 months' imprisonment; on indictment: level 5 (HK$50,000) + up to 300% + 3 years' imprisonment; appeal to Court;
      - s.82A: ADMINISTRATIVE additional tax, no legal proceedings; raised personally by the CIR or a Deputy Commissioner; max 300% of tax undercharged; taxpayer must be given notice and 21 days to make written representations; appeal to the BOARD OF REVIEW (not the Court); bars later prosecution on the same facts under s.80(2)/s.82(1).
    - [中文白話解釋]：s.82A 係行政罰，唔使上法庭，由局長或副局長親自出補加稅評稅，上限係少徵稅款三倍，上訴去 Board of Review 而唔係法院；s.82(1) 係刑事，控方要證到 beyond reasonable doubt 兼證明有欺詐意圖；s.80(1) 同 s.80(2) 係民事，納稅人以 balance of probability 證明自己有 reasonable excuse。

* [Flashcard 6]
  - Front (Question in English): A taxpayer's profits tax of HK$200,000 fell due on 5 January but remained unpaid until 20 September of the same year (over six months late). Compute the total amount payable under the surcharge rules.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Delay not more than 6 months: 5% surcharge on total tax in default → 5% × 200,000 = HK$10,000;
      - Delay over 6 months: a further 10% surcharge on the total tax in default INCLUDING the 5% surcharge already imposed → 10% × (200,000 + 10,000) = HK$21,000;
      - Total payable = 200,000 + 10,000 + 21,000 = **HK$231,000**;
      - Note: the Commissioner may accept payment by instalments, but surcharge may still be added.
    - [中文白話解釋]：遲交稅六個月內加 5%；超過六個月再加 10%，而呢 10% 嘅計算基數係「欠稅＋之前嗰 5%」。所以 200,000 先加 10,000，再用 210,000 乘 10% 得 21,000，總共要畀 231,000。

* [Flashcard 7]
  - Front (Question in English): State an employer's key notification obligations to the IRD under s.52 IRO (new employee, cessation of employment, employee leaving Hong Kong) with the relevant time limits and sections.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - New employee likely chargeable to salaries tax: notify within 3 months of commencement, on Form IR56E (s.52(4));
      - Cessation of employment (or death): notify not later than 1 month before the date of termination (s.52(5));
      - Employee leaving Hong Kong for more than 1 month (other than normal business trips): notify at least 1 month before expected departure (s.52(6)) and withhold all payments due to the employee from the date of filing the departure notice until departure (s.52(7));
      - Employer's return of all employees: file within 1 month of receipt (s.52(2));
      - Payroll records: retain for at least 7 years (s.51C);
      - No general obligation to withhold salaries tax — Hong Kong has no PAYE system.
    - [中文白話解釋]：僱主三大申報死線：新人到職 3 個月內報 IR56E；員工離職前 1 個月報；員工要離港超過 1 個月，離港前 1 個月報兼扣住佢應得嘅錢（由報離港通知到離港日）。香港無 PAYE，所以僱主一般唔使預扣薪俸稅，唯一例外係員工離港個案。

* [Flashcard 8]
  - Front (Question in English): Describe the tax appeal ladder after the CIR determines an objection, including the bypass provisions (ss.66, 67, 69, 69A IRO).
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - s.66: appeal to the Board of Review within 1 month after the CIR's determination, in writing with grounds, copy to the CIR (extension possible for illness/absence from HK);
      - Board of Review: tax tribunal of first instance; panel of 3 (one chairman + two members); in camera; facts found are final; burden of proof on taxpayer (s.68(4)); may impose a fee up to HK$25,000 for vexatious appeals; no costs order;
      - s.67: bypass BOR and go directly to the CFI if BOTH parties agree in writing within 21 days from the date the appeal was lodged with the BOR;
      - s.69: appeal BOR decision to the CFI within 1 month, on a point of LAW only, with leave (reasonable prospect of success / interests of justice);
      - s.69A: leapfrog from the BOR directly to the Court of Appeal with the court's permission where the tax in dispute is significant, the matter is of public importance, or the case is extraordinarily difficult.
    - [中文白話解釋]：上訴階梯：反對 → CIR 裁定 → 一個月內上訴去 BOR → 一個月內（限法律觀點、要申請許可）上 CFI → CA → CFA。兩條「跳級」條文要分清楚：s.67 係雙方 21 天內書面同意繞過 BOR 直去 CFI；s.69A 係經法院許可由 BOR 直接跳去 CA。BOR 審事實同法律，法院只審法律觀點。
