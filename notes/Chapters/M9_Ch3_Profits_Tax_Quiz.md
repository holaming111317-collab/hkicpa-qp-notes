**Question 1 (MCQ) — Capital vs Revenue Receipts**

Harbour Textiles Ltd carries on the business of garment trading in Hong Kong. During the year, it received the following sums. Which one is a REVENUE receipt chargeable to profits tax?

A. Compensation of HK$3,000,000 received from a supplier for the cancellation of a sole distributorship agreement that constituted the entire framework of the company's business.

B. Compensation of HK$400,000 received from an insurer for the loss of use of a delivery truck during the three months in which it was being repaired after an accident.

C. A gain of HK$2,000,000 from the disposal of the company's office premises in Central, which it had occupied for eight years, sold after an unsolicited offer from a developer.

D. A dividend of HK$250,000 received from a Hong Kong company which is itself chargeable to profits tax.

**Question 2 (MCQ) — Deductibility (ss.16–17)**

Which of the following items of expenditure is DEDUCTIBLE in computing assessable profits?

A. A general provision for doubtful debts of HK$80,000 made by a trading company, estimated at 2% of total trade receivables without debtor-by-debtor analysis or supporting documents.

B. A trade debt of HK$120,000 written off during the year, which had previously been included as a taxable trading receipt, after repeated recovery actions proved unsuccessful.

C. Legal and stamp duty costs of HK$95,000 incurred on the acquisition of new business premises occupied by the company for its own operations.

D. A donation of HK$50,000 made to an education fund established and operating exclusively in Mainland China which is not exempt under s.88 of the IRO.

**Question 3 (MCQ) — Source of Trading Profits (DIPN 21)**

Pacific Merchandising Ltd, a Hong Kong company carrying on a merchandise trading business in Hong Kong, purchased goods from a supplier under a contract of purchase negotiated and concluded in Hong Kong, and resold the same goods to a customer under a contract of sale negotiated and concluded in London. Applying the IRD's assessing practice under DIPN 21, the trading profits are:

A. Fully chargeable to profits tax in Hong Kong, because where either the contract of purchase or the contract of sale is effected in Hong Kong, the initial presumption is that the profits are fully taxable.

B. Not chargeable to profits tax, because the contract of sale — the contract which in substance gives rise to the profits — was effected outside Hong Kong.

C. Apportioned on a fifty-fifty basis between Hong Kong and the United Kingdom, in the same way as contract processing arrangements.

D. Not chargeable to profits tax, because the customer is located overseas and the goods were never delivered to Hong Kong.

**Question 4 (Objective Calculation) — Depreciation Allowances (Pooling System)**

Glory Ltd commenced business in Hong Kong and makes up accounts to 31 December each year. During its first year of trading, it purchased the following items of machinery and plant, all used wholly in the production of assessable profits:

| Asset | Cost (HK$) | Annual allowance rate |
|---|---|---|
| Motor lorry | 300,000 | 30% |
| Photocopier | 60,000 | 20% |

None of the assets qualifies under ss.16B, 16G or 16H–16K. Compute the TOTAL depreciation allowances (initial allowance + annual allowance) that Glory Ltd may claim for the year of assessment in which the expenditure was incurred. (State your answer in HK$.)

**Question 5 (Objective Calculation) — Assessable Profits and Profits Tax Payable**

Starbright Ltd is a Hong Kong corporation carrying on a trading business wholly in Hong Kong, and is the only entity in its group electing the two-tiered profits tax rates. For the year of assessment, its profit and loss account shows a net profit before taxation of HK$2,400,000, after taking into account the following items:

- Income credited: gain on disposal of office premises held for ten years as a capital asset HK$300,000; interest on a Hong Kong bank deposit (not used to secure any borrowing) HK$40,000; dividend from a Hong Kong subsidiary chargeable to profits tax HK$60,000.
- Expenses charged: accounting depreciation HK$120,000; a fine for breach of Hong Kong law HK$30,000.

Depreciation allowances agreed with the IRD for the year amount to HK$200,000. There are no losses brought forward and no approved charitable donations.

Compute (i) Starbright Ltd's net assessable profits, and (ii) its profits tax payable for the year. (State both answers in HK$.)
