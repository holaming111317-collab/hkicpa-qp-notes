# M9 Chapter 4 Salaries Tax — Model Answers（完成測驗後先好睇！）

## Question 1 — Answer: A — (I) and (III) only

**Model Answer (English):**

- (I) **Chargeable.** Income from an office (director's fee) is Hong Kong sourced if the company paying the fee is centrally managed and controlled in Hong Kong. The source of an office does not depend on where the director performs his duties, and the employment-related exemptions (ss.8(1A)(b), 8(1B)) do NOT apply to income from an office. Mr. A's absence from Hong Kong is irrelevant.
- (II) **Not chargeable.** Ms. B has a non-Hong Kong employment. Her income is chargeable only in respect of services rendered in Hong Kong (s.8(1A)(a)), but services rendered during visits not exceeding a total of 60 days in the YA are disregarded (s.8(1B)). At exactly 58 days, the 60-day exemption applies.
- (III) **Chargeable.** A pension paid from a fund managed and controlled in Hong Kong is Hong Kong sourced. While a non-government pension attributable to services rendered outside Hong Kong is exempt under s.8(2)(ca), **government pensions are fully assessable irrespective of where the services were rendered**.
- (IV) **Not chargeable.** Ms. D has a Hong Kong employment, but she rendered ALL her services outside Hong Kong and her visits did not exceed 60 days — exempt under ss.8(1A)(b)(ii) and 8(1B).

【解題思路】呢題考三種收入來源規則嘅邊界：(I) 董事酬金係 office 收入，只睇支付公司嘅 central management and control 所在地，僱傭收入嘅 60 日豁免**唔適用**於 office——選項 D 就係靠呢點引人犯錯。(III) 政府退休金無論服務地點都要課稅，s.8(2)(ca) 豁免只限非政府退休金——好多考生會誤以為「喺東京做嘢所以豁免」而漏咗 (III)。(II) 58 日 ≤ 60 日，豁免成立；(IV) 香港僱傭但全部服務境外 + 到訪 ≤ 60 日，s.8(1A)(b)(ii) 豁免。記住口訣：Employment 睇 Goepfert 三因素、Office 睇公司管理控制地、Pension 睇基金管理控制地（政府退休金除外）。

## Question 2 — Answer: C

**Model Answer (English):**

Under s.8(2)(j), income of a crew member of a ship or aircraft is exempt only if BOTH limbs are satisfied: (i) present in Hong Kong for not more than 60 days in the YA concerned; AND (ii) present for not more than 120 days in total over two consecutive YAs, one of which is the YA concerned.

- Limb (i): 50 days ≤ 60 days — satisfied (transit days count as days of presence in Hong Kong: IRBRD D11/13 — "present" means "being here", even within the airport transit area).
- Limb (ii): 75 + 50 = 125 days > 120 days — FAILED.

Therefore the s.8(2)(j) exemption does not apply. His income is chargeable (fully if a Hong Kong employment; on a time apportionment basis if a non-Hong Kong employment).

【解題思路】考官最鍾意考機組人員豁免嘅**雙重門檻**：好多人記得 60 日就忘記咗「兩年合計 ≤ 120 日」呢第二關。選項 A 係典型半桶水答案——只過咗第一關。選項 B 測試 transit day：根據 IRBRD D11/13，即使無入境、只喺機場禁區轉機，都計作「在港出席」，所以唔可以扣返 12 日。選項 D 明顯錯，s.8(2)(j) 本身就係專為船員機組而設嘅豁免。答題時兩個條件要逐一驗證，缺一不可。

## Question 3 — Answer: B

**Model Answer (English):**

- **B is NOT chargeable.** Under the liability test in s.9(1)(a)(iv), a payment by an employer to a third party is not income of the employee if it discharges the employer's own **sole and primary liability** (and no person was surety for it). Since the utility accounts are in the employer's name, the employer is the only party liable, and the benefit cannot be converted into money — not assessable (consistent with DIPN 16 (Revised)).
- **A is chargeable.** Payments in connection with the education of a child of an employee are carved out of the liability test and are ALWAYS assessable — s.9(2A)(b); David Hardy Glynn v CIR (school fees paid by the employer directly to the school were taxable perquisites).
- **C is chargeable.** The employee joined the club and was personally liable for the subscription; the employer's reimbursement discharges the EMPLOYEE's personal liability — an assessable perquisite under s.9(1)(a).
- **D is chargeable.** A holiday passage allowance is a cash allowance fully assessable under s.9(1)(a); the amount actually spent is irrelevant. (Even an employer-paid holiday journey would be chargeable under s.9(2A)(c).)

【解題思路】核心分辨：「僱主找自己嘅數」定「僱主幫僱員找數」。B 嘅關鍵係水電戶口登記喺僱主名下，法律上欠費嘅係僱主，僱員冇 personal liability 被解除，福利亦換唔到錢 → 不課稅。A 係 Glynn case 嘅經典案情，子女教育費係 s.9(2A)(b) 三大例外之一，即使僱主合約上要付都照課。C 係解除僱員個人債務，同私人信用卡簽賬一樣要課稅。D 係現金津貼 100% 課稅，用咗幾多無關——千祈唔好當佢係 holiday journey 福利去諗。

## Question 4 — Answer: Net chargeable income = HK$424,000; Salaries tax payable = HK$54,080

**Model Answer (English):**

| | HK$ |
|---|---|
| Salary | 600,000 |
| Bonus | 40,000 |
| Subtotal for rental value base | 640,000 |
| Add: Rental value — s.9(2): 10% × HK$640,000 | 64,000 |
| **Assessable Income** | **704,000** |
| Less: s.12 deductions | — |
| **Net Assessable Income** | **704,000** |
| Less: Concessionary deductions | |
| — Mandatory MPF contributions (s.26G, cap HK$18,000) | (18,000) |
| — Approved charitable donations (s.26C; 35% cap = 35% × 704,000 = 246,400 > 10,000, so fully deductible) | (10,000) |
| **Net Income (NI)** | **676,000** |
| Less: Personal allowances | |
| — Basic allowance | (132,000) |
| — Child allowance | (120,000) |
| **Net Chargeable Income (NCI)** | **424,000** |

Tax at progressive rates on NCI:
- First HK$200,000: 1,000 + 3,000 + 5,000 + 7,000 = HK$16,000
- Balance: (424,000 − 200,000) × 17% = 224,000 × 17% = HK$38,080
- Total: **HK$54,080**

Tax at standard rate: 15% × NI 676,000 = HK$101,400.

**Salaries tax payable = lower of the two = HK$54,080.**

【解題思路】逐步拆解：
1. **Rental value**：僱主提供免費住所（非酒店），按 s.9(2) 以來自該僱主收入嘅 10% 計：10% × (600,000 + 40,000) = 64,000。冇 s.12(1)(a)/(b) 扣除，所以基數係 640,000。常見錯誤：漏咗花紅唔計入基數，或者誤用實際市值租金。
2. **捐款上限**：35% × assessable income（只扣 s.12(1)(a)/(b) 後）= 246,400，捐款 10,000 全數可扣。注意個 cap 唔係用 NAI 或 NI 計。
3. **NI 與 NCI 分別**：NI = NAI − 特惠扣除 = 676,000；NCI = NI − 個人免稅額 = 424,000。單身有一名 8 歲子女同住 → basic 132,000 + child 120,000。
4. **兩個稅率比較**：累進 = 16,000 + 224,000 × 17% = 54,080；標準 = 676,000 × 15% = 101,400。取低者 54,080。常見錯誤係用 15% 乘 NCI（應該乘 NI），或者累進稅率漏咗首 20 萬嘅固定 16,000。題目已註明忽略 s.100 稅務寬減。

## Question 5 — Answer: HK$660,000

**Model Answer (English):**

Step 1 — Leave days attributable to Hong Kong:

Attributable leave days = (Business days in HK ÷ Total business days) × Leave days
= (120 ÷ 330) × 33 = **12 days**

Step 2 — Total days in Hong Kong:

120 + 12 = **132 days**

Step 3 — Income attributable to services rendered in Hong Kong:

= (Days in HK ÷ Total days in YA) × Annual income
= (132 ÷ 365) × HK$1,825,000
= **HK$660,000**

【解題思路】時間分攤三部曲：
1. **歸屬香港嘅假期日**：leave pay 歸屬在港服務部分都要課稅 (s.8(1A)(a))，公式係「在港工作日 ÷ 總工作日 × 假期日」= 120/330 × 33 = 12 日。常見錯誤：(a) 完全漏咗計假期日；(b) 分母用 365 而唔係總工作日 330（總工作日 + 假期 = 330 + 33 = 363... 呢題 330 已係題目所給總工作日，直接代入）。
2. **總在港日數**：120 + 12 = 132 日。題目已註明 120 日係按 midnight rule 計好（到離兩日合計一日），唔好再自行加返到訪日數。
3. **分攤**：132/365 × 1,825,000 = 660,000。1,825,000/365 = 5,000 一日，132 × 5,000 = 660,000。
提提你：因為 Ms. G 係非香港僱傭，境外服務收入已經自動排除，s.8(1A)(c) 嘅 foreign tax exclusion 喺時間分攤下係**冇作用**嘅——唔好再扣任何外地稅。60 日規則已 fail（題目註明），所以唔存在「首 60 日免稅」呢回事。
