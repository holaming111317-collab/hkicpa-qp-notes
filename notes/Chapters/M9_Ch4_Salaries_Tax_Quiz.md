## Question 1 (MCQ)

Which of the following income items is/are **chargeable to Hong Kong salaries tax** for the year of assessment 2019/20?

(I) A director's fee of HK$200,000 paid to Mr. A by a company which is centrally managed and controlled in Hong Kong. Mr. A resides in London and did not set foot in Hong Kong during the year.

(II) A salary of HK$900,000 earned by Ms. B, who has a non-Hong Kong employment and rendered services in Hong Kong during visits totalling exactly 58 days in the year of assessment.

(III) A pension received by Mr. C, a retired Hong Kong civil servant, from the Hong Kong Government pension fund. His pension is wholly attributable to his past services rendered at the Hong Kong Economic and Trade Office in Tokyo.

(IV) A salary of HK$1,200,000 earned by Ms. D, who has a Hong Kong employment, rendered all her services outside Hong Kong during the year and visited Hong Kong for no more than 60 days in the year of assessment.

A. (I) and (III) only
B. (I), (III) and (IV) only
C. (II) and (IV) only
D. (I), (II), (III) and (IV)

## Question 2 (MCQ)

In the year of assessment 2019/20, he was present in Hong Kong for a total of 50 days, of which 12 days were days he spent inside the Hong Kong airport transit area between flights (he did not pass through immigration control on those days). In the immediately preceding year of assessment 2018/19, he was present in Hong Kong for a total of 75 days. Which of the following statements is correct?

A. His income is exempt under s.8(2)(j) because he was present in Hong Kong for not more than 60 days in the year of assessment 2019/20.
B. His income is exempt under s.8(2)(j) because the 12 transit days are excluded, leaving only 38 days of presence in the year of assessment 2019/20.
C. His income is NOT exempt under s.8(2)(j) because his total presence in Hong Kong over the two consecutive years of assessment 2018/19 and 2019/20 (125 days) exceeds 120 days.
D. His income is NOT exempt because aircraft crew members are never eligible for any salaries tax exemption.

## Question 4 (Calculation)

Applicable figures for YA 2019/20: basic allowance HK$132,000; child allowance HK$120,000; progressive rates — first HK$50,000 at 2%, next HK$50,000 at 6%, next HK$50,000 at 10%, next HK$50,000 at 14%, remainder at 17%; standard rate 15%.

Compute Mr. F's **net chargeable income** and his **salaries tax payable** for YA 2019/20. (Ignore any tax reduction under s.100.)

## Question 5 (Calculation)

The following data apply to the year of assessment:

- Annual remuneration: HK$1,825,000
- Business days in Hong Kong (counted under the midnight rule): 120 days
- Total business days in the year of assessment: 330 days
- Leave days taken during the year (all taken outside Hong Kong): 33 days

Compute Ms. G's **income attributable to services rendered in Hong Kong** chargeable to salaries tax for YA 2019/20. Show your answer in HK$ (round to the nearest dollar).
