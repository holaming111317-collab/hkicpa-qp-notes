# M9 Chapter 4 Salaries Tax — Study Pack (Weeks 5–6)

## Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

### 1. 核心知識架構

- **課稅範圍 (Scope of Charge — s.8(1))**：薪俸稅只對「於香港產生或得自香港」(arising in or derived from Hong Kong) 的**職位 (Office)**、**受僱工作 (Employment)** 及**退休金 (Pension)** 收入徵稅，屬地域來源原則 (territorial basis)；非香港來源收入不徵稅。
- **來源地判定 (Source Rules)**：受僱收入來源看「僱傭所在地」(location of employment)，按 **Goepfert case (CIR v George Andrew Goepfert, 2 HKTC 210)** 三因素判斷；董事酬金 (director's fees) 看支付公司「中央管理及控制」(central management and control) 所在地；退休金看「退休金基金管理及控制地」(where the fund is managed and controlled)。
- **豁免與分攤 (Exemptions & Time Apportionment)**：香港僱傭原則上全數課稅，除非全部服務在境外提供 (s.8(1A)(b)(ii))、60 日豁免 (60-day rule, s.8(1B))、或非協定地區已繳類似稅項 (s.8(1A)(c))；非香港僱傭只就在港服務部分按「時間分攤基準」(time-in-time-out basis, s.8(1A)(a)) 課稅。
- **應課稅收入與福利 (Chargeable Income & Benefits — s.9)**：包括工資、花紅、津貼、perquisite、房屋福利 (rental value)、股份認購權收益 (share option gains, s.9(1)(d))、子女教育費及度假旅程福利 (s.9(2A))；判斷關鍵是「liability test」(s.9(1)(a)(iv)) 及「convertible into money」原則。
- **扣除、免稅額與計算 (Deductions, Allowances & Computation)**：s.12 扣除（開支、折舊免稅額、虧損、進修開支）→ Net Assessable Income → 扣除 Part 4A 特惠扣除 (concessionary deductions) → Net Income → 扣除 Part 5 個人免稅額 (personal allowances) → Net Chargeable Income；稅款取「累進稅率 × NCI」與「標準稅率 15% × NI」兩者較低者 (s.13)。

### 2. 必背考點清單

**A. 課稅範圍與來源 (Scope & Source)**
- **s.8(1)**：對每人在課稅年度 (YA) 內於香港產生或得自香港的 employment、office、pension 收入徵收薪俸稅。
- **Employment 來源 — Goepfert 三因素**：(i) 僱傭合約洽談及訂立地點 (place where the contract was negotiated and concluded)；(ii) 僱主居所 (residence of the employer — 公司看 central management and control 實際所在地)；(iii) 薪酬支付地點 (place where the employee was paid，權重最低)。**提供服務地點無關 (place of services is irrelevant)**。IRD 按 DIPN 10 (Revised) 採「totality of facts / substance over form」。
- **Office 來源**：董事酬金來源 = 支付公司 centrally managed and controlled 之地 (IRBRD D21/13)；董事局會議地點並非決定性。
- **Pension 來源**：基金在香港管理及控制 → 香港來源；非政府退休金中歸屬於境外服務部分豁免 (s.8(2)(ca))；**政府退休金不論服務地點全數課稅**。
- **香港僱傭**：全數課稅 (s.8(1)(a))，無分攤，三條出路：全部服務在境外 (s.8(1A)(b)(ii))；60 日規則 (s.8(1B))；境外服務收入已在**非 DTA 地區**繳納性質基本相同稅項 (s.8(1A)(c) — 須「chargeable」且「已實際繳付」，稅額多少無關；DTA 地區只可用 s.50 foreign tax credit, s.8(1C))。
- **非香港僱傭**：只就在港服務收入課稅 (s.8(1A)(a))，按時間分攤；s.8(1A)(c) 在此不適用（境外收入已自動排除）。
- **60 日豁免 (s.8(1B))**：在港提供服務的「到訪」(visits) 合計**不超過 60 日**→ 豁免；**超過 60 日則全不豁免**（不是分攤！）。計日：**不足一日亦作一日計**（抵港及離港日均計入，IRBRD D20/00、D40/07）；「visits」指日到訪而非工作日 (CIR v So Chak Kwong)；培訓、開會、匯報工作進度均屬在港提供服務。60 日豁免**不適用**於政府僱員、船員及機組人員。
- **機組/船員豁免 (s.8(2)(j))**：在港出席 ≤ 60 日（該 YA）**且** ≤ 120 日（兩個連續 YA，其中一個為該 YA）；**transit days 過境日計算在內** (IRBRD D11/13)。

**B. 時間分攤 (Time Apportionment — s.8(1A)(a))**
- Income attributable to HK services = Annual income × **Days in HK in the YA ÷ Total days in the YA (365/366)**。
- Days in HK = business days in HK + **attributable leave days** = (Business days in HK ÷ Total business days) × Leave days。
- 計日採 **「midnight rule」**：抵港日與離港日**合共只計一日**（與 60 日規則「部分日計一日」不同——常考陷阱！）。
- 若收入可清楚識別 (clearly identifiable, e.g. 合約訂明)，不用分攤 (IRBRD D106/89)；time-in-time-out 只是「rebuttable presumption」(CIR v Lo Wa Ming Patrick [2022] HKCA 710)。

**C. 應課稅收入與福利 (s.9)**
- **s.9(1)(a)**：wages, salary, leave pay, fee, commission, bonus, gratuity, perquisite, allowance — 不論來自僱主或他人。
- **Liability test — s.9(1)(a)(iv)**：僱主代支付給第三者的款項，如屬**僱主本身的 sole and primary liability**（且非他人作擔保的債務）→ 不屬收入；反之，僱主代繳**僱員個人債務**（如私人信用卡簽賬、個人會所會費）→ 應課稅 perquisite (David Hardy Glynn v CIR)。
- **三大例外 — s.9(2A)**（即使僱主負責付款亦必課稅）：(a) 可轉換為金錢的福利 (convertible into money)；(b) **僱員子女教育** (education of a child of an employee)；(c) **度假旅程** (holiday journey)。
- **房屋福利**：
  - 現金房屋/租金/按揭津貼 (housing allowance) → **100% 全數課稅** (s.9(1)(a))；
  - Rent-free residence → 按 **rental value** 課稅 (s.9(1)(b), s.9(2))；
  - 部分補貼住所 → rental value − 僱員已付租金 (s.9(1)(c))；
  - 僱主實付租金或退還租金 → 不視為收入 (s.9(1A))，避免雙重課稅。
  - **Rental value = 10% × 來自該僱主的收入**（扣除 s.12(1)(a) 開支及 s.12(1)(b) 折舊免稅額後，但**不扣** self-education expenses；share option gains 不計入）；hotel/hostel/boarding house：≤2 房 **8%**、≤1 房 **4%** (s.9(2)(a))；共享宿舍按 4%。可選擇以 rateable value 替代（通常不利）。
- **股份認購權 (Share options — ss.9(1)(d), 9(4), 9(5))**：
  - 行使 (exercise)：Gain = **OMV at exercise − consideration paid（股款 + 認購權成本）**；
  - 轉讓/放棄 (assignment/release)：Gain = 轉讓/放棄所得代價 − 取得認購權所付代價；
  - 於**行使/轉讓/放棄年度**課稅（notional gain，毋須實際出售）；相關日期一般為配股日 (date of allotment)；s.9(5) 防止重複課稅。
- **股份獎勵 (Share awards, DIPN 38)**：Upfront approach（授出時有全部經濟利益 → 授出時按 OMV 課稅；其後股息屬投資收入不課稅）vs Back-end approach（設歸屬期、離職即沒收 → 歸屬時按 OMV 課稅；歸屬期內股息屬 perquisite 課稅, IRBRD D21/16）。離職後歸屬的股份視為於最後工作日累算 (s.11D(b)(ii))。
- **退休計劃利益 (ss.8(2)(c),(cb),(cc), 9(1)(aa)–(ae), DIPN 23)**：
  - 退休、死亡、喪失工作能力 (incapacity)、患末期疾病 (terminal illness) 時收取 → 豁免；
  - 終止服務 (termination of service)：服務 **≥ 10 年全免**；< 10 年按 **proportionate benefit (s.8(5)) = Accrued benefit × completed months of service ÷ 120** 計算免稅上限，超出部分課稅 (s.9(1)(ab)(ii), 9(1)(ae))；
  - 僱主非應課利得稅者 (e.g. 大學)：豁免上限 s.8(8) = Annual income × 15/100 × completed years of service + MPF 強制供款累算權益；
  - 非認可計劃 (non-recognised scheme) 中歸屬僱主供款部分 → 全數課稅 (s.9(1)(aa))。
- **收入累算時點 (ss.11B, 11C, 11D)**：s.11B 按累算 (accrue) 歸入 YA；s.11D(b) 有權申索付款時即累算；s.11D(a) 已累算未收取 → 收取時以補加評稅 (additional assessment) 課稅；已提供或按其指示處理 → 視為已收 (deemed received)；**s.11D(b)(i)**：終止僱傭的整筆款項/延付薪酬可書面申請（付款 YA 完結後 **2 年內**）攤回服務期；服務期超過 3 年者按**最後 36 個月**平均攤分。

**D. 扣除項目 (Deductions)**
- **s.12(1)(a) 開支**：必須 (i) 非家庭/私人 (domestic or private)；(ii) 非資本性 (capital)；(iii) **wholly, exclusively and necessarily incurred**；(iv) 已招致 (incurred — 已付款或有確定現存債務)；(v) **in the production of assessable income**（= 履行職務過程中, CIR v Humphrey；住所往返辦公室交通費不可扣）。嚴格解讀下幾乎無開支可扣；IRD 的 extra-statutory concessions：一個專業團體會費（須為 full membership 且是任職先決條件）、指定制服僱員洗衣費定額 HK$2,400、佣金制僱員按佣金收入 **10%** 定額扣除。
- **s.12(1)(b) 折舊免稅額**：機器/設備的資本開支，其使用須「essential to the production of assessable income」；Initial allowance 60% (s.39B(1A)(c))，annual allowance 30% reducing value；出售時 balancing charge 加回收入 (s.12(5))。
- **s.12(1)(c) / s.12A 虧損結轉**：先用於產生虧損者本人的來年收入；聯合評稅 (joint assessment) 下餘額可抵配偶收入 (s.12A(4))。
- **s.12(1)(e) 進修開支 (Self-education expenses)**：須為 gain or maintain qualifications for use in any employment 的 prescribed course；**在付款的 YA 申索**（非開課年度）；已獲僱主發還部分不可扣；上限 **HK$100,000** (Schedule 3A, YAs 2019/20 & 2020/21)。
- **Part 4A 特惠扣除 (Concessionary Deductions)**：
  | 項目 | 條文 | 上限/規則 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Approved charitable donations | s.26C | 須為**金錢**捐贈、合計 ≥ HK$100、給予 s.88 豁免慈善機構或政府；上限 = **35% × (assessable income − s.12(1)(a)/(b) 扣除)** |
  | Elderly residential care expenses | s.26D, Sch 3C | 父母/祖父母 ≥ 60 歲（或符合傷殘津貼）；院舍須在**香港**並持牌；上限 **HK$100,000**/每名長者；同一長者只可由一人申索，且與 DPA/DGA 互斥 (ss.30(5), 30A(5)) |
  | Home loan interest (HLI) | s.26E, Sch 3D | 香港住宅、須為主要居所 (principal place of residence)、業主、按揭貸款用於購置該住宅；上限 **HK$100,000**/年，最多 **20 個 YA**（不須連續）；共同業主按業權比例 (ss.26E(2)(b),(c))；無應課稅收入者可提名配偶申索 (s.26F) |
  | Recognised retirement scheme contributions | s.26G, Sch 3B | MPF 強制供款（或 ROR 供款以假設 MPF 供款額為限），上限 **HK$18,000** |
  | VHIS qualifying premiums | s.26K, Sch 3E | 每名受保人 (specified relative) 上限 **HK$8,000**；多於一名保單持有人視為平均分攤 (s.26K(2))；保單/受保人數目無上限 |
  | Qualifying annuity premiums (QDAP) + TVC | ss.26O, 26S, Sch 3F | 合計上限 **HK$60,000**；**TVC 先扣**，年金保費後扣 (s.26U(2))；TVC 只限本人申索 |

- DPA/DGA 要件：父母/祖父母須 **ordinarily resident in HK**（持香港永久身份證不足夠）、年齡達標、由納稅人或配偶「maintain」（同住 ≥ 6 個月或供養 ≥ HK$12,000/年, s.30(4), Sch 4）。
- 同一受養人只可由**一名納稅人**申索 (s.33)；已婚夫婦的**所有子女免稅額須由一方（經提名）統一申索** (s.31(3))。
- 個人免稅額**只適用於按累進稅率評稅者**；特惠扣除則累進及標準稅率皆可用。

**F. 稅款計算 (Computation — s.13)**
- 步驟：Assessable Income →（減 s.12 扣除、加 balancing charge）→ **Net Assessable Income (NAI)** →（減 Part 4A 特惠扣除）→ **Net Income (NI)** →（減 Part 5 個人免稅額）→ **Net Chargeable Income (NCI)**。
- Salaries tax payable = **lower of**：(i) 累進稅率 (Schedule 2) × NCI：**首 4 個 HK$50,000 分別 2% / 6% / 10% / 14%，餘額 17%**；或 (ii) **15%** (Schedule 1) × NI。
- 稅務寬減：YA 2019/20 為 100% 寬減上限 HK$20,000 (s.100(4))；YA 2020/21 為 100% 上限 HK$10,000 (s.100(1), Sch 43)。**PST 計算不適用稅務寬減**。
- 夫婦各自評稅 (separate taxation) 為原則；如聯合評稅更有利可選 **joint assessment (s.10)**；同性婚姻有效 (Leung Chun Kwong v Secretary for the Civil Service (2019) 22 HKCFAR 127)。
- **Provisional Salaries Tax (PST, s.63B)**：按上一 YA 的 NCI/NI 以**本年度**稅率及免稅額計算 (ss.63C, 63CA)；預期收入大跌可申請緩繳 (holdover, s.63E：繳款到期前 28 日或繳款通知書日期後 14 日，以較後者為準；理由包括 NCI 預計低於上年 90% 等, s.63E(2))。

### 3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

**Pitfall Pair 1: Hong Kong employment vs Non-Hong Kong employment（來源判定 vs 服務地點）**
- **對比**：香港僱傭 → 全數課稅，**沒有分攤**（「once s.8(1) applies, there is no apportionment」），只有三條豁免出路；非香港僱傭 → 只就在港服務日數分攤課稅 (s.8(1A)(a))。判定用 Goepfert 三因素（合約訂立地、僱主居所、支薪地），**在何處上班完全無關**。
- **例子**：Tom 與新加坡公司訂約、薪酬存入新加坡戶口，但被派駐香港分行全年在港工作——他屬 **non-Hong Kong employment**，全年 365 日均在港 → 時間分攤後全部收入照樣課稅。反之，Peter 與香港公司訂約、僱主居所在港，但被派到深圳廠房全年工作、週末回港（在港到訪 > 60 日）——他屬 **Hong Kong employment**，但因週末在港沒有提供服務可主張全部服務在境外 (s.8(1A)(b)(ii))；若到訪 ≤ 60 日則 s.8(1B) 豁免。**千萬不要因「人在香港工作」就說是香港僱傭，或因「人在內地工作」就說收入不用課稅。**

**Pitfall Pair 2: Housing allowance（現金津貼）vs Rent refund / rent-free accommodation（房屋福利）**
- **對比**：現金 housing/rental/mortgage allowance → **100% 全數課稅** (s.9(1)(a))；真正的「退還租金」(refund of rent) 或僱主提供住所 → 只按 **rental value（收入的 10% / 8% / 4%）** 課稅 (s.9(1)(b)/(c), s.9(2))，實付租金不屬收入 (s.9(1A))。分別關鍵：(i) 必須**先有實際租金支出**才有「退款」可言 (IRBRD D92/95)；(ii) 僱主須設有**實際監控**（租約、收據）確保款項只用作交租 (IRBRD D33/97, D149/00)；(iii) 看「付款時實際發生甚麼事」('what transpired at the time of payment' test, CIR v Peter Leslie Page)。
- **例子**：合約寫明「housing benefit 每月上限 HK$38,000，以退租形式發放」，但僱員從不交租單、僱主亦從不查核，僱員不租屋也照收全額 → 這其實是**現金津貼，100% 課稅**（CIR v Peter Leslie Page 正是此案情）。反之，僱員簽真租約、每月交租單給僱主核銷 → 屬 rent refund，只按 10% rental value 課稅，稅負大減。

## Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

* [Flashcard 1]
  - Front (Question in English): What are the three Goepfert factors for determining the location (source) of an employment, and which factor is expressly irrelevant?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - (1) The place where the employment contract was negotiated and concluded;
      - (2) The residence of the employer (for a company — where its central management and control actually abide);
      - (3) The place where the employee was paid (carries the least weight).
      - The place where the services are performed is IRRELEVANT (CIR v George Andrew Goepfert, 2 HKTC 210; DIPN 10 (Revised) adopts a totality-of-facts / substance-over-form approach).
    - [中文白話解釋]：判斷一份僱傭是否「香港僱傭」，不是看你在哪裡上班，而是看合約在哪裡簽、僱主是哪裡人（公司看中央管理控制地）、在哪裡出糧，三件事整體衡量。「人在深圳工作所以不用交香港稅」是錯的——如果僱傭在香港，收入原則上全數課稅。

* [Flashcard 2]
  - Front (Question in English): An employee with a non-Hong Kong employment visited Hong Kong on business for exactly 60 days in the YA (counting the arrival day and departure day as 2 separate days). Is his employment income exempt? What if it were 61 days?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - 60 days or less: EXEMPT under s.8(1B) — services rendered in Hong Kong during visits not exceeding a total of 60 days in the YA are disregarded.
      - 61 days: the exemption FAILS entirely; income attributable to services rendered in Hong Kong is chargeable under s.8(1A)(a) on the time apportionment basis — there is NO "first 60 days free" allowance.
      - Counting rule: part of a day counts as one day; both arrival and departure days are included (IRBRD D20/00, D40/07); training, attending conferences or reporting work progress in HK count as rendering services.
    - [中文白話解釋]：60 日規則是「全有或全無」：60 日或以下全免，第 61 日就連前面 60 日都不保，要按在港日數分攤課稅。計日方法好重要：抵港日同離港日各計一日，朝到夜走都計一日，所以凌晨航班離開會白送一日。

* [Flashcard 3]
  - Front (Question in English): Mr. K has a non-Hong Kong employment with annual income of HK$1,825,000 for the YA (365 days). Under the midnight rule he spent 80 business days in Hong Kong; total business days were 335 and he took 30 days' leave. Compute his income attributable to Hong Kong services under time apportionment.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Attributable leave days = 80 ÷ 335 × 30 = 7.16 days.
      - Days in HK = 80 + 7.16 = 87.16 days.
      - Income attributable to HK = 87.16 ÷ 365 × HK$1,825,000 = **HK$435,800**.
      - Midnight rule: arrival day + departure day together count as ONE day (contrast: part-day = one day under the 60-day rule).
    - [中文白話解釋]：時間分攤三步走：先按「在港工作日 ÷ 總工作日 × 假期日」算出歸屬香港的假期日，加回在港日數，再除以全年日數乘年薪。記住分攤計日用 midnight rule（到離兩日只計一日），同 60 日規則的計法剛好相反，考試最鍾意混你。

* [Flashcard 4]
  - Front (Question in English): Ms. L received a salary of HK$800,000 and a bonus of HK$100,000 from her HK employer, who also provided her with a rent-free flat. She paid HK$5,000 of deductible expenses under s.12(1)(a). Compute the assessable rental value. What if she were given a one-room serviced hotel suite instead?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Rental value (flat) = 10% × (HK$800,000 + HK$100,000 − HK$5,000) = 10% × HK$895,000 = **HK$89,500** (ss.9(1)(b), 9(2)).
      - Only s.12(1)(a) outgoings and s.12(1)(b) depreciation allowances are deducted in the 10% base; self-education expenses and share option gains are EXCLUDED.
      - One-room hotel/hostel/boarding house accommodation: 4% × HK$895,000 = **HK$35,800** (s.9(2)(a)); two rooms: 8%.
      - Actual rent paid or refunded by the employer is NOT income (s.9(1A)).
    - [中文白話解釋]：僱主免費俾屋住，不是按市值租金課稅，而是按「收入 × 10%」這個折扣價（酒店一房 4%、兩房 8%），所以房屋福利通常是香港最著數的薪酬包裝。計 10% 基數時只扣 s.12(1)(a)/(b)，進修開支同認股權收益唔好計落去。

* [Flashcard 5]
  - Front (Question in English): Under the s.9(1)(a)(iv) "liability test", when is a payment made by an employer to a third party NOT assessable to the employee? Name the three statutory exceptions in s.9(2A) that are ALWAYS chargeable.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Not chargeable if the payment discharges the EMPLOYER's own sole and primary liability to that person, and it is not a liability for which any person was surety (e.g. utilities contracts signed in the employer's name).
      - Always chargeable (s.9(2A)): (a) benefits convertible into money by the recipient (other than holiday journey); (b) payments in connection with the education of a child of an employee; (c) payments in connection with a holiday journey.
      - Employer discharging the EMPLOYEE's personal liability (e.g. private credit card bills, personal club subscription) = assessable perquisite (David Hardy Glynn v CIR).
    - [中文白話解釋]：核心問題係「邊個先係真正欠債嗰個」：僱主自己欠水電公司（單據寫公司名）→ 僱員無得益、不課稅；僱主幫你找私人卡數 → 當出糧課稅。三樣野無論點都課：可以換錢的福利、仔女教育費、度假旅行。

* [Flashcard 6]
  - Front (Question in English): In YA 2019/20, an employee exercised an option (granted under his HK employment, cost of option HK$4,000) to acquire 10,000 shares at a strike price of HK$8 each when the open market value was HK$15 per share. He sold the shares two months later at HK$20 each. Compute the chargeable gain and state the year of assessment.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Gain under ss.9(1)(d), 9(4)(a) = OMV at exercise − consideration = (10,000 × HK$15) − (10,000 × HK$8) − HK$4,000 = 150,000 − 80,000 − 4,000 = **HK$66,000**, chargeable in YA 2019/20 (year of exercise).
      - The gain is NOTIONAL — no sale is required; the later sale at HK$20 is irrelevant for salaries tax (not a further taxable event under s.9).
      - Assignment/release: gain = consideration for assignment/release − consideration paid for the grant.
    - [中文白話解釋]：認股權係行使嗰年就要交稅，用行使日市值減行使價同買 option 的成本，唔使你真係賣出股票。之後股價升跌同薪俸稅無關，千祈唔好用賣出價去計。呢個係最常見的計算陷阱。

* [Flashcard 7]
  - Front (Question in English): List the six concessionary deductions under Part 4A with their caps/limits, and state which of them are also available to a taxpayer assessed at the standard rate.
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Approved charitable donations (s.26C): money donations ≥ HK$100 in aggregate to s.88 charities/government; cap = 35% of assessable income after s.12(1)(a)/(b) deductions.
      - Elderly residential care expenses (s.26D): up to HK$100,000 per parent/grandparent; HK licensed care home; mutually exclusive with DPA/DGA.
      - Home loan interest (s.26E): up to HK$100,000 per YA for a maximum of 20 YAs; principal HK residence.
      - Mandatory contributions to recognised retirement schemes (s.26G): up to HK$18,000.
      - VHIS qualifying premiums (s.26K): up to HK$8,000 per insured person.
      - QDAP qualifying annuity premiums + TVC (ss.26O, 26S): aggregate cap HK$60,000; TVC deducted first.
      - ALL concessionary deductions are available to both progressive-rate AND standard-rate taxpayers (unlike personal allowances — progressive rate only).
    - [中文白話解釋]：六項特惠扣除連上限背到滾瓜爛熟：捐款 35%、安老院 10 萬、供樓利息 10 萬（最多 20 年）、強積金 1.8 萬、自願醫保每人 8 千、年金加 TVC 合共 6 萬。記住特惠扣除用標準稅率計稅的人都扣得，但個人免稅額就只係累進稅率先用到。

* [Flashcard 8]
  - Front (Question in English): Mr. T's net assessable income for YA 2019/20 is HK$600,000. He made mandatory MPF contributions of HK$18,000 and donated HK$30,000 cash to an approved charity. He is single with no dependants. Compute his salaries tax payable (before any tax reduction).
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Net income = 600,000 − 18,000 − 30,000 = HK$552,000 (donation within 35% cap: 35% × 600,000 = 210,000 ✓).
      - NCI = 552,000 − basic allowance 132,000 = HK$420,000.
      - Progressive rates: 50,000×2% + 50,000×6% + 50,000×10% + 50,000×14% = 16,000; balance 220,000×17% = 37,400 → HK$53,400.
      - Standard rate: 15% × 552,000 = HK$82,800.
      - Tax payable = lower of the two = **HK$53,400** (s.13).
    - [中文白話解釋]：計稅四步：NAI → 扣特惠扣除得 NI → 扣免稅額得 NCI → 累進稅率乘 NCI 同 15% 乘 NI 比較取低。累進稅率記住首 20 萬係 2/6/10/14% 各五萬，固定稅額 HK$16,000，之後全部 17%。
