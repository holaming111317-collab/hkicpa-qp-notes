**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Which of the following persons may apply in writing to the Commissioner of Inland Revenue for an exemption from property tax under s.5(2)(a) of the IRO?

A. An individual who is a permanent resident of Hong Kong and lets out a residential flat in Hong Kong.
B. A sole proprietor carrying on a business in Hong Kong whose rental income from a shop he owns is chargeable to profits tax.
C. A corporation carrying on a trade, profession or business in Hong Kong, where the rental income from the property is included in its profits chargeable to profits tax.
D. A partnership carrying on a business in Hong Kong which owns and lets out an office unit in Hong Kong.

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

Mr Chan owns an office in Hong Kong. He leases it to Company X, which then sub-lets the whole office to Company Y at a higher rent without Mr Chan's consent being relevant to tax. Which of the following statements is **correct**?

A. Company X is chargeable to property tax on the sub-letting rental income because it receives the rent.
B. Company Y is chargeable to property tax as the occupier of the office.
C. Company X's sub-letting rental income is chargeable to profits tax because Company X is not the owner of the property, and sub-letting amounts to a business under DIPN 14.
D. Mr Chan is chargeable to property tax on Company X's sub-letting rental income received from Company Y.

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

For the year of assessment 2018/19, which of the following receipts by the owner of a Hong Kong property should **NOT** be included in the assessable value for property tax purposes?

A. A refundable rental deposit which will be repaid to the tenant in full upon expiry of the lease.
B. A licence fee received for allowing a telecom company to place an advertisement sign on the roof of the building.
C. A management fee paid by the tenant directly to the owner for cleaning services provided by the owner.
D. The cost of repairing a broken window which was borne by the tenant and not repaid by the owner.

**Question 4 (Calculation)**

Grace solely owns a flat in Hong Kong and let it out under a 3-year lease commencing on 1 July 2018. The terms and events are as follows:

- Monthly rent of HK$18,000, payable in advance on the first day of each month.
- A rent-free period of one month was granted at the beginning of the lease (July 2018).
- A lump sum premium of HK$72,000 was paid by the tenant on 1 July 2018.
- Rates of HK$3,000 per quarter were agreed and paid by Grace for the three quarters falling within the lease period during the year of assessment 2018/19.
- On 1 December 2018, the tenant paid HK$4,000 to repair a window broken by a typhoon. Grace refused to repay the tenant.
- Grace also paid building management fees of HK$1,200 per month to the management company.

Calculate Grace's **property tax payable** for the year of assessment 2018/19. (Ignore provisional property tax and tax reduction.)

**Question 5 (Calculation)**

The net assessable value of Mr Lee's Hong Kong property for the year of assessment 2018/19 has been agreed at HK$180,000. Provisional property tax of HK$24,000 was charged for the year of assessment 2018/19 (based on the preceding year) and has been paid.

Calculate the **total property tax payable** under the 2018/19 notice of assessment (i.e. the balance of final tax for 2018/19 plus the provisional property tax for 2019/20).
