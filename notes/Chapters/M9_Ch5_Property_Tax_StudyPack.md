# M9 Property Tax — Study Pack (Week 7)

## Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

### 1. 核心知識架構

- **徵稅範圍 (Scope of charge, s.5(1) IRO)**：物業稅按「標準稅率」(standard rate, 15%，自 2011/12 課稅年度起）就座落香港的土地及/或建築物之「應評稅淨值」(Net Assessable Value, NAV) 向每位「業主」(owner) 徵收；業主的「居留身份」(residence status) 完全無關，非居民業主同樣要交稅。
- **逐項物業獨立評稅 (Separate assessment per property)**：每個在《差餉條例》(Rating Ordinance) 下獨立評定差餉的物業分開計稅，不可合併；只有「聯名業權」(joint ownership) 才聯合評稅（例如夫婦聯名物業按 50/50 分攤）。
- **應評稅值的組成 (Assessable Value, s.5B)**：以「權責發生制」(accruals basis) 計算業主就「使用權」(right of use) 應收的金錢或金錢等值代價 (consideration in money or money's worth)，包括租金（未收亦計）、許可使用費（如車位費）、傢俬使用費、付予業主的管理費、不可退還按金、租客承擔的業主開支、整筆頂手費 (lump sum premium, s.5B(4)) 及追回租金 (rent recovered, s.7C(2))。
- **可扣減項目 (Allowable deductions)**：只有三項 — (i) 無法收回的租金 (irrecoverable rent, s.7C(1))；(ii) 業主繳付的差餉 (rates, s.5(1A)(b)(i))；(iii) 20% 法定扣除額 (20% statutory deduction, s.5(1)(b)(ii))。實際開支（維修、管理費、按揭利息、地稅）一律不另扣。
- **公司與利得稅的互動 (Corporation–profits tax interface)**：公司出租香港物業屬經營業務 (DIPN 14)，租金須課利得稅 (s.14)；為免雙重徵稅，公司可書面申請豁免物業稅 (s.5(2)(a))，否則已繳物業稅可抵銷利得稅 (s.25)。

### 2. 必背考點清單

| 考點 | 規則（英文術語＋法例依據） |
|---|---|
| 徵稅基礎 | Property tax is charged on every **owner** of any land or buildings, or land and buildings, situated in Hong Kong, at the **standard rate of 15%** on the **net assessable value** (s.5(1)). |
| 課稅年度基期 | Basis period: **1 April – 31 March** of the following year (e.g. YA 2018/19 = 1/4/2018–31/3/2019). |
| 「Owner」定義 (s.2(1)) | Includes: person holding land/buildings directly from the Government; beneficial owner; tenant for life; **mortgagor**; **mortgagee in possession**; person with adverse title receiving rent; person paying a co-operative society to purchase property; holder subject to a ground rent/annual charge; corporation registered under s.8 of the Building Management Ordinance (Cap.344) (common parts); executor of an owner's estate. |
| 非居民業主 | Residence status is **irrelevant**; a non-resident owner of HK property is chargeable. Property outside HK is **not** within scope. |
| Assessable Value (s.5B(2)) | Consideration, in money or money's worth, payable to the owner for the right of use; accounted for on the **accruals basis** — outstanding (unpaid) rent is still assessable; s.5B(6) includes sums payable for services/benefits connected with the right of use (e.g. management fee paid **to the owner**). |
| Lump Sum Premium (s.5B(4); DIPNs 4 & 14) | Premium for a right of use exceeding one year of assessment is deemed paid in equal monthly instalments over the **lease period or three years, whichever is shorter**; spread includes any rent-free period. |
| Non-refundable Deposit | A refundable rental deposit is **not** assessable; a contractually non-returnable deposit is treated as a **premium on lease** (DIPN 4) and is taxable. |
| Owner's Expenditure Borne by Tenant | Owner's expenses (e.g. repairs) actually **borne by the tenant** (owner does not repay) are assessable; if the owner repays directly or by agreed rent adjustment, not assessable. |
| Rent Recovered (s.7C(2)) | Recovery of rent previously deducted as irrecoverable is assessable **in the year of recovery**. |
| Irrecoverable Rent (s.7C(1)) | Deductible in the year the rent is **proved to the satisfaction of the assessor** to be irrecoverable (e.g. tenant bankrupt or untraceable) with documentary evidence; merely unpaid rent is **not** deductible. Any **excess is carried backward** to the immediate preceding year (s.7C(3)). |
| Rates (s.5(1A)(b)(i)) | Deductible only if **agreed and paid by the owner** during the lease period. **Government rent** is **never** deductible. |
| 20% Statutory Deduction (s.5(1)(b)(ii)) | 20% × (assessable value − rates paid by owner); deemed to cover **all** repairs and outgoings — actual repairs, management fees, mortgage loan interest are disregarded. |
| Corporation exemption (s.5(2)(a)) | Only **corporations** (not individuals, sole proprietors or partnerships) carrying on a trade, profession or business in HK may apply **in writing** to the CIR for exemption from property tax, per property; once granted, continues until circumstances change (notify change within 30 days, s.5(2)(c)). |
| Set-off (s.25) | If no exemption is applied, property tax paid is set off against the corporation's **profits tax** for the same YA, and any excess is **refunded**, provided the property income forms part of chargeable business profits or the property is used to produce chargeable profits. |
| Sub-letting (DIPN 14) | Letting and sub-letting by a corporation, and sub-letting by any non-corporate person, amounts to a **business** — rental receipts are chargeable to **profits tax (s.14)**, not property tax (a sub-lessor is not the owner). |
| Provisional Property Tax (s.63L) | Payable at the standard rate on the NAV of the **preceding year**, in **two instalments** (normally November and the following April). Total payable = (final tax − provisional tax charged) + provisional tax for the next year. |
| Owner's Obligations (DIPN 14) | s.51(1): file return (normally within 1 month); s.51(2): notify chargeability within **4 months** after year end if no return received; s.51(6): notify cessation of ownership within **1 month**; s.51(8): notify change of address within **1 month**; s.51D: keep rental records in English or Chinese for **not less than 7 years**; s.5(2)(c): notify change in exemption status within **30 days**; s.56A: each joint/co-owner is **fully responsible** as if sole owner. |

**計算公式 (Property tax computation template per DIPN 14):**

```
Considerations received (rent receivable, licence fees, furniture fee,
  management fee paid to owner, non-refundable deposit,
  owner's expenditure borne by tenant, lump sum premium)      A
Add: Rent recovered (s.7C(2))                                  B
Less: Irrecoverable rent (s.7C(1))                            (C)
Assessable Value                                               D = A + B − C
Less: Rates paid by owner (s.5(1A)(b)(i))                     (E)
                                                               F = D − E
Less: 20% statutory deduction (F × 20%)                       (G)
Net Assessable Value (NAV)                                     H = F − G
Property tax payable = NAV × 15%
```

### 3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

**對比一：Refundable Rental Deposit vs Lump Sum Premium（可退還按金 vs 頂手費）**

| Refundable Rental Deposit | Lump Sum Premium |
|---|---|
| 租約期滿時一般全數退還，只用作抵銷欠租或賠償損毁 | 租約終止時 **不可退還** (non-refundable) 給租客 |
| **不屬** assessable value | **屬** assessable value，按 s.5B(4) 在租期或三年（取較短者）內按月平均攤分 |
| 例子：租約訂明 HK$30,000 按金期滿退還 → 不計稅 | 例子：簽 4 年租約收 HK$90,000 premium → 分 36 個月攤，2017/18 佔 6 個月 = HK$90,000 × 6/36 = HK$15,000 |

**對比二：Outstanding (Unpaid) Rent vs Irrecoverable Rent（欠租 vs 壞賬租金）**

| Outstanding / Unpaid Rent | Irrecoverable Rent |
|---|---|
| 只是延遲付款，假設租客最終會繳付 | 已「令評稅主任信納」(proved to the satisfaction of the assessor) 無法收回（如租客破產或失蹤） |
| 按 accruals basis **仍須計入** assessable value，不得扣減 | 在**證實無法收回的課稅年度**扣減 (s.7C(1))；若超出當年應評稅值，餘額**回撥上一個年度**扣減 (s.7C(3)) |
| 例子：租客欠 2018 年 1–3 月租金，承諾數月內清還 → 2017/18 照計租金收入，無扣減 | 例子：租客 2018 年 7 月 31 日宣佈破產 → 全部欠租在 **2018/19** 年度才可扣，不是在欠租發生的 2017/18 |

**考官報告指出**：考生常犯錯誤是把「未收租金」當作壞賬即時扣減，以及把 20% 法定扣除額錯誤地按「總租金收入」而非「扣減差餉及壞賬後的餘額」計算 — 計算題落筆前務必分清扣除次序：先 irrecoverable rent → 再 rates → 最後 20%。

**考試貼士**：見到管理費 (management fee) 題目，先問「錢付給誰」— 付予**業主**的管理費屬 assessable value；付予**第三方管理公司**（即使經業主轉交）則不計入。見到差餉 (rates) 先問「誰付」— 只有**業主繳付**的差餉才可扣。

## Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

* [Flashcard 1]
  - Front (Question in English): Who is chargeable to property tax, and at what rate and on what base is property tax computed?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Charged for each year of assessment on every **owner** of any land or buildings, or land and buildings **situated in Hong Kong** (s.5(1) IRO).
      - Computed at the **standard rate of 15%** on the **net assessable value (NAV)** of the property.
      - Residence status of the owner is **irrelevant**; each separately rated property is assessed separately.
      - "Owner" (s.2(1)) includes beneficial owner, mortgagor, mortgagee in possession, tenant for life, executor, etc.
    - [中文白話解釋]：只要你是香港物業的業主，不論你住哪裏、是否香港居民，都要就該物業的應評稅淨值按 15% 標準稅率交物業稅；而且每個獨立評差餉的物業分開計算，不能合併報稅。

* [Flashcard 2]
  - Front (Question in English): Which items must be included in the assessable value of a property for property tax purposes?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Rent received **and receivable** (accruals basis — outstanding rent is still assessable).
      - Payments for the right of use of premises under licence (e.g. car park fee, advertisement on wall/roof).
      - Payment for the right of use of **furniture** in the premises.
      - **Management fee paid to the owner** (s.5B(6)); but not fees payable by the tenant to a third-party management company.
      - **Non-refundable deposit** (treated as premium per DIPN 4).
      - **Owner's expenditure borne by the tenant** (e.g. repairs paid by tenant and not repaid by owner).
      - **Lump sum premium** (s.5B(4)) and **rent recovered** (s.7C(2)).
    - [中文白話解釋]：應評稅值用權責發生制計算，凡因「使用權」而應付給業主的代價（不論金錢或實物利益）都要計入，包括欠租在內；口訣是「付給業主的都計，付給第三方的（如管理公司）不計，可退還按金不計」。

* [Flashcard 3]
  - Front (Question in English): A tenant pays a lump sum premium of HK$144,000 for a 2-year lease commencing 1 June 2018. How much premium is assessable in the year of assessment 2018/19?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Under s.5B(4), the premium is deemed paid in equal monthly instalments over the lease period (24 months) or three years, **whichever is shorter** → spread over **24 months**.
      - Months falling in YA 2018/19: 1 June 2018 – 31 March 2019 = **10 months** (including any rent-free period).
      - Assessable premium = HK$144,000 × 10/24 = **HK$60,000**.
    - [中文白話解釋]：頂手費唔係一次過喺收錢嗰年計晒，而係按租期或三年（取較短者）逐月攤分。兩年租約就分 24 個月；2018/19 課稅年度覆蓋租約起計 10 個月，所以該年計 60,000 元，剩餘 84,000 元落入之後兩個課稅年度。

* [Flashcard 4]
  - Front (Question in English): When is irrecoverable rent deductible, and what happens if the amount exceeds the assessable value of that year?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Deductible in the year of assessment in which the rent is **proved to the satisfaction of the assessor** to be irrecoverable (s.7C(1)) — e.g. tenant bankrupt or untraceable; documentary evidence required.
      - Merely **unpaid/outstanding** rent is NOT deductible.
      - Any **excess** is **carried backward** and deducted from the assessable value of that property in the **immediate preceding year** (s.7C(3)).
      - If previously deducted rent is later recovered, the recovery is assessable **in the year of recovery** (s.7C(2)).
    - [中文白話解釋]：欠租唔等於壞賬 — 一定要有證據（例如租客破產或失聯）令評稅主任信納先可以扣，扣嘅年度係「證實收唔返」嗰年，唔係欠租發生嗰年。如果壞賬多過當年收入，多出嚟嘅部分回撥上一個年度扣；之後如果真係收返，就喺收返嗰年重新計返落應評稅值。

* [Flashcard 5]
  - Front (Question in English): What are the only three deductions allowed in arriving at the net assessable value, and in what order are they applied?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - 1. **Irrecoverable rent** proved irrecoverable in the year (s.7C(1)) — deducted from consideration to arrive at the assessable value.
      - 2. **Rates** agreed and **paid by the owner** during the lease period (s.5(1A)(b)(i)) — government rent is NOT deductible.
      - 3. **20% statutory deduction** (s.5(1)(b)(ii)) on the reduced balance (after deducting irrecoverable rent and rates), deemed to cover all repairs and outgoings.
      - Actual repairs, management fees and mortgage loan interest are **never separately deductible**.
      - Property tax = NAV × **15%**.
    - [中文白話解釋]：淨值計算只有三級扣減：先扣證實收唔返嘅租金，再扣業主自己找嘅差餉（地稅唔扣得！），最後先喺餘額上打八折（20% 法定扣除）。呢 20% 當晒係維修同雜費補貼，所以業主實際使咗幾多錢裝修、供樓利息、管理費，一律唔再另扣。

* [Flashcard 6]
  - Front (Question in English): Mr Wu let his Wanchai property at HK$25,000 per month. The tenant failed to pay rent from 1 February 2018 and settled all arrears on 15 May 2018. Rates are paid by Mr Wu. What is the assessable value for YA 2017/18?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Consideration is accounted for on the **accruals basis**: rent receivable for the full 12 months is assessable even though 2 months' rent was only received in May 2018.
      - Assessable value = HK$25,000 × 12 = **HK$300,000** for YA 2017/18.
      - The arrears are merely outstanding, not proved irrecoverable, so no deduction is allowed; and they are not taxed again in 2018/19 when received.
    - [中文白話解釋]：物業稅用權責發生制，唔係收付實現制。雖然有兩個月租金遲咗至 2018 年 5 月先收到，但只要租約涵蓋嗰段期間，2017/18 年度就照計足 12 個月租金，即 300,000 元；而遲收嘅租金在 2018/19 唔會再計一次。

* [Flashcard 7]
  - Front (Question in English): How can a corporation avoid double taxation on Hong Kong rental income that is chargeable to both property tax and profits tax?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - Letting by a corporation amounts to a business (DIPN 14), so rental receipts are chargeable to **profits tax under s.14**.
      - **Option 1 — s.5(2)(a) exemption**: the corporation may apply **in writing** to the CIR for exemption from property tax (per property); available to **corporations only** (not individuals, sole proprietors or partnerships); once granted it continues until circumstances change (notify change within 30 days, s.5(2)(c)).
      - **Option 2 — s.25 set-off**: if no exemption is applied, the property tax paid is **set off against the profits tax** liability for the same year of assessment, and any **excess is refunded**.
    - [中文白話解釋]：公司收租會同時跌入物業稅（因為係業主）同利得稅（因為 DIPN 14 當公司收租係做生意）兩個稅網，所以法例提供兩條出路：一係書面申請豁免物業稅（只有公司先申請得，個人同獨資東主冇份），一係照交物業稅之後喺利得稅度扣返，扣唔晒仲可以退稅，確保同一筆租金只係實際課一次稅。

* [Flashcard 8]
  - Front (Question in English): What are the key filing, notification and record-keeping obligations of a property owner under the IRO?
  - Back (Answer & Explanation):
    - [English Point-form Answer]:
      - **s.51(1)**: file the property tax return within the time limit (normally 1 month from issue).
      - **s.51(2)**: if chargeable but no return received, notify the CIR in writing within **4 months** after the end of the year of assessment.
      - **s.51(6)**: notify cessation of ownership (sale/transfer) within **1 month**.
      - **s.51(8)**: notify change of address within **1 month**.
      - **s.51D**: keep sufficient rental records (lease agreements, duplicate rent receipts) in **English or Chinese** for **not less than 7 years**.
      - **s.5(2)(c)**: an exempted corporation must notify changes in ownership/use within **30 days**.
      - **s.56A**: every joint owner/co-owner is **fully responsible** for all obligations as if sole owner.
    - [中文白話解釋]：業主義務記住幾個時限：交報稅表 1 個月內；收唔到報稅表要自己主動喺課稅年度結束後 4 個月內申報；賣樓或搬地址 1 個月內通知；租務紀錄保留至少 7 年（中文或英文均可，唔使兩份）；聯名業主每個人都要當自己係唯一業主咁承擔全部責任。
