**Question 1 (MCQ)**
Which of the following individuals MAY elect for personal assessment for the year of assessment 2017/18?

A. A 17-year-old individual who ordinarily resides in Hong Kong and whose parents are both alive.

B. An individual who is not ordinarily resident in Hong Kong, is not married, and stayed in Hong Kong for 150 days during the year of assessment 2017/18 and 120 days during 2016/17.

C. A 30-year-old individual who is neither a permanent nor a temporary resident of Hong Kong, but whose spouse is a temporary resident of Hong Kong.

D. An individual holding a Hong Kong permanent identity card who neither ordinarily resides in Hong Kong nor satisfies the temporary resident day tests.

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

C. Mortgage interest on a rental property in excess of that property's net assessable value, carried forward to set off against future rental income.

D. The excess of concessionary deductions over total income, carried forward to future years of assessment.

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

**Question 4 (Calculation)**
Mr Kwan, a single Hong Kong permanent resident, elected for personal assessment for the year of assessment 2017/18. His details:
- Net assessable income from employment (after self-education expenses): HK$400,000
- Rental income from a Hong Kong property: HK$300,000 (no rates paid by him)
- Mortgage interest paid on the loan used to purchase that rental property: HK$260,000
- Loss from his sole-proprietorship business for the year: HK$150,000
- Approved charitable donations (cash): HK$50,000
- Mandatory MPF contributions: HK$18,000

Compute Mr Kwan's **tax payable under personal assessment** for 2017/18. Ignore the one-off tax reduction. (State a single amount in HK$.)

**Question 5 (Calculation)**

Compute the couple's **total tax payable under personal assessment** for 2017/18. Ignore the one-off tax reduction. (State a single amount in HK$.)
