# M9 Stamp Duty — Phase 3 Quiz (100% OTQ, ALL IN ENGLISH)

限時 12.5 分鐘・閉卷・請用英文作答（MCQ 寫選項字母；計算題寫數值及單位）

---

**Question 1 (MCQ)**

Which of the following instruments is NOT chargeable with stamp duty under the Stamp Duty Ordinance (Cap. 117)?

A. A lease of a commercial property in Sha Tin for a term of 10 years at a rent of HK$200,000 per annum.
B. An agreement for sale and purchase of a residential property in Lai Chi Kok.
C. A mortgage over a residential property in Repulse Bay.
D. The issue of a Hong Kong bearer instrument.

---

**Question 2 (MCQ)**

Ms Wong, a Hong Kong permanent resident acting on her own behalf, is the co-owner (with her husband) of a residential property in Ho Man Tin. In June 2024, she signs an agreement for sale to buy a second residential property in Happy Valley for HK$8,000,000 (equal to its market value). The vendor acquired that property 10 years ago. Which of the following statements correctly describes the stamp duty payable on the agreement for sale?

A. AVD is chargeable at Scale 2 rates because Ms Wong is a Hong Kong permanent resident.
B. BSD at 15% is payable because Ms Wong already owns another residential property in Hong Kong.
C. AVD is chargeable at 15% under Scale 1 Part 1, and no BSD is payable.
D. SSD is payable by Ms Wong because this is her second residential property.

---

**Question 3 (MCQ)**

Which of the following statements concerning the stamping of transactions in Hong Kong stock is CORRECT?

A. The issue of new shares by a company incorporated in Hong Kong attracts ad valorem stamp duty at 0.26%.
B. A gift of shares listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange is not dutiable because there is no consideration.
C. Where contract notes for the sale and purchase of Hong Kong stock are duly stamped with ad valorem duty, the instrument of transfer bears a fixed duty of HK$5.
D. The transfer of a bond denominated in Japanese Yen, the transfer of which is required to be registered in Hong Kong, is chargeable to stamp duty as a transfer of Hong Kong stock.

**Question 4 (Calculation)**

On 1 December 2023, Mr Kwan, a Singapore citizen who is not a Hong Kong permanent resident, enters into an agreement for sale and purchase to acquire a residential flat in Mid-Levels for HK$6,000,000. The market value of the flat on that date is HK$6,500,000. The vendor acquired the flat on 1 June 2022. Compute the TOTAL stamp duty (AVD + SSD + BSD, as applicable) payable on the agreement for sale and purchase. State the amount of each duty separately and the total. (State your answers in HK$.)

**Question 5 (Calculation)**

On 1 March 2024, a tenant signs a lease of a shop in Mongkok for a term of 2 years at a monthly rent of HK$28,500. The lease also provides for key money (premium) of HK$600,000 and a refundable deposit of HK$57,000. Compute the total ad valorem stamp duty payable on the lease. (State your answer in HK$.)
