**Question 1 (Fill-in) — 抽考 s.60(1)（你五戰先過關嘅位）**

In January 2027, the IRD discovers that Delta Ltd overstated its deductions for the year of assessment 2020/21 (year of assessment ended 31 March 2021). There is no fraud or wilful evasion. State (i) the latest date by which the IRD may validly raise an additional assessment, (ii) whether the January 2027 discovery is still within time, and (iii) the governing section.

**Question 2 (MCQ) — 抽考 capital vs revenue（你四戰嘅位）**

Which of the following receipts is a REVENUE receipt?

A. Compensation received for the cancellation of the company's sole manufacturing agreement, which constituted the entire framework of its business.
B. Compensation received from an insurer for the destruction of trading stock in a warehouse fire.
C. Proceeds from the sale of the company's head office building.
D. A lump sum received on the surrender of a patent owned by the company.

**Question 3 (Fill-in) — 抽考 rental value + 條文（你五戰嘅位）**

Ms D's employer provided her with a rent-free flat (not a hotel, hostel or boarding house) for the whole year of assessment. Her income from that employer was a salary of HK$900,000 plus a bonus of HK$100,000 (before any deductions and allowances). State the **rental value** to be included in her assessable income AND the governing section **with subsection**.

**Question 4 (Fill-in) — 抽考 SSD 三檔（10/5 錯過嘅位）**

Mr E acquired a residential property on 1 March 2023 and sold it on 1 November 2023 (holding period: 8 months). The sale price was HK$5,000,000; the market value at the date of sale was HK$5,200,000. State the SSD rate applicable and the amount of SSD payable.

**Question 5 (Fill-in) — 抽考 consignment tax（Ch8 高頻位）**

Gross sale proceeds from Hong Kong customers are HK$5,000,000 and from Mainland customers HK$3,000,000. The Commissioner has not approved any reduced rate. State the consignment tax payable under s.20A(3) and the rate used.
