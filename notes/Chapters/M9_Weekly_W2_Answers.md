# M9 保溫卷 W2 — Model Answers & Explanations

**Question 1 — Correct answers: (i) 31 March 2026 (ii) No (iii) s.60(1) IRO**
Under **s.60(1)**, an additional assessment must be raised within **6 years after the end of the year of assessment** in which the income was omitted (10 years only where there is fraud or wilful evasion). YA 2019/20 ended on 31 March 2020 → deadline = 31 March 2026. An assessment raised on 30 April 2026 is **out of time** and invalid.
（W1 考嘅係 within-time；今次反方向——2026 年 4 月先發現 2019/20 嘅漏報，已經過咗限期。記住條文係 s.60(1)，唔好再估。）

**Question 2 — Correct answer: b**
Under **DIPN 21** (and s.15(1)(b) deeming provisions), service fees paid to a non-resident for services performed in Hong Kong are deemed taxable, with **2/3 of the gross fee deemed as assessable profits**: 1,500,000 × 2/3 = 1,000,000. Mr Smith is an **unincorporated** person → two-tiered rate **7.5%** on the first HK$2,000,000: 1,000,000 × 7.5% = **HK$75,000**.
（c) 82,500 = 8.25% 係 corporation 嘅稅率——睇清楚主體係 individual；(d) 123,750 = 用咗全額 1,500,000 × 8.25%。）

**Question 3 — Correct answer: HK$480,000**
Net Assessable Value (NAV) = rental income × 80% = 600,000 × 80% = **480,000**.
Under Personal Assessment, mortgage interest is deductible **up to the NAV** — deductible interest = min(500,000, 480,000) = **HK$480,000**. The excess HK$20,000 is lost (DIPN 51 / D51/04).

**Question 4 — Correct answer: HK$600,000 (rate 10%)**
Holding period 20 months falls within the **>12 months and ≤36 months** band → SSD rate = **10%** on the higher of consideration or market value: 6,000,000 × 10% = **HK$600,000**.
（SSD bands: ≤6 months → 20%；>6–12 months → 15%；>12–36 months → 10%；>36 months → 0%。）

**Question 5 — Correct answer: HK$150,000, s.20A(3)**
Under **s.20A(3)**, where a non-resident sells goods on consignment through a Hong Kong agent/branch and actual profits cannot be readily ascertained, the assessable profits are deemed to be **1% of the gross proceeds from sales to Hong Kong customers**: 15,000,000 × 1% = **HK$150,000**.
