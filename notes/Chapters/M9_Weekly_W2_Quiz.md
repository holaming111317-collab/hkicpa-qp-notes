**Question 1** — 帶落題（W1 Q1 錯：日期啱但 within-time 判斷錯＋估錯條文）→ **新情境（相反方向）**
In April 2026, the Inland Revenue Department discovers that Mr Tong omitted rental income of HK$200,000 in his tax return for the year of assessment 2019/20. There is no evidence of fraud or wilful intent to evade tax. The CIR wishes to raise an additional assessment on 30 April 2026.
(i) State the statutory deadline for raising this additional assessment.
(ii) Can the CIR validly raise the additional assessment on 30 April 2026? (Yes / No)
(iii) Cite the governing section of the IRO.

**Question 2 (MCQ)** — DIPN 21 新情境（主體：個人）
HK Ltd pays a gross service fee of HK$1,500,000 to Mr Smith, a UK-resident individual consultant, for services performed entirely in Hong Kong. Mr Smith has no permanent establishment in Hong Kong.
What is the amount of Hong Kong profits tax payable in respect of this fee?
a) HK$0 — services fees paid to a non-resident are not taxable
b) HK$75,000
c) HK$82,500
d) HK$123,750

**Question 3** — NAV cap 新情境（數字唔同）
Ms Lee is a sole owner of a property let for the whole year of assessment. Her rental income is HK$600,000 and she paid mortgage interest of HK$500,000. She elects Personal Assessment.
Calculate the amount of mortgage interest deductible under Personal Assessment. Show your workings.

**Question 4** — SSD 新情境（持有期：20 個月）
Mr Wong purchased a residential property on 1 February 2025 and sold it on 30 September 2026 (held for 20 months) for HK$6,000,000.
Calculate the Special Stamp Duty (SSD) payable, if any. Show your workings and state the applicable rate.

**Question 5** — Consignment 新情境（數字唔同）
UK Co, a non-resident company, sells goods on consignment through its Hong Kong branch. During the year of assessment, the branch's gross proceeds from sales to Hong Kong customers amount to HK$15,000,000. UK Co's actual profits cannot be readily ascertained.
Calculate the Hong Kong profits tax liability of UK Co. Cite the governing provision.
