**Question 1** — 帶落題（W2 Q5 錯：用咗 10%＋估錯 s.15(5)）→ **新情境**
US Co, a non-resident company, sells goods on consignment through its Hong Kong branch. During the year of assessment, the branch's gross proceeds from sales to Hong Kong customers amount to HK$8,000,000. US Co's actual profits cannot be readily ascertained.
Calculate the Hong Kong profits tax liability of US Co. Cite the governing provision.

**Question 2 (MCQ)** — Rental value 輪轉（s.9(2)）
Mr Chan is employed with an annual salary of HK$720,000. His employer provides him with a rent-free flat and pays the monthly rent of HK$25,000 directly to the landlord.
What is the rental value to be included in Mr Chan's assessable income for the year?
a) HK$300,000 — the actual rent paid by the employer
b) HK$72,000
c) HK$25,000
d) HK$0 — the benefit is not convertible into cash

**Question 3 (MCQ)** — AVD/BSD 輪轉
Ms Wong, a Hong Kong permanent resident, purchases her first residential property (she owns no other residential property) for HK$8,000,000. Which of the following correctly states her stamp duty position?
a) AVD at the flat rate of 15% plus BSD at 15%
b) AVD at Scale 2 rates only; BSD is not payable
c) AVD at Scale 2 rates plus BSD at 15%
d) No stamp duty is payable as she is a first-time buyer

**Question 4 (MCQ)** — Capital vs revenue 輪轉
Trading Ltd receives HK$2,000,000 compensation for the termination of its sole distribution agreement, which represented substantially the whole of its business. The compensation is:
a) A revenue receipt — compensation for loss of income is always taxable
b) A capital receipt — it compensates for the sterilisation of the company's entire profit-making structure
c) A revenue receipt — it was received in the ordinary course of trading
d) 50% capital and 50% revenue, apportioned on a just and reasonable basis

**Question 5** — MPF 輪轉（s.26G）
Mr Ng is an employee with a monthly salary of HK$30,000. Calculate his maximum deductible mandatory MPF contribution for salaries tax purposes for the year. Cite the governing section.
