**Question 1** — 帶落題（W3 Q2 錯：揀咗 actual rent）→ **新情境（計算題，唔係 MCQ——逼你計）**
Ms Ho is employed with an annual salary of HK$600,000. Her employer provides her with a rent-free flat and pays the monthly rent of HK$20,000 directly to the landlord.
Calculate the rental value to be included in Ms Ho's assessable income for the year. Cite the governing section.

**Question 2 (MCQ)** — 帶落題（W3 Q4 錯）→ **新情境（相反方向）**
Agency Ltd acts as distributor under five separate distribution agreements. One supplier terminates its agreement and pays Agency Ltd HK$500,000 compensation. The remaining four agreements continue, and the termination does not significantly affect the company's business structure. The compensation is:
a) A capital receipt — compensation for termination is always capital
b) A revenue receipt — it replaces income from one of several ordinary commercial arrangements
c) A capital receipt — it compensates for the sterilisation of the company's entire profit-making structure
d) Not taxable because it is a windfall gain

**Question 3** — 帶落題（W3 Q5 錯：答咗每月數＋錯條文）→ **新情境**

**Question 4** — s.60(1) 輪轉（保溫：上星期先清，唔好畀佢走甩）
In March 2027, the IRD discovers that Mr Cheung omitted income in his return for the year of assessment 2020/21. There is no fraud or wilful evasion.
(i) State the statutory deadline for raising an additional assessment.
(ii) Is the discovery within the time limit? (Yes / No)
(iii) Cite the governing section.

**Question 5 (MCQ)** — DIPN 21 輪轉（主體：公司）

What is the Hong Kong profits tax payable in respect of this fee?
a) HK$0
b) HK$120,000
c) HK$132,000
d) HK$198,000
