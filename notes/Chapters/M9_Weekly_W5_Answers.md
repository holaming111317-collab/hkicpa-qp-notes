# M9 保溫卷 W5 — Model Answers & Explanations

**Question 1 — Correct answer: HK$165,000, DIPN 21 (deeming provisions)**
Deemed assessable profits = **2/3** of the gross fee = 3,000,000 × 2/3 = 2,000,000. US Inc is a **corporation** → two-tiered rate **8.25%**: 2,000,000 × 8.25% = **HK$165,000**.
（W4 你誤讀咗 "performed entirely in Hong Kong"——意思係**全部服務喺香港做**，所以課稅；個人/unincorporated 用 7.5%，公司用 8.25%。）

**Question 2 — Correct answer: HK$2,000,000 (rate 20%)**
Holding period ≈ 3.5 months → **≤6 months** band → SSD rate **20%** on the higher of consideration or market value: 10,000,000 × 20% = **HK$2,000,000**.
（SSD bands: ≤6m → 20%；>6–12m → 15%；>12–36m → 10%；>36m → 0%。）

**Question 3 — Correct answer: c**
Issue of **new** shares is **not** dutiable (no transfer of existing stock) — (a) wrong. A **gift** of existing shares IS dutiable at **market value** — (b) wrong, (c) correct. An IoT bears a **fixed HK$5** duty (the ad valorem duty is charged on the bought/sold contract notes) — (d) wrong.

**Question 4 — Correct answer: HK$175,000, s.26C**
Approved donations are deductible up to **35% of assessable income**: 500,000 × 35% = **HK$175,000** (donations of 200,000 exceed the cap, so deductible = 175,000).

**Question 5 — Correct answer: b**
Under **s.8(1A)(b)(ii)**, income from employment is exempt where **all** services are rendered outside Hong Kong during the year (visits for holidays do not count as services). Full exemption applies.
