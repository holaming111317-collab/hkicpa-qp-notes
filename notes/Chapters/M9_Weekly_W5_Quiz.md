**Question 1** — 帶落題（W4 Q5 錯：睇錯「performed entirely in Hong Kong」）→ **新情境（計算題）**
HK Ltd pays a gross service fee of HK$3,000,000 to US Inc, a US-resident **company**, for management services **performed entirely in Hong Kong**. US Inc has no permanent establishment in Hong Kong.
Calculate the Hong Kong profits tax payable in respect of this fee. Cite the governing authority.

**Question 2** — SSD 輪轉（計算題）

**Question 3 (MCQ)** — 股票印花稅輪轉

d) An instrument of transfer (IoT) for a sale of shares bears ad valorem duty based on the share price

**Question 4** — Donations 輪轉（s.26C，計算題）

b) Exempt — all of his services were rendered outside Hong Kong
