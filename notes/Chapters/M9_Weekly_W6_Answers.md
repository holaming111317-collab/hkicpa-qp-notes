# M9 保溫卷 W6 — Model Answers & Explanations

**Question 1 — Correct answers: (i) 31 March 2032 (ii) Yes (iii) s.60(1) proviso**
Where there is **fraud or wilful evasion**, the time limit is extended to **10 years** after the end of the year of assessment (s.60(1) proviso). YA 2021/22 ended 31 March 2022 → deadline = **31 March 2032**. Discovery in June 2026 is well within time.
（普通漏報 = 6 年；**fraud / wilful evasion = 10 年**——題目出現 "deliberately / wilful intent to evade" 就要反射十年檔。）

**Question 2 — Correct answer: HK$96,000, s.9(2)**
Rental value = **10% of income from the employer** = 960,000 × 10% = **HK$96,000** (s.9(2)). The actual rent of 360,000 paid by the employer is irrelevant.

**Question 3 — Correct answer: HK$60,000, s.20A(3)**
Under **s.20A(3)**, where actual profits cannot be readily ascertained, assessable profits are deemed to be **1% of the gross proceeds from sales to Hong Kong customers**: 6,000,000 × 1% = **HK$60,000**.

**Question 4 — Correct answer: c**
Held ~18 months → **>12–36 months** band → SSD rate **10%**: 5,000,000 × 10% = **HK$500,000**.
（Bands: ≤6m → 20%；>6–12m → 15%；>12–36m → 10%；>36m → 0%。）

**Question 5 — Correct answer: b**
Deemed assessable profits = 2/3 × 900,000 = 600,000. Mr Bond is an **individual (unincorporated)** → two-tiered rate **7.5%**: 600,000 × 7.5% = **HK$45,000**.
（(c) 49,500 = 8.25% 係公司率；(d) 74,250 = 全額 900,000 × 8.25%。）
