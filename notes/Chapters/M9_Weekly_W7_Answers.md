# M9 保溫卷 W7 — Model Answers & Explanations

**Question 1 — Correct answers: (i) 31 March 2025 (ii) No (iii) s.60(1) proviso**
Fraud / wilful evasion → **10-year** limit from the END of the YA (s.60(1) proviso). YA 2014/15 ended 31 March 2015 → deadline = **31 March 2025**. Discovery in June 2026 is **out of time** — the additional assessment is time-barred.
（口訣：普通 6 年、fraud 10 年，全部由該 YA **完結**起計。W6 你嘅錯：見到 fraud 都係用咗 6 年。）

**Question 2 — Correct answer: HK$84,000, s.9(2)**
Rental value = 10% of income from the employer = 840,000 × 10% = **HK$84,000** (**s.9(2)**). Actual rent irrelevant.
（W6 你寫 s.26(G)——s.26G 係 MPF；rental value 係 **s.9(2)**。金額啱條文錯 = 假熟。）

**Question 3 — Correct answers: (a) HK$720,000 (b) HK$720,000**
NAV = 900,000 × 80% = **720,000**. Under PA, mortgage interest is capped at NAV: min(800,000, 720,000) = **HK$720,000**. The excess 80,000 is lost.

**Question 4 — Correct answer: c**
Under **s.5B(4)**, a premium is spread evenly over the lease term: 240,000 ÷ 24 = 10,000/month. In YA 2025/26, 3 months fall within the year (Jan–Mar 2026): 3 × 10,000 = **HK$30,000**.

**Question 5 — Correct answer: b**
The source of an **office** (directorship) is where the company's **central management and control** is exercised. Here that is the UK → the fee is sourced outside Hong Kong and not assessable. Incorporation place and the director's residence are irrelevant.
（對照：employment 來源用 *Goepfert* 三因素；office 用 central management and control。）
