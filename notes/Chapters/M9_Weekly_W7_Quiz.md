**Question 1** — 帶落題（W6 Q1 錯：fraud 用咗六年檔）→ **新情境（out-of-time 變種）**
In June 2026, the IRD discovers that Mr Sit **deliberately** omitted income in his return for the year of assessment **2014/15**, with evidence of wilful intent to evade tax.
(i) State the statutory deadline for raising an additional assessment.
(ii) Is the CIR still in time? (Yes / No)
(iii) Cite the governing provision.

**Question 2** — Rental value 輪轉（計算題；**條文要寫準**）
Ms Yiu is employed with an annual salary of HK$840,000. Her employer provides a rent-free flat and pays the monthly rent of HK$28,000 directly to the landlord.
Calculate the rental value to be included in her assessable income. Cite the governing section.

**Question 3** — NAV 輪轉（計算題）
Mr Fan solely owns a property let for the whole year. Rental income is HK$900,000; mortgage interest paid is HK$800,000. He elects Personal Assessment.
Calculate (a) the Net Assessable Value and (b) the deductible mortgage interest. 

**Question 4 (MCQ)** — Premium 攤分輪轉（s.5B(4)）
On 1 January 2026, Mr Ko grants a 24-month lease of his property and receives a premium of HK$240,000. How much of the premium is assessable in the year of assessment 2025/26?
a) HK$240,000 — the full premium is assessable when received
b) HK$120,000 — half of the premium
c) HK$30,000
d) HK$0 — premiums are capital and not assessable

**Question 5 (MCQ)** — Source rules 輪轉（office）
Mr Cheuk is a director of a company whose central management and control are exercised in the UK. The company is incorporated in Hong Kong. His director's fee of HK$500,000 is:
a) Assessable in Hong Kong — the company is incorporated in Hong Kong
b) Not assessable in Hong Kong — the source of an office is where central management and control is exercised
c) 50% assessable on an apportionment basis
d) Assessable in Hong Kong because he is a Hong Kong resident
