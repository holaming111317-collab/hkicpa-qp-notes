# M9 保溫卷 W8 — Model Answers & Explanations

**Question 1 — Correct answers: (i) 31 March 2026 (ii) Yes (iii) s.60(1)**
No fraud → 6-year limit from the END of YA 2019/20 (ended 31 March 2020) → deadline = **31 March 2026**. An assessment raised **ON the deadline day itself is within time** — "within 6 years after the end of the YA" includes the last day.

**Question 2 — Correct answer: c**
A **non-HKPR** buyer pays **AVD at the Scale 1 Part 1 flat rate of 15% PLUS BSD at 15%**: (12,000,000 × 15%) + (12,000,000 × 15%) = 1,800,000 + 1,800,000 = **HK$3,600,000**. Scale 2 is for HKPR first-time buyers only; BSD exemption requires HKPR status acting on own behalf.

**Question 3 — Correct answer: HK$1,005**
- Sold contract note: ad valorem duty 0.1% × 1,000,000 = **HK$1,000**（2023 年起稅率 0.1%）
- Instrument of transfer (IoT): **fixed HK$5**
- Seller's total = 1,000 + 5 = **HK$1,005**
（買家同樣畀 1,000 + 5。Issue of new shares 唔課；gift 按市值課。）

**Question 4 — Correct answer: b**
Payment for varying an **ordinary trading contract in the normal course of business** is a **revenue receipt** — it is part of normal trading operations, not the sterilisation of the profit-making structure (contrast: termination of a sole agreement representing the whole business = capital).

**Question 5 — Correct answer: HK$960,000**
Time apportionment under **s.8(1A)(a)**: assessable income = 1,200,000 × (365 − 73) ÷ 365 = 1,200,000 × 292/365 = **HK$960,000**.
（服務日數喺香港以外嘅部分剔除；呢條同 s.8(1A)(b)(ii) 全離港豁免唔同——佢唔係全部服務離港，所以只能 apportion。）
