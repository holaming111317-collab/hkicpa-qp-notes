**Question 1** — s.60(1) 輪轉（**邊界日新考法**）
The IRD raises an additional assessment on Mr Lau on **31 March 2026** for income omitted from his return for the year of assessment **2019/20**. There is no fraud or wilful evasion.
(i) State the statutory deadline for raising this additional assessment.
(ii) Was the additional assessment raised in time? (Yes / No)
(iii) Cite the governing section.

**Question 2 (MCQ)** — AVD/BSD 輪轉（非 HKPR）
Mr Smith, who is **not** a Hong Kong permanent resident, purchases a residential property in Hong Kong for HK$12,000,000. His total stamp duty exposure (AVD + BSD) is:
a) AVD at Scale 2 rates only
b) AVD at the flat rate of 15% only
c) AVD at the flat rate of 15% plus BSD at 15% — total HK$3,600,000
d) BSD at 15% only, because he is a non-HKPR

**Question 3** — 股票轉讓印花稅輪轉（計算題）
Mr Ting sells Hong Kong shares for a consideration of HK$1,000,000. Calculate the **total stamp duty payable by Mr Ting as the seller** (contract note + instrument of transfer). State the rates/amounts.

**Question 4 (MCQ)** — Capital vs revenue 輪轉
Delta Ltd receives HK$800,000 from a supplier for agreeing to vary the delivery terms of an ordinary supply contract in the normal course of its trading operations. The receipt is:
a) Capital — it relates to a contract
b) Revenue — it arises from an ordinary trading arrangement in the normal course of business
c) Capital — it is a one-off payment
d) Not taxable — it is a windfall

**Question 5** — Time apportionment 輪轉（計算題）
Mr Cheung has a Hong Kong employment with annual income of HK$1,200,000. During the year of assessment (365 days), he rendered services outside Hong Kong for **73 days**. He does not qualify for full exemption.
Calculate his assessable income under time apportionment.
