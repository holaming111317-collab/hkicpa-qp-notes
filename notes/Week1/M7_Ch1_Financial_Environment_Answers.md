## 【標準答案 Model Answers — 完成所有題目後先好睇！】

**Question 1 — Answer: B**
- Model answer: Profit maximisation treats the two projects as identical because both produce the same accounting profit of HK$2.0 million. However, one of the key drawbacks of profit maximisation is that it does not distinguish between receiving a dollar today and receiving a dollar in the future — it ignores the time value of money. A correct financial decision framework (maximising share price/shareholders' wealth by ensuring the total present value of cash inflows exceeds cash outflows) would prefer Project B because of its earlier cash collection. Options A and C are wrong because profit maximisation does NOT capture the time value of money; D is wrong because nothing in profit maximisation would lead to rejecting profitable projects.
- 【解題思路】: 兩個項目嘅會計利潤一樣（都係 HK$2.0 million），所以以 profit maximisation 為唯一目標嘅話，公司會對兩者無差異（indifferent）——因為呢個目標根本睇唔到現金收取嘅時間先後。呢個正係 Learning Pack 指出嘅第三個缺點：ignores the time value of money。A 錯喺話 profit maximisation「captures」時間價值；C 錯喺兩個項目會計利潤確認時間相同；D 更離譜，利潤最大化唔會拒絕有利潤嘅項目。正確框架（shareholder wealth maximisation）先會因為 Project B 早收錢而揀 B。

**Question 2 — Answer: C**
- Model answer: Money markets trade short-term debt instruments with maturities of LESS than one year; a nine-month corporate bond is therefore a money market instrument. Option A is wrong because the Hong Kong Stock Exchange is a secondary market — trades occur amongst investors and the original issuer receives no money. Option B is wrong because the DJIA is a price-weighted index of 30 blue-chip companies (excluding transportation and utilities); the market-cap-weighted 500-company index is the S&P 500. Option D is wrong because the description fits a BROKER, not a dealer; dealers make markets from their own inventory and bear risk.
- 【解題思路】: 呢題考你對各個市場概念嘅精準記憶。C 正確因為 money market 嘅定義係「maturities of less than one year」嘅債務工具，九個月合資格。A 係經典陷阱——二手市場交易發行公司收唔到錢（二手車比喻）。B 將 DJIA 同 S&P 500 嘅特徵調亂：DJIA 係 30 隻 blue-chip、price-weighted；500 隻、market-cap-weighted 係 S&P 500。D 就係 broker 嘅定義，唔係 dealer——記住 dealer 揸貨 bear risk。

**Question 3 — Answer: B**
- Model answer: The analyst's strategy relies entirely on publicly available information (annual reports, dividend announcements, management changes). Under the semi-strong form EMH, all publicly available information is already reflected in security prices, so analysing it cannot identify mispriced securities or generate abnormal returns after transaction costs — her results therefore contradict the semi-strong form. Because the strong form incorporates all information contained in the semi-strong form (plus private information), her results also contradict the strong form. The weak form only claims that past price and volume data cannot predict future prices; it does not preclude profits from analysing public fundamental information, so her results are NOT inconsistent with the weak form.
- 【解題思路】: 關鍵係分析師用嘅資料全部係公開資訊。Semi-strong form 話所有公開資訊已經反映喺股價度，分析公開資訊係「pointless」嘅——佢竟然賺到超額回報，即係推翻 semi-strong form；strong form 包含晒 semi-strong 嘅資訊，所以一併被推翻。Weak form 只講歷史價量數據冇用，佢嘅基本面分析同 weak form 並無衝突，所以 A、C（包 weak form）都錯；D 只講 strong form，漏咗 semi-strong form，都錯。記住三個形態係「層層遞增」嘅資訊集合。

**Question 4 — Answer: HK$5,765 million**
- Model answer: Total current assets = Inventories 169 + Trade receivables 9,719 + Prepayments and deposits 1,166 + Cash and cash equivalents 6,170 = HK$17,224 million. Total current liabilities = Trade payables 7,426 + Short-term bank loans 3,000 + Accruals and other payables 1,033 = HK$11,459 million. Net working capital = 17,224 − 11,459 = **HK$5,765 million**. The positive figure indicates sufficient short-term liquidity (total current assets exceed total current liabilities), consistent with the ABC Entertainment illustrative example.
- 【解題思路】: 先將資產負債表項目分類：inventories、trade receivables、prepayments & deposits、cash 全部係 current assets；trade payables、short-term bank loans、accruals & other payables 全部係 current liabilities。兩邊各自加總後相減：17,224 − 11,459 = 5,765。常見錯誤包括漏咗 short-term bank loans（記住短貸係流動負債）、或者漏咗 prepayments & deposits（佢係流動資產）。正數 net working capital 代表短期流動性充足。

**Question 5 — Answer: Broker**
- Model answer: Coastal Securities acts as a **broker**: it brings buyers and sellers together, executes transactions for clients, charges fees, and does not bear any risk of ownership. By contrast, a dealer "makes markets" by buying and selling securities from an inventory it owns and bears the risk that it may not be able to sell a security for more than it paid.
- 【解題思路】: 題目嘅關鍵字係「on behalf of the client」、「charges a fee」同「never takes ownership」——三個都係 broker 嘅標誌性特徵。如果答案寫「dealer」就錯，因為 dealer 係用自己嘅存貨買賣並 bear risk。考試分辨 broker/dealer 嘅核心問題永遠係：邊個承擔擁有權風險（risk of ownership）？Broker 唔承擔，dealer 承擔。
