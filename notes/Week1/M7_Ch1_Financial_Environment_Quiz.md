## Phase 3: Exam-style Quiz 模擬測驗 (100% OTQ, ALL IN ENGLISH)

**Question 1 (MCQ)**
Lion Rock Ventures Ltd is considering two mutually exclusive projects. Project A generates an accounting profit of HK$2.0 million in Year 1, but the related cash will only be collected in Year 3. Project B generates the same accounting profit, with all cash collected in Year 1. Both projects carry identical risk. If the company adopts "profit maximisation" as its sole goal, which of the following best describes the outcome?

A. The company will correctly prefer Project B, because profit maximisation captures the time value of money
B. The company will be indifferent between the two projects, because profit maximisation ignores the timing of cash flows and the time value of money
C. The company will prefer Project A, because accounting profits are recognised earlier
D. The company will reject both projects, because accounting profits are not the same as cash flows

**Question 2 (MCQ)**
Which of the following statements about financial markets is CORRECT?

A. When an investor buys shares on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange from another investor, the issuing company receives the proceeds of the sale
B. The Dow Jones Industrial Average is a market-capitalisation-weighted index of 500 large US companies
C. A bond with a maturity of nine months issued by a corporation is traded in the money market
D. Dealers bring buyers and sellers together, execute transactions for clients and bear no risk of ownership

**Question 3 (MCQ)**
An analyst discovers that by studying companies' published annual reports, dividend announcements and management changes, she consistently earns returns above the market average after transaction costs. Her results are inconsistent with which form(s) of the Efficient Market Hypothesis?

A. Weak form only
B. Semi-strong form and strong form
C. Weak form and semi-strong form
D. Strong form only

**Question 4 (Objective calculation question)**
Harbour Lights Ltd's statement of financial position shows the following (HK$ million): Inventories 169; Trade receivables 9,719; Prepayments and deposits 1,166; Cash and cash equivalents 6,170; Trade payables 7,426; Short-term bank loans 3,000; Accruals and other payables 1,033. Calculate the company's net working capital (total current assets minus total current liabilities), in HK$ million.

**Question 5 (Objective calculation/fill-in question)**
A client of Coastal Securities Ltd wishes to buy shares of Dragon Bay Ltd. Coastal Securities executes the transaction on behalf of the client using the client's funds, charges the client a fee, and never takes ownership of the shares at any point. State the term that describes the role played by Coastal Securities in this transaction.
