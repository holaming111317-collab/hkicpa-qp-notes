## 【標準答案 Model Answers — 完成所有題目後先好睇！】

**Question 1 — Answer: B**

Model answer: Under s.14 of the IRO, a person is chargeable to profits tax where (1) the person carries on a trade, profession or business in Hong Kong, and (2) the person derives Hong Kong source profits from that trade, profession or business. Mr Ko carries on a consultancy business through a Hong Kong office (limb 1 met) and his fees arise from services performed entirely in Hong Kong — under the operation test, what he did to earn the profits (performing consultancy services) was done in Hong Kong (limb 2 met). Residence is not relevant to chargeability under the territorial source principle. Option A is wrong: residence does not determine profits tax liability. Option C is wrong: the fees are business profits of his own consultancy business, not income from an employment, office or pension under s.8. Option D is wrong: the withholding basis applies only to a non-resident who derives Hong Kong source income (e.g. royalties, entertainers' fees) and has NO Hong Kong business presence; Mr Ko operates through a Hong Kong office, so he reports and pays profits tax himself.

【解題思路】第一步認出呢條係考 s.14 two-limbed test：「喺香港經營業務」+「港源利潤」，兩項齊就要交 profits tax。第二步用 operation test 確認來源：服務全部喺香港做，利潤梗係港源。Distractor 分析：A 係經典陷阱——香港睇 source 唔睇 residence，非居民一樣要交稅；C 錯因為佢係自己經營業務而唔係受僱；D 錯因為 WHT 只適用於喺香港**無業務存在**嘅非居民收 royalties／演藝費，Mr Ko 喺香港有辦公室，應自行報稅。

**Question 2 — Answer: C**

Model answer: Decisions of the courts of Hong Kong are binding on the lower courts under the doctrine of precedent: a decision of the Court of Final Appeal binds the Court of Appeal, the Court of First Instance and the Board of Review. Option A is wrong: DIPNs set out the IRD's interpretation and practice but have no legally binding effect (see also BOR D54/06). Option B is wrong: Board of Review decisions are persuasive only and not binding, even on other BoR cases. Option D is wrong: decisions of courts of other common law jurisdictions (e.g. Australia) are generally persuasive rather than binding (only the highest UK courts' decisions, especially pre-1997, are often treated as binding in practice).

【解題思路】呢題考「法律淵源約束力」呢個必考位。記住：statute、DTA、香港上級法院判決 = binding；DIPN = IRD 實務指引，無約束力；Board of Review 裁決 = 只具說服力，連其他 BoR 案都唔綁；海外普通法案例 = 說服力（唯獨 1997 年前英國最高法院判決實務上常被視為有約束力，但題目講澳洲，所以 D 一定錯）。所以唯一 binding 係 CFA 判決。

**Question 3 — Answer: B**

Model answer: There is no withholding tax on Hong Kong source dividends and interest income. Interest paid to a non-resident person is chargeable to profits tax only where that non-resident carries on business in Hong Kong and derives the interest from Hong Kong — and even then it is reported and paid by the non-resident itself, not withheld. Option B is therefore NOT correct. Option A correctly describes the schedular system (separate assessments under salaries tax, property tax and profits tax unless personal assessment is elected). Option C correctly states the IRD's 'assess first, audit later' (AFAL) approach, under which assessments are issued based on returns submitted. Option D correctly reflects Basic Law Article 106 (Hong Kong has independent finances; the Central People's Government shall not levy taxes in the HKSAR).

【解題思路】揀「NOT correct」題要逐項驗證。B 係答案：香港對股息同利息**完全無 WHT**，只有 royalties（在港使用 IP）同非居民演藝／運動員演出費先按預扣基準課稅——呢個係全章最高頻陷阱。A、C、D 全部係 source 白紙黑字嘅正確描述（schedular system、AFAL、Basic Law Article 106），係干擾項。考試貼士：見到 "withhold" 加 "interest/dividend" 嘅組合，八成都係錯。

**Question 4 — Answer: HK$660,000**

Model answer / Working:
- First HK$2,000,000 of assessable profits @ 8.25% = HK$165,000
- Remaining HK$3,000,000 (HK$5,000,000 − HK$2,000,000) @ 16.5% = HK$495,000
- Profits tax payable = HK$165,000 + HK$495,000 = **HK$660,000**

【解題思路】兩級制利得稅計算：第一步，公司首 HK$2 million 應評稅利潤用 8.25%（= HK$165,000）；第二步，超出 HK$2 million 嘅 HK$3 million 用標準稅率 16.5%（= HK$495,000）；第三步加埋 = HK$660,000。常見錯誤：(1) 全部利潤直接用 16.5%（會錯得 HK$825,000）；(2) 用錯非公司業務稅率 7.5%/15%（題目講明 corporation）；(3) 分界額記錯（係 HK$2 million，唔係 HK$1 million）。考試貼士：睇清楚題目係 "corporation" 定 "unincorporated business" 先落筆。

**Question 5 — Answer: HK$43,200**

Model answer / Working:
- Assessable value (AV) = HK$30,000 × 12 = HK$360,000
- Less: 20% statutory allowance for repairs and maintenance = HK$72,000
- Net assessable value (NAV) = HK$360,000 × 80% = HK$288,000
- Property tax payable = HK$288,000 × 15% = **HK$43,200**

【解題思路】物業稅三部曲：AV（全年租金）→ 扣 20% 法定維修免稅額得 NAV → 乘 15% 標準稅率。即 HK$360,000 × 80% × 15% = HK$43,200。題目特登講 Ms Lee 係 non-resident——呢個係煙幕，因為 residence 對物業稅完全無關，只要物業喺香港就課稅（IRO s.5）。常見錯誤：(1) 唔記得扣 20%（錯得 HK$54,000）；(2) 以為非居民免稅（錯）；(3) 誤用累進稅率（嗰個係 salaries tax）。記住物業稅永遠係單一標準稅率 15% 乘 NAV。
