
### 第二十一部分：M7 Ch12 定價策略（10/13 追加）

**核心術語**
- market skimming pricing 撇脂定價（高價切入再逐步降）vs market penetration pricing 滲透定價
- price discrimination：first-degree（每客最高價）/ **second-degree（按用量分段，如 data plan）** / third-degree（按客群）
- relevant cost pricing：**只有 future incremental costs + opportunity costs 相關**；sunk costs 同唔變嘅 absorbed fixed OH 一律唔計
- total cost-plus pricing：**total cost 包晒 variable + fixed manufacturing OH + fixed S&A**；mark-up % = desired profit (ROI × investment) ÷ total cost

**Model Sentence（背熟）**
> "Only future incremental costs and opportunity costs that differ between options are relevant when setting a minimum price for a special order."
