
### 第二十三部分：M7 Ch14 項目後審計（10/14 追加）

**核心術語**
- post-completion / post-appraisal audit：評估項目**有冇達到預期**（唔係對數、唔係問責）
- continuous measurement：每個 phase 尾對照 budget 同 timing standards，**問題未變危機前就發現**
- sunk cost fallacy：「已經洗咗咁多錢」**唔係**繼續嘅理由——決策只睇將來嘅 expected revenues and profits
- **CPI = earned value ÷ actual costs**（<1 = 超支）
- **SPI = authorised duration ÷ final duration**（<1 = 延誤）

**Model Sentence（背熟）**
> "Capital already invested is sunk and does not justify continuing a failing project; the decision rests on whether the future is likely to yield the expected revenues and profits."
