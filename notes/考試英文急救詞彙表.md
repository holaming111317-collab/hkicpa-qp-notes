
### 第二十四部分：M7 Ch15 績效衡量（10/14 追加）

**核心術語**
- ROI = operating income ÷ average operating assets；RI = operating income − (required return × assets)
- **goal congruence / dysfunctional decision**：項目 ROI 20% > 公司要求 15%，但 < 部門現有 25% → 經理會**拒絕**（ROI 本位主義）
- balanced scorecard 四維度：financial / customer / **internal process**（如 production cycle time）/ learning & growth
- **lagging** measures（ROI、RI、EVA——反映過去）vs **leading** indicators（預測將來）
- **EVA = adjusted after-tax operating income − WACC × (total assets − current liabilities)**

**Model Sentence（背熟）**
> "A manager rewarded on ROI will reject a project whose return exceeds the company's required rate but falls below the division's current ROI — a dysfunctional decision that residual income avoids."

### 第二十五部分：M7 Ch16 財務風險（10/15 追加）

**核心術語**
- **financial risk** 類型：credit/default risk（issuer 到期唔還本）、liquidity risk（unlisted asset 冇人接貨）、political risk（foreign government freezes assets）；losing salespeople to a competitor = business/operational risk，**唔係** financial risk
- 風險管理四招（考試必考配對）：**avoidance**（唔喺 flood plain 起廠——完全避開）、**mitigation/reduction**（hardhat 減少但唔消除）、**transfer**（買 insurance 轉畀 insurer）、**acceptance/retention**（cost-benefit 後 self-insure）
- **political risk insurance**：保障海外資產被當地政府徵用/凍結
- **natural hedge**：用**同一貨幣**借錢融資海外資產——借 xeno 配 xeno 資產（唔係借 HKD！）
- **LTV (loan-to-value) = amount borrowed ÷ total value of the asset**，以 percentage + one decimal place 表達
- **risk-based pricing**：LTV 越高 → 貸款越 risky → 利率越高；"will not grant any loan with LTV above X%" = cap → max borrowing = **cap × asset value**（唔好用 100% − cap）

**Model Sentence（背熟）**
> "Loan-to-value equals the amount borrowed divided by the total value of the asset; the higher the LTV, the riskier the loan, so lenders apply risk-based pricing — and the maximum loan is the LTV cap multiplied by the asset value."
