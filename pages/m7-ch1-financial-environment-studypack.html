M7 Financial Management — Chapter 1: Financial Environment — Study Pack (Week 1)

Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

1. 核心知識架構 (Core Learning Objectives)

  • 財務的角色與功能 (Roles and Functions of Finance)：Finance 是研究資金如何在 lenders（放款人）與 borrowers（借款人）之間流動及配置的學問；同時包含「資金如何被管理」(how money is managed) 及「資金如何取得」(the process of acquiring money) 兩層意義。企業在 start up、operate、expand 三個階段都需要資金，而企業的規模與擁有權結構 (ownership structure) 決定其可用的融資選項——小型企業主要依靠銀行貸款 (intermediated finance)，大型企業則可進入 financial markets 籌資。
  • 財務管理的範疇與目標 (Scope and Objectives of Financial Management)：分析為何 profit maximisation 不是最佳目標（定義模糊、會計利潤 ≠ 現金流量、忽略 time value of money、忽略風險），以及為何財務經理人的真正目標是 maximise the company's share price，從而達致 shareholder wealth maximisation。亦須認識主要財務管理人員：CEO、CFO、Corporate Treasurer 及 CRO 的分工。
  • 金融環境 (Financial Environment)：了解 financial markets 的功能與分類——money market vs capital market、primary market vs secondary market、derivatives market、FOREX market；認識 brokers 與 dealers 的角色分別、marketability 與 liquidity 的概念、香港金融市場架構 (HKEx、SEHK、Stock Connect、Bond Connect)，以及主要指數 (HSI、S&P 500、NASDAQ Composite、DJIA)。亦要分辨 financial system 與 banking system 及各類 financial intermediaries。
  • 庫務管理 (Treasury Management)：了解 treasurer 的 roles、treasury department 的 responsibilities 及 activities；掌握 cash budget、cash conversion cycle、float（collection float、disbursement float 及其三個組成部分：transmission / processing / clearing float） 等概念。
  • 效率市場假說 (Efficient Market Hypothesis, EMH)：掌握 EMH 三種形態（weak form、semi-strong form、strong form）的資訊內容、對投資者與發行人的 implications，以及對 EMH 的主要批評（包括 behavioural finance 的觀點）。

2. 必背考點清單 (Must-Memorize List)

A. Profit Maximisation 的四大缺點 (Chapter 核心考點)

  1. Ambiguity of "profit"：利潤定義依會計規則的應用與詮釋而定，可能被扭曲誇大（creative accounting）；亦可透過不投資新設備或延遲維修來壓低今日成本，但損害長遠利益。
  2. Accounting profits ≠ cash flows：收益通常於銷售時確認，早於收現；業主實際需要的是現金（投資及購買貨品服務均需用現金）。
  3. Ignores the time value of money：不區分今天的一元與未來的一元；time value of money 是財務學最重要的概念之一。
  4. Ignores risk：忽略與現金流量相關的風險；投資者是 risk averse，承擔風險須獲得補償，因此風險與預期回報存在 trade-off。

正確目標：Finance managers aim to maximise the share price of their companies, thereby maximising shareholders' wealth，方法是作出 investment and financing decisions，確保 total present value of cash inflows > total value of cash outflows（重點是 maximise cash flows, not profits）。

B. Financial Markets 的分類速查

市場分類 定義（考試關鍵字）
Money market Debt instruments with maturities of less than one year（short-term funds）
Capital market Equity and debt instruments with maturities exceeding one year（long-term funds）
Primary market Companies initially sell NEW security issues；IPO = first-time issuance of shares to the public；通常經 underwriter 協助
Secondary market Securities traded amongst investors；發行公司不會從二手交易收到任何款項；活躍 secondary market → 投資者願付更高價 → 公司 funding cost 較低
Derivatives market Contracts derive value from underlying debt/equity securities（如 futures、options）
FOREX market Electronic markets for buying and selling currencies，用於減低持有過多某種貨幣的風險
Public market vs Private market 公眾經 stockbroker 買賣 vs 兩方直接交易（private placements）
Organised exchange vs OTC market 有實體場所、會員制、席位有限 vs 無中央交易場所、經 dealer 以電話或電腦交易

C. Marketability、Liquidity、Brokers、Dealers

  • Marketability = the ease with which a security can be sold and converted into cash；取決於 availability of investors 及 transaction costs（trading、search、information costs）——成本越低，marketability 越高。
  • Liquidity = the ability to convert an asset into cash quickly without loss of value（關鍵分別：liquidity 隱含價值獲得保存，marketability 則沒有此隱含）。
  • Brokers：bring buyers and sellers together、代客執行交易、收取費用、不承擔擁有權風險 (do not bear any risk of ownership)。
  • Dealers：make markets for securities、以自己的庫存 (inventory) 買賣、必須承擔風險 (bear risk)。

D. 主要市場指數 (必記數字)

Index 關鍵事實
Hang Seng Index (HSI) 1969 年推出；50 stocks；free-float adjusted；10% capping 避免單一股份主導；四個分類指數：commerce & industry、finance、properties、utilities；由 Hang Seng Bank 附屬公司發佈
S&P 500 Market-cap-weighted；500 家美國大型公司；涵蓋約 80% 美國市值；全部成分股必須為 US companies；最少 50% 已發行股份可供交易
NASDAQ Composite 1971 年由 NASD 推出；逾 3,000 隻 NASDAQ 上市股票；強烈 technology focus
Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJIA) Charles Dow 於 1896 年創立；30 家美國 blue-chip 公司；price-weighted；包括所有行業除 transportation 及 utilities；約佔美國股市價值四分一

E. Financial System vs Banking System 及 Intermediaries

  • Financial system：連接 lenders 與 borrowers 的系統（直接或經 intermediaries），核心為 financial markets（primary + secondary）。
  • Banking system：提供金融服務的機構網絡，重點是促成 indirect market transactions；banks 是 borrowers 與 lenders 之間的 financial intermediaries。
  • 主要 financial intermediaries：commercial banks（最大、服務最廣）、life insurance companies、casualty insurance companies、pension funds、investment funds、business finance companies（發行 commercial paper 集資）。

F. Treasury Management 必背

  • Treasury management = cash management；現金提供 negligible or no return 且受通脹侵蝕，故企業只持有足以應付到期債務及義務的最低現金餘額；持有過多現金會損失潛在回報。
  • Cash budget = expected cash receipts and disbursements for the specified period；現金過剩 → 投資或還債；不足 → 減少現金需求、準備借款或作其他應變。
  • Roles of treasurer（7 項）：maintaining relations with banks；ensuring sufficient cash liquidity；investing surplus funds；understanding funding requirements；understanding available sources of finance；keeping abreast of currency and interest rate risks；monitoring relevant taxes and policies。背後原則：fiduciary responsibility（須永遠以公司最佳利益行事）。
  • Responsibilities（5 項）：cash forecasting；monitoring working capital；cash concentration（cash sweeps or notional pooling）；investments；risk management（hedging against fluctuations in asset values, interest rates and currency values）。
  • Cash management 策略：加快收款（縮短 accounts receivable period）、延長持有現金（lengthen accounts payable period——但逾期付款有道德及商譽問題）。
  • Float = 交易中資金傳遞的延誤：
  • Collection float：付款人發出付款 → 款項存入 payee 銀行戶口的時間。
  • Disbursement float：付款人發出付款 → 款項從付款人戶口扣除的時間。
  • 三個組成部分：transmission (delivery) float（付款工具由付款人送至收款人的延誤）、processing float（收款公司內部處理延誤）、clearing float（銀行系統結算延誤）。
  • Treasurer 應設法減少 collection float；pre-authorised cheques 可減少 delivery 及 processing delays；zero-balance account 只轉入足夠支付當日支票的資金以 maximise float。

G. Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH)

  • 定義：All known information is taken into stock prices immediately upon being known to the public；價格已「price in」所有可得資訊（不論真確與否，包括 rumour or gossip）；因此不可能 beat the market，價格只對新資訊作出反應。
  • 三種形態（資訊內容遞增，必背層次）： 1. Weak form：股價反映所有過去及公開的市場數據（歷史價格及成交量）→ technical analysis 不可靠、價格走勢無規律（random movements）。 2. Semi-strong form：反映所有公開資訊（weak form 資訊 + earnings、dividends、corporate investments、management changes）→ 分析公開資訊無法找到 mispriced securities；法律實務採納此形態——以私人資訊交易屬不公平，insider trading 被禁止及懲罰（SFC v 案例、Section 300 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO)）。 3. Strong form：反映所有公開及私人資訊 → 連 insider information 也無法識別 mispriced securities；已被研究推翻（如 1995 年 Dreman and Berry 研究：low P/E stocks 回報高於 high P/E stocks）。
  • Implications for investors：technical analysis 及 fundamental analysis 不大可能在扣除交易成本及稅項後產生 abnormal profits；active trading 不大可能跑贏 passive / buy-and-hold strategies。
  • Implications for issuers：證券發行或回購的時機不重要（timing of security issues or share repurchases is unimportant），因價格由市場決定且長期正確；管理層應監察股價變動以辨別其反映新資訊還是短期動量/過度反應。
  • Criticisms：(1) 不同投資者對同一資訊有不同詮釋，股票價值難以確定；(2) 現實中投資者回報差異極大，有人能持續跑贏大市（Warren Buffett）；(3) behavioural finance 指出投資者並非全部理性，心理因素（情緒、偏見）影響投資決定。

3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall 1：Marketability vs Liquidity

Marketability Liquidity
定義 證券能否容易出售並轉換為現金 資產能否迅速轉換為現金而不損失價值
關鍵差異 不保證出售時價值得以保存 隱含出售時價值獲保存

例子：一隻細價股可能每日都有成交、容易賣出（高 marketability），但要套現時可能要大幅折讓（低 liquidity）；兩者日常使用經常互換，但考試問定義時必須區分「without loss of value」這一句。

Pitfall 2：Semi-strong form vs Strong form EMH

Semi-strong form Strong form
價格反映的資訊 所有公開資訊（public information only） 所有公開及私人資訊（public + private/insider information）
能否靠 insider information 獲超額回報 可以（故 insider trading 被禁止） 不可以（連內幕消息也無用）

例子：Betty Young 利用未公開的收購要約資料買入 Hsinchu Bank 股份獲利 HK$2.9 million——她能獲利，證明市場只是 semi-strong efficient 而非 strong form efficient（法律亦以 semi-strong form 為依據禁止 insider trading）。1995 年 low P/E stocks 跑贏 high P/E stocks 的研究進一步推翻 strong form。

Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

State the four main drawbacks of profit maximisation as the goal of financial decision-making, and identify the appropriate alternative goal.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Ambiguity: "profit" can be defined and manipulated in different ways (creative accounting).
  • Accounting profits are not the same as cash flows (revenue recognised before cash received).
  • It ignores the time value of money (a dollar today vs a dollar in the future).
  • It ignores the risk associated with cash flows (risk–return trade-off).
  • Appropriate goal: maximise the company's share price, thereby maximising shareholders' wealth, by ensuring the total present value of cash inflows exceeds cash outflows.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 利潤可以被會計手法「做靚」，又唔等於真係收到現金，仲忽略咗資金嘅時間價值同風險。所以財務經理人嘅正確目標係令股價最大化，繼而令股東財富最大化——重點係 maximise cash flows，而唔係 profits。
Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

Distinguish between the money market and the capital market, and between the primary market and the secondary market.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Money market: debt instruments with maturities of less than one year (short-term funds).
  • Capital market: equity and debt instruments with maturities exceeding one year (long-term funds).
  • Primary market: companies initially sell NEW security issues (e.g. an IPO with the help of an underwriter).
  • Secondary market: securities are bought and sold amongst investors; the original issuer receives NO money from these trades; an active secondary market lowers the issuer's funding costs.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 一年係分界線：短過一年嘅債務工具喺 money market 買賣，長過一年嘅（包括股票）喺 capital market。公司首次發行新證券集資係 primary market；之後投資者互相轉手就係 secondary market——好似二手車市場咁，車廠喺二手交易中係收唔到錢嘅。
Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

ABC Ltd has total current assets of HK$17,224 million and total current liabilities of HK$11,459 million. Calculate its net working capital and explain what it indicates about short-term liquidity.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Net working capital = Total current assets − Total current liabilities = 17,224 − 11,459 = HK$5,765 million.
  • Total current assets exceed total current liabilities → the company has sufficient short-term liquidity.
  • Positive net working capital indicates the company can meet its short-term obligations as they fall due.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 淨營運資金（net working capital）就係流動資產減流動負債。數字為正，即係話公司短期內可以變現嘅資產多過短期內要還嘅錢，短期流動性充足。呢個正係 Learning Pack 入面 ABC Entertainment 個案嘅分析方法。
Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

Define collection float and disbursement float, and list the three components of float.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Collection float: the time between when a payer sends payment and the funds are CREDITED to the payee's bank account.
  • Disbursement float: the time between when a payer sends payment and the funds are DEDUCTED from the payer's bank account.
  • Three components: (1) transmission (delivery) float; (2) processing float; (3) clearing float.
  • Treasurers seek to reduce collection float; pre-authorised cheques reduce delivery and processing delays; a zero-balance account holds just enough funds to cover the day's cheques presented.
  • [中文白話解釋]: Float 係資金喺交易雙方之間傳遞時嘅延誤。收款一方嘅延誤叫 collection float（錢未入到自己戶口），付款一方嘅延誤叫 disbursement float（錢未扣到自己戶口）。三個組成部分記「寄出—處理—銀行結算」：transmission、processing、clearing。
Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

Compare the roles of a broker and a dealer in secondary markets.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Brokers: bring buyers and sellers together, execute transactions for clients, charge fees, and do NOT bear any risk of ownership.
  • Dealers: "make markets" for securities, buy and sell from an inventory of securities they own, and MUST bear risk (they may not be able to sell a security for more than they paid).
  • Dealers profit the way retailers profit from selling inventory.
  • [中文白話解釋]: Broker 係「中間人」，只負責拉攏買賣雙方、收佣金，唔會揸貨所以冇風險；Dealer 係「莊家」，用自己嘅存貨做買賣，賺買賣差價，所以必須承受存貨跌價嘅風險。考試最鍾意問邊個 bear risk——記住係 dealer。

Question

State the information content reflected in security prices under each of the three forms of the Efficient Market Hypothesis.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Weak form: all past and publicly known market data (historical price and volume information) → technical analysis is unreliable.
  • Semi-strong form: all publicly available information (weak form information + earnings, dividends, corporate investments, management changes) → analysing public information cannot identify mispriced securities; insider trading CAN be profitable (hence banned).
  • Strong form: all public AND private (inside) information → no one can beat the market, even with insider information.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 三個形態嘅資訊層層遞增：weak form 只反映歷史價量數據；semi-strong 反映所有公開資訊，但內幕消息仲有用；strong form 連內幕消息都反映晒。法律實務採納 semi-strong form，所以先至要禁止 insider trading。

Question

Match each index with its key characteristics: HSI, S&P 500, NASDAQ Composite, DJIA.

Answer

Question

List the five key responsibilities of a treasury department and state the guiding principle behind the treasurer's duties.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • (1) Cash forecasting (aggregating information from budgets, accounting records, board minutes on dividends).
  • (2) Monitoring working capital.
  • (3) Cash concentration (channelling cash into a centralised investment account via cash sweeps or notional pooling).
  • (4) Investments (assigning surplus cash according to the firm's investment policy).
  • (5) Risk management (hedging exposure to fluctuations in asset values, interest rates and currency values).
  • Guiding principle: fiduciary responsibility — the obligation to always act in the firm's best interest.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 庫務部門五大職責記口訣「預測—監控—集中—投資—對沖」。Treasurer 做所有嘢都要以 fiduciary responsibility 為大前提，即係永遠以公司最佳利益行事。另外記住 treasury management = cash management，目標係持有最低但足夠嘅現金，多餘嘅錢要攞去投資。
