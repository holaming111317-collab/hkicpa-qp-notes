M7 Chapter 10 Financial Forecasts and Business Planning — Study Pack (Week 9)

Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

1. 核心知識架構

  • 現金流是企業命脈 (cash flow is the life blood)：企業透過生產性資產 (productive assets) 銷售貨品或服務產生現金流；支付營運開支、債權人及稅項後剩餘的稱為剩餘現金流 (residual cash flows)，可用作派息 (cash dividends)、回購股份 (share repurchase) 或再投資 (reinvestment)——再投資是企業增長最根本的方式。
  • 資本預算與增量自由現金流 (capital budgeting & incremental after-tax free cash flows, FCF)：項目評估只計算「有項目」與「無項目」之間公司總稅後自由現金流的差額 (FCF_project = FCF_firm with project − FCF_firm without project)，並應用獨立原則 (stand-alone principle) 把項目視為獨立公司分析；利息開支不包括在內，因為融資成本已反映在折現率 (discount rate) 中。
  • 財務規劃模型與預計財務報表 (financial planning model & pro forma financial statements)：模型由三大元件組成——輸入 (inputs：現行財務報表、銷售預測、投資及財務政策決定)、模型本身 (the model itself)、輸出 (outputs：pro forma 報表、財務比率、現金預算)；銷售預測 (sales forecast) 是最主要的驅動變數。
  • 銷售百分比法與外部融資需求 (percent-of-sales method & external funding needed, EFN)：編製初步預計財務狀況表 (preliminary pro forma statement of financial position) 時，隨銷售變動的賬項以銷售百分比預測，報表不平衡的差額即「plug value」，通常就是 EFN。
  • 業務規劃工具 (business planning tools)：現金流收支平衡分析 (cash flow break-even) 與現金預算 (cash budget) 用於掌握企業現金需求；商業計劃書 (business plan) 則是集資、設定目標、績效基準及對內外溝通的路線圖 (road map)。

2. 必背考點清單

A. 增量自由現金流 (Incremental Free Cash Flow, FCF)

FCF = [(Revenue − Op Ex − D&A) × (1 − t)] + D&A − Cap Exp − Add WC
  • Revenue = 項目的增量收入 (incremental revenue / net sales)
  • Op Ex = 增量現金營運開支 (cash operating expenses)
  • D&A = 增量折舊及攤銷 (depreciation & amortisation)——非現金支出，可扣稅但須加回
  • t = 公司的邊際稅率 (marginal tax rate)（因項目利潤屬增量，故用邊際稅率計算稅項）
  • Cap Exp = 增量資本開支 (incremental capital expenditures：長期有形及無形資產)
  • Add WC = 增量營運資金增加 (incremental additions to working capital：accounts receivable、inventory 減 accounts payable 等)
  • 推導鏈：Revenue − Op Ex = EBITDA；EBITDA − D&A = EBIT；EBIT × (1 − t) = NOPAT；NOPAT + D&A = CF Opns（現金流量來自營運）；CF Opns − Cap Exp − Add WC = FCF
  • 關鍵規則：項目的稅項 = EBIT × marginal tax rate（假設無債務融資）；利息開支及融資現金流不計入 FCF，因為已反映在折現率內。不變的固定成本 (fixed costs payable regardless) 不屬增量，不納入計算。

B. 間接法營運現金流 (Operating Cash Flow, indirect method) - 由應計制淨利 (accrual net income) 轉為現金基礎：資產增加須減去、資產減少須加回；負債減少須減去、負債增加須加回；再加回折舊 (depreciation)。 - 例：OCF = Net income 100,000 − ΔAR 50,000 + ΔInventory 20,000 − ΔAP 25,000 + Depreciation 10,000 = HK$55,000。

C. 銷售增長率 (Sales growth)

%ΔS = (S_{t+1} − S_t) / S_t

D. 派息與留存比率 (Payout & Retention ratios)

Dividend payout ratio = Cash dividends / Net income
Retention (plowback) ratio = Addition to retained earnings / Net income
Payout ratio + Retention ratio = 1

E. 資本密集比率 (Capital intensity ratio)

Capital intensity ratio = Total assets / Net sales
  • 即 total asset turnover 的倒數；比率越高，每 HK$1 銷售所需資產越多，公司越資本密集、風險越高（高固定成本在銷售下跌時令利潤急跌）。

F. 銷售百分比法 (Percent-of-sales method) 與 EFN - 隨銷售直接變動的賬項 (vary directly with sales)：cash、accounts receivable、inventory、accounts payable、net fixed assets（僅在公司滿負荷運作 full capacity 且固定資產可小額遞增時才成立）。 - 不隨銷售變動 (n/a)：notes payable、long-term debt、common stock（由管理層融資決定）；retained earnings 由盈利及股息政策決定，從 pro forma 損益表推算。 - 初步 pro forma 財務狀況表不平衡的差額 = plug value = External Funding Needed (EFN)；資金來源次序：內部保留盈利 (addition to retained earnings) + 自發增加的貿易信貸 (increase in accounts payable) + 外部融資 (debt or equity)。 - 財務模型只產生數字，最終融資決策由管理層作出。

G. 收支平衡分析 (Break-even analysis)

Pre-tax operating cash flow (EBITDA) break-even = FC / (Unit Price − Unit VC)
Accounting operating profit (EBIT) break-even = (FC + D&A) / (Unit Price − Unit VC)
Contribution margin per unit (CM) = Unit Price − Unit VC
Contribution margin ratio = CM / Unit Price
Break-even sales dollars = FC / CM ratio
  • 計算收支平衡單位數必須向上進位 (round up to the next whole unit)。
  • 焦點：最大化每單位邊際貢獻 (maximise per-unit contribution)、最小化經常開支 (minimise overheads)。

H. 現金預算與商業計劃書 (Cash budget & Business plan) - Cash budget：按月 (monthly basis) 列示現金流入流出，顯示資金來源與去向，協助及早籌資。 - Business plan 的作用：集資 (raise capital)、設定目標 (set goals/objectives)、績效評估基準 (benchmark for evaluating and controlling performance)、與利益相關者溝通 (communicate ideas to stakeholders)。 - 典型內容：executive summary、company overview、products & services description、market analysis、marketing & sales activities、operations、management team & organisational structure、ownership structure、capital requirements、歷史財務業績 + 財務預測 + pro forma 報表 + 未來兩三年逐月現金預算 (month-by-month cash budget for two or three years)、appendices。

3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall 1: 自發性賬項 (spontaneous, vary with sales) vs 酌情性賬項 (discretionary, n/a)

對比點 自發性賬項 (e.g. accounts payable, inventory, accounts receivable) 酌情性賬項 (e.g. notes payable, long-term debt, common stock)
變動原因 銷售增加自然帶動（賣得多→入貨多→應付賬款多） 只隨管理層融資/派息決策改變
Percent-of-sales 處理 以銷售百分比預測 初步 pro forma 中維持現值 (enter current year's value)
與 EFN 的關係 增加屬內部/自發資金來源，減少 EFN 是填補 plug value 的外部融資渠道
  • 例子：Wong Sales 銷售增 30%，accounts payable 由 60 升至 104（+44，自發資金）；notes payable 及 long-term debt 在初步報表中維持不變，最後不平衡的 85 才是要靠發債或發股填補的 EFN。考生常誤把 notes payable 當作隨銷售變動，或忘記 retained earnings 要從 pro forma 損益表的 addition to retained earnings 推算。

Pitfall 2: 稅前經營現金流收支平衡 (EBITDA break-even) vs 會計經營利潤收支平衡 (EBIT break-even)

對比點 EBITDA break-even EBIT break-even
分子 FC 只有固定成本 FC + D&A（連折舊攤銷）
衡量 現金角度：要賣幾多先 cover 到現金開支 會計利潤角度：EBIT = 0
單位數 較低 較高（分子多了 D&A）
  • 例子：FC = HK$15,000、D&A = HK$3,000、Price = HK$250、Unit VC = HK$160：EBITDA break-even = 15,000/90 = 167 單位；EBIT break-even = 18,000/90 = 200 單位。考生常錯在把 D&A 加入 EBITDA break-even 的分子，或忘記單位要向上進位。

考試貼士：OTQ 見到 break-even 題目，第一步先問自己「分子包唔包 D&A？」——題目寫 pre-tax operating cash flow / EBITDA 就只放 FC；寫 accounting operating profit / EBIT 就放 FC + D&A。

Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

State the formula for incremental after-tax free cash flow (FCF) for a project and define each component.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • FCF = [(Revenue − Op Ex − D&A) × (1 − t)] + D&A − Cap Exp − Add WC
  • Revenue = incremental net sales; Op Ex = incremental cash operating expenses
  • D&A = incremental depreciation & amortisation (non-cash, tax-deductible, added back)
  • t = the company's marginal tax rate (applied to EBIT to get NOPAT)
  • Cap Exp = incremental capital expenditures (long-term tangible & intangible assets)
  • Add WC = incremental additions to working capital (AR, inventory, AP)
  • Chain: EBITDA → EBIT → NOPAT (= EBIT × (1−t)) → CF Opns (= NOPAT + D&A) → FCF
  • [中文白話解釋]: 計項目的自由現金流，先將增量收入減現金營運開支得出 EBITDA，再減折舊攤銷得出 EBIT，乘以 (1 − 邊際稅率) 得出 NOPAT；由於折舊攤銷並非真實現金流出，要加回去得到營運現金流，最後減去增量資本開支和增量營運資金，就是可以自由分配給債權人和股東的 FCF。
Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

Why are interest expenses and financing cash flows excluded from a project's FCF calculation, and which tax rate is used for project taxes?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Interest is excluded to isolate the cash flows generated by the project itself from how it is financed
  • The cost of debt and equity financing (including the tax effect of debt) is already reflected in the discount rate used in NPV analysis
  • Project taxes = EBIT × the company's marginal tax rate, i.e. taxes owed if no debt is used
  • Marginal rate is used because project profits are incremental to profits the firm already has
  • This makes it easier to estimate NPV under alternative financing structures
  • [中文白話解釋]: 評估項目時故意不理會融資安排，因為借錢的利息和稅務好處已經反映在折現率（cost of capital）裏面，如果再在現金流扣利息就會重複計算。項目稅項用邊際稅率乘 EBIT 計算，因為項目利潤是在公司現有利潤之上的「額外」利潤。
Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

In the percent-of-sales method, which statement of financial position accounts vary directly with sales, and which do not?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Vary with sales (spontaneous): cash, accounts receivable, inventory, accounts payable; net fixed assets only if the firm operates at full capacity and assets can be added in small increments
  • Do NOT vary with sales (marked 'n/a'): notes payable, long-term debt, common stock — these change only through management financing decisions
  • Retained earnings: derived from the pro forma statement of profit or loss (addition to retained earnings), depends on earnings and dividend payout policy
  • Forecast percentages are set by analysing 4–5 years of historical data as a % of sales and spotting trends
  • [中文白話解釋]: 銷售百分比法下，營運資金賬項（現金、應收、存貨、應付）自然跟銷售同步變動；但票據、長期債、股本只會因管理層主動借錢、還錢、發股而改變，所以初步預測時標記 n/a 並維持原值。保留盈利則要從預計損益表的留存盈利增加額推算。
Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

What is the 'plug value' in a preliminary pro forma statement of financial position, and how is external funding needed (EFN) interpreted?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • The preliminary pro forma SOFP normally does not balance; the balancing difference is the plug value
  • The plug value is usually the external funding needed (EFN): growth requires new assets, so total assets exceed total liabilities plus equity
  • Funding hierarchy: (1) addition to retained earnings (internal funds), (2) increase in accounts payable (expanded trade credit), (3) external debt or equity (EFN)
  • Options to close the gap: issue debt, issue equity, cut dividends, or scale back the growth target
  • Financial models compute the number, but management makes the final funding decision
  • [中文白話解釋]: 初步預計財務狀況表一定唔會平衡，差額就是 plug value，通常等於公司要向外籌幾多錢（EFN）。資金先用內部留存盈利，再靠應付賬款自然增加，唔夠先至發債或發股；管理層亦可以減派息或調低增長目標嚟縮小缺口。
Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

A company pays HK$163,961.60 of dividends on net income of HK$298,112. Calculate the dividend payout ratio and the retention (plowback) ratio.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Dividend payout ratio = Cash dividends / Net income = 163,961.60 / 298,112 = 55%
  • Retention (plowback) ratio = Addition to retained earnings / Net income = 1 − 0.55 = 45%
  • Payout ratio + retention ratio = 1 (every dollar of net income is either paid out or retained)
  • Smaller, fast-growing companies retain more; mature, slower-growth companies pay out more
  • [中文白話解釋]: 派息比率 = 股息 ÷ 淨利 = 55%；留存比率 = 1 − 55% = 45%，兩者相加必定等於 1，因為每一元淨利不是派出去就是留低。高速增長公司通常留存多啲錢去再投資。
Flashcard 6 / 共 8 張
Question

Excellent Traders has total assets of HK$513,480 and sales of HK$723,062. Calculate the capital intensity ratio and interpret it.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Capital intensity ratio = Total assets / Net sales = 513,480 / 723,062 = 0.71
  • It is the inverse of the total asset turnover ratio
  • Interpretation: the firm needs HK$0.71 of assets to generate each HK$1 of sales
  • Higher ratio → more capital intensive → higher fixed costs/depreciation → riskier when sales fall
  • [中文白話解釋]: 資本密集比率 = 總資產 ÷ 淨銷售 = 0.71，即每 HK$1 銷售需要 HK$0.71 資產支持。呢個比率係總資產周轉率嘅倒數；比率越高代表公司越重資產、固定成本越高，銷售一跌利潤就跌得好快。
Flashcard 7 / 共 8 張
Question

A product line raises fixed costs to HK$35,000; each unit sells for HK$600 and costs HK$390 to produce. Compute the pre-tax operating cash flow (EBITDA) break-even point, and state how the accounting (EBIT) break-even would differ if D&A were HK$4,200.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Contribution margin per unit = 600 − 390 = HK$210
  • EBITDA break-even = FC / (Price − Unit VC) = 35,000 / 210 = 166.67 → round up = 167 units
  • EBIT (accounting) break-even = (FC + D&A) / CM = (35,000 + 4,200) / 210 = 186.67 → 187 units
  • EBIT break-even is always higher because D&A is added to the numerator
  • Always round break-even units UP to the next whole unit
