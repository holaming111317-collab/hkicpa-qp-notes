M7 Post-Appraisal Audit of Projects — Study Pack (Week 11)

Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

1. 核心知識架構

  • 資本投資項目的五個階段 (five stages of a capital investment project)：Identification → Analysis → Selection → Implementation → Follow-up or post-audit；而 post-completion audit（完成後審計，又稱 follow-up stage）係最後一個階段，目的係判斷項目是否達到預期 (determine if projects have met expectations)。
  • 審計核心做法：將 actual results 與 expected results（包括 expenses、revenues、cash flows 及 initial assumptions）比較；差異 (variances) 並不罕見，但必須解釋 (differences must be explained)。
  • 三大效益 (three main benefits of a post-completion audit)：為未來項目汲取教訓 (lessons for future projects)、識別問題並尋找解決方案 (identifying problems and finding solutions，包括 abandon failing projects to limit losses)、為投資規劃與控制加入結構 (insert structure into investment planning and control)。
  • 有效審計的兩大支柱：team evaluation（團隊評核，用 10 條準則問題）與使用項目開始時已確立嘅評核準則 (criteria established at the start of the project)，配合 yes/no 清單、continuous measurement（分段持續量度）及 baseline performance system（基準表現制度）。
  • 管理層行為 (management behaviour) 與制度：管理者應保持中立 ('neutrality' until done)，避免預設責備 (without pre-assigning blame)；公司應建立 regular times and processes（定期檢討、文件記錄、追蹤 ROI），打破 silo mentality（部門各自為政）。

2. 必背考點清單

本章屬概念性章節，無複雜公式；以下為必背「規則 + 量度指標」清單，全部源自教材。

A. Post-completion audit 的必要性（六個原因，§14.1.1） 1. Monitoring the quality and accuracy of forecasts, predictions and analyses, including underlying assumptions. 2. Identifying systematic errors（包括 overly optimistic forecasts）及其未被及早修正的原因。 3. Tracking business operations associated with the capital investment project. 4. Deciding whether sales or costs are out of line, and why. 5. Generating new ideas for future projects. 6. Providing a benchmark for the analysis of future projects.

B. 失敗項目的處理規則 (abandonment rule, §14.1.1 / Illustrative Example 1) - 已投入的 launch capital 不能成為繼續失敗項目的理由（sunk cost 概念）；若未來不大可能產生預期收入與利潤，應立即削減成本／放棄項目 (cut costs immediately / abandon the project)。 - 項目失敗的典型原因：poor preparation、inadequate marketing、non-competitive pricing、overly optimistic assumptions。

C. Team evaluation 的 10 條準則問題（§14.2.1，須能認出主題） 1. Did the team understand goals and objectives? 2. Does the entire team agree on its goals and objectives? 3. Was the team leadership effective and successful? 4. Were team conflicts dealt with by leaders? 5. Was leadership effective in identifying problems and making decisions? 6. Was the overall process effective (clear instructions, clear goals, positive incentives)? 7. Were procedures clearly expressed and enforced? 8. Was team communication effective? 9. Did the team use resources well? 10. Was there trust among team members?

D. 三種評核方法（§14.2.2）

Method 做法 時點
Yes/No question list 對關鍵問題答 yes/no；可按重要性加權 End-of-project；只係 starting point, not conclusive
Continuous measurement 準則範圍有限但清晰，用 1–10 尺度評分 At the end of each phase（非項目完結時）；可在問題惡化前發現
Baseline performance system 設定 minimally acceptable results，再比較 在項目各 junctures／phases 進行（例如兩年項目分六段，每段末評核）

E. 成功準則的三大範疇（Knowledge Check Q4） - Deliverable success：項目能否解決其成立時要處理的 business problem。 - Process success：管理方式有否改善（budget、timeline、跨部門協作、新管理流程）。 - Stakeholder success：能否獲得各持份者群體的支持。

F. Project Scorecard — Pennypacker (2005) 十項常用績效量度（§14.2.3） 1. Return on Investment (ROI) = percentage return for every dollar invested 2. Productivity = outputs produced per unit of input 3. Cost of Quality — four categories: prevention, appraisal, internal failure, external failure 4. Cost Performance Index (CPI) = Earned Value ÷ Actual Costs（CPI < 1 = 成本效益差／超支） 5. Schedule Performance Index (SPI) = total authorised project duration ÷ total final project duration（SPI < 1 = 延誤） 6. Customer Satisfaction（hard measures：購買/消費行為；soft measures：意見/感受） 7. Cycle Time = 完成項目所需時間（project life cycle） 8. Requirement Performance 9. Employee Satisfaction Index（soft + hard measures，按重要性加權） 10. Alignment to Strategic Business Goals（是否 doing the right projects）

G. 管理中立 ('Neutrality' until done, §14.3) - 管理者在 implementation 及 post-completion audit 期間對 implementation criteria 保持 neutral；中立行為可 minimise the negative impacts of post-completion audits（最大負面影響：員工因怕事後被追究而不敢提出建議書）。 - 中立管理可 pinpoint personal responsibility without pre-assigning blame，減少 project 'padding'/overestimates 及 intra-firm agency problems。 - 五步驟：(1) Know the goals; (2) Give team members a positive environment; (3) Let team members do their work; (4) Emphasise the right matters; (5) Protect team members with safety standards。 - 中立管理所需技巧：self-confidence, focus, self-control, consistency, conflict resolution skills。

H. 定期制度與常見挑戰（§14.4） - 定期檢討可避免 too many review cycles 引致的 delays and extra costs；包括 streamlining decision authority、minimising the number of people involved in decisions。 - 資本投資項目的六個常見挑戰：unrealistic assumptions; lack of reference to life cycle costs and management costs; unsustainable debt; lack of management capacity; lack of funding; abuses or conflicts。 - 改善途徑（須認出主題）：prioritising capital projects; institutional memory; clear investment objectives（避免 pet projects that fail to meet NPV targets）; regular reviews; tracking ROI; streamlining approvals; frequent forecasts; avoiding management silos; continuous improvement。

考試貼士：本章 OTQ 常考「邊個係主要目的/邊個唔係」及「邊個做法屬於 continuous measurement vs end-of-project」。記住連續量度係 每個 phase 完結時 進行，唔係項目最後先做一次。

3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall 1：Post-completion audit 的「主要目的 (primary purpose)」 vs 「附帶用途 (secondary uses)」 - 對比：主要目的只有一個 — to assess the success of the project（判斷項目成功、達標定落空）。修改預算 (finalise/revise the budget)、評核項目經理個人表現 (manager performance review)、為 budget variances 分配責任 (assigning responsibility) 全部唔係主要目的，只係過程中的產物。 - 例子：審計發現實際成本超支 15%，呢個發現用來判斷「項目是否成功」及「將來點樣改善假設」；但如果管理層將審計變成「找替罪羊」大會，就違背咗審計目的，仲會令員工以後唔敢提案（考官報告指出：考生常誤選「評核經理表現」為審計目的）。

Pitfall 2：Continuous measurement vs Baseline performance system（時點與尺度） - 對比：Continuous measurement 用尺度評分（例如 1–10 scale，10 最好），準則範圍有限但清晰，在每個 phase 末做，作用係在問題惡化前發現 (points out emerging problems before they become critical)；Baseline performance system 係設定最低可接受結果 (minimally acceptable results)，將實際結果同 baseline 比較，同樣在項目各 junctures 進行。兩者都係「中段做」而唔係「項目完先做一次」。 - 例子：兩年項目分六段：每段末用 1–10 分評 budgeting accuracy 同 schedule adherence → continuous measurement；每段末檢查「缺陷率 ≤ 2%」呢條最低線 → baseline performance system。若等到 24 個月後先一次過評核，就兩者都唔係，而係較遜色嘅 end-of-project method。

Question

A company invested heavily to launch a new brand, but revenues are far below expectations. Should the invested capital justify continuing the project? State the rule.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • No — significant capital already invested does NOT justify continuing a project that appears to be failing.
  • Rule: if the future is unlikely to yield expected revenues and profits, cut costs immediately; the evaluation may lead to abandonment or to improvement steps based on comparing initial assumptions with actual results.
  • Review initial assumptions: marketing/consumer information, product testing, pricing competitiveness.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 已經洗咗嘅錢係沉沒成本，唔應該影響去留決定。決定繼續定放棄，要睇將來預期收入同利潤，而唔係「已經投資咗咁多，停咗好蝕」— 呢種諗法只會越蝕越多。

Question

What are the three main benefits (lessons learned) of undertaking a post-completion audit?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • (1) Find lessons for future projects — improve planning, execution and streamline the strategic planning process.
  • (2) Identify problems and find solutions — make changes to improve cash flows and revenues; abandon failing projects and limit losses.

Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

Compare the three evaluation methods: yes/no question list, continuous measurement, and baseline performance system. When is each performed and what are their weaknesses/strengths?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Yes/no list: end-of-project; answer key questions 'yes' or 'no' (may be weighted by importance); only a starting point, NOT conclusive (e.g. 6 out of 10 'yes' is ambiguous).
  • Continuous measurement: limited but clearly expressed criteria measured on a scale (e.g. 1–10); performed at the end of each phase; highlights emerging problems before they become critical — preferred over end-of-project methods.
  • Baseline performance system: set minimally acceptable results in a list of areas; compare results to the baseline at junctures within the project (e.g. a two-year project split into six segments).
  • [中文白話解釋]: 記住三個關鍵分別：yes/no 清單只係起步、唔係結論；continuous measurement 用評分尺度、分段做、可以早啲發現問題；baseline 就係訂條「最低合格線」再逐段對照。考試最鍾意考「continuous measurement 係 phase 末做，唔係項目完先做」。
Flashcard 6 / 共 8 張
Question

A project has an earned value of $2,400,000 and actual costs of $3,000,000. The authorised duration was 24 months but the final duration was 30 months. Calculate the CPI and SPI and interpret them.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • CPI = Earned Value ÷ Actual Costs = $2,400,000 ÷ $3,000,000 = 0.80 → cost efficiency is poor; the work performed cost more than its value (cost overrun).
  • SPI = total authorised project duration ÷ total final project duration = 24 ÷ 30 = 0.80 → project management efficiency on schedule is below 1; the project overran its authorised duration.
  • Both indices < 1 indicate underperformance; compare against benchmarks to decide on improvements.
  • [中文白話解釋]: CPI 係「賺到嘅價值 ÷ 實際使咗嘅錢」，細過 1 即係蝕本／超支；SPI 係「原訂工期 ÷ 最終工期」，細過 1 即係延誤。兩個都係 0.8，代表呢個項目又貴又慢，post-completion audit 要查背後原因。

Flashcard 8 / 共 8 張
Question

What does 'neutral management behaviour' mean in the context of post-completion audits, and what are the five steps managers should follow?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Meaning: managers stay neutral towards the implementation criteria during implementation and the audit; they pinpoint personal responsibility without pre-assigning blame, minimise the negative impacts of post-completion audits (e.g. staff withholding proposals for fear of criticism), and reduce project 'padding'/overestimates and agency problems.
  • Five steps: (1) Know the goals — all team members agree on them; (2) Give team members a positive environment; (3) Let team members do their work (minimum interference); (4) Emphasise the right matters — priorities consistent with known goals; (5) Protect team members with safety standards.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 「中立」即係管理層唔預設立場、唔會未查先鬧，令員工唔使驚住被秋後算賬而唔敢提案；同時靠五招（認清目標、正面環境、唔好 micromanage、捉錯重點、安全保障）令團隊順暢運作。
