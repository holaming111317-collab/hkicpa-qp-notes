M7 Effective Performance Measurement — Model Answers（完成測驗後先好睇！）

Question 1 — Answer: B

Model answer / working: - Project ROI = HK$100,000 / HK$500,000 = 20%. - Division's current ROI = 25%. Because the manager's bonus depends on divisional ROI, accepting a project with ROI of 20% would dilute the division's ROI: - New ROI = (25% × 2,000,000 + 100,000) / (2,000,000 + 500,000) = (500,000 + 100,000) / 2,500,000 = 600,000 / 2,500,000 = 24%. - The manager is therefore incentivised to REJECT the project, even though the project's return (20%) exceeds the company's required rate of return (15%). This is the classic behavioural limitation of ROI: managers avoid projects that lower divisional ROI even when those projects benefit the company as a whole. - Check under RI: project RI = 100,000 − (15% × 500,000) = 100,000 − 75,000 = +HK$25,000 → positive. If the manager were evaluated on RI, the project would be accepted and the decision would be goal-congruent.

【解題思路】 呢題考 ROI 嘅行為缺陷（behavioural implication）。關鍵係經理人嘅花紅同 divisional ROI 掛鈎，所以佢會用「新項目 ROI vs 現行 ROI」做比較，而唔係用公司嘅 required rate of return。項目 ROI 20% < 現行 25% → 拖低部門 ROI 至 24% → 拒絕。呢個就係課本講嘅「managers avoid projects that lower divisional ROI even if they improve ROI for the whole company」。 - A 錯：20% > 15% 從公司角度係啱，但經理人被 ROI 評核，唔會咁諗 —— A 描述嘅其實係用 RI 評核先會出現嘅 goal-congruent 結果。 - C 錯：項目 RI 係正數（+HK$25,000），唔係負數。 - D 錯：RI 確實係絕對金額，但經理人係被 ROI 評核；而且「always motivates acceptance」講得太盡 —— 只有項目回報 ≥ required rate of return 時 RI 先會增加。

Question 2 — Answer: C

Model answer: - Internal process perspective measures the effectiveness and efficiency of the processes that turn inputs into outputs — e.g. cycle times, process efficiencies, yield, defect rates. Production cycle time is therefore an internal process measure.

【解題思路】 平衡計分卡分類題，考你將指標放入正確構面： - A (ROE) 屬 Financial perspective —— 課本列明最常見財務指標係 ROE、sales growth、net profit。 - B (Customer retention rate) 屬 Customer perspective —— 同 satisfaction surveys、complaints、market share 一類。 - C (Production cycle time) 正確 —— cycle time、process efficiency、yield 係 internal process 嘅典型指標。 - D (% of employees trained in food sanitation) 屬 Learning and Growth perspective —— 課本 Exam Practice Q10 明確將呢項歸入 learning and growth，係最常見嘅混淆陷阱（易誤當 internal process）。

Question 3 — Answer: B

Model answer: - ROI, RI and EVA are lagging measures reflecting past performance. Lagging performance measures contain limited information about an organisation's potential for future performance, especially when growth rates are rapid or market circumstances have changed. Companies therefore combine lagging and leading indicators and use both financial and non-financial measures.

【解題思路】 呢題考 financial vs non-financial measures 嘅本質分別，課本原句：「ROI, RI and EVA are lagging measures reflecting past performance」。 - A 錯：剛好調轉 —— ROI/RI/EVA 係 lagging，唔係 leading；leading indicators（如 customer satisfaction、return rates）先係用嚟驅動未來表現。 - C 錯：財務指標嘅缺點之一正正係只反映公司/分部嘅 aggregate performance，唔會突顯個別環節（individual segment）嘅問題。 - D 錯：財務指標提供嘅係 historical（歷史）數據，唔係 forward-looking，所以對即時糾正行動幫助有限 —— 呢個正係要配搭非財務指標嘅原因。

Question 4 — Answers: (a) ROI = 24%; (b) RI = HK$150,000

Model answer / working: - (a) ROI = Net profit (operating income) / Average operating assets - ROI = HK$360,000 / HK$1,500,000 = 0.24 = 24% - (b) RI = Operating income − (Required rate of return × Average operating assets) - Capital charge = 14% × HK$1,500,000 = HK$210,000 - RI = HK$360,000 − HK$210,000 = HK$150,000 - Both measures indicate acceptable performance: ROI of 24% exceeds the 14% required rate, and RI is positive.

【解題思路】 計算步驟： 1. ROI 係比率：利潤 ÷ 平均營運資產，記住分母係 average operating assets（唔係 total assets），所以 360,000 ÷ 1,500,000 = 24%。 2. RI 係絕對金額：先計 capital charge = 14% × 1,500,000 = 210,000，再用 360,000 − 210,000 = 150,000。 常見錯誤： - 將 ROI 同 RI 掉換（RI 答成百分比、ROI 答成金額）。 - RI 誤用 net profit 減去「14% × 利潤」—— required return 係乘營運資產，唔係乘利潤。 - 忘記 RI 公式入面嘅括弧次序：先乘後減。

Question 5 — Answer: EVA = HK$1,000,000

Model answer / working: - EVA = Adjusted after-tax operating income − [WACC × (Adjusted total assets − Current liabilities)] - Step 1 — Capital employed (invested capital): Adjusted total assets − Current liabilities = HK$38,000,000 − HK$6,000,000 = HK$32,000,000. - Step 2 — Capital charge: WACC × capital employed = 10% × HK$32,000,000 = HK$3,200,000. - Step 3 — EVA = HK$4,200,000 − HK$3,200,000 = HK$1,000,000. - Positive EVA means the company generated HK$1,000,000 of value above its after-tax cost of long-term financing.

【解題思路】 EVA 嘅結構同 RI 一樣，但有三個差異要記住：(1) 用 adjusted after-tax operating income；(2) 用 WACC 做資金成本率；(3) 投入資本 = adjusted total assets 減 current liabilities。 逐步做： 1. 先減流動負債：38,000,000 − 6,000,000 = 32,000,000（呢步最常漏 —— 直接乘 38,000,000 會錯得 3,800,000 嘅 capital charge、EVA 變 400,000）。 2. 乘 WACC：32,000,000 × 10% = 3,200,000。 3. 最後相減：4,200,000 − 3,200,000 = 1,000,000。 另一常見錯誤：將 WACC 當成金額直接減，或者忘記 EVA 係「稅後」口徑 —— 題目已直接俾 adjusted after-tax operating income，唔使再乘 (1 − 稅率)。
