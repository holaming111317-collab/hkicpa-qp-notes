M7 Effective Performance Measurement — Study Pack (Week 12)

Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

1. 核心知識架構

  • 績效指標與組織架構：本章由 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) 出發，說明如何按策略選擇、監察及更新指標，並以 Balanced Scorecard（平衡計分卡）的四大構面 —— Financial / Customer / Internal Process / Learning and Growth —— 把策略連結到規劃與控制。
  • 責任中心 (Responsibility Centres)：按經理人的「可控範圍」分為四類 —— Cost Centre（只管成本）、Revenue Centre（只管收入）、Profit Centre（管收入＋成本，以 controllable margin 評核）、Investment Centre（再管投資，以 ROI 評核）。核心原則：只把經理人可控制 (controllable) 的項目納入其 responsibility report。
  • 三大財務績效衡量指標：Return on Investment (ROI)、Residual Income (RI)、Economic Value Added (EVA)。三者計算不同、行為含義 (behavioural implications) 亦不同 —— 這是 OTQ 最常考的辨析點。
  • Segment Reporting：上市公司須作分部報告；判斷門檻為 10%（收入、損益或資產）、已報分部合計須達總收入 75%，分部數目一般以 10 個為上限。
  • 財務 vs 非財務指標及分權 (Decentralisation)：ROI/RI/EVA 屬 lagging measures（反映過去）；須配搭 leading、非財務指標（如 company reputation、customer value、competition、innovative initiatives）。分權有 empowerment 等好處，亦有成本、衝突、風險等壞處。

2. 必背考點清單（公式與規則）

【公式一】Return on Investment (ROI) - ROI = Net profit / Average operating assets（Investment centre 層面：Controllable margin / Average operating assets） - Operating assets = 用於生產商品/服務的所有資產（cash、accounts receivable、inventory、plant and equipment）；排除 non-operating assets（如對其他實體的投資、租予他人的物業）。 - 用 average（期初＋期末平均）營運資產的兩個原因：(1) 衡量整段期間而非期末時點；(2) 防止經理人在衡量時點暫時壓低投資以操縱指標。 - 評核分部時，只應包括經理人可控制的資產。

【公式二】DuPont 分解 - ROI = (Net profit / Sales) × (Sales / Average operating assets) = Net profit margin × Asset turnover - 用途：找出 ROI 偏低的根源 —— 是 profit margin 問題還是 asset turnover 問題。 - 課本例子：Net profit margin 3.8% × Asset turnover 1.5 = ROI 5.7%。

【公式三】Residual Income (RI) - RI = Operating income − (Required rate of return × Average operating assets) - Required rate of return 又稱 capital charge。RI 是絕對金額 (absolute dollar value)，投資額只用來計算 required return，不作分母 → 投資規模對 RI 的影響小於對 ROI 的影響。 - 行為含義：以 RI 評核時，只要項目回報 ≥ required rate of return，經理人便願意投資 —— 與公司整體利益一致 (goal congruence)。

【公式四】Economic Value Added (EVA) - EVA = Adjusted after-tax operating income − [WACC × (Adjusted total assets − Current liabilities)] - EVA 是經調整的 residual income：以 after-tax operating income 取代 EBIT（與 after-tax WACC 一致，並給予經理人減稅誘因）；R&D 在 EVA 下通常資本化（鼓勵長遠投資）；GAAP 下的 operating leases 在 EVA 下當 capital leases 處理（防止借租賃低估資產投資）。 - 缺點：cost of capital 及調整均涉 judgement；複雜、需顧問、昂貴費時，較適合公司較高層次使用。

【公式五】Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) - WACC = (E/V × Re) + (D/V × Rd × (1 − Tc)) - Re = cost of equity；Rd = cost of debt；E、D = 權益及債務的市值 (market value)（不用 book value）；V = E + D；Tc = corporate tax rate。 - WACC 是滿足債權人與投資者所需的最低回報；用 WACC 折現項目現金流，只在 (1) 項目的 systematic risk 與公司整體相若、及 (2) 項目採用與公司相同的債/股融資比例時才適當。否則會錯誤接受負 NPV 項目（項目 beta 高於公司）或錯誤拒絕正 NPV 項目（項目 beta 低於公司）— 參考 SML–WACC 圖（Exhibit 15.1）。

【規則六】四類 Responsibility Centres（LO 6.01.02）

Centre 控制範圍 評核基礎
Cost centre 成本（不直接產生收入） Controllable costs vs budget
Revenue centre 收入（及銷售/市場費用） Actual vs budgeted revenue
Profit centre 收入＋成本 Controllable margin = controllable revenues − controllable costs（direct/traceable fixed costs 可控要扣；indirect/common fixed costs 不可控不扣）
Investment centre 收入＋成本＋投資 ROI（經理人可控制公式兩個因子）

【規則七】Segment Reporting 門檻 - 僅 publicly held entities 必須作 segment reporting；private companies 不需要。 - 單獨披露門檻：分部佔公司收入、損益或資產 ≥10%。 - 已報分部收入合計 <75% 總收入時，須增加分部。 - 分部超過 10 個時宜合併；性質相近（產品、服務、流程、客戶、分銷方法、監管環境）的分部可 aggregate。

【規則八】Financial vs Non-financial Measures - ROI/RI/EVA 是 lagging measures（反映過去表現）；須配搭 leading / non-financial measures 驅動未來表現。 - 四大非財務衡量領域：company reputation、customer value、competition、innovative initiatives。 - 非財務指標優點：連結策略、追蹤無形資產、預示未來、變數較少受外間控制。缺點：追蹤成本可能高於效益、無 standard denominator（主觀）、缺乏因果關係 (lack of causality)、統計上不可靠（依賴調查）、指標太多會瓦解衡量本身。

【規則九】Decentralisation - 優點：empowerment（員工自主、減少官僚）、營運負擔分散（高層可專注策略）、應對突發事件較有效、較易擴張。 - 缺點：成本較高（需更多有技能的經理人、工作重複）、部門間衝突與 fragmentation、公司層面緊急事件下或無權決策、決策錯誤風險增加（資訊不全）、不適合須中央決策的服務（如 accounting）。

3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

對比一：ROI vs RI —— 比率 vs 絕對金額，行為後果大不同 - ROI 是百分比：分部經理人會拒絕任何回報率低於分部現行 ROI 的項目 —— 即使該項目回報高於公司 required rate of return、對公司整體有利。RI 是絕對金額：只要項目回報 ≥ required rate of return，RI 便增加，經理人便會接受，決策與公司目標一致。 - 例子：分部現行 ROI = 30%，新項目 ROI = 20%，RRR = 15%。用 ROI 評核 → 經理人拒絕（拖低 30%）；用 RI 評核 → 接受（項目貢獻正 RI）。但 RI 的弱點是不能跨規模比較——大分部天然 RI 較高（如新界人口稀少地區的項目 RI 幾乎必然低於九龍密集地區）。 - 考試貼士：題目問「經理人被評核指標誘導作出次優決策」時，先判斷該經理人是被 ROI 還是 RI 評核 —— 答案方向完全相反。

對比二：Financial (lagging) vs Non-financial (leading) measures - Financial measures（ROI、RI、EVA、net profit）是 lagging indicators：反映過去表現，屬歷史數據，對快速糾正行動幫助有限，且傾向只顯示公司/分部整體而掩蓋個別環節問題（net profit 上升時 net return 可能正在下降）。 - Non-financial measures（customer satisfaction、defect rate、on-time delivery、employee training）多為 leading indicators：指向未來價值的驅動因素，但主觀、無統一計量基準、難證明因果。 - 例子：一家餐廳 net profit 連年上升，但 customer complaints 急增、retention rate 下跌 —— 單看財務指標會誤判；平衡計分卡正是要同時監察 hard/soft、internal/external、short-term/long-term 指標。 - 考試貼士：OTQ 問「以下哪項是 lagging indicator?」—— 記住課本原句：「ROI, RI and EVA are lagging measures reflecting past performance」。

Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

What are the four types of responsibility centres, and what does each manager control?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Cost centre – incurs costs/expenses but does not directly generate revenue (e.g. purchasing dept); assessed on controllable costs vs budget.
  • Revenue centre – primarily responsible for generating revenue (e.g. sales dept); does not control costs or asset investment.
  • Profit centre – incurs costs AND generates revenue; evaluated on controllable margin (controllable revenues − controllable costs; direct/traceable fixed costs are deducted, indirect/common fixed costs are not).
  • Investment centre – controls costs, revenue AND investment funds; evaluated using ROI.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 四類責任中心的分別在於經理人「管到幾多」：成本中心只管成本、收入中心只管收入、利潤中心收入成本都管（用 controllable margin 評核，只扣可控的 direct fixed costs）、投資中心連投資決策都管（用 ROI 評核）。記住大原則：評核報告只包括經理人可控制的項目。
Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

What makes a good KPI, and what is the main behavioural risk of KPI monitoring?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • A good KPI is: easy to understand; readily quantifiable; easy to monitor; linked to company strategy.
  • KPIs may be operational (short-term, efficiency-focused) or strategic (long-term, firm-specific).
  • Main risk: over-emphasis on a defined KPI gives employees a perverse incentive to hit the target at the expense of quality or accuracy (e.g. 5,000 units per shift target leading to higher defect rates).
  • [中文白話解釋]: 好 KPI 要易明、易量化、易監察、同公司策略掛鈎。但要小心「為達標而達標」的扭曲誘因 —— 課本用 Toyota 的 Jidoka 做例子：原本員工不敢叫停生產線，改為任何人發現缺陷即可停線後，質素和整體效率反而提升。
Flashcard 3 / 共 8 張
Question

State the ROI formula, and show how the DuPont model breaks it down.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • ROI = Net profit / Average operating assets.
  • Operating assets = assets used in production of goods/services (cash, AR, inventory, plant & equipment); non-operating assets are excluded.
  • Average (opening + closing) is used to (1) capture operations over the whole period and (2) prevent manipulation by temporarily cutting investment at measurement date.
  • DuPont: ROI = (Net profit / Sales) × (Sales / Average operating assets) = Net profit margin × Asset turnover.
  • [中文白話解釋]: ROI 即「賺幾多利潤相對於投入的營運資產」。DuPont 模型將它拆成兩瓣：利潤率（每元銷售賺幾多）乘以資產週轉（資產一年轉幾多次）。考試若話 ROI 低過行業平均，就要用 DuPont 診斷到底係 margin 定 turnover 出問題。記住要用「平均」營運資產，唔好用期末數。
Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

A division reports operating income of HK$240,000 on average operating assets of HK$1,000,000. The company's required rate of return is 12%. Calculate the division's residual income (RI) and its ROI.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • RI = Operating income − (Required rate of return × Average operating assets)
  • RI = 240,000 − (12% × 1,000,000) = 240,000 − 120,000 = HK$120,000.
  • ROI = 240,000 / 1,000,000 = 24%.
  • Interpretation: the division earns HK$120,000 above the capital charge; both measures signal acceptable performance.
  • [中文白話解釋]: RI 好簡單：實際營運利潤減去「資本收費」（required return × 營運資產）。呢個部門賺 24 萬，公司要求最低回報 12 萬，所以剩餘收益係 12 萬。同一組數字，ROI 係 24%。考試經常同一個 scenario 叫你計兩樣，記住 RI 係絕對金額、ROI 係百分比。
Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

State the EVA formula and name two accounting adjustments typically made in EVA calculations.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • EVA = Adjusted after-tax operating income − [WACC × (Adjusted total assets − Current liabilities)].
  • Adjustment 1: substitute after-tax operating income for EBIT — consistent with after-tax WACC and incentivises managers to reduce taxes.
  • Adjustment 2: R&D costs, expensed under most accounting standards, are often capitalised in EVA — encourages long-term investment.
  • Adjustment 3: long-term (operating) leases under GAAP are treated as capital leases in EVA — removes the incentive to understate asset investment via operating leases.
  • Disadvantages: cost of capital and adjustments involve judgement; EVA is complex, can be expensive/lengthy, and suits higher organisational levels.
  • [中文白話解釋]: EVA 其實係「改良版 RI」：用稅後營運利潤，扣 WACC 乘以投入資本（調整後總資產減流動負債）。重點調整：R&D 由費用改為資本化、經營租賃當融資租賃計 —— 目的係防止經理人因會計處理而唔敢作長遠投資。代價係複雜同主觀。
Flashcard 6 / 共 8 張
Question

A firm has market value of equity E = HK$30m and debt D = HK$10m; cost of equity Re = 14%, pre-tax cost of debt Rd = 6%, corporate tax rate Tc = 16.5%. Calculate WACC, and state the two conditions under which WACC is an appropriate project discount rate.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • V = E + D = 40; E/V = 0.75; D/V = 0.25.
  • WACC = (0.75 × 14%) + (0.25 × 6% × (1 − 16.5%)) = 10.5% + 1.2525% = 11.7525% ≈ 11.75%.
  • Weights use MARKET values, not book values.
  • WACC is appropriate as project discount rate only if: (1) the project's systematic risk is the same as the company's overall portfolio; and (2) the project uses the same debt/preference/common share financing proportions as the company.
  • [中文白話解釋]: WACC 係債同股按市值加權的平均資金成本，債息要乘 (1 − 稅率) 因為利息可扣稅。用 WACC 折現項目有兩個前提：項目系統性風險同公司整體一樣、融資比例一樣。否則（參考 SML–WACC 圖）會錯收高風險負 NPV 項目、錯拒低風險正 NPV 項目，長遠拖低股東價值。
Flashcard 7 / 共 8 張
Question

Name the four perspectives of the balanced scorecard and give one typical measure for each.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Financial perspective: ROE, sales growth, net profit — shows the bottom-line impact of strategy.
  • Customer perspective: customer satisfaction surveys, number of complaints, market share, retention rates.
  • Internal process perspective: cycle times, process efficiency, yield, defect rates.
  • Learning and growth perspective: employee training, innovation/new product development, employee satisfaction/turnover.
  • The scorecard balances financial/non-financial, short/long-term, leading/lagging, hard/soft, and internal/external measures.

Question

What are the key thresholds/guidelines that determine which segments must be separately reported in segment reporting?

Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Segment reporting is required for publicly held entities only (not private companies); disclosed with the financial statements.
  • Report separately any segment representing ≥10% of the company's revenues, profits/losses or assets.
  • If total revenue of reported segments is less than 75% of total company revenue, add more segments.
  • If more than 10 segments would be reported, consider combining segments.
  • Segments with similar products, services, processes, customers, distribution methods or regulatory environments may be aggregated.
