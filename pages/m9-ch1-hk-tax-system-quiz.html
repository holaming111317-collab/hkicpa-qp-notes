Phase 3: Exam-style Quiz 模擬測驗 (100% OTQ, ALL IN ENGLISH)

Question 1 (MCQ) Mr Ko is a tax resident of the Mainland who stays in Hong Kong for only 60 days in the year of assessment. He carries on a consultancy business through a Hong Kong office and derives fees of HK$800,000 from clients for services performed entirely in Hong Kong. Which of the following statements is CORRECT?

A. Mr Ko is not chargeable to Hong Kong profits tax because he is not a Hong Kong resident. B. Mr Ko is chargeable to profits tax because he carries on a business in Hong Kong and derives Hong Kong source profits from that business. C. Mr Ko is chargeable to salaries tax because the fees are earned from services rendered in Hong Kong. D. Mr Ko is chargeable to profits tax on a withholding basis because he is a non-resident.

Question 2 (MCQ) Identify which of the following has legally binding effect in Hong Kong tax law.

A. Departmental Interpretation and Practice Notes issued by the IRD B. Decisions of the Inland Revenue Board of Review C. A decision of the Hong Kong Court of Final Appeal on a point of tax law D. A decision of an Australian court on a similar tax provision

Question 3 (MCQ) Identify which of the following statements about the Hong Kong tax system is NOT correct.

A. Hong Kong adopts a schedular system under which salary income, rental income and business profits are assessed separately unless personal assessment is elected. B. A Hong Kong company paying interest to a non-resident bank with no business presence in Hong Kong must withhold profits tax from the payment. C. The IRD adopts an 'assess first, audit later' approach in administering the IRO. D. Under Article 106 of the Basic Law, the Central People's Government shall not levy taxes in the Hong Kong SAR.

Question 4 (Objective calculation) Bright Star Limited, a corporation carrying on business in Hong Kong, has Hong Kong source assessable profits of HK$5,000,000 for the year of assessment. Assuming the two-tiered profits tax rates apply, calculate the company's profits tax payable (in HK$).

Question 5 (Objective calculation) Ms Lee, an individual, owns a flat in Hong Kong which she let for the full year of assessment at a monthly rent of HK$30,000. She is a non-resident of Hong Kong. Calculate the property tax payable for the year of assessment (in HK$).
