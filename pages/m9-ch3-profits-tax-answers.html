Retest R1 — Answer: (i) 31 March 2027; (ii) Yes, in time; (iii) s.60(1) IRO

Model Answer: Under s.60(1) IRO, an additional assessment must be raised within six years of the end of the year of assessment to which it relates. The year of assessment 2020/21 ended on 31 March 2021, so the latest date is 31 March 2027. The audit discovery in September 2026 is therefore within time and the additional assessment may validly be raised. The extended ten-year limit under s.60(1)(b) applies only where the undercharge involves fraud or wilful evasion, which is excluded here.

【解題思路】計時起點係 YA 結束日（31/3/2021），唔係首次評稅日（10/6/2021）、唔係 audit 發現日。6 年 → 31/3/2027。10 年條款只限 fraud/wilful evasion。Fill-in 電報式作答示範：31 March 2027; yes, in time; s.60(1) — 6 years from end of YA 已經足夠攞滿分。

Question 1 — Correct Answer: B

English Model Answer:

Option B is a revenue receipt. Compensation received for the temporary loss of use of an asset is revenue in nature because it represents the trading profits which the owner would have earned had it had the use of the asset (London & Thames Haven Oil Wharves Ltd v Attwooll (1966) 43 TC 491). The sum takes the character of the trading profits it replaces (Lincolnshire Sugar v Smart (1937) AC 697).

【解題思路】 - 核心概念：賠償金的性質取決於它「取代了甚麼」——取代營業利潤 → revenue（應課稅）；取代資本資產或業務結構 → capital（s.14(1) 豁免）。 - A 錯：取消構成企業整個業務框架（the very structure of the business）的總代理協議，賠償屬 capital receipt，不課稅（Van den Berghs Ltd v Clark (1935) AC 431）。結構性合約本身視同資本資產。 - C 錯：Harbour Textiles 是成衣貿易商，寫字樓物業不是其 trading stock；自用八年、被動接受收購建議，按 badges of trade 整體評估屬出售資本資產，收益獲 s.14(1) 豁免。 - D 錯：來自已課利得稅公司的股息按 s.26 豁免（避免雙重課稅）。 - 邊界陷阱：本題正是 capital / revenue 的分界線——「暫時失去使用權」的賠償是 revenue，「資產永久喪失或絕育（sterilisation）」的賠償才是 capital（Glenboig Union Fireclay Co Ltd v IRC (1921)）。

Question 2 — Correct Answer: B

English Model Answer:

Option B is deductible under s.16(1)(d). A trade debt is allowable where it (i) has been proved to the satisfaction of the assessor to have become bad during the year (commercially irrecoverable — recovery actions taken but in vain), and (ii) was previously included as a taxable trading receipt (or was money lent in the ordinary course of a money-lending business carried on in Hong Kong).

【解題思路】 - A 錯：一般撥備（general provision）憑粗略估計、無足夠文件證據，不被視為已「招致（incurred）」的開支（CIR v Lo & Lo (1984)），不可扣；只有具體撥備（specific provision）才可扣。 - C 錯：購置營業處所的成本（包括相關印花稅及律師費）屬資本性開支，被 s.17(1)(c) 禁止扣除（可考慮 CBA 每年 4%，但不能直接扣除）。 - D 錯：認可慈善捐款（s.16D）必須捐予按香港法律屬慈善、獲 s.88 豁免的機構；僅在內地運作的教育基金不符合資格，全數不可扣（亦不在 HK$100–35% 的扣除機制之內）。 - 答題順序口訣：先問 s.16(1)（是否為產生應課稅利潤而招致）→ 再問 s.17 有無禁止 → 最後看 ss.16A–16L 有無明訂扣除。

Question 3 — Correct Answer: A

English Model Answer:

Under DIPN 21, the locality of trading profits is generally determined by the place where the contracts of purchase and sale are effected, "effected" embracing the whole process of contract formation (solicitation of orders, negotiation, conclusion, trade financing, shipment and performance — CIR v Magna Industrial Co Ltd (1997) HKLRD 171). Where either contract is effected in Hong Kong, the IRD's initial presumption is that the profits are fully taxable, although in practice greater weight is usually attached to the contract of sale. Here, the purchase contract was effected in Hong Kong, so the profits are prima facie Hong Kong source and fully chargeable.

【解題思路】 - B 錯：雖然實務上售貨合約（contract of sale）權重較大，但 DIPN 21 的初步推定是「買或賣其中一份在港洽訂 → 全部應課稅」，不能單憑售貨合約在倫敦就斷定離岸。 - C 錯：50:50 分攤只適用於 contract processing（來料加工——港方參與內地製造工序且法律所有權不轉移）的製造業安排；本案是純貿易（trading），不適用，稅局實務上對貿易利潤採「全有或全無（all-or-nothing）」取態。 - D 錯：客戶所在地及貨物交付地並非決定性因素；來源地看的是產生利潤的運作（operations test, CIR v Hang Seng Bank (1991)），即合約洽訂的實質過程在哪裡進行。 - 考試貼士：見到 trading profit 來源題，立即寫出「where the contracts of purchase and sale are effected」再逐步分析，切勿用居民身分或收付款地點作答。

【解題思路】 - 第一步：按年度免稅額率分 pool——motor lorry 入 30% pool，photocopier 入 20% pool。 - 第二步：每項新購資產先有 IA = 成本 × 60%（s.39B(1)）：lorry 300,000 × 60% = 180,000；photocopier 60,000 × 60% = 36,000。 - 第三步：扣完 IA 後的 reducing value 再按 pool 率計 AA：lorry 120,000 × 30% = 36,000；photocopier 24,000 × 20% = 4,800。 - 第四步：IA + AA 合計 = 216,000 + 40,800 = HK$256,800。 - 常見錯誤：(i) 把 AA 按原成本而非 reducing value 計（AA 會錯計為 90,000 + 12,000 = 102,000，合計錯得 318,000）；(ii) 忘記 IA 是 60% 而非 100%（100% 只適用於 s.16G PFA 等明訂扣除，題目已表明不適用）；(iii) 把不同比率的資產放入同一 pool。 - 考試貼士：題目列明 “None of the assets qualifies under ss.16B, 16G or 16H–16K” 就是要排除 100% 即扣的誘惑——讀到這句要立刻確認走 s.39B pooling 路線。

Question 5 — Correct Answers: (i) HK$1,950,000; (ii) HK$160,875

English Model Answer & Working:

Starbright Ltd — Profits Tax Computation

HK$
Net profit before taxation 2,400,000
Less: Gain on disposal of office premises (capital asset, exempt under s.14(1)) (300,000)
Less: Hong Kong bank deposit interest (exempt — Exemption from Profits Tax (Interest Income) Order; deposit not used to secure any borrowing) (40,000)
Less: Dividend from Hong Kong subsidiary chargeable to profits tax (exempt under s.26) (60,000)
Add: Accounting depreciation (non-deductible, s.17(1)(c)) 120,000
Add: Fine for breach of law (non-deductible, s.17) 30,000
2,150,000
Less: Depreciation allowances (Part VI / s.18F) (200,000)
Net assessable profits 1,950,000

Profits tax payable (two-tiered rates, corporation): - HK$1,950,000 ≤ HK$2,000,000 → wholly at 8.25% - Tax payable = 1,950,000 × 8.25% = HK$160,875

【解題思路】 - 按利得稅計算五部曲：(1) 扣非應評稅收入；(2) 加回不可扣開支；(3) 加 balancing charge（本題無）；(4) 扣折舊免稅額；(5) 扣結轉虧損（本題無）。 - 豁免收入三兄弟：資本收益（s.14(1)）、港銀行存款利息（存款無抵押可扣稅借款，Exemption Order 適用）、已課稅公司股息（s.26）——全部要從會計利潤剔除。 - 加回項目：會計折舊屬資本性攤分，s.17(1)(c) 不扣，由稅務折舊免稅額（HK$200,000）取代；罰款（penalties/fines for breaking the law）明確不可扣。 - 稅款計算：應評稅利潤 HK$1,950,000 未超過 HK$2M 門檻，全額按 8.25%，無須分段。HK$1,950,000 × 8.25% = HK$160,875。 - 常見錯誤：(i) 忘記剔除資本收益，把 HK$300,000 留低計稅；(ii) 誤以為銀行利息一律要課稅——留意題目關鍵句 “not used to secure any borrowing”，若存款用作抵押而借款利息獲扣，豁免即不適用；(iii) 兩級制下誤用 16.5% 全額計，或對首 HK$2M 誤用 15%/7.5%（非法團稅率）；(iv) 忘記扣除稅務折舊免稅額而直接以 HK$2,150,000 計稅。
