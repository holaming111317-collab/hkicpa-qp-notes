M9 Property Tax — Model Answers（完成測驗後先好睇！）

Question 1 — Correct Answer: C

Model Answer:

Under s.5(2)(a) of the IRO, only a corporation carrying on a trade, profession or business in Hong Kong may apply in writing to the CIR for an exemption from property tax, where the property is occupied or used by it for the purpose of producing profits chargeable to profits tax. The exemption is not available to individuals, sole proprietors, partnerships or other persons. "Corporation" is defined in s.2(1) to mean any company incorporated or registered under any enactment or charter in force in Hong Kong or elsewhere, but excludes a co-operative society or a trade union. The application is made for each property and, once granted, continues in subsequent years until the circumstances change (any change must be notified within 30 days under s.5(2)(c)).

【解題思路】

  1. 第一步認住 s.5(2)(a) 豁免的唯一受惠對象係「corporation」，而且要「carrying on a trade, profession or business in Hong Kong」，租金收入已計入應課利得稅利潤 — C 完全吻合。
  2. A 錯：個人（不論是否香港永久居民）絕對不能申請物業稅豁免，residence status 無關。
  3. B 錯：係常見陷阱 — 獨資東主 (sole proprietor) 就算租金收入課緊利得稅，都唔係 corporation，冇資格申請豁免。
  4. D 錯：合夥 (partnership) 同樣唔係 s.2(1) 定義下的 corporation，不能申請。
  5. 記憶口訣：「exemption for corporations only；個人想享此待遇，要成立公司並將物業轉名畀公司」。

Question 2 — Correct Answer: C

Model Answer:

Property tax is charged on the owner of land/buildings situated in Hong Kong (s.5(1)); the occupier or tenant is never chargeable to property tax. A person who sub-lets a property is not the owner, so property tax cannot be charged on sub-letting income. Under DIPN 14, sub-letting by any person other than a corporation (and letting/sub-letting by a corporation) amounts to a business, and the rental receipts are chargeable to profits tax under s.14 of the IRO. Mr Chan remains chargeable to property tax only on the consideration payable to him by Company X — not on Company X's sub-letting income.

【解題思路】

  1. 核心概念：物業稅徵稅對象係業主，唔係佔用人 (occupier)、租客或二房東。所以先排除 B（佔用人 Company Y 唔使交物業稅）。
  2. A 錯：Company X 雖然收租，但佢唔係業主（佢只係租客再分租），物業稅徵唔到佢。
  3. D 錯：Mr Chan 只係就自己應收嘅代價（即 Company X 付畀佢嘅租金）交物業稅；Company X 向 Company Y 收取嘅分租租金與 Mr Chan 無關。
  4. C 啱：DIPN 14 講明任何非公司人士分租 (sub-letting) 都構成業務，收入課利得稅 (s.14) 而唔係物業稅 — 呢個正正係 property tax 與 profits tax 嘅接口位。
  5. 考官報告指出：考生常混淆「邊個係納稅人」，記住物業稅永遠跟業主身份走，唔跟「邊個收錢」走。

Question 3 — Correct Answer: A

Model Answer:

The assessable value is the consideration in money or money's worth payable to the owner for the right of use of the property (s.5B(2)). It includes: rent received and receivable; payments for the right of use of premises under licence (e.g. advertisement signs on a wall or roof); payment for the use of furniture; management fees paid to the owner (s.5B(6)); non-refundable deposits (treated as premiums per DIPN 4); owner's expenditure borne by the tenant; lump sum premiums; and rent recovered. A refundable rental deposit, which is repayable to the tenant in full upon expiry of the lease if no default occurs, is not consideration for the right of use and is not included in the assessable value.

【解題思路】

  1. 逐個選項用「係咪就使用權應付畀業主嘅代價」嚟測試。
  2. A 啱（即係唔計入）：可退還按金只係擔保性質，期滿要還，唔係代價；只有不可退還按金先當 premium 課稅（DIPN 4）。呢個係 money's worth 概念最容易考倒人嘅位。
  3. B 錯（要計入）：許可證費用（licence fee，例如喺外牆/天台放廣告牌、車位費）屬 right of use 嘅代價，要計入應評稅值。
  4. C 錯（要計入）：管理費付畀業主本人（而非第三方管理公司）屬 s.5B(6) 下與使用權相關嘅服務代價，要計入。
  5. D 錯（要計入）：業主開支（維修）由租客承擔而業主拒絕償還，視作 assessable value。
  6. 考官報告指出：考生經常將「按金」同「頂手費」混為一談 — 分辨關鍵係期滿可唔可以退還。

Question 4 — Correct Answer: HK$18,840

Model Answer / Working:

Grace — Property Tax Computation for the year of assessment 2018/19

Item Working HK$
Rent HK$18,000 × 8 months (9 months in YA 2018/19 from 1 Jul 2018 to 31 Mar 2019, less 1 rent-free month; no rent accrues in a rent-free period) 144,000
Lump sum premium HK$72,000 × 9/36 (3-year lease → spread over the shorter of 36 months or 3 years = 36 months; 9 months fall in YA 2018/19 including the rent-free month) 18,000
Owner's expenditure borne by tenant Repair paid by tenant and not repaid by owner 4,000
Assessable value 166,000
Less: Rates paid by owner HK$3,000 × 3 quarters (s.5(1A)(b)(i)) (9,000)
157,000
Less: 20% statutory deduction HK$157,000 × 20% (s.5(1)(b)(ii)) (31,400)
Net assessable value 125,600
Property tax payable HK$125,600 × 15% 18,840

Note: The building management fees paid by Grace to the management company are not deductible — all repairs and outgoings are deemed covered by the 20% statutory deduction.

【解題思路】

  1. 租金月份數：租約 2018 年 7 月 1 日開始，2018/19 課稅年度覆蓋 9 個月（7 月至翌年 3 月），扣除 1 個月免租期 → 8 個月 × $18,000 = $144,000。常見錯誤係計 9 個月租金 — 免租期冇租金應收，唔計。
  2. 頂手費攤分：s.5B(4) 規定按租期或三年取較短者 — 3 年租 = 36 個月。注意：攤分基期包括免租期，所以 2018/19 佔 9 個月，$72,000 × 9/36 = $18,000。常見錯誤係用 8 個月（跟租金）或分 3 年直接除 3。
  3. 租客代付維修費：業主拒絕償還 → $4,000 屬 assessable value；若業主有償還則唔計。
  4. 差餉：業主自己同意並繳付的差餉先扣得（$3,000 × 3 = $9,000）；次序係扣咗差餉之後先計 20%。
  5. 20% 法定扣除：按 $157,000（扣差餉後餘額）計，唔係按總租金 — 呢個係考官常抓嘅錯。
  6. 管理費：業主付畀管理公司嘅管理費屬實際開支，畀 20% 扣除額覆蓋，不得另扣；按揭利息、政府地租都係同一道理。

Question 5 — Correct Answer: HK$30,000

Model Answer / Working:

Item Working HK$
Final property tax for YA 2018/19 HK$180,000 × 15% 27,000
Less: Provisional property tax charged for 2018/19 (paid) (24,000)
Balance of final tax payable 3,000
Add: Provisional property tax for 2019/20 Standard rate on the NAV of the preceding year: HK$180,000 × 15% (s.63L) 27,000
Total property tax payable under the 2018/19 notice of assessment 30,000

The provisional property tax is payable in two instalments — normally the first instalment falls in November of the year of issue and the second in April of the following year.

【解題思路】

  1. 第一步用公式：應評稅淨值 × 15% = 最終稅款（final tax）= $180,000 × 15% = $27,000。
  2. 第二步扣減已繳的本年度暫繳稅 $24,000 → 尾數 $3,000。呢步最易漏 — 暫繳稅係「預繳」，final tax 要對銷。
  3. 第三步加返下一年度 (2019/20) 暫繳稅：按 s.63L 用上一年度嘅 NAV 按標準稅率計 → 同樣係 $27,000。
  4. 總應繳 = $3,000 + $27,000 = $30,000，分兩期繳交（通常 11 月及翌年 4 月）。
  5. 常見錯誤：(i) 忘記扣已繳暫繳稅，答 $54,000；(ii) 忘記加下年暫繳稅，只答 $3,000。記住評稅通知書上嘅總額 = 尾數 + 新一年暫繳。
