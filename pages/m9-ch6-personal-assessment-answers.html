Question 1 — Answer: C

Full Model Answer (English) Under IRO s.41, personal assessment may be elected by an individual who is (i) aged 18 or above (permitted under that age only if both parents are dead), and (ii) either a permanent resident (one who ordinarily resides in Hong Kong — s.41(4)) or a temporary resident (stays in Hong Kong for more than 180 days during the year of assessment, or more than 300 days over two consecutive years of assessment, one of which is the election year). Up to and including 2017/18, a married individual who is himself/herself neither a permanent nor a temporary resident may nevertheless elect if his/her spouse is a permanent or temporary resident of Hong Kong. Option C satisfies this spousal route (assuming the age requirement is met — he is 30).

  • A is wrong: an individual below 18 may elect only if both parents are dead; here they are alive.
  • B is wrong: 150 days in the election year fails the 180-day test, and 150 + 120 = 270 days fails the 300-day two-year test; the individual is neither a permanent nor a temporary resident and has no spouse to rely on.
  • D is wrong: holding a Hong Kong permanent identity card (right of abode) does not necessarily mean the individual "ordinarily resides" in Hong Kong (Board of Review case D11/15).

【解題思路】呢題考「邊個有資格選擇」嘅概念分辨。記住三個條件：個人、18 歲以上（父母雙亡例外）、通常居港或通過 180/300 天測試。干擾項設計全部圍繞常見謬誤：A 係年齡例外搞錯（父母要雙亡先可以未滿 18 歲選擇）；B 係日數測試計錯（150 日唔夠 180，兩年加埋 270 日又唔夠 300）；D 係 D11/15 案例嘅重點 — 有居留權／永久身份證 ≠ 通常居住。C 係已婚人士靠配偶居民身份取得資格（適用至 2017/18），所以正確。

Question 2 — Answer: B

Full Model Answer (English) Under personal assessment, current-year business losses from a sole-proprietorship business (and any share of partnership losses) may be offset against the individual's other income (net assessable income, net assessable value, assessable profits of other businesses). Where the aggregate of concessionary deductions and business losses exceeds total income, only the excess representing business losses is carried forward to set off against future total income.

  • A is wrong: offshore business losses are NOT deductible under personal assessment.
  • C is wrong: mortgage interest on a rental property is capped at that property's net assessable value; the excess can neither create a loss nor be carried forward (D51/04).
  • D is wrong: any excess over total income that represents concessionary deductions (not business losses) lapses permanently — it cannot be carried forward.

【解題思路】呢題考「PA 下邊啲虧損／扣除可以抵銷」。B 係正確嘅完整描述：本年業務虧損可跨收入類別抵銷，用唔晒嘅部分（只限業務虧損）結轉。A 係 offshore loss，明確不可扣；C 係 NAV 上限陷阱 — 超額利息永久作廢；D 係結轉規則陷阱 — concessionary deductions 超額部分唔可以結轉（只有 business loss 部分可以），呢點源自 Illustrative Example 13，係最易中嘅伏。

Question 3 — Answer: B

Full Model Answer (English) For a married couple electing personal assessment, the reduced total incomes are first computed individually and then aggregated; tax is computed on the joint total, and the tax payable is apportioned between the husband and wife in the proportion that each spouse's reduced total income bears to the joint reduced total income. Separate notices of assessment are issued to each spouse for the apportioned tax.

  • A is wrong: up to and including 2017/18 there is a joint election requirement — where both spouses have assessable income and both are eligible, BOTH must elect; separate election by one spouse is only possible from 2018/19 onwards (or where only one spouse has assessable income / is eligible).
  • C is wrong: under s.42(5) IRO, where personal assessment is elected, a spouse's business loss IS set off against the total income of both spouses before any residue is carried forward (inter-spouse loss set-off).
  • D is wrong: a couple who marry during a year of assessment are deemed to be married at the commencement of that year.

【解題思路】呢題考夫婦規則。B 係正確：稅款合併計、按 reduced total income 比例分攤、各自收評稅通知書（唔可以由一方代收）。A 係年份陷阱 — 2017/18 或之前必須共同選擇，2018/19 起先可以單獨選；C 同 s.42(5) 講法剛好相反 — PA 正正就係容許配偶間虧損抵銷；D 違反「年中結婚視為年初已結婚」嘅 deemed 規則。

【解題思路】一步步嚟： 1. 先將租金轉做 NAV：HK$300,000 × 80% = HK$240,000（唔好直接用租金入賬）。 2. 利息扣除封頂喺 NAV — 實付 HK$260,000 只扣 HK$240,000，多咗嘅 HK$20,000 作廢。呢度係第一個陷阱。 3. 捐款檢查 35% 上限：base = 總入息減利息 = HK$400,000，35% = HK$140,000，實捐 HK$50,000 可以全扣。 4. 扣埋 MPF HK$18,000、業務虧損 HK$150,000，得出 reduced total income HK$182,000。 5. 再扣 basic allowance HK$132,000 得 chargeable income HK$50,000。 6. 兩條數取低：累進 = 900 + 350 = HK$1,250；標準稅率 = 182,000 × 15% = HK$27,300（記住標準稅率嗰邊唔扣免稅額）。答案 HK$1,250。 常見錯誤：利息唔封頂（會令虧損結轉計錯）、標準稅率邊扣埋免稅額、忘記捐款 35% 上限檢查。
