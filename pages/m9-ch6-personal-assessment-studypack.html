M9 Personal Assessment — Study Pack (Week 7)

Phase 1: Core Concepts & Formula Cheat Sheet 重點梳理與速查表

1. 核心知識架構

  • Personal Assessment (PA) 的性質：個人入息課稅是一種稅務寬減 (a relief from tax)，而不是一項獨立稅種 (not a separate charge of tax)。它把個人來自物業 (Net Assessable Value, NAV)、僱傭 (Net Assessable Income, NAI) 及業務 (Assessable Profits, AP) 的收入合併 (aggregation) 計稅，讓納稅人享有物業稅／利得稅下沒有的扣除與免稅額。
  • 選擇資格 (Eligibility, IRO s.41)：必須是個人 (individual)、年滿 18 歲（父母雙亡者除外）、並為香港永久性居民 (permanent resident, s.41(4) — ordinarily resides) 或暫時性居民 (temporary resident — 課稅年度內留港超過 180 天，或連續兩個課稅年度（其中一年為選擇年度）合計超過 300 天)。至 2017/18 年度止，已婚人士如配偶為香港永久或暫時性居民，即使本人非居民亦可選擇。已故納稅人可由遺囑執行人 (executor) 代為選擇。
  • 夫婦規則 (Husband and Wife)：2017/18 或之前，如夫婦雙方均有應評稅入息且均符合資格，必須共同選擇 (joint election)；2018/19 起可各自單獨選擇。稅款按夫婦合併入息計算，再按各自 reduced total income 比例分攤 (apportionment)，並分別發出評稅通知書。
  • 計算三步驟：(1) 合併 NAV + NAI + AP；(2) 扣除出租物業利息（上限為該物業 NAV）、concessionary deductions（ACD、ERCE、HLI、RRS/MPF contributions）、本年業務虧損及承前 PA 虧損，得出 reduced total income，再扣 personal allowances 得 total chargeable income；(3) 稅款取累進稅率 (progressive rates) 計於 total chargeable income 與標準稅率 (standard rate 15%) 計於 reduced total income（未扣免稅額） 兩者之較低者。
  • 選擇期限 (Time Limit)：以較後者為準 — 有關課稅年度結束後 2 年內；或構成總入息的評稅按 s.70 成為最終及決定性後 1 個月（實際即評稅發出後 2 個月）；或局長酌情容許的較長期限。須以書面（稅表有關部分或 Form IR76C）提出。

2. 必背考點清單

A. 選擇個人入息課稅的五大好處（Key Learning Point） 1. Deduction of interest expense on rental property（物業稅下不可扣；PA 下可扣，但上限為該物業的 NAV，超出部分既不能製造虧損、不可結轉，亦不可抵銷其他物業的 NAV — Board of Review case D51/04）。 2. Concessionary deductions：Approved Charitable Donations (ACD)、Elderly Residential Care Expenses (ERCE)、Home Loan Interest (HLI)、Mandatory Contributions to Recognised Retirement Schemes (MPF/RRS)。只有租金收入者在物業稅下完全不能扣任何 concessionary deductions；只有業務收入者在利得稅下只能扣 ACD。 3. Offset of business losses：獨資或合夥業務的本年虧損可抵銷個人（及其配偶 — s.42(5) inter-spouse loss set-off）的其他應稅收入。注意：offshore business losses 不可在 PA 下扣除。 4. Deduction of personal allowances（basic allowance、married person's allowance、child allowance 等）— 物業稅／利得稅下沒有。 5. Application of progressive tax rates。

B. PA 計算格式（Individual, 2017/18 年度數字按來源）

項目 內容
NAV – property Rental income + recovered bad rent − irrecoverable rent − rates paid by landlord − 20% statutory deduction
NAI – employment Assessable income − outgoings/expenses (s.12(1)(a)) − depreciation allowance (s.12(1)(b)) − self-education expenses (s.12(1)(e)) + balancing charge (s.12(5))；before concessionary deductions
AP – business 已扣除承前業務虧損及 ACD 後的應評稅利潤
Total income NAV + NAI + AP
Less: Interest on rental property（上限 = 該物業 NAV）；ACD；ERCE；HLI；RRS/MPF contributions；current-year business losses；PA loss brought forward
Reduced total income
Less: Personal allowances（Basic HK$132,000；Married person's HK$264,000；Child HK$100,000/名）
Total chargeable income
Tax payable Lower of: progressive rates × total chargeable income OR standard rate (15%) × reduced total income
PA loss carried forward 只有超出部分中屬於 business loss 的部分可結轉（上限為本年業務虧損）；concessionary deductions 超出部分不可結轉

C. 累進稅率（2017/18，來源章節所用） - First HK$45,000 @ 2% = HK$900 - Next HK$45,000 @ 7% = HK$3,150 - Next HK$45,000 @ 12% = HK$5,400 - （首 HK$135,000 合共稅款 = HK$9,450） - Remainder @ 17% - Standard rate = 15%

D. ACD 上限公式（35% rule） - 最高可扣 ACD = 35% ×（PA total income before business losses − mortgage interest on rental property + ACD already allowed under profits tax + self-education expenses allowed under salaries tax），再減去已在利得稅下扣除的 ACD，得出 PA 下尚可扣除的 ACD。 - 配偶一方未用盡的 ACD 可轉移 (transfer) 給另一方在 PA 下扣除。

E. 一次性稅務寬減 (One-off Tax Reduction)

課稅年度 寬減百分比 每份評稅上限
2012/13–2013/14 75% HK$10,000
2014/15–2016/17 75% HK$20,000
2017/18 75% HK$30,000
- 注意：PA 只有一份評稅即只有一次寬減；分開評稅則薪俸稅及利得稅各有一次寬減，可能削弱 PA 的好處。

F. 何時不宜選擇 PA - 高收入納稅人：累進稅率的邊際稅率 (17%) 高於標準稅率 (15%)，合併入息後物業收入可能被推到 17% 稅階（見 Illustrative Example 8 — Miss Chan）。若誤作選擇，IRD 會分別發出薪俸稅及物業稅評稅，並以 Assessor's note 提示選擇不利。 - 只有薪俸收入者選擇 PA 沒有任何好處（concessionary deductions 數額與薪俸稅下相同）。

G. 其他考點 - 選擇須書面作出：稅表相關部分或 Form IR76C。 - 2017/18 年度選擇 PA 的最後期限（無評稅發出的情況）為 31 March 2020。 - 年中結婚的夫婦，就 PA 而言被視為在該課稅年度開始時已結婚。 - 「Ordinarily resides」案例：Lysaght v CIR（regular visits of substantial period year to year）；Cooper v Cadwalader（maintaining a place of abode）；BoR case D11/15（保留居所／居留權／與港密切聯繫 ≠ ordinarily resident；23–40 天短暫留港不構成通常居住）。 - Self-education expenses 只可在薪俸稅下計算 NAI 時扣除，不能在 PA 下對租金或業務收入扣除。 - DIPN 18 適用於個人入息課稅（LO 2.23.03）。

3. 易混淆概念對比 (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall 1：Mortgage Interest on Rental Property vs Home Loan Interest (HLI) - Interest on loan for income-producing property：只有選擇 PA 才可扣；上限為該出租物業的 NAV；超出部分不可結轉、不可製造虧損、不可抵銷另一物業的 NAV（D51/04）。 - Home Loan Interest (HLI)：屬 concessionary deduction，針對自住居所 (residence) 的按揭利息；薪俸稅下及 PA 下均可扣，無 NAV 上限概念。 - 例子：陳先生有出租物業（NAV HK$160,000，按揭利息 HK$200,000）及自住物業（按揭利息 HK$100,000）。PA 下：出租物業利息只扣 HK$160,000（多出的 HK$40,000 永遠作廢）；自住 HLI HK$100,000 則可全數作為 concessionary deduction 扣除。考試最常見錯誤是把兩者混淆，以為出租物業利息可全數扣或 HLI 受 NAV 限制。

Pitfall 2：可分開評稅 (Schedular Taxes) 下的虧損處理 vs PA 下的虧損抵銷 - 分開評稅：同一納稅人 Business A 的虧損不可抵銷 Business B 的利潤（每盤生意獨立評稅）；業務虧損不可抵銷薪俸收入；配偶之間的虧損不可互相抵銷。 - PA 下：本年獨資／合夥業務虧損可抵銷 NAI、NAV 等其他收入，更可透過 s.42(5) 抵銷配偶收入 (inter-spouse loss set-off)，未用盡餘額才結轉。 - 但謹記兩個限制：offshore business loss 不可扣；當 concessionary deductions 與業務虧損合計超過總入息時，只有業務虧損部分可結轉，concessionary deductions 的超出部分會永久作廢（見 Illustrative Example 13）。 - 例子：李先生 Business A 虧損 HK$500,000、薪俸收入 HK$500,000。分開評稅：薪俸稅照交、虧損只能留待抵銷將來 Business A 利潤；選擇 PA：虧損直接抵銷薪俸收入，該年稅款變為 Nil。

Phase 2: Bilingual Flashcards 雙語記憶閃卡

Flashcard 1 / 共 8 張
Question

What is the nature of personal assessment, and who may elect for it under IRO s.41?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • PA is a relief from tax, not a separate charge of tax; it is an alternative assessment on an individual's aggregated total income.
  • Eligible person: an individual, aged 18 or above (under 18 permitted only if both parents are dead), who is either a permanent resident (ordinarily resides in HK — s.41(4)) or a temporary resident (stays in HK > 180 days in the year of assessment, or > 300 days over two consecutive years of assessment, one being the election year).
  • Up to 2017/18, a married non-resident may elect if his/her spouse is a permanent or temporary resident; an executor may elect on behalf of an eligible deceased taxpayer.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 個人入息課稅唔係一種稅，而係一種「計稅寬免方法」，將物業、薪俸、業務收入合併計稅。資格要記三點：個人、滿 18 歲（父母雙亡例外）、通常居港或符合 180/300 天留港測試；已婚者可靠配偶的居民身份取得資格，遺囑執行人亦可代已故者選擇。
Flashcard 2 / 共 8 張
Question

State the FIVE benefits of electing for personal assessment, and one situation where election is NOT advantageous.

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • (1) Deduction of interest expense on rental property (capped at the property's NAV); (2) concessionary deductions (ACD, ERCE, HLI, MPF/RRS contributions); (3) offset of business losses (including inter-spouse set-off under s.42(5)); (4) deduction of personal allowances; (5) application of progressive tax rates.
  • NOT advantageous: high-income earners — the top progressive marginal rate (17%) exceeds the standard rate (15%), so aggregating property/business income with salaries taxed at 17% increases total tax; also no benefit for a taxpayer whose only income is from employment.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 五大好處口诀：「息、扣、損、免、累」— 出租物業利息、concessionary deductions、業務虧損抵銷、個人免稅額、累進稅率。反過來，高收入人士因為累進稅率最高 17% 高過標準稅率 15%，合併後可能交更多稅，所以唔應該選擇。
Flashcard 3（計算卡） / 共 8 張
Question

Mr X let out a property at an annual rent of HK$240,000 (no rates paid by landlord) and paid mortgage interest of HK$200,000 on the loan used to purchase it. He has elected for personal assessment. How much interest is deductible, and what happens to any excess?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • NAV = HK$240,000 × 80% = HK$192,000 (after 20% statutory deduction).
  • Interest deductible is limited to the NAV → HK$192,000; total chargeable income from the property = Nil.
  • The excess interest of HK$8,000 (HK$200,000 − HK$192,000) cannot create a loss, cannot be carried forward, and cannot be offset against the NAV of any other property (D51/04).
  • [中文白話解釋]: 先計 NAV（租金 × 80%），利息扣除以 NAV 為上限，封頂後多出嚟嘅利息（呢度係 HK$8,000）就永遠作廢 — 唔可以結轉、唔可以製造虧損、唔可以搬去另一個物業扣。呢個係考試最常考嘅陷阱。
Flashcard 4 / 共 8 張
Question

How is the maximum deduction for approved charitable donations (ACD) determined under personal assessment?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Maximum ACD = 35% × (PA total income before business losses − mortgage interest on rental property + ACD already allowed under profits tax + self-education expenses allowed under salaries tax), then less ACD already allowed under profits tax.
  • PA total income before business losses = NAV + NAI + AP.
  • ACD not fully used by one spouse may be transferred to the other spouse for deduction under PA.
  • ACD is deductible under salaries tax, profits tax and PA — but NOT under property tax.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 捐款上限係合併總入息嘅 35%，但計個 base 時要加返已在利得稅扣咗嘅捐款同自修費用，再扣返已扣嘅利得稅捐款，先得出 PA 下仲可以扣幾多。記住物業稅下係完全唔可以扣捐款，所以得租金收入嘅人可以靠 PA 嚟扣捐款。
Flashcard 5 / 共 8 張
Question

How are business losses treated under personal assessment, including the carry-forward rule and inter-spouse set-off?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Current-year losses from sole-proprietorship and share of partnership losses may be offset against the individual's other income (NAI, NAV, AP of other businesses) — impossible under separate schedular assessments.
  • Under s.42(5), when PA is elected, a loss is set off against the total income of both the loss-making spouse and the other spouse before any residue is carried forward (inter-spouse loss set-off).
  • If concessionary deductions + business losses exceed total income, only the excess representing business losses (max = current-year business loss) is carried forward; excess concessionary deductions lapse.
  • Offshore business losses are NOT deductible under PA.
  • [中文白話解釋]: PA 最大賣點之一就係虧損可以「跨界」抵銷：抵自己其他收入、再經 s.42(5) 抵配偶收入，用唔晒先結轉。但結轉額只限業務虧損部分 — 如果係因為捐款、供款等 concessionary deductions 扣到變負數，嗰部分係會作廢嘅。海外業務虧損一律唔扣得。
Flashcard 6（計算卡） / 共 8 張
Question

For 2017/18, a single Hong Kong permanent resident has NAV of HK$384,000 (no other income, no deductions). Compute his tax payable under personal assessment (before any tax reduction) using both bases, and state which applies. (Basic allowance HK$132,000; progressive rates: first HK$45,000 @2%, next HK$45,000 @7%, next HK$45,000 @12%, remainder @17%; standard rate 15%.)

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Progressive basis: Net chargeable income = HK$384,000 − HK$132,000 = HK$252,000. Tax = (HK$252,000 − HK$135,000) × 17% + HK$9,450 = HK$19,890 + HK$9,450 = HK$29,340.
  • Standard-rate basis: HK$384,000 × 15% = HK$57,600 (applied on reduced total income before personal allowances).
  • Tax payable = the lower → HK$29,340 (progressive basis).
  • Key rule: standard rate applies to reduced total income without deducting personal allowances.
  • [中文白話解釋]: PA 稅款永遠係「兩條數取其低」：累進稅率乘（扣咗免稅額嘅 chargeable income），同標準稅率 15% 乘（未扣免稅額嘅 reduced total income）。最常犯錯係用標準稅率嗰邊都扣埋免稅額 — 記住標準稅率邊係 before deduction of personal allowances。

Question

What are the rules for married couples electing personal assessment (up to 2017/18 vs from 2018/19), and how is the joint tax apportioned?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Up to and including 2017/18: joint election required where both spouses have assessable income and both are eligible. Relaxed if only one spouse has assessable income, or only one spouse is eligible.
  • From 2018/19 onwards: a married person may elect separately or jointly; joint election requires both spouses to have assessable income; if jointly assessed under salaries tax, PA election must be made by both.
  • Tax is computed on the aggregated reduced total income, then apportioned between the spouses in the ratio of their individual reduced total incomes; separate notices of assessment are issued to each spouse.
  • A couple marrying during the year of assessment are deemed married at the commencement of that year.
  • [中文白話解釋]: 2017/18 或之前兩公婆都有收入又都合資格就一定要一齊選；2018/19 起可以分開選。稅係合併計，但交係按各自 reduced total income 比例分攤，每人收自己嘅 demand note — 唔可以由其中一方代收。年尾先結婚都當成年頭已結婚。

Question

What is the time limit and procedure for electing personal assessment?

撳卡翻面 👆
Answer
  • [English Point-form Answer]:
  • Election must be made in writing — via the relevant section of the tax return or Form IR76C lodged with the Commissioner.
  • Deadline: the later of — (a) two years after the end of the year of assessment (e.g. 2017/18 election by 31 March 2020); (b) one month after an assessment forming part of total income becomes final and conclusive under s.70 (in effect, two months after the date of issue of the assessment); or (c) such further period as the Commissioner may allow.
