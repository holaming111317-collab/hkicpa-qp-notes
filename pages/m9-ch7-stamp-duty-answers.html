M9 Stamp Duty — Model Answers（完成測驗後先好睇！）

Question 1 — Correct Answer: C

Model Answer: A mortgage over immovable property in Hong Kong is NOT chargeable with stamp duty. A conveyance or transfer made for nominal consideration for the purpose of securing the repayment of an advance or loan is a transaction under which no beneficial interest passes (s.27(5) SDO), so mortgages are not dutiable. The other instruments are all chargeable: - A: a lease of Hong Kong immovable property is chargeable under Head 1(2) of the First Schedule. - B: an agreement for sale of Hong Kong immovable property is chargeable to AVD under Head 1(1A). - D: the issue of a Hong Kong bearer instrument is chargeable under Head 3 at 3% ad valorem.

【解題思路】呢題考「Heads of Charge 概念分辨」。印花稅係 schedular tax：唔喺 First Schedule Heads 1–4 入面嘅文書一律唔使交稅。按揭（mortgage）只係為擔保還款而作嘅名義轉讓，無實益權益轉移（s.27(5)），所以唔課稅。選項 A 租約屬 Head 1(2)；選項 B 售賣協議屬 Head 1(1A)（住宅嘅 agreement for sale 自 1992 年起已可課稅）；選項 D 不記名文書發行屬 Head 3。常見陷阱：以為「option to purchase」唔使交——其實 s.29A(1) 將 option/right of pre-emption 視作 chargeable agreement for sale。

Question 2 — Correct Answer: C

Model Answer: - AVD: Scale 1 Part 1 (flat 15%) applies. Although Ms Wong is an HKPR acting on her own behalf, Scale 2 is only available to an HKPR who does NOT own any other residential property in Hong Kong at the time of acquisition (first home / home switchers, s.29BD). As she co-owns the Ho Man Tin property, she fails that condition → AVD = HK$8,000,000 × 15% = HK$1,200,000. - BSD: NOT payable. BSD is not chargeable where the purchaser is an HKPR acting on his/her own behalf, regardless of whether he/she already owns other residential property. - SSD: NOT payable. SSD is charged on the vendor's disposal within 36 months of acquisition; the vendor held the property for 10 years.

【解題思路】呢題專門考 AVD Scale 2 同 BSD 嘅「雙重標準」——呢個係考生最易混淆嘅位： - 選項 A 錯：Scale 2 唔係「係 HKPR 就用得」，仲要名下無其他香港住宅（首置/換樓）或屬近親轉讓等先可以。佢已聯名持有何文田住宅，所以要用 Scale 1 Part 1 嘅 15%。 - 選項 B 錯：BSD 嘅豁免只問「買方係咪以本人身份行事嘅 HKPR」，完全唔理佢有幾多層樓。HKPR 買第二、第三層樓一樣免 BSD。 - 選項 D 錯：SSD 係針對「賣方」喺取得後 36 個月內轉售而徵收，同買方擁有幾多物業無關；而且賣方已持有 10 年，遠超 36 個月。 記口訣：「AVD Scale 2 睇首置，BSD 只睇 HKPR 身份」。

Question 3 — Correct Answer: C

Model Answer: C is correct. Under s.19 SDO, contract notes must be made and stamped for every sale and purchase of Hong Kong stock (Head 2(1)). Where ad valorem duty has been paid on the contract notes, the instrument of transfer (IoT) bears only a nominal fixed duty of HK$5.

【解題思路】逐個選項拆： - A 錯：發行新股（issue of shares）唔屬任何 Head of Charge，完全唔課稅——印花稅只對「轉讓」（transfer）香港證券徵稅。 - B 錯：饋贈香港證券屬 Head 2(3)「轉讓實益權益非以買賣方式」，一樣按市值課從價稅；s.27 亦將無代價轉讓視作按市值計稅。 - D 錯：外幣（如日圓）計值嘅債券屬 loan capital，除非以港元計值或唔屬 commercial paper，否則唔算 Hong Kong stock，轉讓免稅。「要喺香港登記」本身唔足以令外幣債券變成 Hong Kong stock。 - C 啱：contract notes 交咗從價稅之後，IoT 只收 HK$5 固定稅（對照物業做法：agreement for sale 交咗 AVD，assignment 收 HK$100）。

Question 4 — Correct Answer: AVD HK$975,000; SSD HK$650,000; BSD HK$975,000; Total HK$2,600,000

Model Answer (working):

Step 1 — Dutiable value: ad valorem duties are charged on the higher of the stated consideration or the market value of the property (s.27(4) SDO; Heads 1–3). Higher of HK$6,000,000 (consideration) and HK$6,500,000 (market value) = HK$6,500,000.

Step 2 — AVD (Head 1(1A)): the purchaser is not an HKPR, so Scale 2 is unavailable; Scale 1 Part 1 flat 15% applies (residential property, instrument executed on/after 5 November 2016): HK$6,500,000 × 15% = HK$975,000

Step 3 — SSD (Head 1AA/1B): the vendor acquired the flat on 1 June 2022 (on/after 27 October 2012) and disposed of it by chargeable agreement on 1 December 2023. Holding period = 18 months, i.e. more than 12 months but not more than 36 months → rate 10%: HK$6,500,000 × 10% = HK$650,000

Step 4 — BSD: the purchaser is not an HKPR acting on his own behalf → 15% (ss.29CB/29DB), payable by the buyer alone: HK$6,500,000 × 15% = HK$975,000

Step 5 — Total stamp duty on the agreement for sale and purchase: 975,000 + 650,000 + 975,000 = HK$2,600,000 (The subsequent assignment, conforming to the stamped agreement, would bear only the fixed duty of HK$100.)

【解題思路】呢類「三稅合一」計算題嘅標準步驟： 1. 先定計稅基礎——永遠取「代價 vs 市值」較高者。呢度市值 650 萬高過作價 600 萬，所以用 650 萬。考生常犯錯誤係直接用作價。 2. AVD：問「買方係咪名下無其他住宅嘅 HKPR？係咪近親轉讓/法庭命令？」全部唔係 → Scale 1 Part 1 劃一 15%。 3. SSD：計持有期用「chargeable agreement for sale」日期對日期——2022 年 6 月 1 日至 2023 年 12 月 1 日 = 18 個月，落喺「>12 個月 ≤36 個月」→ 10%。常見錯誤：誤用 assignment 日期、或者計錯檔次用 15%。 4. BSD：買方非 HKPR → 15%，冇豁免（唔係近親聯名、無 s.45、無法庭命令）。 5. 三稅分開列、分開計、最後先加總。記住三者係獨立嘅稅，可以同時全部適用。

Question 5 — Correct Answer: HK$27,210

Model Answer (working):

Step 1 — Rent component: the term is 2 years, i.e. "exceeds 1 year but does not exceed 3 years" → rate is 0.5% of the yearly rent (Head 1(2)). Yearly rent = HK$28,500 × 12 = HK$342,000 Round up to the nearest HK$100 → HK$342,000 (already a multiple of 100) Duty on rent = 342,000 × 0.5% = HK$1,710

Step 2 — Premium (key money) component: key money is mentioned in the lease and rent is also payable → 4.25% of the consideration: HK$600,000 × 4.25% = HK$25,500

Step 3 — Deposit: any deposit mentioned in the lease is NOT taken into account in assessing stamp duty → HK$57,000 is ignored.

Step 4 — Total stamp duty on the lease: 1,710 + 25,500 = HK$27,210

【解題思路】租約印花稅口訣：「租金、頂手費，分開計；按金唔計」。 1. 租期檔次：唔確定或 ≤1 年 → 0.25%（≤1 年用租期內總租金）；>1 年 ≤3 年 → 0.5%；>3 年 → 1%。2 年租期用 0.5% × 年租。注意邊界：「does not exceed 3 years」即係 3 年整都仲係 0.5%，超過 3 年先用 1%。 2. 年租要 round up to the nearest HK$100——呢題 342,000 啱啱係 100 嘅倍數所以唔使進位，但考試時要記得檢查呢步。 3. Key money/premium：因為租約同時要交租，所以用 4.25%；如果係「免租、純付頂手費」，就要當賣樓咁按售賣不動產稅率計。 4. 按金（deposit）係常見干擾數據（distractor），法例講明唔計入。好多考生會錯誤咁將按金加落去計稅。
