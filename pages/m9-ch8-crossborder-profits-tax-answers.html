Question 1 — Correct Answer: C

Model Answer (English): Under Inland Revenue Rules Rule 5, a PE is "a branch, management or other place of business, but does not include an agent unless the agent has, and habitually exercises, a general authority to negotiate and conclude contracts on behalf of his principal or has a stock of merchandise from which he regularly fills orders on his behalf". Option C satisfies this agency-PE limb. For a non-CDTA territory resident, the PE definition in the IRO (Schedule 17G, which largely follows the 2017 OECD Model Tax Convention) applies.

  • Option A is wrong: a place used solely for the storage, display or delivery of goods is expressly on the IRD's list of activities NOT constituting a PE.
  • Option B is wrong: purchasing, advertising or collecting information for the enterprise are preparatory or auxiliary activities and do not create a PE.
  • Option D is wrong: mere use of the same hotel room is not a fixed place of business within the PE definition (cf. Knowledge Check Q1 of the chapter).

【解題思路】PE 題目第一步：判斷有冇 CDTA（冇 → 用 IRO/Schedule 17G 定義）；第二步：逐項對照「唔構成 PE 清單」——儲存／展示／交付、採購／廣告／收集資料、輔助性質，全部唔算。代理人要構成 PE，關鍵字係 habitually negotiates and concludes contracts（慣常性、有權簽約）；一次半次或者只係 promotion 唔夠。酒店房只係個人逗留地方，唔係 fixed place of business。呢類題目考官最鍾意用「聽落好似做生意但其實係 preparatory/auxiliary」嘅選項做干擾。

Question 2 — Correct Answer: B

Model Answer (English): Following CIR v Asia Television Ltd (1987) 2 HKTC 198, where a Hong Kong person deals with a non-resident on a principal-to-principal basis (the non-resident receives income directly from, not "through", the Hong Kong person), the Hong Kong payer is not an "agent" under s.2 and s.20A cannot apply. To close this gap, the Inland Revenue (Amendment) Ordinance 1989 enacted s.20B (effective 1 April 1989), under which a non-resident entertainer or sportsman performing in Hong Kong in connection with a commercial occasion or event is chargeable to tax in the name of any person in Hong Kong who pays or credits the relevant sums. Under s.20B(2), ShowTime HK Ltd must withhold a sum sufficient to meet the tax liability, and it is indemnified under s.20B(3).

  • Option A is wrong: payment alone does not create agency; the ATV case decided exactly the opposite.
  • Option C is wrong: although s.20A fails, s.20B was specifically introduced so that an assessment CAN be raised in the payer's name.
  • Option D is wrong: there is no 183-day rule for profits tax chargeability of entertainers under the IRO; performing even for one day in HK gives rise to HK-sourced profits under s.14/s.20B. (The 183-day concept belongs to certain CDTA articles, not the domestic charging provisions.)

【解題思路】見到「principal-to-principal」呢個關鍵詞就要即刻諗起 ATV 案同 s.20B 嘅立法背景。s.20A 只適用於真正嘅 agent（s.2 定義：透過佢收取香港收入嘅人）。考試常見陷阱：考生以為「香港公司付款俾非居民＝自動係 agent」（A 選項），或者將 salaries tax／DTA 嘅 183 日概念錯誤移植到利得稅（D 選項）。記住：香港利得稅係 territorial source principle，一日表演賺嘅錢都係香港來源，照課。

Question 3 — Correct Answer: B

Model Answer (English): Under s.20A(3), where a non-resident sells goods in Hong Kong through a Hong Kong agent, the agent must withhold and pay consignment tax = 1% of the gross sale proceeds from sales in Hong Kong (a 0.5% rate is possible only with the Commissioner's approval, which has not been obtained here). Consignment tax applies notwithstanding that the non-resident has no PE and does not carry on a business in Hong Kong.

Consignment tax = HK$8,000,000 × 1% = HK$80,000

The agent (DairyLink HK Ltd) must also file quarterly returns (Form BIR52B) disclosing the gross HK sales proceeds, retain sufficient funds for the tax, and is indemnified under s.20A(2). Alpen Foods AG may alternatively elect to be assessed on actual profits supported by financial statements.

  • Option A is wrong: Mainland China sales must NOT be included — Hong Kong and Mainland China are separate and distinct tax jurisdictions; only gross proceeds from HK customers count.
  • Option C is wrong: it applies the ordinary corporate profits tax rate of 16.5% to gross proceeds; consignment tax is a special 1%-of-proceeds regime, not the standard rate on gross sales.
  • Option D is wrong: absence of a PE/business in HK does not exempt the non-resident; s.20A(3) specifically catches sales of goods in HK through a HK agent.

【解題思路】Consignment tax 三大考點：(1) 稅率係 1% of gross sale proceeds（經批准先可以 0.5%），唔係 16.5%；(2) 稅基只計 香港客戶 嘅銷售——內地同香港係獨立稅區，千祈唔好加埋；(3) 冇 PE／冇喺香港做生意 唔係豁免理由——s.20A(3) 正正就係為呢種情況而設。呢題同教科書 Knowledge Check Q3 係同一考點，屬高頻題型。

Question 4 — Model Answer

Correct Answer: Tax to be withheld = HK$120,000 (deemed assessable profits = HK$1,600,000)

Full Working (English): 1. RacketStar is a non-resident individual performing in Hong Kong in connection with a commercial event; the sum is caught by s.20B, and the HK promoter must withhold under s.20B(2). 2. Deemed profit method (s.21; DIPN 17 (Revised) para. 14): one-third of the gross fee is treated as allowable expenses; two-thirds is deemed assessable profits: - Deemed assessable profits = HK$2,400,000 × 2/3 = HK$1,600,000 3. Applicable rate: the recipient is an individual (unincorporated person); under the two-tiered profits tax regime (effective from YA 2018/19), the first HK$2,000,000 of assessable profits is taxed at 7.5%. Since HK$1,600,000 ≤ HK$2,000,000, the whole amount is at 7.5%: - Tax withheld = HK$1,600,000 × 7.5% = HK$120,000 4. RacketStar may instead elect the actual profit method by filing a return with documentary support for actual expenses; any tax overpaid is refundable under s.79(3) (either the promoter or RacketStar may claim, but not both).

【解題思路】四步曲：(1) 認人——非居民、個人、商業表演 → s.20B；(2) 視作利潤——2/3 做利潤、1/3 做開支（2,400,000 × 2/3 = 1,600,000）；(3) 揀稅率——個人用 unincorporated rate 7.5%（首 200 萬），唔好用公司嘅 8.25% 或者全額 15%；1,600,000 未過 200 萬上限，所以全段 7.5%；(4) 扣繳人係香港 promoter，唔理佢係咪 agent。常見錯誤：唔記得乘 2/3 直接 2,400,000 × 7.5% = 180,000；或者用錯 15%；或者誤以為表演日數太少唔使課稅。

Question 5 — Model Answer

Correct Answer: Tax to be withheld = HK$74,250 (deemed assessable profits = HK$900,000)

Full Working (English): 1. The licence fee is paid for the right to use copyright material in Hong Kong — it is a deemed trading receipt under s.15(1)(b), even though CloudWare Ltd has no business or PE in Hong Kong. (s.15 deeming provisions apply precisely where s.14 does not catch the receipt.) 2. Under s.21A, because TechSoft HK Ltd and CloudWare Ltd are unrelated parties, the deemed assessable profits = 30% of the sum paid: - Deemed assessable profits = HK$3,000,000 × 30% = HK$900,000 3. CloudWare Ltd is a corporation; under the two-tiered profits tax regime, the first HK$2,000,000 is taxed at 8.25%: - Tax withheld = HK$900,000 × 8.25% = HK$74,250 4. TechSoft HK Ltd must withhold this sum at the time of payment/credit under s.20B(2), retain it until the IRD issues a demand note, and is indemnified by s.20B(3).

【解題思路】同表演費題目分清楚：呢題係 royalty / licence fee，用嘅視作比率係 s.21A 嘅 30%（非關聯方），唔係 2/3——2/3 只適用於藝人／運動員表演。稅率方面，收款人係公司，用 8.25%（首 200 萬）。常見錯誤：(1) 用 2/3 代替 30%；(2) 用全額 3,000,000 計稅（唔記得 30%）；(3) 如果雙方係關聯公司兼且個 IP 之前由香港業務人士持有，就要用 100%——本題明言 unrelated，所以唔適用；(4) 以為冇 PE 就唔使課稅——s.15(1)(b) 係 deeming provision，正正捕捉呢類收入。
