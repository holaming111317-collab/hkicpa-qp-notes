Ch1 Key Aspects of the Tax System in Hong Kong

  • 三大稅表（schedular system）：Property tax — IRO s.5｜Salaries tax — IRO s.8｜Profits tax — IRO s.14
  • Territorial source principle：只問 source、不問 residence；residence 只用於 DTA（individual：單一 YOA 逗留 >180 日 或 連續兩個 YOA >300 日）
  • Profits tax s.14 two-limbed test：(1) carry on trade/business in HK + (2) HK-source profits；source 用 operation test（what was done to earn the profit, and where）
  • Two-tiered profits tax rates（2018/19 起）：Corporations 首 HK$2M @ 8.25%、其後 16.5%；Unincorporated 首 HK$2M @ 7.5%、其後 15%
  • Salaries tax：累進 2%/6%/10%/14%/17%（每層 HK$50,000）；與「income before allowances × 15% standard rate」取較低；無 PAYE，75%/25% 兩期繳
  • Property tax：15% × NAV；NAV = AV − 20% statutory repairs allowance
  • Stamp duty（SDO）：對 instruments 徵稅；HK stock transfer 0.26%（1 Aug 2021 起）；住宅 36 個月內轉售有 SSD；non-HKPR 買住宅有 BSD
  • WHT：dividends 及 interest 無 WHT；只有 royalties（HK IP use）及 non-resident entertainers/sportsmen 按 withholding basis
  • 香港無：capital gains / estate / VAT / gift / dividend / payroll / turnover / sales taxes
  • 法律淵源：IRO/SDO（statute）+ DTAs（約 40 個）+ case law；DIPNs 及 BoR decisions 無法律約束力；Basic Law Art. 106 / 108；DIPN 29（HK–Mainland）
  • 合規：business/rent records 保存 ≥7 年（s.51C / s.51D）；notify chargeability 4 個月內（s.51(2)）；objection 1 個月內；IRD 採 AFAL (assess first, audit later)
