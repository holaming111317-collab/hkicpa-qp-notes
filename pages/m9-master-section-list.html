M9 Principles of Taxation — Master Section & Rate List（累積式總表）

每完成一章自動追加。考前第 13 週呢份就係你嘅終極複習表。

Ch1 Key Aspects of the Tax System in Hong Kong

  • 三大稅表（schedular system）：Property tax — IRO s.5｜Salaries tax — IRO s.8｜Profits tax — IRO s.14
  • Territorial source principle：只問 source、不問 residence；residence 只用於 DTA（individual：單一 YOA 逗留 >180 日 或 連續兩個 YOA >300 日）
  • Profits tax s.14 two-limbed test：(1) carry on trade/business in HK + (2) HK-source profits；source 用 operation test（what was done to earn the profit, and where）
  • Two-tiered profits tax rates（2018/19 起）：Corporations 首 HK$2M @ 8.25%、其後 16.5%；Unincorporated 首 HK$2M @ 7.5%、其後 15%
  • Salaries tax：累進 2%/6%/10%/14%/17%（每層 HK$50,000）；與「income before allowances × 15% standard rate」取較低；無 PAYE，75%/25% 兩期繳
  • Property tax：15% × NAV；NAV = AV − 20% statutory repairs allowance
  • Stamp duty（SDO）：對 instruments 徵稅；HK stock transfer 0.26%（1 Aug 2021 起）；住宅 36 個月內轉售有 SSD；non-HKPR 買住宅有 BSD
  • WHT：dividends 及 interest 無 WHT；只有 royalties（HK IP use）及 non-resident entertainers/sportsmen 按 withholding basis
  • 香港無：capital gains / estate / VAT / gift / dividend / payroll / turnover / sales taxes
  • 法律淵源：IRO/SDO（statute）+ DTAs（約 40 個）+ case law；DIPNs 及 BoR decisions 無法律約束力；Basic Law Art. 106 / 108；DIPN 29（HK–Mainland）
  • 合規：business/rent records 保存 ≥7 年（s.51C / s.51D）；notify chargeability 4 個月內（s.51(2)）；objection 1 個月內；IRD 採 AFAL (assess first, audit later)

Ch2 Administration of the Tax System in Hong Kong

IRD structure & powers - IRD: one of nine departments under the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau; headed by CIR + 2 deputy CIRs; six units (Commissioner's Unit; Headquarters Unit; Units 1–4). Unit 2: processing of salaries tax, profits tax (sole-proprietorships), property tax (sole owners), personal assessment; Unit 4: field audits and investigations. - Secrecy: s.4 IRO (officers must preserve secrecy); exception where disclosure required by law (s.49(5)); DTA/TIEA exchange of information permitted (s.51(4AA)). - Information powers: s.51(1) returns; s.51(3) fuller/further returns; s.51(4)(a)-(b) information/documents/attendance incl. third parties; s.51(4A) property transactions; s.51A statement of assets & liabilities (with BOR consent); s.51B search warrant from magistrate; s.52 information from government bodies/employers. - Board of Inland Revenue: chairman = Financial Secretary; prescribes return forms; makes Inland Revenue Rules (LegCo approval). - Board of Review (s.65): independent statutory tax tribunal of first instance; chairman + 10 deputy chairmen + ≤150 members; panel of 3; majority decisions; in camera; no costs order; fee ≤ HK$25,000 for vexatious appeals; facts found are final; burden of proof on taxpayer (s.68(4)).

Returns — deadlines - s.51(2): notify CIR of chargeability within 4 months after end of basis period (if no return issued). - BIR60 (individuals): issued first working day of May; file within 1 month (sole proprietors: first working day of August, +1 month if e-filed). - BIR51 (corporations) / BIR52 (others): issued first working day of April; file within 1 month (Dec year-end → mid-Aug; Mar year-end → mid-Nov). Provisional profits tax at 16.5% (8.25% on first HK$2M under two-tiered rates). - BIR57: property tax return for jointly/co-owned property; BIR54: profits tax return for non-residents. - Employer (s.52): IR56E within 3 months of new employee commencing (s.52(4)); 1 month before cessation (s.52(5)); 1 month before employee's departure >1 month from HK (s.52(6)) + withhold payments due until departure (s.52(7)); employer's return within 1 month of receipt (s.52(2)); payroll records ≥ 7 years (s.51C).

Assessments - Standard assessment — s.59(1) (acceptable return filed); Estimated assessment — s.59(3) (no/unacceptable return; unsatisfactory books); Additional assessment — s.60(1) (taxpayer not adequately taxed). - Additional assessment time limit: 6 years after end of the year of assessment; 10 years for fraud or wilful evasion (s.60(1)(b)). No time limit on collection of tax. - AFAL: 'assess first, audit later' policy for profits tax returns. - Final and conclusive (s.70): no timely objection/appeal; objection withdrawn or dismissed; revised assessment agreed (s.64(3)); BOR determination refusing referral to court. - s.51C: business records kept ≥ 7 years after transactions.

Objection, holdover, error/omission - Objection (s.64(1)): in writing, precise grounds, within 1 month after date of notice of assessment; late acceptance only for absence from HK/illness/other reasonable excuse (s.64(1)(a)); vs estimated assessment without return — valid return must accompany the objection within the 1-month period. CIR determines (confirm/reduce/increase/annul) within reasonable time (s.64(2)). - Holdover of disputed tax (s.71(2)): conditional holdover = purchase TRCs (Tax Reserve Certificates Ordinance Cap. 289) or banker's undertaking; unconditional holdover rare — tax + interest (Chief Justice rate) payable if objection fails. - Holdover of provisional tax: property tax s.63O / salaries tax s.63E / profits tax s.63J; key ground = likely less than 90% of provisional amount assessed. Deadline: 28 days before payment due date OR 14 days after the notice for payment of provisional tax, whichever later; no discretion to extend. - Error or omission claim (s.70A): within 6 years after end of year of assessment OR 6 months after notice of assessment served, whichever later; no correction if assessment followed prevailing practice; deliberate acts not 'errors' (Moulin Global Eyecare Trading Ltd v CIR (2014)); 'error or omission' includes error of law (Extramoney Ltd v CIR).

Penalties (Exhibit 2.1) - s.80(1): civil; no tax undercharged; fine level 3 (HK$10,000); Court assesses; taxpayer proves on balance of probability; appeal to Court. - s.80(2): civil; incorrect return with tax undercharged; level 3 fine + up to 300% of tax undercharged; Court assesses; appeal to Court. - s.82(1): criminal (wilful evasion); prosecution proves beyond reasonable doubt + mens rea; summary conviction: level 3 fine + 300% + 6 months' imprisonment; indictment: level 5 fine (HK$50,000) + 300% + 3 years' imprisonment; appeal to Court. - s.82A: administrative additional tax; raised personally by CIR/Deputy Commissioner; notice + 21 days for written representations; max 300% of tax undercharged; appeal to Board of Review; bars later prosecution on same facts under s.80(2)/s.82(1). - Compounding: s.80(5) / s.82(2) (DIPN 11 [Revised]). - Reasonable excuse accepted: honest belief of non-taxability; reasonable reliance on expert advice; simple slip of the mind. NOT accepted: ignorance of law; low education; incompetent staff. - Koo Ming Kown & Murakami Tadao v CIR [2021] HKCA 1037: company penalties not enforceable against directors personally. - Fine levels (Sch 8 Criminal Procedure Ordinance): L1 $2,000 / L2 $5,000 / L3 $10,000 / L4 $25,000 / L5 $50,000 / L6 $100,000.

Appeals - s.66: CIR determination → BOR within 1 month (written grounds; copy to CIR; extension for illness/absence). Documents: notice of assessment, CIR's determination, representations, statement of grounds of appeal. - s.67: bypass BOR → CFI if both parties agree in writing within 21 days of lodging the BOR appeal. - s.69: BOR → CFI within 1 month of the Board's decision; point of law only; leave required (reasonable prospect of success / interests of justice). - s.69A: leapfrog BOR → Court of Appeal with court's permission (significant tax / public importance / extraordinary difficulty).

Payment & recovery - Surcharges: ≤6 months late = 5%; >6 months late = further 10% on total in default including the earlier 5%. - Salaries tax: usually two instalments (payment dates generally Jan–Apr); property/profits tax: one due date. Provisional tax credited against final tax. - Recovery: court fee HK$630 + writ cost HK$300 + judgment interest; recovery notices to third parties (employers, banks, debtors); Departure Prevention Direction to stop defaulter leaving HK.

Ch3 Profits Tax

Charge & Scope - s.14(1): profits tax on every person carrying on a trade, profession or business in HK in respect of HK-source assessable profits (three-limbed test); excludes gains from sale of capital assets - s.2(1): “person” includes corporation, partnership, trustee, body of persons; “trade” includes every adventure and concern in the nature of trade; “business” includes letting/sub-letting by corporations etc. - Rates (Sch. 1, 8, 8A, 8B): corporations 16.5% / 8.25% on first HK$2M; non-corporations 15% / 7.5% on first HK$2M (two-tiered, from 2018/19); only ONE entity per group of connected entities may elect two-tiered rates - Badges of trade (Marson v Morton 1986): profit motive; subject matter; number of transactions; length of ownership; supplementary work; circumstances of realisation; manner of sale; method of acquisition; source of finance — NOT a checklist, holistic assessment (CIR v Church Body of HK Sheng Kung Hui 2016); share dealings by individuals generally investment not trade (Lee Yee Shing v CIR 2008)

Source of Profits (operations test — CIR v Hang Seng Bank 1991; DIPN 21) - Trading profits: where contracts of purchase AND sale are effected (Magna Industrial 1997 — includes solicitation, negotiation, conclusion, trade financing, shipment, performance); if EITHER contract effected in HK → initial presumption fully taxable; greater weight on sale contract - Manufacturing profits: where goods are manufactured (HK-TVB International 1992); contract processing → 50:50 apportionment (no transfer of legal title, DIPN 21 para 34); import processing → trading profits rules, no apportionment (CIR v Datatronic 2009) - Commissions/service fees: where services performed (Indosuez WI Carr 2002; Li & Fung (Trading) 2012 — back-office in HK insufficient) - Real estate: location of property (Rhodesia Metals 1940); listed securities: stock exchange of listing; unlisted: where contracts effected - Interest (non-FI): provision of credit test — where funds first made available to borrower (DIPN 13); FI/money-lending: operations test (Orion Caribbean 1997) - Royalties not under s.15(1)(a)/(b)/(ba): place of acquisition and granting of licence (DIPN 49) - HK bank deposit interest exempt for non-FIs (Exemption from Profits Tax (Interest Income) Order) — unless deposit secures a borrowing with deductible interest - “Rare case” dictum (HK-TVB, obiter); Newfair Holdings v CIR [2022]: taxed on what it does, not what it is

Deemed Trading Receipts & Exemptions - s.15(1)(a): film/TV tape/sound recording/advertising material royalties used in HK - s.15(1)(b): royalties for use in HK of patent, design, trade mark, copyright, layout-design, performer’s right, plant variety right, secret process/formula - s.15(1)(ba): royalties for use OUTSIDE HK, deductible to a HK payer (tax symmetry) - s.15(1)(bb): performer/organiser sums for assignment of performer’s right re HK performance - s.21A: deemed assessable profit = 30% of gross royalty; 100% if from associated person AND IP previously owned by a person carrying on business in HK - s.20B: HK payer withholds and pays over tax (effective 4.95% = 30% × 16.5%; HK’s only withholding tax) - s.15(1)(f)/(g)/(i): HK-source interest of corporations / non-corporates (business funds) / financial institutions - s.15C: trading stock on cessation deemed disposed at market value; s.15D: post-cessation receipts/payments deemed assessable/deductible in year of cessation; s.15BA: reclassification trading↔capital at fair market value (Sharkey v Wernher principle) - s.14A: qualifying debt instruments (<7 yrs) — half rate; s.26A(1): ≥7 yrs — exempt - Exemptions: capital gains (s.14(1)); dividends from corporations chargeable to profits tax (s.26); offshore funds (ss.20AC/20ACA)

Deductions - s.16(1): all outgoings/expenses to the extent incurred in production of chargeable profits (apportionment allowed, IRR Rule 2A; “incurred” = paid/due/absolutely committed — Lo & Lo 1984; contingent liabilities excluded) - s.16(1)(a)–(h): interest (subject to s.16(2) conditions + ss.16(2A)–(2C) limits); rent to spouse/partners capped at assessable value (b); foreign taxes on income (c; s.16(2J) excludes DTA-credit cases); bad debts (d — previously taxed receipts or money-lending loans; specific provision OK, general provision not; recovery taxable); repairs excl. improvements (e); replacement of implements/utensils (f); trademark/copyright/patent registration (g) - Interest deductibility — cumulative tests: production of profits test (D68/87 apportionment formula); tax symmetry test (non-FI lender must be HK-taxable on the interest, s.16(2)); secured loan test (s.16(2A)); interest flow-back test (ss.16(2B)/(2C); market-maker exception s.16(2G)) - s.16A: non-regular approved retirement scheme contributions — 20% p.a. over 5 years - s.16B: R&D — Type B super-deduction 300% on first HK$2M, 200% thereafter (HK activities only); Type A 100% - s.16C: technical education payments; s.16D: approved charitable donations HK$100–35% of assessable profits (s.88 charities only) - s.16E: patent rights/know-how — 100% in year incurred; proceeds on sale taxable up to deduction; no deduction if acquired from associate - s.16EA: specified IPRs (registered TM/design, copyright, etc.) — 20% p.a. over 5 years (shorter protection → shorter spread); unregistered not eligible; associate acquisitions excluded; disposal adjustments capped at deductions claimed - s.16F: refurbishment of non-domestic buildings — 5 equal annual instalments; NOT for first-use enabling or change-of-use works - s.16G: prescribed fixed assets (manufacturing P&M, computer hardware/software) — 100% in year of acquisition; EXCLUDES leased-out assets and hire-purchase assets; no double deduction - ss.16H–16L: environmental protection machinery/vehicles — 100%; environmental protection installations — 100% from 1.4.2018 (previously 5-year spread; residue election s.16L) - s.17 non-deductible: (a) domestic/private; (c) capital expenditure, improvements, capital losses; (e) sums recoverable under insurance/indemnity; (f) rent of premises not used to produce profits; (g) taxes under IRO (except employees’ salaries tax); (h) retirement contributions >15% of employee’s total emoluments; (2) salaries/interest to partners or their spouses (appropriation of profits)

Capital vs Revenue - Capital = the plant; revenue = the fruit; fixed vs circulating capital - Atherton (1926): once-and-for-all expenditure bringing enduring benefit → capital - Compensation: for lost trading profits → revenue (Lincolnshire Sugar); sterilisation of capital asset/total cessation → capital (Glenboig); temporary loss of use → revenue (London & Thames Haven); cancellation of structural contract → capital (Van den Berghs); ordinary trading contract → revenue (Kelsall Parsons); restraint of trade → capital (Higgs v Olivier) - Debt forgiveness not taxable (British Mexican Petroleum), but s.15(2) taxes remitted debts previously deducted

Depreciation Allowances (Part VI; s.18F) - P&M pooling (s.39B; DIPN 7): IA 60% of cost; AA 10%/20%/30% on reducing value; balancing charge/allowance on disposal (s.39D; proceeds capped at original cost); private-to-business use: no IA, notional WDV (s.37(2A)/s.39B(6)); hire purchase (s.37A): IA 60% of capital portion paid in basis period, AA on reducing value; transfers to pool on completion (s.39C) - IBA: qualifying trade (manufacture, processing, transport, qualifying storage, farming, R&D; offices/retail/showrooms/hotels excluded; 10% mixed-use rule); qualifying expenditure = construction cost excl. land (incl. pre-completion finance interest); IA 20% (s.34(1)); AA 4% (s.34(2)); balancing adjustment on disposal (s.35, charge capped at allowances granted); used building: AA = residue after sale × 1/(years from sale Y/A to 25th year after first use); unused building (s.35B): developer seller → building price; non-developer → lower of construction cost and price - CBA (s.33A): AA 4% only, no IA; s.35B not applicable; balancing adjustment under s.35; pre-1998/99 buildings deemed first used 1998/99

Computation, Basis Period, Partnerships, Losses - 5-step computation: deduct non-assessable income → add back non-deductible expenses → add balancing charges → deduct allowances/specific deductions (donations after this step, ≤35%) → set off losses b/f (accounting cannot override IRO — Secan 2000; Nice Cheer 2013: unrealised profits not taxable) - s.51C: business records kept ≥7 years; s.51(2): notify chargeability within 4 months after basis period end - Basis period (ss.18–18E): continuing business = accounting year ended in Y/A (s.18B); commencement (s.18C): first accounts ≤12m ending in commencement year → commencement to first accounting date; ≤12m ending next year → Nil for commencement year; >12m → split (first Y/A = stub, second Y/A = 12m back from accounting date); cessation (s.18D): new business = day after preceding basis period to cessation date - Partnerships (s.22(1)): assessed in partnership name; allocation required if PA election / corporate partner / losses b/f; reallocation so no partner has loss when partnership has profit (and vice versa); retired individual partner’s share of loss lapses unless transferred to PA; s.22(3): partnership continues if at least one partner stays - Losses (DIPN 8): must be HK trading losses (offshore/capital losses not allowable); sole proprietor — same trade only, surrendered on cessation (unless PA); partnership — allocated to partners; corporation — carried forward indefinitely, set off against profits of ANY trade/business of the corporation

Ch4 Salaries Tax

Scope & source (s.8) - s.8(1): charge on income arising in or derived from HK from an employment, office or pension. - Employment source — Goepfert (2 HKTC 210) factors: (1) place contract negotiated & concluded; (2) residence of employer (company: where central management and control abide); (3) place of payment (least weight). Place of services irrelevant. DIPN 10: totality of facts / substance over form. - Office (director's fee) source: where paying company is centrally managed and controlled; employment exemptions do not apply to office income. - Pension source: where fund is managed and controlled; s.8(2)(ca) exempts non-government pension attributable to services outside HK; government pensions fully taxable. - s.9(6): "employee" includes office holder.

HK employment (s.8(1)(a)) — fully taxable, no apportionment, unless: - s.8(1A)(b)(ii): all services rendered outside HK; - s.8(1B): 60-day rule — services rendered in HK during visits ≤ 60 days in YA disregarded (part of a day = one day; arrival & departure days both counted; training/conference/reporting = rendering services; CIR v So Chak Kwong: test is visits, not workdays). Not available to government employees or ship/aircraft crew; - s.8(1A)(c): income from services in non-DTA jurisdiction exempt if chargeable to substantially similar tax there AND actually paid (amount irrelevant; nil liability = no exclusion). DTA jurisdictions: only s.50 FTC via s.8(1C). - s.8(2)(j): ship/aircraft crew exempt if present in HK ≤ 60 days in YA AND ≤ 120 days over 2 consecutive YAs; transit days count (D11/13).

Non-HK employment — s.8(1A)(a) time apportionment: - Income attributable to HK = Annual income × (Days in HK ÷ Total days in YA). - Days in HK = business days in HK + (business days in HK ÷ total business days) × leave days. - Midnight rule: arrival + departure day = 1 day (contrast 60-day rule: part-day = 1 day). - Not applied if HK income clearly identifiable (D106/89); rebuttable presumption (CIR v Lo Wa Ming Patrick [2022] HKCA 710).

Income chargeable (s.9) - s.9(1)(a): wages, salary, leave pay, fee, commission, bonus, gratuity, perquisite, allowance (from employer or others). - s.9(1)(a)(iv) liability test: employer paying its own sole & primary liability to a third party = not income (no surety). Discharging employee's personal liability = taxable (Glynn). - s.9(2A) always chargeable: (a) benefit convertible into money; (b) child education; (c) holiday journey. - Housing: cash housing/rental/mortgage allowance 100% taxable (s.9(1)(a)); rent-free residence taxed on rental value (s.9(1)(b)); subsidised residence: rental value − rent paid by employee (s.9(1)(c)); actual rent paid/refunded not income (s.9(1A)). Rent refund requires actual rent paid + genuine employer control (D149/00; CIR v Peter Leslie Page: "what transpired at the time of payment"). - Rental value (s.9(2)) = 10% × income from that employer (after s.12(1)(a)/(b) deductions; excludes self-education expenses & share option gains); hotel/hostel/boarding house: 8% (≤2 rooms) / 4% (≤1 room); shared quarters 4%; election for rateable value possible. - Share options (ss.9(1)(d), 9(4), 9(5); DIPN 38): exercise gain = OMV at exercise − consideration (shares + option cost); assignment/release gain = consideration received − cost of grant; taxed in year of exercise/assignment/release (notional; allotment date generally used); s.9(5) prevents double tax. Conditional options (HK employment): fully taxable unless exemption applies in EVERY YA of vesting period; non-HK employment: vesting time-basis apportionment. - Share awards: upfront approach (full economic benefits at grant → OMV at grant; later dividends not taxable) vs back-end approach (vesting period/forfeiture → OMV at vest; interim dividends taxable perquisite, D21/16). Post-cessation vesting deemed accrued on last day of employment (s.11D(b)(ii)). Departure election: deemed vesting/exercise (within 7 days before final return, or departure date if elected within 3 months after departure). - Retirement benefits: retirement/death/incapacity/terminal illness → exempt (ss.8(2)(c),(cb),(cc)(i)); termination of service: ≥10 years' service fully exempt; <10 years exempt up to proportionate benefit s.8(5) = accrued benefit × completed months of service ÷ 120, excess taxable (ss.9(1)(ab)(ii), 9(1)(ae)); employer not chargeable to profits tax: s.8(8) cap = annual income × 15% × completed years of service + MPF mandatory accrued benefits; non-recognised schemes: employer contribution element fully taxable (s.9(1)(aa)). Deemed receipt when MPF voluntary benefit retained/transferred on termination (s.8(9)).

Ascertainment of income (ss.11B–11D) - s.11B: income accruing in YA; s.11D(b): accrues when entitled to claim payment; s.11D(a): accrued but not received → excluded until received (additional assessment for YA of accrual); income made available or dealt with on behalf = deemed received; received but not accrued (e.g. sign-on bonus subject to clawback, D26/07) not taxable until accrued. - s.11D(b)(i): lump sum on cessation / deferred pay may be related back (written application within 2 years after end of YA of payment); service > 3 years → spread evenly over last 36 months; not for leave pay.

Deductions (s.12) - s.12(1)(a): outgoings wholly, exclusively and necessarily incurred in the production of assessable income; not domestic/private; not capital. Home-office travel not deductible (CIR v Humphrey). Extra-statutory concessions (DIPN 9): one professional subscription (full membership prerequisite), uniformed staff laundry HK$2,400 flat, commission-based employees flat 10% of commission. - s.12(1)(b): depreciation allowances on plant/machinery essential to production of income; IA 60% (s.39B(1A)(c)); AA 30% reducing value; balancing charge on disposal (s.12(5)). - s.12(1)(c)/s.12A: loss carried forward; joint assessment: own income first, then spouse's (s.12A(4)). - s.12(1)(e): self-education expenses — gain/maintain qualifications for employment; prescribed course; claimed in YA PAID; reimbursed portion excluded; cap HK$100,000 (Sch 3A).

Concessionary deductions (Part 4A) — available under both progressive and standard rate: - s.26C approved charitable donations: money only, ≥ HK$100 aggregate, to s.88 charities/government; cap 35% of (assessable income − s.12(1)(a)/(b) deductions); one claim per donation (s.26C(3)). - s.26D elderly residential care expenses: cap HK$100,000 (Sch 3C) per parent/grandparent aged 60+ (or disability allowance eligible); HK licensed care home only; one claimant; mutually exclusive with DPA/DGA (ss.30(5), 30A(5)). - s.26E home loan interest: cap HK$100,000 (Sch 3D); max 20 YAs; HK dwelling, principal residence, secured loan for acquisition; joint owners by ownership share (s.26E(2)(b)/(c)); car park in same development included (s.26E(8)); nomination to spouse if no income (s.26F). - s.26G recognised retirement scheme contributions: cap HK$18,000 (Sch 3B). - s.26K VHIS qualifying premiums: cap HK$8,000 per insured specified relative (Sch 3E); multiple policyholders deemed equal shares (s.26K(2)). - ss.26O/26S QDAP annuity premiums + TVC: aggregate cap HK$60,000 (Sch 3F); TVC deducted first (s.26U(2)); TVC claimable only by the contributor.

Personal allowances (Part 5, Sch 4; 2019/20 & 2020/21; progressive-rate taxpayers only): - Basic s.28: 132,000 | Personal disability s.28A: 75,000 | Married person's s.29: 264,000 - DPA/DGA ss.30/30A: 50,000 (≥60) / 25,000 (55–59); additional (residing together, less than full valuable consideration): same amounts; parent must be ordinarily resident in HK; maintain = reside ≥6 months or contribute ≥ HK$12,000 (s.30(4)) - Dependent brother/sister s.30B: 37,500 | Child s.31: 120,000 (max 9 children); additional child in year of birth s.31(1A): 120,000; all child allowances claimed by one nominated spouse (s.31(3)) - Disabled dependant s.31A: 75,000 | Single parent s.32: 132,000 (first child only; sole or predominant care — Sit Kwok Keung v CIR) - Same dependant: one claimant only (s.33).

Computation (ss.12B, 13): - AI − s.12 deductions (+ balancing charge) = NAI; NAI − Part 4A concessionary deductions = NI; NI − Part 5 allowances = NCI. - Tax payable = lower of: progressive rates (Sch 2: 2%/6%/10%/14% on first 4 × 50,000; 17% balance) × NCI; or 15% (Sch 1) × NI. - Tax reduction: YA 2019/20 — 100% capped HK$20,000 (s.100(4)); YA 2020/21 — 100% capped HK$10,000 (s.100(1), Sch 43). Not applicable to PST. - Joint assessment election s.10 if beneficial; separate taxation default; same-sex marriage recognised (Leung Chun Kwong (2019) 22 HKCFAR 127). - PST (s.63B): based on preceding year's NCI/NI at current year's rates/allowances (ss.63C, 63CA); holdover s.63E — later of 28 days before due date / 14 days after payment notice; grounds incl. NCI likely < 90% of preceding year (s.63E(2)).

Ch5 Property Tax

Charge & rate - s.5(1) IRO — Property tax is charged for each year of assessment on every owner of any land or buildings, or land and buildings situated in Hong Kong, at the standard rate of 15% (2011/12 onwards) on the net assessable value (NAV). - Basis period of each year of assessment: 1 April – 31 March of the following year. - s.7A — "land and buildings" includes piers, wharves and other structures; "buildings" includes any part of a building, walls, advertising signs, lightboxes (DIPN 14). - s.2(1) — "owner" includes: holder directly from the Government; beneficial owner; tenant for life; mortgagor; mortgagee in possession; person with adverse title receiving rent; person paying a co-operative society (Cap.33) for purchase; holder subject to ground rent/annual charge; corporation registered under s.8 Building Management Ordinance (Cap.344) re common parts; executor of an owner's estate. - Residence status of owner is irrelevant; each separately rated property is assessed separately; joint owners are jointly assessed (each fully responsible, s.56A).

Assessable value (AV) - s.5B(2) — AV = consideration, in money or money's worth, payable to the owner for the right of use; accruals basis (outstanding rent still assessable). - s.5B(6) — consideration includes sums payable for services/benefits connected with the right of use (e.g. management fee paid to the owner; NOT fees payable by tenant to third-party management company). - AV includes: rent received/receivable; licence payments for right of use of premises (car park fee, wall/roof advertisements); furniture use payments; management fee paid to owner; non-refundable deposit (premium per DIPN 4; refundable deposits NOT assessable); owner's expenditure borne by tenant (unless owner repays); lump sum premium; rent recovered. - s.5B(4) — Lump sum premium spread in equal monthly instalments over the lease period or 3 years, whichever is shorter (spread includes rent-free periods). - s.7C(2) — Rent recovered (previously deducted as irrecoverable) is assessable in the year of recovery.

Deductions (only 3) - s.7C(1) — Irrecoverable rent deductible in the year proved to the satisfaction of the assessor (evidence required: bankruptcy, untraceable tenant); merely unpaid rent NOT deductible. s.7C(3) — excess carried backward to the immediate preceding year. - s.5(1A)(b)(i) — Rates deductible only if agreed and paid by the owner during the lease period; government rent never deductible. - s.5(1)(b)(ii) — 20% statutory deduction on the balance after irrecoverable rent and rates; deemed to cover ALL repairs/outgoings (actual repairs, management fees, mortgage loan interest disregarded).

Corporation relief (double taxation) - DIPN 14 — letting/sub-letting by a corporation, and sub-letting by any non-corporate person, amounts to a business → chargeable to profits tax (s.14); a sub-lessor is not an owner so no property tax on sub-letting income. - s.5(2)(a) — corporations ONLY (not individuals/sole proprietors/partnerships) may apply in writing to the CIR for exemption from property tax, per property; continues until circumstances change; s.5(2)(c) — notify change within 30 days. - s.25 — if no exemption applied, property tax paid is set off against profits tax for the same year of assessment; any excess refunded.

Provisional property tax - s.63L — payable at standard rate on the NAV of the preceding year, in two instalments (normally November and following April). Total payable = (final tax − provisional tax charged) + provisional tax for next year.

Owner's obligations (DIPN 14) - s.51(1) — file return within time limit (normally 1 month from issue). - s.51(2) — notify chargeability within 4 months after end of year of assessment if no return received. - s.51(6) — notify cessation of ownership within 1 month of sale/transfer. - s.51(8) — notify change of address within 1 month. - s.51D — keep sufficient rental records (agreements, duplicate rent receipts) in English or Chinese for not less than 7 years.

Ch6 Personal Assessment

Election / Eligibility (IRO s.41) - Personal assessment: a relief from tax, NOT a separate charge of tax; alternative assessment on aggregated total income. - Eligible: individual; aged 18+ (under 18 only if both parents dead); permanent resident (ordinarily resides — s.41(4); Lysaght v CIR; Cooper v Cadwalader; BoR D11/15) or temporary resident (>180 days in the YOA, or >300 days over 2 consecutive YOAs, one being the election year). - Up to 2017/18: married non-resident may elect if spouse is permanent/temporary resident. Executor may elect for eligible deceased taxpayer. - Election in writing (tax return section or Form IR76C). Deadline: later of (a) 2 years after end of YOA (e.g. 2017/18 → 31 Mar 2020); (b) 1 month after relevant assessment final & conclusive under s.70 (= effectively 2 months after issue); (c) further period at Commissioner's discretion. (DIPN 18 applies.)

Husband & Wife - Up to 2017/18: joint election required if both spouses have assessable income and both are eligible; relaxed if only one spouse has income / is eligible. From 2018/19: separate election allowed (joint election needs both to have assessable income; if jointly assessed under salaries tax, both must elect). - Couple marrying during the YOA deemed married at commencement of that year. - Tax computed on aggregated reduced total income; apportioned M1:M2 (ratio of individual reduced total incomes); separate notices of assessment issued.

Aggregation & Deductions (Computation Steps) - Total income = NAV (property) + NAI (employment, after self-education expenses, before concessionary deductions) + AP (business, after business losses b/f and ACD). - Deduct: interest on rental-property loan — capped at that property's NAV; excess cannot create a loss, be carried forward, or offset another property's NAV (D51/04). - Concessionary deductions: ACD (max 35% of [PA total income before business losses − rental mortgage interest + ACD allowed under profits tax + self-education expenses allowed under salaries tax], less ACD already allowed under profits tax; unused ACD transferable between spouses); ERCE; HLI; MPF/RRS contributions (2017/18 cap HK$18,000). - Business losses (current year + PA losses b/f; sole-proprietorship + share of partnership). Inter-spouse loss set-off under s.42(5) before carry-forward. Only the business-loss portion of any excess is carried forward; excess concessionary deductions lapse. Offshore business losses NOT deductible. - Personal allowances (2017/18): Basic HK$132,000; Married person's HK$264,000; Child HK$100,000 each.

Tax Payable — Lower of Two Bases - (a) Progressive rates on (reduced total income − personal allowances); OR (b) standard rate 15% on reduced total income BEFORE personal allowances. - Progressive rates 2017/18: first HK$45,000 @2%; next HK$45,000 @7%; next HK$45,000 @12% (first HK$135,000 = HK$9,450); remainder @17%. - One-off tax reduction: 75%, capped HK$10,000 (2012/13–13/14); HK$20,000 (2014/15–16/17); HK$30,000 (2017/18) — only ONE reduction per PA assessment (vs one per schedular assessment). - Not advantageous for high-income earners (marginal 17% > standard 15%); no benefit if only salaries income.

Ch7 Stamp Duty

Statute & general rules - Stamp Duty Ordinance (SDO) (Cap. 117); s.4 = general charging provision; First Schedule = Heads 1–4. Schedular & territorial tax on INSTRUMENTS, not transactions. - Substance over form: Limmer Asphalte Paving Co v IRC (1872); charge fixed at time of execution: William Cory & Son Ltd v IRC (1965). - s.2: not duly stamped unless correct duty; s.15: unstamped instrument inadmissible in civil proceedings (solicitor's undertaking exception s.15(1A)); s.15(2): cannot be registered/filed. - Ad valorem duty on HIGHER of consideration or value (s.27(4)); round duty up to nearest HK$1. Contingency principle: uncertain sum → maximum payable. - Non-cash consideration: stock = value at conveyance date (s.22(1)); other securities = principal + interest due (s.22(2)); debt waived = amount waived (s.24(1)), relief to use property value on adjudication (s.24(2)). Exchange = equality money only (s.29C(10)). - Liability: transferor & transferee jointly and severally; BSD = buyer only; Head 3 = issuer only.

Heads of charge (First Schedule) - Head 1(1): conveyance on sale / assignment of HK immovable property — AVD. - Head 1(1A): agreement for sale of HK immovable property (incl. provisional/conditional; options, declarations of trust passing beneficial interest — s.29A(1)) — AVD; stamp within 30 days (s.29B(3)); cancelled/rescinded agreement not chargeable (s.29C(5A)(a)). - Head 1(1) assignment conforming to stamped agreement for sale = fixed HK$100. - Head 1(2): lease / agreement for lease of HK immovable property (s.16(2)); lease executed pursuant to stamped agreement for lease = HK$3. - Head 2(1): sale & purchase of Hong Kong stock (contract notes, s.19) — ad valorem; Head 2(3): transfer of beneficial interest otherwise (e.g. gift); IoT = HK$5 when contract notes stamped. - Head 3: issue of Hong Kong bearer instruments — 3%; stamp before issue; subsequent transfers not dutiable. - Head 4: duplicates & counterparts — HK$5 each (or same as original if original duty ≤ HK$5); stamp within 7 days after original's time limit.

AVD rates (Head 1) - Scale 1 Part 1: residential, instruments on/after 5 Nov 2016 — flat 15% (companies, non-HKPRs, HKPRs owning other HK residential property; agents/trustees). - Scale 1 Part 2: marginal 1.5% → 8.5% (residential 23 Feb 2013–5 Nov 2016; non-residential 23 Feb 2013–26 Nov 2020). - Scale 2: marginal HK$100 (≤HK$2M) / 1.5% / 2.25% / 3% / 3.75% / 4.25% (top) — HKPR first home/switching (no other HK residential), close-relative transfers, court orders; non-residential on/after 26 Nov 2020.

SSD (Heads 1AA/1B; ss.29CA, 29DA) — residential acquired on/after 27 Oct 2012, disposed within 36 months; on higher of consideration/market value: - ≤6 months: 20% | >6–12 months: 15% | >12–36 months: 10% - Exempt: nomination to close relatives (spouse, parents, children, siblings); add/delete close-relative names; court-ordered sale/transfer. Acquisition/disposal date = chargeable agreement for sale date.

BSD (ss.29CB, 29DB) — residential; buyer not HKPR acting on own behalf; flat 15% on higher of consideration/value; buyer alone liable. - Exempt: no AVD chargeable; each purchaser HKPR acting on own behalf (regardless of other properties); s.45 relief; court order (incl. foreclosure); joint purchase with closely-related HKPR.

Lease rates (Head 1(2)) — rent rounded up to nearest HK$100; deposits ignored: - Term uncertain: 0.25% of yearly/average yearly rent - ≤1 year: 0.25% of total rent over term - >1–3 years: 0.5% of yearly/average yearly rent - >3 years: 1% of yearly/average yearly rent - Key money/premium: 4.25% of consideration if rent also payable; otherwise same duty as sale of immovable property

Hong Kong stock (Head 2) — stock whose transfer must be registered in HK (s.2): HK-incorporated company shares; HKSE-listed shares. Foreign-currency loan capital NOT HK stock. - Rate: aggregate 0.2% (0.1% each party) of higher of consideration/value; 0.26% effective 1 Aug 2021. Unlisted shares valued at net asset value per latest audited accounts. Issue of shares NOT dutiable. Gifts dutiable (Head 2(3)) at market value. - Time limits: contract notes 2 days (30 days if effected outside HK); Head 2(3) voluntary disposition 7 days (HK) / 30 days (abroad).

Voluntary disposition inter vivos (s.27) — gift/undervalue transfer deemed at market value. s.27(5) exceptions (no beneficial interest passes): new trustee appointment; liquidation/dissolution distribution (Ayerst v C&K (Construction) Ltd); trustee → beneficiary; nominal consideration securing loan repayment (mortgages not dutiable); vesting on merger (Nomura Funds Ireland v CSR). Settlement on trust (settlor → trustee) IS dutiable.

Exemptions & reliefs - s.45 group relief: associated bodies corporate — one beneficially owns ≥90% of other, or third body corporate owns ≥90% of each; tracing through layers allowed. Total exemption AVD+SSD+BSD; by adjudication (HK$50 fee); 2-year clawback rule. Arrowtown Assets (2004): worthless B shares fail purposive 90% test; Ramsay principle applies to stamp duty. - s.39: Government/public officer conveyances, Government grants & leases, Housing Authority instruments, bankruptcy/winding-up instruments (BO s.125; CWUMPO s.281). - s.43: consular/international organisation premises. s.44: gifts to recognised charities (not sales). s.47A: MPF scheme unit trust transfers.

Administration - Late stamping penalties (s.9): ≤1 month = 2× duty; >1–2 months = 4×; >2 months = 10×; Collector may remit. - Offences: s.11(2) fraud/intent to defraud; s.60 — fine level 6 (HK$100,000) + 1 year imprisonment; s.53 — officers of body corporate also liable; burden = beyond reasonable doubt. - Adjudication s.13 (executed instruments only; HK$50 fee); mandatory for s.24(2), s.27(3)/s.29F(2) gifts, s.45 relief, s.44(3) charity gifts, foreclosure orders. - Appeal s.14: to District Court within 1 month of assessment; "pay first, argue later" (security s.14(1A); hardship s.14(1B)); case stated — 7 days to set down for hearing, strict (Bangkok Capital Antique Co Ltd v CSR).

Ch8 Profits Tax Liabilities for Cross-border Transactions

Core principle — Territorial source (IRO s.14(1)) - Profits tax charged on every person (resident OR non-resident) carrying on a trade, profession or business in HK on profits arising in or derived from HK. Tax residency is NOT a chargeability factor; no distinction between residents/non-residents under the IRO; same rates for both. Residence matters only for CDTAs/DTAs. - "Non-resident" for s.20A/s.20B purposes (DIPN 17 (Revised) para. 8): person with no permanent business presence in HK. - Resident person (s.20AB(2)): individual ordinarily residing in HK, or >180 days in the YA, or >300 days over 2 consecutive YAs; company/partnership/trustee with central management and control in HK.

Permanent Establishment (PE) - IRR Rule 5: "a branch, management or other place of business"; agent NOT a PE unless he habitually exercises general authority to negotiate and conclude contracts for the principal, or has a stock of merchandise from which he regularly fills orders. - NOT a PE: storage/display/delivery only; maintenance of goods (incl. for processing by another enterprise); purchasing/advertising/information collection; preparatory or auxiliary activities; Representative Office (activities for the enterprise itself, not directly profit-generating, only supporting overseas parent). - PE definition: CDTA-territory residents → PE article of the relevant CDTA (prevails); non-CDTA residents → IRO Schedule 17G (introduced by Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No. 6) Ordinance 2018, effective 13 July 2018; follows 2017 OECD Model Tax Convention). - PE in HK → deemed carrying on business in HK; profits attributable to the PE are taxable. No PE ≠ exempt (may still be taxed under s.15 deeming provisions etc.).

Special provisions on non-residents - s.15(1)(a): exhibition/use in HK of cinematograph/TV film or tape, sound recordings, connected advertising material. - s.15(1)(b): use of/right to use in HK patents, designs, trademarks, copyright, secret process/formula (or imparting connected knowledge). - s.15(1)(ba): use of such IP outside HK where the sum is deductible to the HK payer (anti-avoidance; post-Emerson Radio; IRD practice effective 25 June 2004). - s.15(1)(bb): non-resident's sale/assignment of a performer's economic right. - s.15(1)(d): hire/rental charges for movable property used in HK. - s.20: business with closely connected resident persons. s.20AA: broker/investment adviser for non-residents deemed not an agent (DIPN 30). - s.21: assessable profits on a fair percentage of turnover. s.21A: deemed profits for s.15(1)(a)/(b)/(ba) = 30% (unrelated) / 100% (related AND IP previously owned by a person carrying on business in HK). - s.79(3): refund of tax overpaid — either the withholding person or the non-resident (not both) may claim.

Assessing non-residents — three methods: s.20A(1) direct assessment / in agent's name; s.20A(3) consignment tax; s.20B assessment via HK payer (royalties, licence fees, entertainers/sportsmen).

Consignment tax (s.20A(3)): non-resident selling goods in HK via HK agent → tax = 1% of gross HK sale proceeds (0.5% with Commissioner's approval); generally a final tax; agent files quarterly Form BIR52B, retains sufficient funds, indemnified (s.20A(2)); exemption if non-resident gives undertaking; non-resident may elect actual-profits assessment.

ATV case & s.20B: CIR v Asia Television Ltd (1987) 2 HKTC 198 — principal-to-principal payer is not an "agent" (s.2), so s.20A inapplicable → s.20B (effective 1 April 1989) charges the non-resident in the name of any HK person paying/crediting: (i) ss.15(1)(a)/(b)/(ba)/(bb) sums; (ii) sums for HK performances by non-resident entertainers/sportsmen on commercial occasions/events (s.20B(4): "a person other than a corporation"). s.20B(2): withhold at time of payment/credit even if paid to another non-resident (e.g. manager) or to another resident for the non-resident's account; s.20B(3): payer indemnified; retain tax until IRD demand note.

Withholding computations - Royalties etc. under ss.15(1)(a)/(b)/(ba): standard/corporate rate on 30% or 100% of the sum (s.21A). - Entertainer/sportsman performance fees: standard/corporate rate on 2/3 of the sum (s.21; DIPN 17 (Revised) para. 14 — 1/3 treated as allowable expenses); actual-profit election available with supporting documents. - Two-tiered rates (from YA 2018/19): corporations 8.25% / 16.5% (first HK$2M split); unincorporated/individuals 7.5% / 15%.

Branch profit computation - IRR Rule 5 (non-banks): (a) actual profit method (accounts disclose true HK profits); (b) proportionate method: Assessable profits = Adjusted worldwide profits × HK turnover / Worldwide turnover; (c) fair percentage of HK turnover (s.21) if (a)/(b) impracticable or inequitable. - IRR Rule 3 (banks): as above but ratio = HK assets / Worldwide assets. Objection under s.64(1).

Key cases - CIR v Emerson Radio Corporation (2000) HKRC 90-102: trademark royalties apportionable; affixing trademark in HK during manufacture = "use in HK" → taxable. IRD practice (from 25 June 2004): manufactured+sold offshore with mark affixed offshore → not assessable under (b) but watch (ba); mark affixed in HK → taxable; made & sold in HK → taxable; made offshore but sold in HK → taxable. - Lam Soon Trademark Ltd v CIR (2005) HKRC 90-171: strategic decisions, licence negotiations/agreements, trademark registration and protection all in HK → carrying on business in HK, royalty HK-sourced under s.14; s.60 additional assessment possible despite prior s.15(1)(b) charge.
