M9 Mock Examination 1 — Model Answers（完成測驗後先好睇！）

Question 1 — Correct Answer: HK$3,005

Model Answer (English): - Contract note (seller): HK$3,000,000 × 0.1% = HK$3,000 - Instrument of transfer: fixed HK$5 - Total stamp duty payable by the seller = 3,000 + 5 = HK$3,005

【解題思路】 - 第 3 次交手。口訣：「0.1% + HK$5」。0.1% = 0.001，唔係 0.01（1%）、唔係 0.026。 - 呢條再錯 = 直接罰抄「Stamp duty on a share transfer = 0.1% on the contract note plus fixed HK$5 on the instrument of transfer」五十次，冇得傾。

Question 2 — Correct Answer: (i) 31 March 2026 (ii) Yes (iii) s.60(1)

Model Answer (English): "Under s.60(1) IRO, an additional assessment must be raised within 6 years after the end of the year of assessment. YA 2019/20 ended on 31 March 2020 → deadline = 31 March 2026. An assessment raised in February 2026 is in time (Yes)."

【解題思路】 - 步驟固定：YA 完結日（31 Mar 2020）+ 6 年 = 死線；issued 日期早過或等於死線 = Yes。 - 條文三寶齊全先攞滿分：日期 + Yes/No + s.60(1)。

Question 3 — Correct Answer: (i) 31 March 2026 (ii) Yes (iii) s.60(1) proviso

Model Answer (English): "Under the proviso to s.60(1) IRO, where there is fraud or wilful evasion, the time limit is extended to 10 years after the end of the year of assessment. YA 2015/16 ended on 31 March 2016 → deadline = 31 March 2026. An assessment raised in March 2026 is in time (Yes)."

【解題思路】 - 見到 fraud / wilful evasion 關鍵字 → 即刻轉十年檔。同 Q2 對照：同樣死線 2026，但一個係 6 年檔一個係 10 年檔——考試就係咁考你分唔分到。

Question 4 — Correct Answer: B

Model Answer (English): "Under s.16(1) IRO, expenses are deductible to the extent they are incurred in the production of assessable profits. Rent for business premises qualifies. Fines for criminal offences are not incurred in the production of profits; capital expenditure is excluded by s.17(1)(c); domestic expenses are excluded by s.17(1)(a)."

【解題思路】 - 罰款、資本開支、私人開支 = 唔扣得三大類。s.16(1) 係大閘、s.17 係負面清單。

Question 5 — Correct Answer: B

Model Answer (English): "Compensation for the termination of a sole distributorship agreement representing substantially the whole of the company's business is capital in nature — it compensates for the sterilisation of the entire profit-making structure, not merely for lost profits."

【解題思路】 - Sole agreement = capital；眾多協議中其中一份 = revenue。關鍵字 "sole" / "substantially the whole business" 一見就反射 capital。

Question 6 — Correct Answer: HK$80,000 — s.20A(3)

Model Answer (English): "Under s.20A(3) IRO, where the actual Hong Kong profits of a non-resident from consignment sales cannot be readily ascertained, the assessable profits are deemed to be 1% of the gross proceeds from sales to Hong Kong customers: 1% × 8,000,000 = HK$80,000."

【解題思路】 - 1% 唔係 10%。條文寫全 s.20A(3)（唔好寫 s.20(A)、唔好估 s.15(5)）。

Question 7 — Correct Answer: B

Model Answer (English): "Under the two-tiered profits tax rates, a corporation pays 8.25% on the first HK$2,000,000 of assessable profits and 16.5% on the remainder: 2,000,000 × 8.25% + 500,000 × 16.5% = 165,000 + 82,500 = HK$247,500."

【解題思路】 - 公司：8.25% / 16.5%；unincorporated（個人/獨資）先係 7.5% / 15%。見到 corporation 反射 8.25%。

Question 8 — Correct Answer: HK$60,000

Model Answer (English): "Under DIPN 21, sums paid to a non-resident entertainer for a performance in Hong Kong are chargeable on a withholding basis; assessable profits are deemed to be 2/3 of the gross fee: 2/3 × 1,200,000 = 800,000. As the entertainer is an individual (unincorporated), the two-tiered rate of 7.5% applies: 800,000 × 7.5% = HK$60,000."

【解題思路】 - 兩步：deemed 2/3 → 再按身份揀率。Individual = 7.5%；corporation = 8.25%。身份睇錯就全條錯（你中過）。

Question 9 — Correct Answer: HK$72,000 — s.9(2)

Model Answer (English): "Under s.9(2) IRO, the rental value of a rent-free residence provided by an employer is 10% of the employee's income from the employer: 10% × 720,000 = HK$72,000."

【解題思路】 - 永遠 10% of income——唔係實際租金、唔係差餉估值。條文 s.9(2)（唔好寫 s.9(1) 或 s.26(G)）。

Question 10 — Correct Answer: B

Model Answer (English): "Following the principle in Glynn, an education benefit provided by an employer — school fees for the employee's child paid directly to the school — is an assessable benefit in kind under s.9(2A)(b) IRO, even though it is not paid to the employee and cannot be converted into cash."

【解題思路】 - Education benefit 係「convertible into cash」原則嘅法定例外——直付學校都照課。見 school fees / education 反射 taxable。

Question 11 — Correct Answer: HK$900,000 — s.8(1A)(a)

Model Answer (English): "Under s.8(1A)(a) IRO, where services are rendered partly in and partly outside Hong Kong, assessable income is time-apportioned: income × days in Hong Kong / 365 = 1,095,000 × 300/365 = HK$900,000."

【解題思路】 - 乘 留港日數（300），唔係離港日數（65）——你 W8 就係方向倒轉錯呢度。數完日數問自己：留港多啲 → 應課稅應該大份。

Question 12 — Correct Answer: HK$18,000 — s.26G

Model Answer (English): "Under s.26G IRO, the deduction for mandatory MPF contributions is capped at HK$18,000 per year. Contributions of HK$21,000 exceed the cap → deduction = HK$18,000."

【解題思路】 - 全年 cap 18,000（= 每月 1,500 × 12）。實際供款超 cap → 封頂。條文 s.26G。

Question 13 — Correct Answer: HK$175,000 — s.26C

Model Answer (English): "Under s.26C IRO, the deduction for approved charitable donations is capped at 35% of income (before the deduction): 35% × 500,000 = 175,000. Donations of 200,000 exceed the cap → deduction = HK$175,000."

【解題思路】 - 兩關：實捐 200,000 vs cap 175,000 → 取細者。個 % 係 35%，唔係 10%、唔係 15%。

Question 14 — Correct Answer: HK$278,400

Model Answer (English): "Assessable value = rent 30,000 × 12 = 360,000, less rates paid by the owner 12,000 = 348,000. NAV = 348,000 × 80% (after the 20% statutory deduction for repairs and outgoings) = HK$278,400."

【解題思路】 - 次序：年租 − 業主付嘅差餉 → × 80%。業主冇付差餉就唔好減；20% 係法定定額，唔使問實際維修使咗幾多。

Question 15 — Correct Answer: HK$40,000 per year — s.5B(4)

Model Answer (English): "Under s.5B(4) IRO, a premium is spread evenly over the lease term (up to a maximum of 36 months): 120,000 ÷ 3 years = HK$40,000 per year to be included in the assessable value (before the 20% statutory deduction)."

【解題思路】 - Premium 唔係一 take 過課——按租期攤（封頂 36 個月）。3 年 = 36 個月啱啱好。

Question 16 — Correct Answer: A

Model Answer (English): "Personal assessment is available only to individuals who are Hong Kong permanent residents (or temporary residents) aged 18 or above. Companies and partnerships cannot elect; a non-resident with no Hong Kong income has nothing to assess."

【解題思路】 - PA 係個人嘅評稅方式，用嚟攞返免稅額/扣減。有限公司、合夥、非居民冇得揀。

Question 17 — Correct Answer: HK$1,800,000

Model Answer (English): "A non-Hong Kong permanent resident buyer pays AVD at the flat rate of 15% (Scale 1) plus BSD at 15%: AVD = 6,000,000 × 15% = 900,000; BSD = 6,000,000 × 15% = 900,000; total = HK$1,800,000."

【解題思路】 - Scale 2 優惠只限 HKPR 首置。非 HKPR = 15% + 15% 雙重。身份係第一道閘——睇錯身份全條錯（你嘅舊債）。

Question 18 — Correct Answer: C

Model Answer (English): "SSD rates by holding period: 6 months or less = 20%; more than 6 up to 12 months = 15%; more than 12 up to 36 months = 10%; more than 36 months = nil. 18 months falls in the third band → 10%."

【解題思路】 - 三檔口訣 20 / 15 / 10，邊界係 6、12、36 個月。啱啱 12 個月 = 15%（W9 考過）；18 個月 = 10%。

Question 19 — Correct Answer: B

Model Answer (English): "The source of an office is located where the company is centrally managed and controlled — not where the director happens to attend meetings, resides, or is paid."

【解題思路】 - Office（directorship）同 employment 嘅 source test 唔同：office 睇 central management and control；employment 先睇合約喺邊度傾/執行/出糧。

Question 20 — Correct Answer: C

Model Answer (English): "There is NO withholding tax on Hong Kong source dividends and interest. Only royalties (for intellectual property used in Hong Kong) and fees of non-resident entertainers/sportsmen are taxed on a withholding basis."

【解題思路】 - 見到 "withhold + dividends/interest" 嘅組合，八成都係錯。Royalties 同表演者費用先係香港嘅 withholding 範圍。
