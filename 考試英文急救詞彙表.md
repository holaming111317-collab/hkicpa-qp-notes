
### 第二十二部分：M7 Ch13 投資評估（10/13 追加）

**核心術語**
- mutually exclusive projects 互斥項目：揀 **higher NPV**（唔係 higher IRR）
- sunk cost 沉沒成本（唔相關）vs opportunity cost 機會成本（相關）
- net working capital (NWC)：**OUT at t₀, recovered (added back, discounted) at the end**
- discounted payback period：**逐年折現累積**＋尾段插值（fraction = shortfall ÷ next year's discounted CF）；≥ simple payback；兩者都唔計 cut-off 後嘅現金流
- cost-plus 謹記：profit **只加一次**（total cost + desired profit = target price）

**Model Sentence（背熟）**
> "For mutually exclusive projects, select the one with the higher NPV — it represents the larger increase in firm value."
